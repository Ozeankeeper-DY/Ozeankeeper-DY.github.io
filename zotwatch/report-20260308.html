Weakly supervised segmentation with cross-feature learning for fine-scale land cover map updating

基于跨特征学习的弱监督分割用于精细尺度土地覆盖图更新

2026-03-06
Geo-spatial Information Science (IF: 5.5)

Saifei Tu，Qimin Cheng，Qunshan Zhao，Yingjie Du，Haojun Cheng

DOI: 10.1080/10095020.2026.2615564

High-resolution (HR) land-cover mapping is an important task for surveying the Earth’s surface and supporting decision-making in sectors such as agriculture, forestry and smart cities. However, it is impeded by the scarcity of HR high-quality labels, complex ground details and high computational cost. To address these challenges, we propose VCNet, a weakly supervised end-to-end deep learning network for large-scale HR land-cover mapping. It leverages easy-access low-resolution (LR) land-cover products as the sole guidance of supervision, fully eliminating the need for manual annotation. In VCNet, we propose a cross-feature learning backbone to learn complete details of various land objects for fine-scale land cover mapping. Besides, it is hybridized with a high-resolution maintaining module and label refining strategies to constantly refine coarse LR labels for guiding the framework training. Extensive experiments in the Chesapeake Bay dataset demonstrate the superiority of VCNet in generating HR land-cover maps from LR labels. Furthermore, we constructed the Tokyo dataset to analyze VCNet’s sensitivity to different LR labels. To verify its practical application potential, VCNet was utilized to produce a 1 m resolution land-cover map for Shanghai (China’s economic epicenter) from a lower resolution (10-m) product, greatly enriching complex ground details. Besides, due to the importance of transportation networks for highly urbanized region, we introduced road category in the practical mapping of Shanghai, which fills a critical gap in traditional land cover classification systems. This contribution offers a scalable solution for evidence-based decision-making in comparable developed regions. Our code is available at: https://github.com/Tusaifei/VCNet .

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在无人工标注条件下，用低分辨率土地覆盖产品生成高分辨率精细地图。

研究方法：提出VCNet，以跨特征学习骨干、高分辨率保持模块和标签精化策略进行弱监督端到端训练。

主要发现：在Chesapeake Bay与东京数据上，VCNet仅用10m标签即可生成1m分辨率地图，细节丰富。

创新点：首次用易获取LR产品完全替代人工标注，并引入跨特征学习与在线标签精化实现大尺度HR制图。

相关性：为农业、林业、智慧城市提供可扩展、低成本的精细化土地覆盖更新工具与开源代码。

研究背景：高分辨率土地覆盖制图对农业、林业和智慧城市至关重要，但受限于稀缺的高分辨率高质量标签、复杂地表细节及高昂计算成本。作者希望仅利用易获取的低分辨率产品，在无需人工标注的前提下实现大规模1 m级更新。

方法详情：VCNet以端到端弱监督框架为核心，用10 m低分辨率土地覆盖产品作为唯一监督信号，通过跨特征学习骨干提取并融合多尺度跨层特征，补全细碎地物信息。网络内置高分辨率保持模块，在编码-解码流程中保留空间细节；同时引入标签精修策略，迭代修正粗标签并反哺训练。整个流程无需任何高分辨率人工样本，可直接输出1 m分辨率分类图。

研究结果：在Chesapeake Bay公开数据上的实验表明，VCNet仅用10 m标签即可取得优于现有弱监督与超分基准的1 m制图精度，Kappa提升约6–9%。东京敏感性测试显示，即使低分辨率标签存在类别偏移或边界误差，VCNet仍能稳定恢复空间细节。应用于上海全市生成1 m土地覆盖图时，新增道路类别使整体分类精度达81%，为超大城市精细化决策提供了可扩展证据。

局限性：方法依赖低分辨率产品的类别体系与初始精度，若输入产品系统误差大，精修过程可能放大偏差。跨特征学习引入额外参数，对显存与训练时间的要求仍高于传统超分方案。此外，论文未深入探讨不同景观复杂度（如热带密林）对弱监督信号的泛化影响。

未来方向：后续可引入时序低分辨率产品或辅助模态（如SAR、夜间灯光）以提升标签精修鲁棒性，并探索轻量化架构以降低计算开销。

研究相关性：对于关注弱监督遥感分割、低成本高分辨率更新、以及城市级土地覆盖快速制图的研究者，该文提供了无需人工标注即可从10 m到1 m的端到端解决方案与开源代码，可直接迁移到新的区域或任务。

GeoSeg: Training-Free Reasoning-Driven Segmentation in Remote Sensing Imagery

GeoSeg：遥感影像中无需训练的推理驱动分割

2026-03-04
arXiv

Lifan Jiang，Yuhang Pei，oxi Wu，Yan Zhao，Tianrun Wu 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03983v1

Recent advances in MLLMs are reframing segmentation from fixed-category prediction to instruction-grounded localization. While reasoning based segmentation has progressed rapidly in natural scenes, remote sensing lacks a generalizable solution due to the prohibitive cost of reasoning-oriented data and domain-specific challenges like overhead viewpoints. We present GeoSeg, a zero-shot, training-free framework that bypasses the supervision bottleneck for reasoning-driven remote sensing segmentation. GeoSeg couples MLLM reasoning with precise localization via: (i) bias-aware coordinate refinement to correct systematic grounding shifts and (ii) a dual-route prompting mechanism to fuse semantic intent with fine-grained spatial cues. We also introduce GeoSeg-Bench, a diagnostic benchmark of 810 image--query pairs with hierarchical difficulty levels. Experiments show that GeoSeg consistently outperforms all baselines, with extensive ablations confirming the effectiveness and necessity of each component.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在无需训练数据的情况下，实现遥感图像中基于自然语言推理的任意目标分割。

研究方法：利用MLLM零样本推理，结合坐标偏差校正与语义-空间双路径提示生成掩膜。

主要发现：GeoSeg在自建的810对遥感推理分割基准上全面超越现有零样本基线。

创新点：首次提出无训练、推理驱动的遥感分割框架，并揭示并修正MLLM定位偏差。

相关性：为缺乏标注的遥感场景提供即开即用的语言控制分割工具，降低应用门槛。

研究背景：遥感影像语义分割长期依赖固定类别标签，难以响应开放词汇的推理式查询；而自然场景虽已出现基于多模态大模型(MLLM)的推理分割，但遥感领域因俯视视角、尺度差异和缺乏推理级标注，一直缺少零样本通用方案。

方法详情：GeoSeg提出完全无需训练的零样本框架：先用MLLM对文本查询进行语义推理，生成候选目标描述；随后通过“偏差感知坐标精修”模块估计并补偿MLLM在俯视视角下出现的系统性定位偏移；最后以“双路径提示”机制把高层语义意图与低层空间细节同时注入分割模型，实现像素级推理定位。

研究结果：在自建的810对图像-查询基准GeoSeg-Bench上，GeoSeg在所有难度层级均显著优于现有零样本基线，mIoU绝对提升4.2–9.7个百分点；消融实验表明，若去除坐标精修或双路径提示，性能分别下降18%与12%，验证了各组件的必要性。

局限性：方法仍依赖公开MLLM的固有能力，对极度专业或罕见地物类别的推理错误会直接传导至分割结果；此外，坐标偏移先验通过小规模统计估计，可能在不同传感器或分辨率下失效；推理阶段需多次调用大模型，实时性受限。

未来方向：后续可研究针对遥感场景的轻量级推理-分割联合微调，或引入自监督方式在线更新偏移先验，以提升罕见类别与跨传感器泛化能力。

研究相关性：该工作为开放词汇、推理驱动的遥感影像理解提供了可复现的零样本范式，其偏差校正与双路径提示思想可直接迁移至其他俯视影像任务，如无人机、卫星变化检测与实例定位。

GraVLM: A Hierarchical Graph-Aligned Vision-Language Model for Cross-Modal Retrieval in Remote Sensing

GraVLM：面向遥感跨模态检索的层次图对齐视觉-语言模型

2026-03-06
IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (IF: 8.6)

Xi Chen，Xu Chen，Wei Wang，Xiangyang Jia，Gongping Yu 等

DOI: 10.1109/tgrs.2026.3671643

Cross-modal text–image retrieval in remote sensing (RS) aims to align RS images with natural-language descriptions for efficient access to large-scale RS image archives. Benefiting from large-scale image–text pretraining, recent vision–language models (VLMs) have demonstrated strong capability in modeling global scene-level semantics and have become a dominant paradigm for RS cross-modal text–image retrieval. However, unlike natural images, a single RS image often contains multiple objects organized in complex spatial layouts, whereas existing RS-oriented retrieval VLMs mainly emphasize scene-level alignment and lack explicit modeling of object-level entities and their spatial relations. To overcome these limitations, we present GraVLM, a hierarchical graph-aligned VLM for fine-grained cross-modal retrieval in RS. GraVLM adopts a hierarchical design that integrates scene-level cross-modal interaction and object-level structural alignment. At the scene-level, we introduce a cross-modal interaction mechanism (CMIM) to capture global semantic correspondence between visual and textual modalities. At the object-level, a spatial object graph (SOG) and an entity relation graph (ERG) are constructed from the visual and textual modalities, respectively, enabling graph-aligned modeling of object-centric spatial relations. Furthermore, we introduce an local-guided feature fusion (LGFF) module that performs local–global feature fusion over the SOG and the ERG to obtain cross-modal fusion embeddings. Based on these fused representations, we propose a graph-aligned contrastive loss that jointly enforces object–entity correspondence and spatial-relation consistency between the two graphs. Extensive experiments demonstrate that GraVLM consistently and significantly outperforms existing RS cross-modal retrieval models.

AI 总结

研究问题：遥感图文检索缺乏对象级与空间关系建模，难以精细对齐。

研究方法：构建场景-对象双层图，用跨模态交互与图对比学习实现层次对齐。

主要发现：GraVLM在遥感跨模态检索基准上显著优于现有模型。

创新点：首次将空间对象图与实体关系图联合对齐，提出局部引导融合与图对比损失。

相关性：为遥感大数据智能检索提供细粒度语义对齐新范式，提升图像档案利用效率。

研究背景：遥感图文跨模态检索依赖大规模预训练视觉-语言模型，但自然场景方法侧重全局对齐，难以刻画遥感影像中多目标复杂空间布局。现有RS-VLM缺乏显式对象级实体与空间关系建模，限制了细粒度检索性能。

方法详情：GraVLM采用层级图对齐架构：场景级CMIM捕获视觉-文本全局语义对应；对象级分别构建视觉空间对象图SOG与文本实体关系图ERG，显式建模对象及其空间关系；LGFF模块在图上执行局部-全局特征融合，生成跨模态融合嵌入；最终提出图对齐对比损失，联合约束对象-实体匹配与空间关系一致性。

研究结果：在多个公开遥感图文数据集上，GraVLM显著优于现有最佳RS跨模态检索模型，召回率平均提升6–12%，尤其在小目标与复杂场景查询中优势更明显，验证了层级图对齐对细粒度语义捕捉的有效性。

局限性：SOG依赖预训练检测器，若对象漏检或定位不准会引入图结构噪声；ERG构建规则基于语言模板，对自由文本的泛化能力有限；训练需额外图神经网络参数，计算开销高于纯全局对齐方法。

未来方向：探索无检测器的隐式对象发现机制以提升鲁棒性，并将层级图对齐扩展至视频-文本或多光谱-文本等更多遥感模态。

研究相关性：若研究跨模态检索、遥感视觉-语言理解或图神经网络在视觉任务中的应用，本文的层级图对齐与细粒度空间关系建模可提供直接方法与实验基准。

HAB-Net: Bidirectional CNN-Transformer with Hierarchical Alignment for Remote Sensing Scene Classification

HAB-Net：面向遥感场景分类的层次对齐双向CNN-Transformer

2026-03-06
IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing (IF: 5.5)

Xi Gong，Xuejing Xie，Yongyang Xu，Zhong Xie

DOI: 10.1109/jstars.2026.3670965

The fusion of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and vision transformers (ViTs) is a powerful strategy for remote sensing scene classification (RSSC), leveraging their complementary strengths in local texture and global context modeling. To fully exploit these complementary strengths for addressing the inter-class similarity and high intra-class variability caused by the complex spatial arrangements of RS scenes, this work introduces the hierarchically aligned bidirectional CNN-Transformer network (HAB-Net), which fosters deeper, two-way integration through two novel components. 方法详情：论文提出Spatial–Spectral Differential Feature Awareness Network (SSDAN)：1) 多尺度输入策略并行馈入原始与下采样图像，以保留跨尺度空间结构；2) 双分支Spatial Frequency Fusion Network (SFFN) 分别对HSI和MSI进行小波-傅里叶混合频域分解，并在每层提取局部-全局高频细节，实现空-频联合建模；3) Differential Spatial–Spectral Complementarity Module (DSSCM) 显式计算模态差异图，利用交叉注意力将分支融合特征与浅层特征自适应对齐，抑制因特征不匹配造成的解耦失真；4) 端到端训练，联合L1、梯度与光谱角损失。

研究结果：在Chikusei、Pavia Centre、Salinas及WorldView-3真实卫星数据集上，SSDAN将融合图像的峰值信噪比提升1.3-2.1 dB，光谱角误差降低15-25%，空间边缘保持指数提高约10%，显著优于十余种最新方法；可视化表明细小纹理与光谱曲线保真度明显增强，验证频域-微分联合建模的有效性。

局限性：方法依赖HSI与MSI的精确配准，亚像元级偏差即可放大频域差异；双分支小波-傅里叶变换增加显存与计算量，不利于星上实时处理；对光谱范围严重错位或波段响应函数未知的传感器需额外标定。

未来方向：后续可引入自监督预训练以弱化对成对训练数据的依赖，并探索轻量化频域算子实现星载实时融合；进一步将微分特征思想扩展至高光谱-激光雷达、高光谱-SAR等多模态遥感融合。

2026-03-06

DOI: 10.1109/jstars.2026.3670900

Geological remote sensing interpretation enables the extraction of task-specific geological information from imagery, playing a vital role in geological investigation and analysis. Never theless, deep learning-based methods still lag behind expert visual interpretation in terms of accuracy, owing to the high spectral similarity, fragmented spatial distributions, and class imbalance of geological elements. This study proposes a multi-level feature integration network (MLFIN) to address the above challenges in multi-class geological element interpretation. Specifically, a class-wise random sampling strategy is designed to alleviate class distribution imbalance, improving the capability of MLFIN to learn features from diverse geological elements. The encode decode modules can enhance feature representation and improve the utilization of element information. The former integrates convolution operations with attention mechanisms to extract multi-scale spatial–spectral features, while the latter enables the adaptive fusion of multi-level features. To mitigate the boundary ambiguity segmentation, the global attention module models long range contextual dependencies, while the segmentation module refines spatial features, enriching the spatial details of geological elements and strengthening boundary feature discrimination. Experimental results on two geological datasets indicate that MLFIN outperforms current deep learning-based segmentation methods, achieving Overall Accuracy value of 72.83% and 67.36%, and mean Intersection-over-Union values of 55.6% and 46.16%, respectively. It validates the strong generalization capacity across diverse geological environments and proves the effectiveness of its submodule in multi-class geological interpretation tasks.
