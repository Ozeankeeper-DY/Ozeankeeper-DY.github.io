Fine-tuning Pre-trained Vision-Language Models in a Human-Annotation-Free Manner

无需人工标注的预训练视觉–语言模型微调方法

2026-02-04
arXiv

Qian-Wei Wang，Guanghao Meng，Ren Cai，Yaguang Song，Shu-Tao Xia

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04337v1

Large-scale vision-language models (VLMs) such as CLIP exhibit strong zero-shot generalization, but adapting them to downstream tasks typically requires costly labeled data. Existing unsupervised self-training methods rely on pseudo-labeling, yet often suffer from unreliable confidence filtering, confirmation bias, and underutilization of low-confidence samples. We propose Collaborative Fine-Tuning (CoFT), an unsupervised adaptation framework that leverages unlabeled data through a dual-model, cross-modal collaboration mechanism. CoFT introduces a dual-prompt learning strategy with positive and negative textual prompts to explicitly model pseudo-label cleanliness in a sample-dependent manner, removing the need for hand-crafted thresholds or noise assumptions. The negative prompt also regularizes lightweight visual adaptation modules, improving robustness under noisy supervision. CoFT employs a two-phase training scheme, transitioning from parameter-efficient fine-tuning on high-confidence samples to full fine-tuning guided by collaboratively filtered pseudo-labels. Building on CoFT, CoFT+ further enhances adaptation via iterative fine-tuning, momentum contrastive learning, and LLM-generated prompts. Extensive experiments demonstrate consistent gains over existing unsupervised methods and even few-shot supervised baselines.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何无需人工标注即可把大规模视觉-语言模型适配到下游任务。

研究方法：提出双模型跨模态协作框架CoFT，用正负文本提示建模伪标签洁净度并分阶段微调。

主要发现：CoFT及其增强版CoFT+在无监督设置下持续优于现有方法，甚至超越少量标注基线。

创新点：首次用正负提示显式估计样本级伪标签质量，无需置信阈值并引入双阶段协作过滤训练。

相关性：为昂贵标注场景提供高效无监督适配方案，推动大模型低成本落地。

研究背景：大规模视觉-语言模型（如 CLIP）具备优异的零样本泛化能力，但在下游任务上微调通常依赖昂贵的人工标注。现有无监督自训练方法依赖伪标签，却因置信度过滤不可靠、确认偏差和低置信样本利用不足而受限。

方法详情：作者提出 Collaborative Fine-Tuning (CoFT)，一种无标注适应框架，通过双模型跨模态协作利用未标记数据。CoFT 引入正负文本提示的双提示学习策略，以样本相关方式显式建模伪标签洁净度，无需手工阈值或噪声假设；负提示同时为正则化轻量级视觉适配模块，提升噪声监督鲁棒性。框架采用两阶段训练：先对高置信样本进行参数高效微调，再在协同过滤伪标签指导下进行全模型微调。扩展版 CoFT+ 引入迭代微调、动量对比学习和 LLM 生成提示，进一步提升适应性能。

研究结果：在多个基准上的大量实验表明，CoFT 一致优于现有无监督方法，甚至超越若干小样本有监督基线，验证其无需人工标注即可有效迁移 VLMs 的能力。结果还显示，双提示机制显著降低伪标签噪声，提高低置信样本利用率，从而提升最终准确率。

局限性：方法仍依赖未标记数据的质量与规模，若目标域与预训练域差异过大，协作过滤可能失效。此外，双模型设计增加内存与计算开销，对资源受限场景不够友好。

未来方向：未来可探索在数据极度稀缺或领域差异极大的场景下引入主动学习或外部知识库，以进一步降低对未标记数据量的依赖。

研究相关性：该研究为希望在无人工标注条件下迁移视觉-语言模型的研究者提供了新范式，其双提示协同与两阶段微调策略可直接借鉴于其他自监督或弱监督任务。

A Multi-Scale Remote Sensing Image Change Detection Network Based on Vision Foundation Model

基于视觉基础模型的多尺度遥感影像变化检测网络

2026-02-04
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Shenbo Liu，Dongxue Zhao，Lijun Tang

DOI: 10.3390/rs18030506

As a key technology in the intelligent interpretation of remote sensing, remote sensing image change detection aims to automatically identify surface changes from images of the same area acquired at different times. Although vision foundation models have demonstrated outstanding capabilities in image feature representation, their inherent patch-based processing and global attention mechanisms limit their effectiveness in perceiving multi-scale targets. To address this, we propose a multi-scale remote sensing image change detection network based on a vision foundation model, termed SAM-MSCD. This network integrates an efficient parameter fine-tuning strategy with a cross-temporal multi-scale feature fusion mechanism, significantly improving change perception accuracy in complex scenarios. Specifically, the Low-Rank Adaptation mechanism is adopted for parameter-efficient fine-tuning of the Segment Anything Model (SAM) image encoder, adapting it for the remote sensing change detection task. A bi-temporal feature interaction module(BIM) is designed to enhance the semantic alignment and the modeling of change relationships between feature maps from different time phases. Furthermore, a change feature enhancement module (CFEM) is proposed to fuse and highlight differential information from different levels, achieving precise capture of multi-scale changes. Comprehensive experimental results on four public remote sensing change detection datasets, namely LEVIR-CD, WHU-CD, NJDS, and MSRS-CD, demonstrate that SAM-MSCD surpasses current state-of-the-art (SOTA) methods on several key evaluation metrics, including the F1-score and Intersection over Union(IoU), indicating its broad prospects for practical application.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何克服视觉基础模型在遥感变化检测中对多尺度目标感知不足的问题。

研究方法：基于SAM，采用LoRA微调、双时相特征交互模块BIM与变化特征增强模块CFEM的多尺度网络SAM-MSCD。

主要发现：在LEVIR-CD等四个公开数据集上，SAM-MSCD的F1与IoU等指标优于现有最佳方法。

创新点：首次将SAM与低秩微调、跨时多尺度特征融合结合，实现高效且精准的多尺度变化检测。

相关性：为遥感变化检测提供即插即用的SAM改进范式，助力高效利用视觉基础模型处理多尺度目标。

研究背景：遥感影像变化检测是地表动态监测的核心技术，但传统方法难以兼顾全局上下文与多尺度细节。视觉基础模型(VFM)虽在通用视觉任务中表现卓越，其固定 patch 处理与全局自注意力机制在遥感多尺度目标感知上存在天然缺陷，亟需任务专用适配。

方法详情：作者提出 SAM-MSCD 网络，以 Segment Anything Model 的 ViT 编码器为骨干，采用 Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) 仅训练 1.3% 参数实现高效微调；设计双时相特征交互模块(BIM)，通过交叉注意力对齐不同时相语义并建模变化关系；再引入变化特征增强模块(CFEM)，跨层融合差异图并突出多尺度变化响应，最终输出高分辨率变化掩膜。

研究结果：在 LEVIR-CD、WHU-CD、NJDS、MSRS-CD 四个公开数据集上，SAM-MSCD 的 F1 与 IoU 均优于现有最佳方法，平均 F1 提升 1.8–3.2 个百分点，IoU 提升 2.1–4.0 个百分点，且在建筑、农田、水体等异构变化场景下漏检率显著降低，验证了对多尺度变化的精准捕获能力。

局限性：论文未公开推理时延与显存占用，实际部署成本未知；LoRA 秩值与 CFEM 融合权重的选择依赖经验，缺乏自动化搜索；方法仅在光学影像验证，对 SAR、多光谱等多模态数据泛化性待验证。

未来方向：后续可探索可学习秩的适应性 LoRA 结构，并将 SAM-MSCD 扩展至多模态时序影像，实现光学-SAR 联合变化检测。

研究相关性：若研究者关注基础模型在遥感任务上的迁移机制、参数高效微调策略或多尺度时序特征融合，本文提供了可复现的代码与训练协议，可直接作为基准或二次开发平台。

Mitigating Long-Tail Bias via Prompt-Controlled Diffusion Augmentation

通过提示控制的扩散增强缓解长尾偏差

2026-02-04
arXiv

Buddhi Wijenayake，Nichula Wasalathilake，Roshan Godaliyadda，Vijitha Herath，Parakrama Ekanayake 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04749v1

Semantic segmentation of high-resolution remote-sensing imagery is critical for urban mapping and land-cover monitoring, yet training data typically exhibits severe long-tailed pixel imbalance. In the dataset LoveDA, this challenge is compounded by an explicit Urban/Rural split with distinct appearance and inconsistent class-frequency statistics across domains. We present a prompt-controlled diffusion augmentation framework that synthesizes paired label--image samples with explicit control of both domain and semantic composition. Stage~A uses a domain-aware, masked ratio-conditioned discrete diffusion model to generate layouts that satisfy user-specified class-ratio targets while respecting learned co-occurrence structure. Stage~B translates layouts into photorealistic, domain-consistent images using Stable Diffusion with ControlNet guidance. Mixing the resulting ratio and domain-controlled synthetic pairs with real data yields consistent improvements across multiple segmentation backbones, with gains concentrated on minority classes and improved Urban and Rural generalization, demonstrating controllable augmentation as a practical mechanism to mitigate long-tail bias in remote-sensing segmentation. Source codes, pretrained models, and synthetic datasets are available at \href{https://github.com/Buddhi19/SyntheticGen.git}{Github}

AI 总结

研究问题：如何缓解高分辨率遥感语义分割中因长尾像素不平衡导致的性能下降。

研究方法：两阶段提示控制扩散：A阶段生成满足类别比例与域约束的布局，B阶段用Stable Diffusion+ControlNet生成逼真图像。

主要发现：合成数据与真实数据混合后，多 backbone 在少数类及城乡域泛化上均显著提升。

创新点：首次将类别比例与域信息显式注入扩散生成流程，实现可控的遥感图像-标签对增广。

相关性：为遥感长尾分割提供即插即用的可控合成数据方案，无需额外采集即可提升模型公平性。

研究背景：高分辨率遥感影像语义分割是城市制图与土地覆盖监测的核心任务，但训练像素普遍存在极端的长尾分布，少数类别样本极少。LoveDA 数据集进一步将影像划分为城市/乡村两大域，二者外观差异显著且类别频率统计不一致，加剧了长尾偏差并带来域泛化难题。

方法详情：作者提出两阶段“提示控制扩散增强”框架，可显式指定域类型与类别比例，生成成对的标签-影像样本。Stage A 采用域感知、带掩码的比例条件离散扩散模型，在尊重类别共现结构的前提下合成满足用户指定类别占比的布局图。Stage B 将布局图作为条件，利用带 ControlNet 的 Stable Diffusion 生成与目标域外观一致的真实感影像。最终将比例与域受控的合成样本与真实数据混合训练分割网络。

研究结果：在 LoveDA 上，该方法对 DeepLab-V3+、SegFormer、Mask2Former 等多种主干带来一致提升，mIoU 绝对增益 2.4–4.1 个百分点，提升主要集中于少数类别；城市与乡村子集的跨域泛化误差分别降低 6% 和 8%，表明可控增强有效缓解长尾偏差并提升域鲁棒性。

局限性：框架依赖两阶段串行生成，推理与训练成本高于单阶段方案；扩散模型在乡村域的影像细节保真度仍低于城市域，可能影响罕见类别的边缘精度；方法需预先指定类别比例，实际应用中目标比例不易获得且可能因场景变化而失效。

未来方向：未来可探索单阶段端到端的比例-域联合扩散模型，并引入在线自适应比例估计，使增强过程随训练动态调整。

研究相关性：对于研究遥感语义分割、长尾学习、域适应或生成式数据增强的研究者，该文提供了可控扩散生成与地理空间下游任务结合的新范式及开源代码，可直接扩展至其他土地覆盖数据集或城市场景理解任务。

Vision Foundation Models (VFMs) have achieved remarkable success when applied to various downstream 2D tasks. Despite their effectiveness, they often exhibit a critical lack of 3D awareness. We conduct a comprehensive evaluation on a suite of downstream tasks, including monocular depth estimation, surface normal estimation, multi-view correspondence, and semantic segmentation. Prior attempts to inject 3D awareness either rely on slow per-scene optimization or naïve feature averaging, producing static, artifact-prone teachers. The authors therefore ask whether a fast, feed-forward 3D lifting module can let a teacher “imagine” new viewpoints on-the-fly and distill consistent geometric knowledge into the student.

方法详情：The pipeline, dubbed Splat and Distill, appends a lightweight feed-forward network to any frozen VFM teacher: given a single RGB image, the network predicts a set of 3D Gaussian primitives whose positions, covariances, and feature vectors are learned from the teacher’s 2D feature map. These Gaussians are differentiable-splatted to arbitrary novel views, yielding synthetic feature maps that encode both appearance and geometry. A student network is then trained to match these synthetic features under an L2 + perceptual loss, while the teacher itself is periodically updated via exponential moving average of the student, creating a co-evolving loop. Unlike NeRF-style optimization, the entire 3D lifting step runs in ~8 ms on a single GPU and needs no test-time optimization.

研究结果：On eight 3D-aware benchmarks the distilled student beats prior 3D distillation methods by 15–30 % relative error reduction, and even surpasses specialized single-task models on depth and normal estimation. Ablation shows that removing the Gaussian splatting and using plain feature warping drops performance by ~10 %, confirming the value of explicit 3D modeling.

局限性：The lifting network is trained on synthetic multi-view data (Objaverse) and may generalize poorly to radically different domains such as indoor scenes or aerial imagery. Gaussian primitives assume Lambertian surfaces, so highly reflective or transparent objects yield view-consistent feature hallucinations that can mislead the student. Memory footprint grows linearly with image resolution because each pixel can spawn its own Gaussian, hindering deployment above 1 k×1 k images.

未来方向：Replace isotropic Gaussians with anisotropic, view-dependent primitives or implicit voxels to handle specular and translucent materials, and integrate temporal lifting for video VFMs to enforce multi-frame consistency.
