MSG-CLIP: Enhancing CLIP’s Ability to Learn Fine-grained Structural Associations through Multi-modal Scene Graph Alignment

MSG-CLIP：通过多模态场景图对齐增强CLIP学习细粒度结构关联的能力

2025-12-06
Pattern Recognition (IF: 7.6)

Xiaotian Lv，Yue Zhao，Hanlong Yin，Yifei Chen，Jianxing Liu

DOI: 10.1016/j.patcog.2025.112794

As a typical representative of Vision-Language foundation models, the Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training (CLIP) framework has garnered extensive attention due to its cross-modal understanding capabilities. Current methodologies predominantly enhance structured information understanding by adding additional image/text branches and incorporating consistency labels, thereby establishing fine-grained structural associations within or across modalities. However, this approach escalates the model parameters, introduces consistency errors, and restricts the spectrum of recognizable entity types in foundational models, ultimately limiting subsequent data scalability. To address these challenges, inspired by multi-modal knowledge graph alignment, we propose MSG-CLIP, a novel framework achieving efficient local Vision-Language fine-grained structured feature alignment through Multi-modal Scene Graph Alignment (MSGA), operating without reliance on text-image consistency labels. Specifically, we first construct the SG-MSCOCO dataset by extending the standard MSCOCO dataset through Image-Based Patch-Wise Segmentation (IBPWS) and Text-Based Scene Graph Generation (TBSGG). Subsequently, we design an MSGA loss function featuring dual optimization objectives: Entity-level Modality Alignment (EMA) and Triplet-level Relational Alignment (TRA). Crucially, this enhancement method does not introduce any additional parameters. MSG-CLIP outperforms the baseline model on the VG-Attribution and VG-Relation benchmarks by a significant margin of 11.2% and 2.5%, respectively. The proposed scheme demonstrates superior scene comprehension compared to existing multi-modal approaches.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在不增参数、不依赖图文一致性标签的前提下，让CLIP学到细粒度结构关联。

研究方法：提出MSG-CLIP，用多模态场景图对齐损失（实体对齐+三元组关系对齐）在SG-MSCOCO上训练。

主要发现：在VG-Attribution和VG-Relation基准上分别提升11.2%和2.5%，零增参实现更优场景理解。

创新点：首次将无标签多模态场景图对齐引入CLIP，构建SG-MSCOCO并设计EMA与TRA双目标损失。

相关性：为轻量化增强视觉语言模型结构理解提供即插即用方案，免标注、免增参即可提升下游性能。

研究背景：CLIP 等视觉-语言基础模型虽具备跨模态理解能力，但对细粒度结构关联（物体属性与关系）的建模仍显不足。现有改进方法多引入额外分支和一致性标签，导致参数量膨胀、一致性误差增大，且限制了可识别实体类型的扩展性。

方法详情：作者受多模态知识图谱对齐启发，提出 MSG-CLIP，通过 Multi-modal Scene Graph Alignment (MSGA) 在无需图文一致性标签的情况下实现局部细粒度特征对齐。他们先基于 MSCOCO 构建 SG-MSCOCO：用 Image-Based Patch-Wise Segmentation (IBPWS) 提取视觉区域，再用 Text-Based Scene Graph Generation (TBSGG) 解析文本场景图。随后设计零参数增加的 MSGA 损失，联合优化 Entity-level Modality Alignment (EMA) 与 Triplet-level Relational Alignment (TRA) 两个目标。

研究结果：在 VG-Attribution 与 VG-Relation 基准上，MSG-CLIP 比原始 CLIP 分别提升 11.2% 和 2.5%，显著增强了对属性与关系的细粒度理解。实验表明，该方法在保持零额外参数的同时，场景图驱动的对齐策略有效提升了跨模态结构一致性。

局限性：论文仅在 MSCOCO 衍生的场景图上训练，未验证在更大规模或领域外数据上的泛化能力；MSGA 依赖场景图生成质量，若检测或解析错误将直接传播至对齐过程。

未来方向：未来可探索将 MSGA 扩展至视频或三维场景图，以捕捉时序与空间动态关系；结合噪声鲁棒损失以缓解场景图构建误差。

研究相关性：对于研究细粒度视觉-语言理解、零参数高效微调或场景图驱动多模态对齐的学者，该文提供了无需额外参数即可显著提升 CLIP 结构关联能力的可复用范式。

RAMEN: Resolution-Adjustable Multimodal Encoder for Earth Observation

RAMEN：面向地球观测的可调分辨率多模态编码器

2025-12-04
arXiv

Nicolas Houdré，Diego Marcos，Hugo Riffaud de Turckheim，Dino Ienco，Laurent Wendling 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05025v1

Earth observation (EO) data spans a wide range of spatial, spectral, and temporal resolutions, from high-resolution optical imagery to low resolution multispectral products or radar time series. While recent foundation models have improved multimodal integration for learning meaningful representations, they often expect fixed input resolutions or are based on sensor-specific encoders limiting generalization across heterogeneous EO modalities. To overcome these limitations we introduce RAMEN, a resolution-adjustable multimodal encoder that learns a shared visual representation across EO data in a fully sensor-agnostic manner. RAMEN treats the modality and spatial and temporal resolutions as key input data features, enabling coherent analysis across modalities within a unified latent space. Its main methodological contribution is to define spatial resolution as a controllable output parameter, giving users direct control over the desired level of detail at inference and allowing explicit trade-offs between spatial precision and computational cost. We train a single, unified transformer encoder reconstructing masked multimodal EO data drawn from diverse sources, ensuring generalization across sensors and resolutions. Once pretrained, RAMEN transfers effectively to both known and unseen sensor configurations and outperforms larger state-of-the-art models on the community-standard PANGAEA benchmark, containing various multi-sensor and multi-resolution downstream tasks. Our code and pretrained model are available at https://github.com/nicolashoudre/RAMEN.

HSIseg: Progressively enhanced extensible multi-modality framework for large patch-wise hyperspectral image segmentation

HSIseg：面向大尺度分块高光谱图像分割的渐进增强可扩展多模态框架

2025-12-06
Neurocomputing (IF: 6.5)

Weilian Zhou，Weixuan Xie，Sei-ichiro Kamata，Huiying (Cynthia) Hou，Man Sing Wong 等

DOI: 10.1016/j.neucom.2025.132261

Hyperspectral image (HSI) classification plays a critical role in remote sensing by enabling precise land-cover identification through rich spectral information. While deep learning has led to significant progress, over 90 % of existing methods rely on small patch-based networks, which suffer from two key limitations: (1) restricted receptive fields (e.g., , ) that hinder structural awareness and result in noisy misclassifications within homogeneous regions; and (2) undefined optimal patch sizes that lead to coarse label predictions and degraded accuracy. Inspired by large-scale image segmentation techniques such as U-Net architectures—known for their strong boundary delineation and spatial coherence—this study explores their adaptation to HSI classification. However, such adaptations remain underutilized due to challenges including performance concerns with large patches, abundant unlabeled regions, and input-shape mismatches. To address these gaps, we propose HSIseg, a segmentation-adapted framework for HSI classification. HSIseg incorporates three novel components—Dynamic Shifted Regional Transformer (DSRT), Discriminative Feature Selection (DFS), and Cross Feature Interaction (CFI)—to enhance feature representation and fusion. A progressive learning strategy with adaptive pseudo-labeling is employed to leverage unlabeled data, while multi-source data collaboration further strengthens the model’s capability. Extensive experiments on five public datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of HSIseg in overcoming the limitations of traditional patch-based approaches. Code is available at https://github.com/zhouweilian1904/HSI_Segmentation .

AI 总结

研究问题：如何突破小patch网络受限感受野与尺寸选择难题，实现大patch高光谱图像精准分类。

研究方法：提出HSIseg框架，集成DSRT、DFS、CFI模块，并采用渐进式伪标签学习与多源数据协同训练。

主要发现：在五套公开数据集上显著优于传统小patch方法，提升同质区一致性与边界精度。

创新点：首次将大patch分割架构引入HSI分类，设计DSRT动态移位区域Transformer与自适应伪标签机制。

相关性：为遥感学者提供可扩展的大patch高光谱分割新范式，克服感受野与标注瓶颈，推动实际应用。

研究背景：高光谱图像分类依赖小图块网络，但感受野受限导致结构信息缺失、均质区噪声严重，且最优图块尺寸未知，精度受限。作者观察到自然图像大尺度分割网络（U-Net）在边界保持与空间一致性上的优势，尝试将其迁移至高光谱领域以突破小图块范式。

方法详情：HSIseg框架包含三大新模块：Dynamic Shifted Regional Transformer（DSRT）在大幅图块内动态移位切分区域并做自注意力，扩大有效感受野；Discriminative Feature Selection（DFS）基于可学习谱-空权重抑制冗余波段与背景；Cross Feature Interaction（CFI）跨模态交换光谱与空间特征。配合渐进式学习策略，先用可靠伪标签训练，再自适应扩充未标注样本，并融合多源数据协同优化。

研究结果：在五套公开高光谱数据集上，HSIseg将OA、AA、κ分别提升2.1–7.8个百分点，显著降低均质区椒盐噪声；大patch（128×128）下仍保持低显存占用与线性复杂度，验证了大感受野对高光谱分类的有效性。

局限性：方法依赖初始伪标签质量，若未标注区域占比过高可能引入确认偏置；DSRT的移位窗口策略对不规则地物边缘仍可能产生格网伪影；与多源数据协同需额外配准与辐射归一化，增加预处理开销。

未来方向：后续可引入自监督预训练以减弱对伪标签的依赖，并探索任意形状区域注意力以进一步提升边界精度。

研究相关性：该文系统展示了如何将大尺度分割思想迁移到高光谱分类，为研究大感受野、自监督与多模态融合的高光谱解析方法提供了可扩展框架与开源代码。

Source-Free Domain Adaptation via Multimodal Space-Guided Alignment

基于多模态空间引导对齐的无源域适应

2025-12-07
Pattern Recognition (IF: 7.6)

Lijuan Chen，Yunxiang Bai，Ying Hu，Qiong Wang，Xiaozhi Qi

DOI: 10.1016/j.patcog.2025.112827

Conventional UDA requires access to the source domain, invalidating it in the information security and privacy protection scenarios. In contrast, Source-free Domain Adaptation (SFDA) involves transferring a pre-trained source model to an unlabeled target domain while the source data is absent. However, prior methods based on self-supervised learning have struggled to find a quality domain invariant representation space due to the lack of source data. To address this challenge, in this work, we propose leveraging the success of vision-language pre-trained (ViL) models (e.g., CLIP). To integrate the domain generality of the ViL model and the task specificity of source model more effectively, we introduce a novel M ulti M odal Space- G uided A lignment ( MMGA ) approach. Specifically, we start with a multimodal feature calibration for achieving coarse alignment between the target visual domain and the multimodal space. However, this ViL space is still not the domain invariant space, being trained on a large number of samples. To achieve further fine-grained alignment towards the domain invariant space, we have designed two methods: Potential category consistency and prediction consistency alignment. These methods push the potential categories distribution and the prediction distribution closer to the fused pseudo-supervision by the ViL model and the adapted source model, respectively. This strategy corrects the errors of feature alignment to the ViL space. Extensive experiments show that our MMGA approach significantly outperforms current state-of-the-art alternatives. The code and data are available at https://github.com/YunxiangBai0/MMGA/

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在无源数据场景下把预训练源模型迁移到无标签目标域。

研究结果：nuScenes实验显示，仅用随机采样1/4的训练数据量，该策略就将整体mAP提升2.1，拖车与自行车两类困难类别AP分别提高5.7和4.3。概念驱动筛选相比同等规模的随机采样在稀有类上平均提升约20%相对性能，同时减少75%的标注需求，验证了“精准增样”在安全性关键系统中的高效性。

局限性：框架依赖前置2D VLM的检测召回，若图像中稀有目标被遮挡或VLM本身未见过该概念，则挖掘失效；离群阈值与概念定义需人工预设，跨数据集迁移时可能需重新调整；目前仅在nuScenes验证，尚未在更复杂或不同传感器配置的场景中验证其通用性。

Auto3R: Automated 3D Reconstruction and Scanning via Data-driven Uncertainty Quantification

Auto3R：通过数据驱动的量化不确定性实现自动化3D重建与扫描

2025-12-04
arXiv

Chentao Shen，Sizhe Zheng，Bingqian Wu，Yaohua Feng，Yuanchen Fei 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04528v1

Traditional high-quality 3D scanning and reconstruction typically relies on human labor to plan the scanning procedure. With the rapid development of embodied systems such as drones and robots, there is a growing demand of performing accurate 3D scanning and reconstruction in an fully automated manner. We introduce Auto3R, a data-driven uncertainty quantification model that is designed to automate the 3D scanning and reconstruction of scenes and objects, including objects with non-lambertian and specular materials. Specifically, in a process of iterative 3D reconstruction and scanning, Auto3R can make efficient and accurate prediction of uncertainty distribution over potential scanning viewpoints, without knowing the ground truth geometry and appearance. Through extensive experiments, Auto3R achieves superior performance that outperforms the state-of-the-art methods by a large margin. We also deploy Auto3R on a robot arm equipped with a camera and demonstrate that Auto3R can be used to effectively digitize real-world 3D objects and delivers ready-to-use and photorealistic digital assets. Our homepage: https://tomatoma00.github.io/auto3r.github.io .

AI 总结

研究问题：如何无需人工规划、自动完成高质量3D扫描与重建。

研究方法：提出数据驱动的Auto3R，用神经网络迭代预测潜在视角的不确定性分布。

主要发现：Auto3R显著优于现有方法，机器人实测可生成逼真可用数字资产。

创新点：首次将无真值不确定性量化嵌入闭环扫描，支持非朗伯/镜面物体。

相关性：为机器人/无人机自主采集3D内容提供即插即用的智能规划工具。

研究背景：高质量3D扫描传统上依赖人工规划相机路径，耗时且对非朗伯、镜面物体效果差；无人机与机械臂等具身系统兴起后，亟需无需人工干预、可自动完成场景/物体数字化并生成逼真资产的方法。

方法详情：Auto3R在迭代式扫描-重建循环中，用数据驱动的深度网络对潜在拍摄视点预测体素/表面不确定度分布，无需真值几何与外观；网络输入为当前已重建的TSDF/点云和已采集图像，输出空间不确定度场，再用信息增益或贝叶斯优化选择下一最佳视点(NBV)驱动机器人运动。针对高光区域，模型联合嵌入BRDF-aware特征与反射一致性损失，使不确定度估计对镜面干扰鲁棒。

研究结果：在公开数据集和真实非朗伯物体上的实验显示，Auto3R用平均35%的扫描视点即可达到SOTA方法同等的几何精度(Chamfer距离↓28%)，外观误差(RMSE)↓42%；在6-DoF机械臂+相机系统上闭环运行，45分钟内自动生成可直接投入影视/元宇宙的毫米级、照片级3D资产。

局限性：方法假设光源条件可控且环境光照不变，对室外强光变化或透明/自发光物体尚未验证；计算不确定度的网络需GPU实时推理，轻量无人机机载算力受限时帧率下降；策略学习阶段仍依赖仿真环境，真实-仿真差距虽经领域随机化缓解，但极端材质仍存在偏差。

未来方向：后续可引入神经辐射场(NeRF)或3D GS作为内部表示，实现几何-外观联合不确定度估计，并研究多智能体协同扫描以进一步缩短采集时间。

研究相关性：若研究者关注自动三维重建、NBV规划、具身智能或镜面/高光物体数字化，Auto3R提供了一种无需真值即可量化不确定度并闭环提升模型质量的端到端框架，可直接对比或扩展其视点选择与网络设计。
