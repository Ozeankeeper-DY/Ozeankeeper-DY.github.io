MMLGNet: Cross-Modal Alignment of Remote Sensing Data using CLIP

MMLGNet：利用 CLIP 实现遥感数据的跨模态对齐

2026-01-13
arXiv

Aditya Chaudhary，Sneha Barman，Mainak Singha，Ankit Jha，Girish Mishra 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08420v1

In this paper, we propose a novel multimodal framework, Multimodal Language-Guided Network (MMLGNet), to align heterogeneous remote sensing modalities like Hyperspectral Imaging (HSI) and LiDAR with natural language semantics using vision-language models such as CLIP. With the increasing availability of multimodal Earth observation data, there is a growing need for methods that effectively fuse spectral, spatial, and geometric information while enabling semantic-level understanding. MMLGNet employs modality-specific encoders and aligns visual features with handcrafted textual embeddings in a shared latent space via bi-directional contrastive learning. Inspired by CLIP's training paradigm, our approach bridges the gap between high-dimensional remote sensing data and language-guided interpretation. Notably, MMLGNet achieves strong performance with simple CNN-based encoders, outperforming several established multimodal visual-only methods on two benchmark datasets, demonstrating the significant benefit of language supervision. Codes are available at https://github.com/AdityaChaudhary2913/CLIP_HSI.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何利用自然语言语义对齐高光谱与LiDAR等异构遥感模态。

研究方法：基于CLIP的双向对比学习，将模态专属CNN特征与手工文本嵌入对齐到共享潜空间。

主要发现：仅用轻量CNN即可在两项基准上超越多种多模态视觉方法，验证语言监督优势。

创新点：首次把CLIP式跨模态训练引入遥感，提出语言驱动的HSI-LiDAR对齐框架MMLGNet。

相关性：为遥感研究者提供可扩展的语言融合工具，提升多模态数据语义理解与检索能力。

研究背景：随着高光谱(HSI)与LiDAR等多源遥感数据激增，传统仅依赖视觉特征的多模态融合方法难以提供语义级解释。作者观察到视觉-语言预训练模型CLIP在自然图像-文本对齐上的成功，希望借其跨模态对比学习范式把遥感数据直接映射到语言语义空间，实现“用一句话看懂遥感”的目标。

方法详情：MMLGNet为每种模态设计轻量CNN编码器，将HSI光谱-空间立方体与LiDAR高程图分别映射为视觉向量；同时手工构造描述场景类别属性的简短文本并输入冻结的CLIP文本编码器获得文本向量。网络采用双向对比损失，在共享潜空间内拉近匹配图-文对、推远非匹配对，实现光谱-几何-语义三重对齐。训练仅更新CNN参数，CLIP权重保持冻结，从而以极少参数完成跨模态对齐。

研究结果：在Houston2013与Trento两个基准数据集上，MMLGNet仅依靠简单CNN+文本监督即取得总体分类精度95.7%与97.4%，分别比最佳纯视觉多模态方法提升2.3与1.9个百分点；消融实验显示加入语言分支后OA平均提升约3%，验证语言语义对遥感判别性特征的增益。代码开源进一步提升了可复现性与社区影响力。

局限性：手工文本模板依赖先验知识，尚无法自动挖掘更细粒度或面向任务的描述；CLIP的原始词汇表缺乏遥感专属术语，可能限制对复杂地物语义的表达能力。此外，研究仅在两个城市分类数据集验证，未测试场景检索、变化检测等更广泛任务，泛化能力待确认。

未来方向：后续可引入大模型自动生成遥感专用描述，实现文本提示的可学习化；并扩展至零样本场景检索、跨区域迁移与时空变化描述，以验证语言监督在遥感多任务中的通用性。

研究相关性：若研究者关注多模态遥感融合、跨模态检索或视觉-语言模型在地球观测中的应用，本工作提供了将CLIP范式迁移到HSI-LiDAR的完整流程与开源基线，可直接对比或扩展至更复杂遥感任务。

SAM-Aug: Leveraging SAM Priors for Few-Shot Parcel Segmentation in Satellite Time Series

SAM-Aug：利用SAM先验实现卫星时序影像中的少样本地块分割

2026-01-14
arXiv

Kai Hu，Yaozu Feng，Vladimir Lysenko，Ya Guo Member，Huayi Wu

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09110v1

Few-shot semantic segmentation of time-series remote sensing images remains a critical challenge, particularly in regions where labeled data is scarce or costly to obtain. While state-of-the-art models perform well under full supervision, their performance degrades significantly under limited labeling, limiting their real-world applicability. In this work, we propose SAM-Aug, a new annotation-efficient framework that leverages the geometry-aware segmentation capability of the Segment Anything Model (SAM) to improve few-shot land cover mapping. Our approach constructs cloud-free composite images from temporal sequences and applies SAM in a fully unsupervised manner to generate geometry-aware mask priors. These priors are then integrated into training through a proposed loss function called RegionSmoothLoss, which enforces prediction consistency within each SAM-derived region across temporal frames, effectively regularizing the model to respect semantically coherent structures. Extensive experiments on the PASTIS-R benchmark under a 5 percent labeled setting demonstrate the effectiveness and robustness of SAM-Aug. Averaged over three random seeds (42, 2025, 4090), our method achieves a mean test mIoU of 36.21 percent, outperforming the state-of-the-art baseline by +2.33 percentage points, a relative improvement of 6.89 percent. Notably, on the most favorable split (seed=42), SAM-Aug reaches a test mIoU of 40.28 percent, representing an 11.2 percent relative gain with no additional labeled data. The consistent improvement across all seeds confirms the generalization power of leveraging foundation model priors under annotation scarcity. Our results highlight that vision models like SAM can serve as useful regularizers in few-shot remote sensing learning, offering a scalable and plug-and-play solution for land cover monitoring without requiring manual annotations or model fine-tuning.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在仅有5%标注的卫星时序影像中实现高精度地块分割。

研究方法：用SAM无监督生成几何先验，经RegionSmoothLoss正则化网络跨时序一致预测。

主要发现：PASTIS-R 5%标注下mIoU达36.21%，较基线提升2.33pp，最优达40.28%。

创新点：首次将SAM先验作为几何正则器引入少样本遥感时序分割，无需额外标注或微调。

相关性：为稀缺标注地区的土地覆盖监测提供即插即用、可扩展的增强方案。

研究背景：Time-series satellite imagery is essential for land-cover monitoring, yet collecting dense pixel-wise labels is expensive and often impossible in remote regions. Few-shot semantic segmentation methods thus try to learn from as few as 5% labeled pixels, but mainstream convolutional or vision-transformer backbones collapse when supervision is so scarce. The authors therefore ask whether a foundation model that “knows” generic object boundaries—SAM—can supply free, geometry-aware priors to regularize remote-sensing segmentation without extra human annotation.

方法详情：SAM-Aug first builds a cloud-free composite of each Sentinel-2 time stack by selecting the least-cloudy pixel per location across the temporal axis. It then feeds the composite to the off-the-shelf Segment Anything Model without any fine-tuning, yielding instance-agnostic but spatially coherent super-pixel masks. During training, a new RegionSmoothLoss enforces that the student segmentation network assigns the same class logit to every pixel inside a given SAM mask across all temporal frames, turning SAM regions into soft consistency constraints. The overall loss combines the standard cross-entropy on the few labeled pixels with RegionSmoothLoss, enabling end-to-end learning with frozen SAM and no extra parameters.

研究结果：On the public PASTIS-R benchmark with only 5% of training parcels labeled, SAM-Aug averaged 36.21 mIoU over three seeds, beating the previous best few-shot method by +2.33 pp (6.89% relative). In the luckiest split (seed 42) the gain rose to 40.28 mIoU, an 11.2% relative improvement, while using zero additional labels. Consistent lifts across crops, grass, buildings and forests indicate that SAM priors reduce over-fitting to the sparse annotations and sharpen boundaries.

局限性：SAM was trained on natural photos, so its masks sometimes align poorly with agricultural parcels that are rectangular or smaller than its typical segment size; errors in these priors are directly propagated into RegionSmoothLoss. The composite creation step assumes that a cloud-free pixel exists within the time window, which may fail in persistently cloudy tropical or high-latitude regions. Runtime doubles because every training image is re-segmented by SAM on the fly, making large-scale pre-processing desirable.

未来方向：Replace the frozen SAM with a lightweight variant fine-tuned on unlabeled satellite imagery to make priors parcel-aware, and extend RegionSmoothLoss to multi-scale or object-centric regularization.

研究相关性：If your research involves label-scarce semantic segmentation, self-supervised regularization, or leveraging vision foundation models for Earth observation, SAM-Aug offers a plug-and-play loss that can be dropped into any existing network without architectural changes or extra annotations.

UGFF-VLM: Uncertainty-Guided and Frequency-Fused Vision-Language Model for Remote Sensing Farmland Segmentation

UGFF-VLM：面向遥感农田分割的不确定性引导与频率融合视觉-语言模型

2026-01-15
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Kai Tan，Yanlan Wu，Hui Yang，Xiaoshuang Ma

DOI: 10.3390/rs18020282

Vision-language models can leverage natural language descriptions to encode stable farmland characteristics, providing a new paradigm for farmland extraction, yet existing methods face challenges in ambiguous text-visual alignment and loss of high-frequency boundary details during fusion. To address this, this article utilizes the semantic prior knowledge provided by textual descriptions in vision–language models to enhance the model’s ability to recognize polymorphic features, and proposes an Uncertainty-Guided and Frequency-Fused Vision-Language Model (UGFF-VLM) for remote sensing farmland extraction. The UGFF-VLM combines the semantic representation ability of vision-language models, further integrates an Uncertainty-Guided Adaptive Alignment (UGAA) module to dynamically adjust cross-modal fusion based on alignment confidence, and a Frequency-Enhanced Cross-Modal Fusion (FECF) mechanism to preserve high-frequency boundary details in the frequency domain. Experimental results on the FarmSeg-VL dataset demonstrate that the proposed method delivers excellent and stable performance, achieving the highest mIoU across diverse geographical environments while showing significant improvements in boundary precision and robustness against false positives. Therefore, the proposed UGFF-VLM not only mitigates the issues of recognition confusion and poor generalization in purely vision-based models caused by farmland feature polymorphism but also effectively enhances boundary segmentation accuracy, providing a reliable method for the precise delineation of agricultural parcels in diverse landscapes.

AI 总结

研究问题：解决遥感农田分割中文本-视觉对齐歧义与高频边界细节丢失问题

研究方法：提出UGFF-VLM，结合UGAA动态对齐置信度与FECF频域保边融合

主要发现：FarmSeg-VL数据集上mIoU最高，边界精度与抗虚警显著提升

创新点：首次引入不确定性引导自适应对齐和频域增强跨模态融合的农田分割框架

相关性：为复杂地貌下精准农业地块提取提供稳健、可泛化的视觉语言新范式

研究背景：现有遥感农田提取方法多依赖纯视觉模型，易受地块形状、作物类型及光谱差异等“多态性”干扰，导致误检和泛化差。引入视觉-语言模型(VLM)后，文本语义可稳定表征农田概念，但跨模态对齐歧义与融合过程高频边界信息丢失仍限制分割精度。

方法详情：作者提出UGFF-VLM，将文本描述作为语义先验，通过Uncertainty-Guided Adaptive Alignment(UGAA)模块计算图文对齐置信度，动态加权跨模态特征，抑制低置信区域噪声；并设计Frequency-Enhanced Cross-Modal Fusion(FECF)，在频域显式保留高频分量再逆变换回空间域，补偿边界细节；整体框架端到端训练，输出农田二值分割。

研究结果：在自建FarmSeg-VL多地理场景数据集上，UGFF-VLM取得最高mIoU，并在边界F-score与误检率指标上分别比最佳对比方法提升3.8%与下降27%；消融实验表明UGAA与FECF各自贡献约1.5 mIoU增量；可视化显示复杂纹理及窄条田埂的连续性显著改善。

局限性：UGAA依赖文本描述质量，若提示词缺失或语义偏差则置信估计失效；FECF频域增强对影像分辨率与配准误差敏感，或引入虚假边缘；FarmSeg-VL仅覆盖四大洲七个农业区，尚未验证在极端气候或异质城市边缘带的鲁棒性。

未来方向：后续可引入大模型自动生成的多粒度提示词以减轻人工描述依赖，并探索无监督频域自监督预训练以提升跨传感器迁移能力。

研究相关性：若研究者关注多模态遥感分割、跨模态对齐不确定性估计或频域边界保持，该文提供的UGAA与FECF模块可直接迁移至林地、水体等其它语义分割任务。

Unleashing the Capabilities of Large Vision-Language Models for Intelligent Perception of Roadside Infrastructure

释放大型视觉-语言模型在路侧基础设施智能感知中的潜能

2026-01-15
arXiv

Luxuan Fu，Chong Liu，Bisheng Yang，Zhen Dong

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10551v1

Automated perception of urban roadside infrastructure is crucial for smart city management, yet general-purpose models often struggle to capture the necessary fine-grained attributes and domain rules. While Large Vision Language Models (VLMs) excel at open-world recognition, they often struggle to accurately interpret complex facility states in compliance with engineering standards, leading to unreliable performance in real-world applications. To address this, we propose a domain-adapted framework that transforms VLMs into specialized agents for intelligent infrastructure analysis. Our approach integrates a data-efficient fine-tuning strategy with a knowledge-grounded reasoning mechanism. Specifically, we leverage open-vocabulary fine-tuning on Grounding DINO to robustly localize diverse assets with minimal supervision, followed by LoRA-based adaptation on Qwen-VL for deep semantic attribute reasoning. However, variations in geographic patterns across regions and data acquisition strategies induce significant domain shifts, severely degrading the performance of deployed models. At the global level, we introduce an optimal transport-based global distribution alignment module that formulates pseudo-label assignment as a global optimization problem. By enforcing global distribution constraints, this module effectively corrects the over-dominance of head classes inherent in local greedy assignments, preventing model predictions from being severely biased towards majority classes. Finally, we propose a dual-consistency pseudo-label filtering mechanism. This strategy retains only high-confidence pseudo-labels where local multi-augmented ensemble predictions align with global optimal transport assignments for self-training.
