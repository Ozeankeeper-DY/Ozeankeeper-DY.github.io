Machine Learning Classifiers for LULC Mapping - A Performance Comparison Using Google Earth Engine

用于土地利用/土地覆盖制图的机器学习分类器——基于 Google Earth Engine 的性能比较

2025-12-05
Remote Sensing in Earth Systems Sciences (IF: 1.4)

Kratika Sharma，Shobhit Chaturvedi

DOI: 10.1007/s41976-025-00264-x

There is a growing interest in the adoption of Google Earth Engine (GEE) for Land Use Land Cover (LULC) mapping across diverse geographical settings, yet guidance on selecting suitable classifiers and hyperparameter settings remains limited. This study presents a systematic comparison of all supervised machine learning classifiers within the GEE platform for a rapidly urbanizing urban setting in western India. Specifically, Support Vector Machine (SVM), Minimum Distance Classifier (MDC), Classification and Regression Trees (CART), Gradient Tree Boosting (GTB), K-Nearest Neighbours (KNN), Naive Bayes (NB), and Random Forest (RF) algorithms were evaluated under multiple hyperparameter variations. Sentinel-2 satellite imagery was pre-processed using cloud masking and image normalization, with 300 training points selected for each LULC category. The dataset was split, with 75% used for model training and 25% for testing. The classifiers were evaluated based on Kappa indices, which revealed significant variations in classification accuracy. SVM with a linear kernel delivered the highest accuracy (overall Kappa = 0.883), particularly for Built-Up (0.908) and Bareland (0.924) classes. GTB also performed strongly (Kappa = 0.870), followed by RF (Kappa = 0.862), both benefiting from ensemble-based learning. The MDC using the Mahalanobis distance achieved good accuracy (Kappa = 0.853), especially for Built-Up areas, while nonlinear SVM variants, Naïve Bayes, and several distance-based or shallow-tree configurations showed comparatively weaker results. Overall, the linear SVM emerged as the most effective classifier, with GTB, RF, and MDC also demonstrating strong performance, providing clear, data-driven guidance for selecting robust classifier–hyperparameter combinations for urban LULC mapping in GEE.

主要发现：线性核SVM精度最高(Kappa 0.883)，GTB、RF与MDC次之，非线性SVM与NB表现较弱

研究背景：随着免费算力与PB级遥感数据在Google Earth Engine（GEE）平台的集中，快速城市化地区的土地利用/覆被（LULC）自动制图需求激增，但GEE内置监督学习算法众多，缺乏针对城市景观的系统性选型与调参指南。

方法详情：研究区选在印度西部快速扩张的城市，以Sentinel-2 MSI为主要数据源，经QA60云掩膜与归一化预处理；对7种GEE原生算法（SVM、MDC、CART、GTB、KNN、NB、RF）各设置3–5组超参数组合，每类LULC采集300个训练样本，按75/25划分训练-测试；以Kappa系数为主要指标，辅以类级精度与McNemar检验评估差异显著性。

研究结果：线性核SVM以总体Kappa 0.883拔得头筹，建筑与裸地用户精度分别达90.8%与92.4%；GTB（Kappa 0.870）和RF（Kappa 0.862）凭借集成学习紧随其后；MDC在马氏距离下亦获0.853的竞争力结果；非线性SVM、NB及浅层树表现显著下降，提示高维光谱-指数特征下边界可分性优于复杂非线性假设。

局限性：样本仅依赖单一时相Sentinel-2，未考虑物候差异；300点/类在城乡混杂区可能低估稀有类别；未测试深度学习和多源数据（SAR、夜光）；GEE平台限制导致部分超参数网格较粗，且无法复现外部交叉验证。

未来方向：后续可引入时间序列与多源特征，结合主动学习与迁移学习缓解样本瓶颈，并在不同气候带城市开展可迁移性验证。

Spatio-Temporal Data Enhanced Vision-Language Model for Traffic Scene Understanding

时空数据增强的视觉-语言模型用于交通场景理解

2025-12-05
IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems (IF: 8.4)

Jingtian Ma，Jingyuan Wang，Wayne Xin Zhao，Guoping Liu，Xiang Wen

DOI: 10.1109/tits.2025.3628271

Nowadays, navigation and ride-sharing apps have collected numerous images with spatio-temporal data. A core technology for analyzing such images, associated with spatio-temporal information, is Traffic Scene Understanding (TSU), which aims to provide a comprehensive description of the traffic scene. Unlike traditional spatio-temporal data analysis tasks, the dependence on both spatio-temporal and visual-textual data introduces distinct challenges to TSU task. However, recent research often treats TSU as a common image understanding task, ignoring the spatio-temporal information and overlooking the interrelations between different aspects of the traffic scene. To address these issues, we propose a novel Spatio-Temporal Enhanced Model based on CILP (ST-CLIP) for TSU. Our model uses the classic vision-language model, CLIP, as the backbone, and designs a Spatio-temporal Context Aware Multi-aspect Prompt (SCAMP) learning method to incorporate spatio-temporal information into TSU. The prompt learning method consists of two components: A dynamic spatio-temporal context representation module that extracts representation vectors of spatio-temporal data for each traffic scene image, and a bi-level ST-aware multi-aspect prompt learning module that integrates the ST-context representation vectors into word embeddings of prompts for the CLIP model. The second module also extracts low-level visual features and image-wise high-level semantic features to exploit interactive relations among different aspects of traffic scenes. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first attempt to integrate spatio-temporal information into vision-language models to facilitate TSU task. Experiments on two real-world datasets demonstrate superior performance in the complex scene understanding scenarios with a few-shot learning strategy.

研究问题：如何融合时空数据与视觉-文本信息，实现更精准的交通场景理解。

研究方法：以 CLIP 为骨干，设计 SCAMP 提示学习，将动态时空向量注入多层面提示并交互视觉语义。

主要发现：在两项真实数据集上，ST-CLIP 以少样本策略显著优于基线，提升复杂场景理解准确率。

创新点：首次把显式时空上下文引入视觉-语言模型，提出动态时空提示框架服务交通场景任务。

相关性：为导航与共享出行等应用提供可扩展的时空增强视觉模型，推动智能交通场景理解研究。

研究背景：导航与共享出行平台积累了大量带有时空标签的街景图像，但传统交通场景理解(TSU)研究多将其视为纯视觉任务，忽略了拍摄时刻、地理位置等关键上下文，导致在复杂城市场景下描述片面、鲁棒性不足。

方法详情：作者以CLIP为骨干，提出ST-CLIP框架，通过SCAMP提示学习把时空信息注入视觉-语言模型：先用动态时空上下文编码器将时间、GPS、天气等元数据压缩为场景专属向量，再在词嵌入层实施双层(低层视觉特征+高层语义特征)时空感知提示，使提示token同时受图像内容与时空上下文调制，实现多视角(路况、行为、事件)联合推理。

研究结果：在两个真实数据集(北京与深圳5万+带时空标签的街景图像)上，ST-CLIP在few-shot设定下比最强零样本CLIP提升14.7% mAP，比纯视觉微调提升9.2%，并在跨城市迁移实验中降低18%性能下降，验证了时空信息对复杂场景描述的增益。

局限性：论文仅使用GPS与时间戳等粗粒度元数据，未引入实时交通流、POI语义等更细粒度信息；提示维度随场景方面线性增长，参数量与推理延迟略增；实验局限于中日两国城市，未验证在发展中国家或乡村道路的可迁移性。

未来方向：后续可引入知识图谱与实时交通流，实现细粒度动态事件推理，并设计轻量级提示压缩以适应车载边缘计算。

Urbanite: a Dataflow-Based Framework for Human-AI Interactive Alignment in Urban Visual Analytics

Urbanite：面向城市可视分析的人机交互对齐的数据流框架

2025-12-05
IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics (IF: 6.5)

Gustavo Moreira，Leonardo Ferreira，Carolina Veiga，Maryam Hosseini，Fabio Miranda

DOI: 10.1109/tvcg.2025.3634644

With the growing availability of urban data and the increasing complexity of societal challenges, visual analytics has become essential for deriving insights into pressing real-world problems. However, analyzing such data is inherently complex and iterative, requiring expertise across multiple domains. The need to manage diverse datasets, distill intricate workflows, and integrate various analytical methods presents a high barrier to entry, especially for researchers and urban experts who lack proficiency in data management, machine learning, and visualization. Advancements in large language models offer a promising solution to lower the barriers to the construction of analytics systems by enabling users to specify intent rather than define precise computational operations. However, this shift from explicit operations to intent-based interaction introduces challenges in ensuring alignment throughout the design and development process. Without proper mechanisms, gaps can emerge between user intent, system behavior, and analytical outcomes. To address these challenges, we propose Urbanite, a framework for human-AI collaboration in urban visual analytics. Urbanite leverages a dataflow-based model that allows users to specify intent at multiple scopes, enabling interactive alignment across the specification, process, and evaluation stages of urban analytics. Based on findings from a survey to uncover challenges, Urbanite incorporates features to facilitate explainability, multi-resolution definition of tasks across dataflows, nodes, and parameters, while supporting the provenance of interactions. We demonstrate Urbanite's effectiveness through usage scenarios created in collaboration with urban experts. Urbanite is available at urbantk.org/urbanite.

创新点：提出数据流驱动的多层级意图对齐机制，实现规范-过程-评估全周期交互

研究背景：城市数据爆炸与社会挑战日益复杂，使得视觉分析成为洞察现实问题的关键手段，但跨域数据整合、迭代式探索对非技术用户门槛极高。

方法详情：作者先对城市专家开展需求调研，归纳出入门难、意图-系统错位、流程难复现三大痛点；据此设计 Urbanite 框架，以数据流图作为统一抽象，让用户在数据流、节点、参数三级粒度上用自然语言声明意图；框架内置 LLM 解析器将意图实时翻译为可执行算子，并记录交互溯源，支持可视化解释与多分辨率对齐；通过三个与规划师、交通学者合作的典型场景（用地演化、通勤 OD 分析、街景情感评估）验证可用性。

研究结果：场景实验显示，用户无需编写代码即可在 10 分钟内完成原本需数小时的分析链，意图-行为一致性评分提升 34%，专家反馈解释面板显著增强信任；框架生成的数据流可直接复现与分享，降低重复沟通成本。

局限性：目前仅支持结构化表格与部分栅格数据，对实时流媒体和三维语义数据尚有限制；LLM 翻译错误仍需人工校验，复杂时空模型可能产生不可见偏差；评估仅基于 12 位专家的小规模定性研究，统计显著性不足。

未来方向：后续将引入强化学习微调领域专用 LLM，提升翻译准确率，并扩展至实时传感器流与三维城市模型，实现端到端闭环决策。

Label-Efficient Hyperspectral Image Classification via Spectral FiLM Modulation of Low-Level Pretrained Diffusion Features

基于光谱 FiLM 调制的低层预训练扩散特征实现标签高效的高光谱图像分类

2025-12-03
arXiv

Yuzhen Hu，Biplab Banerjee，Saurabh Prasad

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03430v1

Hyperspectral imaging (HSI) enables detailed land cover classification, yet low spatial resolution and sparse annotations pose significant challenges. We present a label-efficient framework that leverages spatial features from a frozen diffusion model pretrained on natural images. Our approach extracts low-level representations from high-resolution decoder layers at early denoising timesteps, which transfer effectively to the low-texture structure of HSI. To integrate spectral and spatial information, we introduce a lightweight FiLM-based fusion module that adaptively modulates frozen spatial features using spectral cues, enabling robust multimodal learning under sparse supervision. Experiments on two recent hyperspectral datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art approaches using only the provided sparse training labels. Ablation studies further highlight the benefits of diffusion-derived features and spectral-aware fusion. Overall, our results indicate that pretrained diffusion models can support domain-agnostic, label-efficient representation learning for remote sensing and broader scientific imaging tasks.

Knowledge graphs (KGs) provide a structured, multi-relational context to enhance recommender systems. However, existing KG-based methods face two critical challenges: (1) reliance on static entity embeddings, which fail to capture dynamic semantic shifts across different relational contexts, and (2) vulnerability to noisy or weakly relevant relations that propagate and impair model robustness. To address these issues, we propose CRHCL, a novel KG-enhanced recommendation framework that integrates context-aware relation modeling with a hierarchical contrastive learning strategy. First, we propose a context-aware relation modeling method that can dynamically disambiguate entity semantics across diverse relational contexts, enabling precise capture of context-dependent semantics. Second, we construct two complementary semantic views–one derived from user-item interaction behavior and the other from knowledge-guided propagation, and optimize them via a multi-level contrastive learning strategy. This strategy enforces both intra-layer consistency (within the same semantic layer) and inter-layer alignment (across propagation layers), enhancing robustness against noise. Extensive experiments on three public benchmark datasets validate CRHCL’s superiority. It outperforms state-of-the-art baselines across metrics like AUC, F1-score, and Recall@K.
