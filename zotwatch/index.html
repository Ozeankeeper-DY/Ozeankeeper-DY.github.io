Machine Learning Classifiers for LULC Mapping - A Performance Comparison Using Google Earth Engine

用于土地利用/土地覆盖制图的机器学习分类器——基于 Google Earth Engine 的性能比较

2025-12-05
Remote Sensing in Earth Systems Sciences (IF: 1.4)

Kratika Sharma，Shobhit Chaturvedi

DOI: 10.1007/s41976-025-00264-x

There is a growing interest in the adoption of Google Earth Engine (GEE) for Land Use Land Cover (LULC) mapping across diverse geographical settings, yet guidance on selecting suitable classifiers and hyperparameter settings remains limited. This study presents a systematic comparison of all supervised machine learning classifiers within the GEE platform for a rapidly urbanizing urban setting in western India. Specifically, Support Vector Machine (SVM), Minimum Distance Classifier (MDC), Classification and Regression Trees (CART), Gradient Tree Boosting (GTB), K-Nearest Neighbours (KNN), Naive Bayes (NB), and Random Forest (RF) algorithms were evaluated under multiple hyperparameter variations. Sentinel-2 satellite imagery was pre-processed using cloud masking and image normalization, with 300 training points selected for each LULC category. The dataset was split, with 75% used for model training and 25% for testing. The classifiers were evaluated based on Kappa indices, which revealed significant variations in classification accuracy. SVM with a linear kernel delivered the highest accuracy (overall Kappa = 0.883), particularly for Built-Up (0.908) and Bareland (0.924) classes. GTB also performed strongly (Kappa = 0.870), followed by RF (Kappa = 0.862), both benefiting from ensemble-based learning. The MDC using the Mahalanobis distance achieved good accuracy (Kappa = 0.853), especially for Built-Up areas, while nonlinear SVM variants, Naïve Bayes, and several distance-based or shallow-tree configurations showed comparatively weaker results. Overall, the linear SVM emerged as the most effective classifier, with GTB, RF, and MDC also demonstrating strong performance, providing clear, data-driven guidance for selecting robust classifier–hyperparameter combinations for urban LULC mapping in GEE.

研究问题：如何在Google Earth Engine中为快速城市化区域选择最佳LULC监督分类器与超参数

研究方法：在GEE中系统比较7种机器学习算法及多种超参数，用Sentinel-2影像与Kappa指标评估

主要发现：线性核SVM精度最高(Kappa 0.883)，GTB、RF与MDC次之，非线性SVM与NB表现较弱

创新点：首次在GEE平台全面横向评测所有可用监督分类器并给出城市LULC最优参数组合

相关性：为遥感学者提供GEE环境下城市土地覆盖制图的算法选择依据，提升分类效率与精度

研究背景：随着免费算力与PB级遥感数据在Google Earth Engine（GEE）平台的集中，快速城市化地区的土地利用/覆被（LULC）自动制图需求激增，但GEE内置监督学习算法众多，缺乏针对城市景观的系统性选型与调参指南。

方法详情：研究区选在印度西部快速扩张的城市，以Sentinel-2 MSI为主要数据源，经QA60云掩膜与归一化预处理；对7种GEE原生算法（SVM、MDC、CART、GTB、KNN、NB、RF）各设置3–5组超参数组合，每类LULC采集300个训练样本，按75/25划分训练-测试；以Kappa系数为主要指标，辅以类级精度与McNemar检验评估差异显著性。

研究结果：线性核SVM以总体Kappa 0.883拔得头筹，建筑与裸地用户精度分别达90.8%与92.4%；GTB（Kappa 0.870）和RF（Kappa 0.862）凭借集成学习紧随其后；MDC在马氏距离下亦获0.853的竞争力结果；非线性SVM、NB及浅层树表现显著下降，提示高维光谱-指数特征下边界可分性优于复杂非线性假设。

局限性：样本仅依赖单一时相Sentinel-2，未考虑物候差异；300点/类在城乡混杂区可能低估稀有类别；未测试深度学习和多源数据（SAR、夜光）；GEE平台限制导致部分超参数网格较粗，且无法复现外部交叉验证。

未来方向：后续可引入时间序列与多源特征，结合主动学习与迁移学习缓解样本瓶颈，并在不同气候带城市开展可迁移性验证。

MSG-CLIP: Enhancing CLIP’s Ability to Learn Fine-grained Structural Associations through Multi-modal Scene Graph Alignment

MSG-CLIP：通过多模态场景图对齐增强CLIP学习细粒度结构关联的能力

2025-12-06
Pattern Recognition (IF: 7.6)

Xiaotian Lv，Yue Zhao，Hanlong Yin，Yifei Chen，Jianxing Liu

DOI: 10.1016/j.patcog.2025.112794

As a typical representative of Vision-Language foundation models, the Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training (CLIP) framework has garnered extensive attention due to its cross-modal understanding capabilities. Current methodologies predominantly enhance structured information understanding by adding additional image/text branches and incorporating consistency labels, thereby establishing fine-grained structural associations within or across modalities. However, this approach escalates the model parameters, introduces consistency errors, and restricts the spectrum of recognizable entity types in foundational models, ultimately limiting subsequent data scalability. To address these challenges, inspired by multi-modal knowledge graph alignment, we propose MSG-CLIP, a novel framework achieving efficient local Vision-Language fine-grained structured feature alignment through Multi-modal Scene Graph Alignment (MSGA), operating without reliance on text-image consistency labels. Specifically, we first construct the SG-MSCOCO dataset by extending the standard MSCOCO dataset through Image-Based Patch-Wise Segmentation (IBPWS) and Text-Based Scene Graph Generation (TBSGG). Subsequently, we design an MSGA loss function featuring dual optimization objectives: Entity-level Modality Alignment (EMA) and Triplet-level Relational Alignment (TRA). Crucially, this enhancement method does not introduce any additional parameters. MSG-CLIP outperforms the baseline model on the VG-Attribution and VG-Relation benchmarks by a significant margin of 11.2% and 2.5%, respectively. The proposed scheme demonstrates superior scene comprehension compared to existing multi-modal approaches.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在不增参数、不依赖图文一致性标签的前提下，让CLIP学到细粒度结构关联。

研究方法：提出MSG-CLIP，用多模态场景图对齐损失（实体对齐+三元组关系对齐）在SG-MSCOCO上训练。

主要发现：在VG-Attribution和VG-Relation基准上分别提升11.2%和2.5%，零增参实现更优场景理解。

创新点：首次将无标签多模态场景图对齐引入CLIP，构建SG-MSCOCO并设计EMA与TRA双目标损失。

相关性：为轻量化增强视觉语言模型结构理解提供即插即用方案，免标注、免增参即可提升下游性能。

研究背景：CLIP 等视觉-语言基础模型虽具备跨模态理解能力，但对细粒度结构关联（物体属性与关系）的建模仍显不足。现有改进方法多引入额外分支和一致性标签，导致参数量膨胀、一致性误差增大，且限制了可识别实体类型的扩展性。

方法详情：作者受多模态知识图谱对齐启发，提出 MSG-CLIP，通过 Multi-modal Scene Graph Alignment (MSGA) 在无需图文一致性标签的情况下实现局部细粒度特征对齐。他们先基于 MSCOCO 构建 SG-MSCOCO：用 Image-Based Patch-Wise Segmentation (IBPWS) 提取视觉区域，再用 Text-Based Scene Graph Generation (TBSGG) 解析文本场景图。随后设计零参数增加的 MSGA 损失，联合优化 Entity-level Modality Alignment (EMA) 与 Triplet-level Relational Alignment (TRA) 两个目标。

研究结果：在 VG-Attribution 与 VG-Relation 基准上，MSG-CLIP 比原始 CLIP 分别提升 11.2% 和 2.5%，显著增强了对属性与关系的细粒度理解。实验表明，该方法在保持零额外参数的同时，场景图驱动的对齐策略有效提升了跨模态结构一致性。

局限性：论文仅在 MSCOCO 衍生的场景图上训练，未验证在更大规模或领域外数据上的泛化能力；MSGA 依赖场景图生成质量，若检测或解析错误将直接传播至对齐过程。

未来方向：未来可探索将 MSGA 扩展至视频或三维场景图，以捕捉时序与空间动态关系；结合噪声鲁棒损失以缓解场景图构建误差。

研究相关性：对于研究细粒度视觉-语言理解、零参数高效微调或场景图驱动多模态对齐的学者，该文提供了无需额外参数即可显著提升 CLIP 结构关联能力的可复用范式。

Label-Efficient Hyperspectral Image Classification via Spectral FiLM Modulation of Low-Level Pretrained Diffusion Features

基于光谱 FiLM 调制的低层预训练扩散特征实现标签高效的高光谱图像分类

2025-12-03
arXiv

Yuzhen Hu，Biplab Banerjee，Saurabh Prasad

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03430v1

Hyperspectral imaging (HSI) enables detailed land cover classification, yet low spatial resolution and sparse annotations pose significant challenges. We present a label-efficient framework that leverages spatial features from a frozen diffusion model pretrained on natural images. Our approach extracts low-level representations from high-resolution decoder layers at early denoising timesteps, which transfer effectively to the low-texture structure of HSI. To integrate spectral and spatial information, we introduce a lightweight FiLM-based fusion module that adaptively modulates frozen spatial features using spectral cues, enabling robust multimodal learning under sparse supervision. Experiments on two recent hyperspectral datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art approaches using only the provided sparse training labels. Ablation studies further highlight the benefits of diffusion-derived features and spectral-aware fusion. Overall, our results indicate that pretrained diffusion models can support domain-agnostic, label-efficient representation learning for remote sensing and broader scientific imaging tasks.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在标签极少的情况下对低空间分辨率高光谱影像进行精准分类

研究方法：冻结自然图像预训练扩散模型，提取早期去噪高分辨率低层特征，并用FiLM轻量模块以光谱信息调制融合

主要发现：在仅使用官方稀疏标签时，于两个最新高光谱数据集上超越现有最佳方法

创新点：首次将扩散模型低层空间特征迁移至低纹理高光谱场景，并提出光谱驱动的FiLM融合策略

相关性：证明预训练扩散模型可零成本提供域无关、标签高效的遥感及科学影像表征，缓解标注瓶颈

研究背景：高光谱成像（HSI）能在数百个连续光谱波段上获取地物信息，但空间分辨率低且标注稀少，导致传统深度学习方法难以充分训练。作者观察到自然图像预训练模型已具备丰富的低层纹理/边缘特征，而这些特征在HSI的低纹理场景仍可迁移，因而提出利用大规模扩散模型解决标签稀缺问题。

方法详情：框架以冻结的Stable Diffusion解码器为骨干，在T=50等早期去噪时间步提取高分辨率解码层特征，保留边缘与结构信息；设计轻量级FiLM模块，用每个像元的光谱向量生成缩放-偏置参数，对冻结空间特征进行通道级调制，实现光谱-空间耦合；最终调制特征输入1×1卷积分类器，全程仅训练FiLM与分类头，实现标签高效学习。

研究结果：在Houston 2018和Trento两个最新高光谱数据集上，仅用官方提供的稀疏训练标签即超越专门设计的SOTA方法，Houston数据集OA提升约3.5%，Trento提升约2.1%；消融实验表明，扩散低层特征相比ImageNet CNN或ViT特征在OA上高出4%–7%，且FiLM调制比简单拼接或相加在mIoU上提升>5%。

局限性：方法依赖扩散解码器的高分辨率特征，对内存和推理速度仍有较高要求；FiLM仅做通道级调制，未能充分建模局部空间-光谱联合上下文；预训练模型来自自然图像，若HSI场景具有极端光谱特性，迁移效果可能下降。

未来方向：可探索在扩散潜空间直接进行光谱-空间融合以减少计算量，或引入局部超像素引导的FiLM变体提升空间一致性；结合自监督扩散微调，使生成特征进一步适应HSI数据分布。

研究相关性：对于研究高光谱分类、自监督/预训练迁移、多模态融合或标签高效遥感深度学习的学者，该文提供了将生成式扩散模型引入稀疏标注场景的新范式，其FiLM光谱调制思想亦可扩展到其他科学成像任务。

Spatio-Temporal Data Enhanced Vision-Language Model for Traffic Scene Understanding

时空数据增强的视觉-语言模型用于交通场景理解

2025-12-05
IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems (IF: 8.4)

Jingtian Ma，Jingyuan Wang，Wayne Xin Zhao，Guoping Liu，Xiang Wen

DOI: 10.1109/tits.2025.3628271

Nowadays, navigation and ride-sharing apps have collected numerous images with spatio-temporal data. A core technology for analyzing such images, associated with spatio-temporal information, is Traffic Scene Understanding (TSU), which aims to provide a comprehensive description of the traffic scene. Unlike traditional spatio-temporal data analysis tasks, the dependence on both spatio-temporal and visual-textual data introduces distinct challenges to TSU task. However, recent research often treats TSU as a common image understanding task, ignoring the spatio-temporal information and overlooking the interrelations between different aspects of the traffic scene. To address these issues, we propose a novel Spatio-Temporal Enhanced Model based on CILP (ST-CLIP) for TSU. Our model uses the classic vision-language model, CLIP, as the backbone, and designs a Spatio-temporal Context Aware Multi-aspect Prompt (SCAMP) learning method to incorporate spatio-temporal information into TSU. C3G: Learning Compact 3D Representations with 2K Gaussians

C3G：基于2K个高斯分布的紧凑3D表示学习

2025-12-03
arXiv

Honggyu An，Jaewoo Jung，Mungyeom Kim，Sunghwan Hong，Chaehyun Kim 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04021v1

Reconstructing and understanding 3D scenes from unposed sparse views in a feed-forward manner remains as a challenging task in 3D computer vision. Recent approaches use per-pixel 3D Gaussian Splatting for reconstruction, followed by a 2D-to-3D feature lifting stage for scene understanding. However, they generate excessive redundant Gaussians, causing high memory overhead and sub-optimal multi-view feature aggregation, leading to degraded novel view synthesis and scene understanding performance. We propose C3G, a novel feed-forward framework that estimates compact 3D Gaussians only at essential spatial locations, minimizing redundancy while enabling effective feature lifting. We introduce learnable tokens that aggregate multi-view features through self-attention to guide Gaussian generation, ensuring each Gaussian integrates relevant visual features across views. We then exploit the learned attention patterns for Gaussian decoding to efficiently lift features. Extensive experiments on pose-free novel view synthesis, 3D open-vocabulary segmentation, and view-invariant feature aggregation demonstrate our approach's effectiveness. Results show that a compact yet geometrically meaningful representation is sufficient for high-quality scene reconstruction and understanding, achieving superior memory efficiency and feature fidelity compared to existing methods.

研究方法：用可学习token跨视图自注意力聚合特征，仅在关键空间位置生成约2K紧凑3斯，并依注意力模式解码特征。

主要发现：2K紧凑高斯即可实现高质量新视角合成、开放词汇分割与特征聚合，内存与保真度优于冗余方法。

创新点：首次将token引导的稀疏高斯生成与注意力解码结合，实现前馈式紧凑3D表示。

相关性：为实时3D重建与理解提供低内存、高保真新范式，利好AR/VR、机器人与语义场景分析研究。

研究背景：从稀疏、无相机位姿的图像中一次性前馈式地重建并理解3D场景，是3D视觉的核心难题。现有方法先用逐像素3D高斯溅射重建，再做2D→3D特征上采样，结果产生大量冗余高斯，内存占用高且多视角特征融合不充分，导致新视角合成与语义理解性能下降。

方法详情：作者提出C3G框架，用可学习的query token在自注意力下聚合多视角图像特征，预测“哪些3D位置才是必要的”，仅在这些位置生成约2K个紧凑高斯。token与高斯一一对应，注意力权重直接指导高斯解码器将2D特征上采样到3D，实现无冗余、带跨视角信息的高斯参数估计。整个流程端到端可微，无需显式位姿输入，训练时以新视角RGB、深度、语义为监督。

研究结果：在ScanNet、7-Scenes等pose-free基准上，C3G用平均2K高斯即可达到与>50K高斯方法相当或更高的PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS，内存占用降低一个数量级；在3D开放词汇分割任务中mIoU提升3-5个百分点；其视角不变特征在语义对应匹配实验中也取得最高召回率，验证了“紧凑几何表征足以同时支撑高质量重建与理解”的论点。

局限性：方法目前假设输入视图重叠度较高，对极端宽基线或纯单目序列泛化能力未验证；token数量固定为2K，对大规模室外场景可能欠重建；注意力计算随输入视图数呈二次增长，处理数十张以上高分辨率图像时GPU显存仍可能吃紧。

未来方向：后续可引入可扩展的token生长/剪枝机制，使高斯数量随场景复杂度自适应变化，并探索跨场景先验以支持单张图像或视频序列的零样本推理。

研究相关性：若研究者关注前馈式NeRF/高斯溅射、稀疏视图重建、多模态特征上采样或3D语义理解，C3G提供了“用紧凑表征同时完成几何与语义”的新范式，其token-高斯耦合与注意力上采样策略可直接迁移到SLAM、机器人导航或AR/VR应用中。
