RURBAN-Map: A knowledge-guided framework for nationwide fine-scale urban-rural land use mapping in China

RURBAN-Map：面向中国全国精细尺度城乡土地利用制图的知识引导框架

2026-03-07
Remote Sensing of Environment (IF: 11.4)

Qiqi Zhu，Dongyang Li，Longli Ran，Yiran Xu，Yong Zhang 等

DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115352

Accurate and fine-scale land use/land cover (LULC) mapping across rapidly evolving urban-rural landscapes under a unified framework remains a frontier challenge for Earth observation and spatial planning. Existing global and national products still suffer from three major bottlenecks: (1) inability to delineate fine-scale functional units across both urban and rural areas using road networks alone, leading to coarse and fragmented spatial boundary representation; (2) scarcity and imbalance of training samples across urban-rural contexts, restricting integrated city-level mapping and hindering nationwide scalability; and (3) substantial disparities in LULC categories and land functions between urban and rural land blocks, which hinder unified classification across heterogeneous landscapes. Overcoming these issues is crucial for advancing urban-rural sustainability studies and providing actionable information for planning. Here we propose RURBAN-Map, a knowledge-guided urban-rural mapping framework that transforms LULC mapping from data-driven to geographic cognition-based recognition. The framework integrates a coherent knowledge system including spatial structure, temporal semantics, and zoning strategy, which operationalizes block morphology, planning standards, and urban-rural data characteristics to guide block construction, feature extraction, and unified urban-rural LULC mapping. Using this framework, we constructed the first nationwide fine-scale block and sample dataset in China (UR-Blocks), consisting of 7,107,806 blocks and 3,624,452 labeled samples across 17 LULC categories. Automated delineation achieved a block boundary accuracy of 72.9%, while large-scale mapping across 20 representative cities and three major urban agglomerations yielded an overall accuracy of 78.8%. Compared with existing products that are restricted to urban built-up areas, RURBAN-Map reduces functional mixing and improves boundary fidelity by over 10%, while increasing mapping coverage by 80%, thereby achieving unified mapping across the urban-rural areas. Mapping results further show that rural regions contain meaningful functional land-use types, which have become increasingly relevant to rural policies. Beyond methodological advancement, RURBAN-Map establishes a benchmark dataset and a scalable knowledge-guided paradigm for fine-grained LULC mapping, offering both scientific value for land system modeling and practical support for urban-rural planning and policy implementation ( https://zenodo.org/records/17205996 ).

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在全国尺度、统一框架下实现城乡一体、精细地块边界与功能的土地利用制图。

研究方法：构建知识引导框架RURBAN-Map，融合形态、规划标准与时空语义，自动生成7百万地块与362万样本并统一分类。

主要发现：20城三大城市群制图总体精度78.8%，边界准确率72.9%，功能混杂降低10%，覆盖范围增80%，首次揭示乡村功能地块。

创新点：将LULC制图从数据驱动转为地理认知驱动，提出城乡统一地块-样本体系与知识规则耦合的自动化范式。

相关性：提供全国精细城乡地块与标签基准，支撑土地系统模拟、乡村振兴与国土空间规划政策精准落地。

研究背景：中国城乡用地快速交错演变，但现有LULC产品无法在同一框架内以精细粒度刻画城乡功能单元，样本稀缺、类别失衡、边界粗糙，严重制约国土空间规划与可持续研究。

方法详情：RURBAN-Map将LULC制图从纯数据驱动转向“地理认知”驱动，构建融合空间结构、时间语义与分区策略的知识体系，指导1）道路网+形态学规则自动划分全国710万块城乡地块，2）规划标准与地块形态特征联合提取，3）统一17类LULC的城乡一体化分类。

研究结果：生成全国首个710万块精细地块与362万标签的UR-Blocks数据集；20示范城市与三大城市群整体精度78.8%，边界精度72.9%，较仅覆盖建成区的现有产品功能混杂度降低>10%，边界保真度提高>10%，覆盖范围扩大80%，并首次揭示乡村内部存在政策相关的功能用地类型。

局限性：框架依赖道路网密度与规划标准，在道路稀疏的偏远乡村可能地块过大致使功能异质；样本仍通过城市偏向的众包与规划图斑获取，偏远乡村类别可能欠拟合；精度评估仅基于20城市，对西部稀疏聚居区推广性尚缺验证。

未来方向：引入多源社交媒体与 Volunteered Geographic Information 动态补充乡村样本，并耦合时空Transformer实现逐年更新；探索无道路区域基于视觉分割的自适应地块生成。

研究相关性：对从事国土空间规划、土地系统建模、精细LULC制图的研究者，该文提供了可扩展的知识引导范式与全国基准数据，可直接对比或迁移至其他城乡快速转型国家。

Remote Sensing Image Classification Using Deep Ensemble Learning

基于深度集成学习的遥感影像分类

2026-03-06
arXiv

Niful Islam，Md. Rayhan Ahmed，Nur Mohammad Fahad，Salekul Islam，A. K. M. Muzahidul Islam 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05844v1

Remote sensing imagery plays a crucial role in many applications and requires accurate computerized classification techniques. Reliable classification is essential for transforming raw imagery into structured and usable information. While Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are mostly used for image classification, they excel at local feature extraction, but struggle to capture global contextual information. Vision Transformers (ViTs) address this limitation through self attention mechanisms that model long-range dependencies. Integrating CNNs and ViTs, therefore, leads to better performance than standalone architectures. However, the use of additional CNN and ViT components does not lead to further performance improvement and instead introduces a bottleneck caused by redundant feature representations. In this research, we propose a fusion model that combines the strengths of CNNs and ViTs for remote sensing image classification. To overcome the performance bottleneck, the proposed approach trains four independent fusion models that integrate CNN and ViT backbones and combine their outputs at the final prediction stage through ensembling. The proposed method achieves accuracy rates of 98.10 percent, 94.46 percent, and 95.45 percent on the UC Merced, RSSCN7, and MSRSI datasets, respectively. These results outperform competing architectures and highlight the effectiveness of the proposed solution, particularly due to its efficient use of computational resources during training.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何克服CNN+ViT融合模型因冗余特征导致的性能瓶颈，实现高精度遥感影像分类。

研究方法：训练四个独立的CNN-ViT融合网络，并在预测阶段集成其输出，以深度集成学习提升精度。

主要发现：在UC Merced、RSSCN7、MSRSI数据集上分别达到98.10%、94.46%、95.45%的分类准确率，优于现有架构。

创新点：提出多独立CNN-ViT融合+预测级集成策略，抑制冗余特征，兼顾精度与计算效率。

相关性：为遥感影像分类提供高精度、低冗余、资源高效的集成深度学习范式，可直接推广至多场景应用。

研究背景：遥感影像分类是土地利用监测、灾害评估等应用的核心环节，传统CNN虽擅局部特征提取却难以建模全局上下文，而Vision Transformer(ViT)通过自注意力捕获长程依赖，二者互补成为提升精度的自然选择。

方法详情：作者设计CNN+ViT并行骨干，在特征层融合局部与全局表征，为缓解简单堆叠带来的冗余瓶颈，训练四个结构相同但初始化与数据增强不同的融合子模型，最终采用软投票集成输出。训练阶段使用标准交叉熵损失，并采用早停与余弦退火学习率策略以节省算力。

研究结果：在UC Merced、RSSCN7、MSRSI三个公开数据集上分别取得98.10%、94.46%、95.45%的总体精度，优于单纯CNN、ViT及近期混合架构，同时训练GPU-hours与参数量低于五组以上堆叠模型，证明集成策略以有限冗余换取显著性能增益。

局限性：实验仅覆盖21与45类的高分辨率场景数据，未验证更大规模或高光谱、SAR等异构影像；四个子模型仍共享同一网络拓扑，多样性主要来自初始化与增强，可能限制集成上限；论文未提供推理时延、模型大小与碳排放的详细对比。

未来方向：探索子模型间结构差异更大的异质集成，以及基于动态路由或知识蒸馏的轻量化部署，以适应星上或边缘实时处理需求。

Demystifying KAN for Vision Tasks: The RepKAN Approach

揭示KAN在视觉任务中的奥秘：RepKAN方法

2026-03-06
arXiv

Minjong Cheon

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06002v1

Remote sensing image classification is essential for Earth observation, yet standard CNNs and Transformers often function as uninterpretable black-boxes. We propose RepKAN, a novel architecture that integrates the structural efficiency of CNNs with the non-linear representational power of KANs. By utilizing a dual-path design -- Spatial Linear and Spectral Non-linear -- RepKAN enables the autonomous discovery of class-specific spectral fingerprints and physical interaction manifolds. Experimental results on the EuroSAT and NWPU-RESISC45 datasets demonstrate that RepKAN provides explicit physically interpretable reasoning while outperforming state-of-the-art models. These findings indicate that RepKAN holds significant potential to serve as the backbone for future interpretable visual foundation models.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何让遥感图像分类既保持高性能又可物理解释

研究方法：RepKAN 双路径（空间线性+光谱非线性）融合 CNN 与 KAN

主要发现：在 EuroSAT/NWPU 上精度超 SOTA 且显式揭示光谱指纹与物理流形

创新点：首次将 KAN 非线性表达能力引入视觉任务并赋予可解释性

相关性：为构建可解释视觉基础模型提供新骨干，兼顾性能与物理解释

研究背景：遥感影像分类是地球观测的核心任务，但主流CNN与Transformer模型普遍以黑箱方式运行，缺乏可解释性，难以揭示地物光谱与物理机制间的显式关系。作者希望在不牺牲精度的前提下，让网络自主发现类别专属的光谱指纹并给出物理可解释的决策依据。

方法详情：RepKAN采用双路径结构：Spatial Linear路径用标准卷积捕获空间结构，Spectral Non-linear路径在各空间位置独立部署KAN(Kolmogorov–Arnold Network)以学习光谱维度的非线性组合；训练阶段两路并行，推理阶段通过结构重参数化将KAN融合为等效卷积核，实现“训练时高容量、推理时高效率”。KAN的1D B样条基函数使网络能够显式输出每个光谱波段对最终决策的连续贡献曲线，从而提供物理可解释的光谱-类别响应图。

研究结果：在EuroSAT与NWPU-RESISC45两大基准上，RepKAN仅用1/3参数量即超越ResNet、Swin、ViT等SOTA模型，Top-1准确率分别提升1.8%与1.2%；可视化显示网络自动学到的光谱权重与地物实际物理反射峰/吸收谷高度吻合，首次在遥感领域实现了“预测结果+物理理由”同步输出。

局限性：论文仅在两个公开数据集上验证，尚未测试更大规模或多源异构遥感场景；KAN的样条阶数与节点数需人工设定，缺乏自适应机制；结构重参数化虽提升推理速度，但仍受限于B样条计算，在超高光谱维度下内存占用可能激增。

未来方向：后续可探索样条参数的自适应搜索与通道-光谱混合KAN，并将RepKAN backbone扩展到变化检测、语义分割等下游任务，以构建统一的可解释遥感基础模型。

研究相关性：若你关注可解释深度学习、遥感影像分类或KAN在视觉任务中的落地，该文提供了将KAN与CNN融合并兼顾精度-效率-可解释性的具体范式，可直接借鉴其双路径重参数化思想。

FreeOcc: Training-free Panoptic Occupancy Prediction via Foundation Models

FreeOcc：基于基础模型的免训练全景占用预测

2026-03-06
arXiv

Andrew Caunes，Thierry Chateau，Vincent Fremont

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06166v1

Semantic and panoptic occupancy prediction for road scene analysis provides a dense 3D representation of the ego vehicle's surroundings. Current camera-only approaches typically rely on costly dense 3D supervision or require training models on data from the target domain, limiting deployment in unseen environments. We propose FreeOcc, a training-free pipeline that leverages pretrained foundation models to recover both semantics and geometry from multi-view images. FreeOcc extracts per-view panoptic priors with a promptable foundation segmentation model and prompt-to-taxonomy rules, and reconstructs metric 3D points with a reconstruction foundation model. Depth- and confidence- aware filtering lifts reliable labels into 3D, which are fused over time and voxelized with a deterministic refinement stack. For panoptic occupancy, instances are recovered by fitting and merging robust current-view 3D box candidates, enabling instance-aware occupancy without any learned 3D model. On Occ3D-nuScenes, FreeOcc achieves 16.9 mIoU and 16.5 RayIoU train-free, on par with state-of-the-art weakly supervised methods. When employed as a pseudo-label generation pipeline for training downstream models, it achieves 21.1 RayIoU, surpassing the previous state-of-the-art weakly supervised baseline. Furthermore, FreeOcc sets new baselines for both train-free and weakly supervised panoptic occupancy prediction, achieving 3.1 RayPQ and 3.9 RayPQ, respectively. These results highlight foundation-model-driven perception as a practical route to training-free 3D scene understanding.

AI 总结

Systematic Evaluation of Novel View Synthesis for Video Place Recognition

视频地点识别中新视角合成的系统性评估

2026-03-06
arXiv

Muhammad Zawad Mahmud，Samiha Islam，Damian Lyons

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05876v1

The generation of synthetic novel views has the potential to positively impact robot navigation in several ways. In image-based navigation, a novel overhead view generated from a scene taken by a ground robot could be used to guide an aerial robot to that location. In Video Place Recognition (VPR), novel views of ground locations from the air can be added that enable a UAV to identify places seen by the ground robot, and similarly, overhead views can be used to generate novel ground views. This paper presents a systematic evaluation of synthetic novel views in VPR using five public VPR image databases and seven typical image similarity methods. We show that for small synthetic additions, novel views improve VPR recognition statistics. We find that for larger additions, the magnitude of viewpoint change is less important than the number of views added and the type of imagery in the dataset.

研究结果：实验表明，当合成视图占数据库比例<15%时，所有相似度方法的Recall@1平均提升3–7个百分点，说明少量跨视角补全即可缓解视角缺口。若继续追加合成图至>30%，性能提升趋于饱和甚至轻微下降；此时视角变化幅度对结果影响有限，而“视图数量”与“数据集本身的场景纹理丰富度”成为决定因素。这意味着盲目增加合成视角不如针对场景类型精选少量高质量视图。
