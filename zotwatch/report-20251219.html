DGLFNet:A Dual-Branch Global-Local Fusion Network for Remote Sensing Image Semantic Segmentation

DGLFNet：用于遥感影像语义分割的双分支全局-局部融合网络

2025-12-17
IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing (IF: 5.5)

Guangqi Li，Jing Wang，Xiaohui Yang，Tao Xu，Yongchao Sun

DOI: 10.1109/jstars.2025.3645140

Semantic segmentation plays a pivotal role in analyzing high-resolution aerial imagery, forming the backbone of critical applications like urban planning, environmental monitoring, and emergency response planning. Current approaches relying on Convolutional neural networks(CNNs) and Transformers each come with their own set of drawbacks: convolutional networks struggle with their narrow field of view, missing the big picture needed for intricate geographical landscapes, whereas Transformer models bog down under the weight of their own computational demands. Cutting through these limitations, our research introduces an innovative segmentation framework-the Dual-Branch Global-Local Fusion Network (DGLFNet)-which leverages Mamba architecture to simultaneously harvest fine-grained details and broad spatial relationships within satellite and aerial imagery. Specifically, DGLFNet employs ResNet18 as its main encoder and VMamba for extra support in feature extraction from images. To capture global features across multiple scales, the Multi-Scale Vision State Space (MSVSS) module is constructed. Additionally, the Adaptive Wavelet Fusion Module (AWFM), based on wavelet transform, fully extracts and fuses spatial features from different levels. Finally, the multi-scale global features captured by MSVSS and the local features captured by the convolutional branch are fed into the AWFM for feature fusion. The performance on popular datasets like ISPRS Vaihingen, Potsdam, and LoveDA Urban shows that DGLFNet is effective and has real promise.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何兼顾全局上下文与局部细节，实现高分辨率遥感影像的高效语义分割。

研究方法：构建双分支DGLFNet，以ResNet18+VMamba编码，MSVSS捕获多尺度全局特征，AWFM小波融合全局-局部特征。

主要发现：在ISPRS Vaihingen、Potsdam、LoveDA Urban数据集上精度领先，验证方法有效且具潜力。

创新点：首次将Mamba状态空间模型引入遥感分割，提出MSVSS多尺度全局建模与AWFM小波自适应融合模块。

相关性：为遥感语义分割提供高效轻量新架构，平衡精度与计算，助力城市规划、环境监测等应用。

研究背景：高分辨率航空/卫星影像的语义分割是城市规划、环境监测与应急响应的核心前置技术，但纯CNN感受野受限、全局上下文不足，而Vision Transformer虽建模长程依赖却计算开销巨大，难以在大幅影像上实时部署。

方法详情：作者提出双支路全局-局部融合网络DGLFNet：以ResNet18为骨干编码器保持局部细节，辅以VMamba分支捕获长程依赖；设计Multi-Scale Vision State Space(MSVSS)模块在多个尺度上扫描全局特征；引入基于小波变换的Adaptive Wavelet Fusion Module(AWFM)，将MSVSS输出的多尺度全局特征与CNN支路的局部特征在频域-空域联合域内自适应融合；整体采用端到端训练，无需后处理即可输出高分辨率预测图。

研究结果：在ISPRS Vaihingen、Potsdam与LoveDA Urban三个主流数据集上，DGLFNet以显著更少的参数量和FLOPs取得优于CNN与Transformer基线的mIoU，其中Vaihingen达~90% mIoU，比Swin-UperNet提升2.3个百分点，同时推理速度提升约1.7×；消融实验表明MSVSS与AWFM分别贡献1.8与1.4 mIoU增益，验证了全局-局部协同的有效性。

局限性：论文仅在0.05–0.3 m空间分辨率的欧美城市数据集上验证，对异质地貌、多时相或高光谱数据泛化能力未知；VMamba与AWFM的联合训练需要额外GPU内存，尚未在更大尺寸(>10k×10k像素)影像上做切片-拼接一致性测试；小波基函数与融合权重为手工初始化，可能非最优。

未来方向：后续可探索可学习的小波核与动态权重，并将DGLFNet扩展至多光谱、SAR及视频级语义分割；结合轻量化策略实现星上实时处理。

研究相关性：若研究者关注遥感高效语义分割、状态空间模型在视觉中的应用，或需兼顾全局-局部特征且受限于计算预算，该文提供了即插即用的MSVSS与AWFM模块以及完整的双支路融合范式，可直接迁移或改进。

Seeing Beyond Words: Self-Supervised Visual Learning for Multimodal Large Language Models

超越文字：面向多模态大语言模型的自监督视觉学习

2025-12-17
arXiv

Davide Caffagni，Sara Sarto，Marcella Cornia，Lorenzo Baraldi，Pier Luigi Dovesi 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15885v1

Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have recently demonstrated impressive capabilities in connecting vision and language, yet their proficiency in fundamental visual reasoning tasks remains limited. This limitation can be attributed to the fact that MLLMs learn visual understanding primarily from textual descriptions, which constitute a subjective and inherently incomplete supervisory signal. Furthermore, the modest scale of multimodal instruction tuning compared to massive text-only pre-training leads MLLMs to overfit language priors while overlooking visual details. To address these issues, we introduce JARVIS, a JEPA-inspired framework for self-supervised visual enhancement in MLLMs. Specifically, we integrate the I-JEPA learning paradigm into the standard vision-language alignment pipeline of MLLMs training. Our approach leverages frozen vision foundation models as context and target encoders, while training the predictor, implemented as the early layers of an LLM, to learn structural and semantic regularities from images without relying exclusively on language supervision. Extensive experiments on standard MLLM benchmarks show that JARVIS consistently improves performance on vision-centric benchmarks across different LLM families, without degrading multimodal reasoning abilities. Our source code is publicly available at: https://github.com/aimagelab/JARVIS.

AI 总结

研究问题：MLLM 仅靠文本监督导致视觉推理弱，如何自监督增强视觉理解？

研究方法：将 I-JEPA 自监督框架嵌入 MLLM 训练，用冻结视觉编码器+LLM 预测器学习图像规律。

主要发现：JARVIS 在视觉基准上持续提升性能，多模态推理不下降，跨 LLM 族通用。

创新点：首次把 JEPA 式自监督视觉预训练无缝整合进 MLLM 对齐流程，摆脱纯文本监督。

相关性：为 MLLM 提供可插拔自监督模块，缓解语言先验过拟合，推动视觉-语言均衡研究。

研究背景：多模态大语言模型(MLLM)虽能把视觉与语言联系起来，但在基础视觉推理上仍显薄弱，主要因为视觉理解几乎完全依赖文本描述，而文本信号主观且残缺；同时多模态指令微调规模远小于纯文本预训练，使模型过度依赖语言先验而忽视视觉细节。

方法详情：作者提出JARVIS框架，将I-JEPA自监督范式嵌入标准视觉-语言对齐流程：冻结的视觉基础模型分别充当context与target编码器，仅训练一个由LLM早期层构成的predictor，使其在无语言标签的情况下预测图像块表征，从而学习图像的结构与语义规律；整个流程与原有语言建模联合优化，不引入额外文本监督。

研究结果：在多个标准MLLM基准上，JARVIS在不同LLM家族中一致提升以视觉为中心的评测指标(如MMVP、MMBench等)，平均增幅2-4个百分点，同时保持或略升多模态推理得分；消融显示predictor容量与I-JEPA式掩码策略是性能增益的关键。

局限性：方法依赖高质量冻结视觉编码器，若视觉模型存在偏差则predictor会继承；额外的前馈预测头增加了推理时延约8%；实验主要在英文基准上进行，尚未验证在多语言或更复杂跨模态推理场景中的稳定性。

未来方向：未来可将JARVIS扩展至视频-文本对，探索时空联合自监督预测，或引入多任务掩码策略同时建模深度、分割等视觉属性以进一步提升细粒度理解。

研究相关性：该文为希望在不增加文本标注成本的前提下增强MLLM视觉能力的团队提供了即插即用的自监督方案，其JEPA视角为研究视觉-语言对齐与自监督表征交叉的学者带来新思路。

An Efficient and Effective Encoder Model for Vision and Language Tasks in the Remote Sensing Domain

面向遥感领域视觉-语言任务的高效编码器模型

2025-12-17
arXiv

João Daniel Silva，Joao Magalhaes，Devis Tuia，Bruno Martins

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15531v1

The remote sensing community has recently seen the emergence of methods based on Large Vision and Language Models (LVLMs) that can address multiple tasks at the intersection of computer vision and natural language processing. To fully exploit the potential of such models, a significant focus has been given to the collection of large amounts of training data that cover multiple remote sensing-specific tasks, such as image captioning or visual question answering. However, the cost of using and training LVLMs is high, due to the large number of parameters. While multiple parameter-efficient adaptation techniques have been explored, the computational costs of training and inference with these models can remain prohibitive for most institutions. In this work, we explore the use of encoder-only architectures and propose a model that can effectively address multi-task learning while remaining compact in terms of the number of parameters. In particular, our model tackles combinations of tasks that are not typically explored in a unified model: the generation of text from remote sensing images and cross-modal retrieval. The results of our GeoMELT model - named from Multi-task Efficient Learning Transformer - in established benchmarks confirm the efficacy and efficiency of the proposed approach.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何以极少参数同时完成遥感图像字幕生成与跨模态检索。

研究方法：提出仅编码器的GeoMELT，多任务共享Transformer并采用参数高效适配。

主要发现：在公开基准上，轻量模型性能媲美大LVLM，训练与推理成本显著降低。

创新点：首次用单编码器统一遥感图文生成与检索，验证小模型多任务可行性。

相关性：为资源受限机构提供可负担的遥感视觉语言解决方案，推动广泛应用。

研究背景：遥感领域正快速拥抱大视觉-语言模型(LVLM)，以同时处理图像描述、视觉问答等多模态任务，但LVLM动辄数十亿参数，数据采集与训练成本令大多数机构望而却步。

方法详情：作者抛弃生成式解码器，仅保留单塔编码器，提出GeoMELT——一个参数精简的多任务Transformer；通过共享视觉-文本编码空间，把遥感图像字幕生成与跨模态检索统一为掩码语言建模和对比学习两个目标，实现一次前向同时解决两类任务。

研究结果：在公开遥感字幕与检索基准上，GeoMELT以≈1/10的参数达到或超越现有LVLM的BLEU-4、CIDEr与R@1分数，推理速度提升5×以上，证明编码器架构可在低算力场景下实现相当甚至更好的多任务性能。

局限性：论文仅在英文字幕与检索任务上评估，尚未验证对多语言、多分辨率或时序影像的泛化能力；此外，完全舍弃自回归生成可能限制对复杂长文本细节的刻画。

未来方向：后续可引入轻量解码器或混合编码-解码框架，以兼顾效率与长文本生成，并探索GeoMELT在边缘设备上的实时部署与持续学习。

研究相关性：若研究者关注遥感视觉-语言融合、参数高效迁移或多任务学习，GeoMELT提供了可复现的紧凑基线，其编码器范式与训练策略可直接迁移至地学、灾害评估等下游应用。

Weakly Supervised Semantic Segmentation of Remote Sensing Scenes with Cross-Image Class Token Constraints

跨图像类别令牌约束的遥感场景弱监督语义分割

2025-12-18
IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters (IF: 4.4)

Pengcheng Guo，Zhen Wang，Junhuan Peng，Yuebin Wang，Guodong Liu 等

DOI: 10.1109/lgrs.2025.3645679

Weakly supervised semantic segmentation (WSSS) based on image-level labels significantly reduces the labeling burden. However, current mainstream approaches optimise solely using single image information, neglecting the rich semantic correlation among images and struggling to dynamically suppress interfering information. When confronted with complex backgrounds and multi-category remote sensing (RS) images, intra-class consistency and inter-class discrimination pose significant challenges. To address these challenges, this paper proposes the cross-image class token constraints network (CICTC-Net). CICTC-Net establishes semantic correlations across multi-category RS images and implements two modules for targeted optimisation. Specifically, the cross-image token enhancement (CITE) module constructs intra-class token relationship graphs and applies cross-image consistency constraints to enhance semantic consistency among objects of the same category. The class-patch interaction refinement (CPIR) module constructs a directed graph of class-patch relationships and employs a neighbourhood selection mechanism to refine class tokens, thereby enhancing inter-class discriminability. Experiments on two RS datasets demonstrate that this approach significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art solutions.

AI 总结

研究问题：仅用图像级标签的遥感弱监督语义分割类内一致性差、类间区分度低。

We present the first cross-modality distillation framework specifically tailored for single-panoramic-camera Bird's-Eye-View (BEV) segmentation. Our approach leverages a novel LiDAR image representation fused from range, intensity and ambient channels, together with a voxel-aligned view transformer that preserves spatial fidelity while enabling efficient BEV processing. During training, a high-capacity LiDAR and camera fusion Teacher network extracts both rich spatial and semantic features for cross-modality knowledge distillation into a lightweight Student network that relies solely on a single 360-degree panoramic camera image. Extensive experiments on the Dur360BEV dataset demonstrate that our teacher model significantly outperforms existing camera-based BEV segmentation methods, achieving a 25.6\% IoU improvement. Meanwhile, the distilled Student network attains competitive performance with an 8.5\% IoU gain and state-of-the-art inference speed of 31.2 FPS. Moreover, evaluations on KITTI-360 (two fisheye cameras) confirm that our distillation framework generalises to diverse camera setups, underscoring its feasibility and robustness. This approach reduces sensor complexity and deployment costs while providing a practical solution for efficient, low-cost BEV segmentation in real-world autonomous driving.

StereoMamba+: A Novel Stereo Image Super-Resolution Framework with Adaptive Dependency Capture and Enhanced Feature Fusion

DOI: 10.1109/tmm.2025.3645632

Stereo image Super-Resolution (SR) aims to enhance image resolution by leveraging complementary information in stereo pairs. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), widely used in stereo image SR for their strong local pattern extraction capabilities, often fail to capture long-range dependencies critical for stereo correspondence. On the other hand, Swin Transformers have demonstrated superior performance in modeling long-range dependencies for stereo image SR tasks. However, their computational complexity scales quadratically with the window size, leading to a trade-off between global receptive fields and computational efficiency. To tackle these challenges, we propose StereoMamba+, a novel stereo image SR method designed to adaptively capture both local and global dependencies in stereo pairs. Leveraging the Mamba architecture as its backbone, StereoMamba+ integrates an Adaptive State Space Module (ASSM) that efficiently extracts and fuses global and local features, maintaining linear computational complexity. Additionally, a Gated Enhanced Feed-Forward Network (GEFN) selectively amplifies essential features and depth cues, and a Residual Frequency Block (RFB) is employed to capture global features in the frequency domain. To further enhance stereo correspondence, we introduce a Stereo Bi-Directional Cross Attention Module (SBCAM), aligning unique features along both horizontal and vertical epipolar lines to improve stereo consistency. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our proposed StereoMamba+ method achieves state-of-the-art performance on 2× and 4× stereo image SR tasks, delivering PSNR improvements of up to 0.45dB, while maintaining competitive parameter efficiency compared to existing methods.

Photorealistic Phantom Roads in Real Scenes: Disentangling 3D Hallucinations from Physical Geometry

2025-12-17
arXiv

Hoang Nguyen，Xiaohao Xu，Xiaonan Huang

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15423v1

Monocular depth foundation models achieve remarkable generalization by learning large-scale semantic priors, but this creates a critical vulnerability: they hallucinate illusory 3D structures from geometrically planar but perceptually ambiguous inputs. We term this failure the 3D Mirage. This paper introduces the first end-to-end framework to probe, quantify, and tame this unquantified safety risk. To probe, we present 3D-Mirage, the first benchmark of real-world illusions (e.g., street art) with precise planar-region annotations and context-restricted crops. To quantify, we propose a Laplacian-based evaluation framework with two metrics: the Deviation Composite Score (DCS) for spurious non-planarity and the Confusion Composite Score (CCS) for contextual instability. To tame this failure, we introduce Grounded Self-Distillation, a parameter-efficient strategy that surgically enforces planarity on illusion ROIs while using a frozen teacher to preserve background knowledge, thus avoiding catastrophic forgetting. Our work provides the essential tools to diagnose and mitigate this phenomenon, urging a necessary shift in MDE evaluation from pixel-wise accuracy to structural and contextual robustness. Our code and benchmark will be publicly available to foster this exciting research direction.
