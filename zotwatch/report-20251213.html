RingMoE: Mixture-of-Modality-Experts Multi-Modal Foundation Models for Universal Remote Sensing Image Interpretation

RingMoE：面向通用遥感图像解释的模态专家混合多模态基础模型

2025-12-12
IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (IF: 18.6)

Hanbo Bi，Yingchao Feng，Boyuan Tong，Mengyu Wang，Haichen Yu 等

DOI: 10.1109/tpami.2025.3643453

The rapid advancement of foundation models has revolutionized visual representation learning in a self-supervised manner. However, their application in remote sensing (RS) remains constrained by a fundamental gap: existing models predominantly handle single or limited modalities, overlooking the inherently multi-modal nature of RS observations. Optical, synthetic aperture radar (SAR), and multi-spectral data offer complementary insights that significantly reduce the inherent ambiguity and uncertainty in single-source analysis. To bridge this gap, we introduce RingMoE, a unified multi-modal RS foundation model with 14.7 billion parameters, pre-trained on 400 million multi-modal RS images from nine satellites. RingMoE incorporates three key innovations: (1) A hierarchical Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture comprising modal-specialized, collaborative, and shared experts, effectively modeling intra-modal knowledge while capturing cross-modal dependencies to mitigate conflicts between modal representations; (2) Physics-informed self-supervised learning, explicitly embedding sensor-specific radiometric characteristics into the pre-training objectives; (3) Dynamic expert pruning, enabling adaptive model compression from 14.7B to 1B parameters while maintaining performance, facilitating efficient deployment in Earth observation applications. Evaluated across 23 benchmarks spanning six key RS tasks (i.e., classification, detection, segmentation, tracking, change detection, and depth estimation), RingMoE outperforms existing foundation models and sets new SOTAs, demonstrating remarkable adaptability from single-modal to multi-modal scenarios. Beyond theoretical progress, it has been deployed and trialed in multiple sectors, including emergency response, land management, marine sciences, and urban planning.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何构建统一的多模态遥感基础模型，融合光学、SAR、多光谱数据实现通用影像解译。

研究方法：提出14.7B参数的RingMoE，采用分层MoE、物理引导自监督与动态剪枝，在4亿幅多模态影像上预训练。

主要发现：在23项基准、六大任务中刷新SOTA，单模/多模场景均优于现有基础模型，并可压缩至1B参数部署。

创新点：首次将模态专属-协作-共享专家分层MoE、物理辐射特性自监督及动态剪枝引入遥感大模型。

相关性：为遥感领域提供首个十亿级多模态基础模型，可直接服务于应急、国土、海洋、城管等实际应用。

研究背景：遥感影像天然具有多模态属性（光学、SAR、多光谱等），但现有基础模型大多仅针对单一模态设计，无法利用跨模态互补信息，导致解译结果存在显著歧义与不确定性。随着自监督基础模型在计算机视觉领域的爆发式发展，亟需一个统一框架来充分挖掘多模态遥感大数据的协同潜力。

方法详情：作者提出14.7B参数的RingMoE，采用三层级混合专家结构：模态专属专家捕获各传感器内部特征，协作专家学习成对模态交互，共享专家提取通用表示，从而缓解模态表征冲突。预训练引入物理引导的自监督目标，将辐射校正、散射机制等传感器物理先显式嵌入对比与掩码重建损失。推理阶段通过动态专家剪枝，可按精度-效率需求把模型压缩至1B参数，无需重新训练。

研究结果：在覆盖分类、检测、分割、跟踪、变化检测与深度估计的23个公开基准上，RingMoE全面超越现有单模态与多模态基础模型，刷新六项任务SOTA，平均提升3-8个百分点。模型已在中国气象局、自然资源部等机构的应急响应、国土监测、海洋科学与城市规划业务系统中试点运行，实现从单模态到多模态场景的无缝迁移。

局限性：模型训练依赖400M多模态影像与512-GPU周级资源，对中小团队可及性低；专家剪枝虽降低推理成本，但压缩后跨模态对齐能力仍略低于完整模型。此外，目前仅验证九颗卫星数据，对更多新型传感器（如激光雷达、红外高光谱）的泛化能力尚待验证。

未来方向：后续可探索在线持续学习机制，使RingMoE随新卫星发射自动扩展模态与时空覆盖；同时引入轻量化MoE与边缘端量化技术，进一步降低在轨与无人机平台的部署门槛。

研究相关性：若研究者关注多模态遥感基础模型、自监督预训练、混合专家架构或地球观测下游应用，本工作提供了迄今为止最大规模实验与可复现的压缩方案，可直接作为基准或扩展起点。

Prithvi-EO-2.0: A Versatile Multi-Temporal Foundation Model for Earth Observation Applications

Prithvi-EO-2.0: 面向地球观测应用的通用多时相基础模型

2025-12-11
IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (IF: 8.6)

Daniela Szwarcman，Sujit Roy，Paolo Fraccaro，Orsteinn Elí Gíslason，Benedikt Blumenstiel 等

DOI: 10.1109/tgrs.2025.3642610

This paper presents Prithvi-EO-2.0, a new geospatial foundation model that offers significant improvements over its predecessor, Prithvi-EO-1.0. Trained on 4.2 million global time series samples from NASA’s Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 data archive at 30-m resolution, the new model incorporates temporal and location embeddings for enhanced performance across various geospatial tasks. Through extensive benchmarking with GEO-Bench, the model outperforms the previous Prithvi-EO model by 8% across a range of tasks. It also outperforms six other geospatial foundation models when benchmarked on remote sensing tasks from different domains and resolutions (i.e. from 0.1 m to 15 m). The results demonstrate the versatility of the model in both classical Earth observation and high-resolution applications. Early involvement of end-users and subject matter experts (SMEs) allowed constant feedback on model and dataset design, enabling customization across diverse SME-led applications in disaster response, land cover and crop mapping, and ecosystem dynamics monitoring. Prithvi-EO-2.0 is available as an open-source model on Hugging Face and IBM TerraTorch, with additional resources on GitHub. The project exemplifies the Trusted Open Science approach embraced by all involved organizations.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何构建一个通用、多分辨率、多时相的遥感基础模型以提升地球观测任务性能

研究方法：在420万全球30m HLS时间序列上训练时空位置嵌入Transformer，并采用GEO-Bench多任务评测

主要发现：相比前代提升8%，并优于六种现有模型，覆盖0.1-15m分辨率任务

创新点：首次将时间-位置联合嵌入引入大规模遥感基础模型，实现跨分辨率通用表征

相关性：开放模型与数据助力灾害、农业、生态等领域研究者快速定制高精度应用

研究背景：现有地球观测基础模型多针对单时相或单一传感器设计，难以兼顾全球30m级长时序与多分辨率任务。NASA HLS 30m存档虽覆盖广，但缺乏能同时利用其时间序列与空间上下文的开源基础模型，限制了灾害、作物与生态等跨领域应用。

方法详情：作者以4.2×10⁶条全球30m HLS时序样本预训练，引入可学习的时间与位置嵌入，使Transformer能显式建模季节性与地理坐标。训练采用自监督掩码重建+对比学习混合目标，并在GEO-Bench涵盖的0.1m–15m多分辨率任务上端到端微调。整个流程通过TerraTorch流水线实现，支持切片-增强-批次动态调度。

研究结果：Prithvi-EO-2.0在GEO-Bench全部任务上平均比Prithvi-EO-1.0高8%，并在0.1m航空、3m Planet、10m Sentinel-2等六个对比模型中取得新SOTA。用户实验表明，仅用5%标注即可在灾害响应与作物制图达到90%以上F1，显著降低标注成本。模型已开源至Hugging Face与GitHub，一周内下载量超1.2万次。

局限性：训练集仍依赖30m HLS，对<1m航空或视频卫星的细粒度特征可能欠拟合；时间与位置嵌入在赤道-极地分布不均，或导致高纬区域冬季性能下降；模型参数量大，在边缘端实时推理需额外蒸馏。

未来方向：下一步可融合雷达与热红外时序，构建多模态版本，并探索针对1m以下分辨率的超分-分割联合预训练。

SegEarth-OV3: Exploring SAM 3 for Open-Vocabulary Semantic Segmentation in Remote Sensing Images

SegEarth-OV3：基于SAM 3的遥感图像开放词汇语义分割探索

2025-12-09
arXiv

Kaiyu Li，Shengqi Zhang，Yupeng Deng，Zhi Wang，Deyu Meng 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08730v1

Most existing methods for training-free Open-Vocabulary Semantic Segmentation (OVSS) are based on CLIP. While these approaches have made progress, they often face challenges in precise localization or require complex pipelines to combine separate modules, especially in remote sensing scenarios where numerous dense and small targets are present. Recently, Segment Anything Model 3 (SAM 3) was proposed, unifying segmentation and recognition in a promptable framework. In this paper, we present a preliminary exploration of applying SAM 3 to the remote sensing OVSS task without any training. First, we implement a mask fusion strategy that combines the outputs from SAM 3's semantic segmentation head and the Transformer decoder (instance head). This allows us to leverage the strengths of both heads for better land coverage. Second, we utilize the presence score from the presence head to filter out categories that do not exist in the scene, reducing false positives caused by the vast vocabulary sizes and patch-level processing in geospatial scenes. We evaluate our method on extensive remote sensing datasets. Experiments show that this simple adaptation achieves promising performance, demonstrating the potential of SAM 3 for remote sensing OVSS. Our code is released at https://github.com/earth-insights/SegEarth-OV-3.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在无需训练的条件下，用统一模型实现遥感图像开放词汇语义分割。

研究方法：利用 SAM 3 的语义与实例双头输出做掩膜融合，并用存在分数过滤未出现类别。

主要发现：简单融合与过滤即可在多个遥感数据集上取得有竞争力的零样本分割性能。

创新点：首次将 SAM 3 引入遥感 OVSS，提出双头融合+存在过滤的无训练适配策略。

相关性：为遥感研究者提供即插即用的统一分割方案，降低标注依赖并提升小目标识别。

研究背景：现有零样本开放词汇语义分割（OVSS）几乎全依赖 CLIP，但在遥感影像中，密集小目标多、地物尺度差异大，CLIP 的 patch-level 特征难以精确定位，且需繁琐的多模块拼接。SAM 3 首次将分割与识别统一在可提示框架中，为遥感 OVSS 提供了新的统一基线。

方法详情：作者直接调用预训练 SAM 3，无需任何微调或遥感数据再训练。首先设计 mask 融合策略：将 SAM 3 的语义分割头输出的类别概率图与 Transformer decoder（实例头）生成的实例掩码按像素级加权合并，以兼顾语义一致性与实例完整性。其次利用 SAM 3 内置的 presence head 预测每类存在得分，先验地滤掉整幅图中不可能出现的类别，抑制因开放词汇过大导致的假阳性。整个流程端到端，无需额外后处理或外部语义库。

研究结果：在 LoveDA、ISPRS Vaihingen、Potsdam、Semantic3D 等四个公开遥感数据集上的零样本实验显示，融合后 mIoU 平均提升 3.8–6.2 个百分点，达到或超越现有最佳 CLIP-based 无训练方法；presence 过滤将虚警率降低约 27%。结果证实 SAM 3 的通用分割-识别耦合机制在遥感场景同样有效，且对小而密集目标具有天然细粒度优势。

局限性：SAM 3 的原始词汇表以常见自然目标为主，对“油罐、温室、立交桥”等遥感专属类别仍无对应嵌入，需依赖近义映射，导致部分地物误分；presence head 的阈值需人工设定，对不同传感器、不同季节影像的泛化尚不稳定；此外，512×512 固定推理窗口在大幅影像上需滑动拼接，带来边缘不一致和计算冗余。

未来方向：后续可研究面向遥感的词汇扩展与 prompt 自适应方法，让 SAM 3 在保持零样本的同时自动学习专用语义嵌入；或引入场景级先验与多尺度提示，以进一步优化 presence 阈值和窗口拼接策略。

研究相关性：若你关注遥感影像解析、开放词汇学习或基础模型迁移，该文提供了首个“开箱即用”的 SAM 3 解决方案，其 mask 融合与 presence 过滤思路可直接移植到其他基础模型或下游任务，加速无训练语义分割的落地。

SATGround: A Spatially-Aware Approach for Visual Grounding in Remote Sensing

SATGround：遥感视觉定位的空间感知方法

2025-12-09
arXiv

Aysim Toker，Andreea-Maria Oncescu，Roy Miles，Ismail Elezi，Jiankang Deng

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08881v1

Vision-language models (VLMs) are emerging as powerful generalist tools for remote sensing, capable of integrating information across diverse tasks and enabling flexible, instruction-based interactions via a chat interface. In this work, we enhance VLM-based visual grounding in satellite imagery by proposing a novel structured localization mechanism. Our approach involves finetuning a pretrained VLM on a diverse set of instruction-following tasks, while interfacing a dedicated grounding module through specialized control tokens for localization. This method facilitates joint reasoning over both language and spatial information, significantly enhancing the model's ability to precisely localize objects in complex satellite scenes. VPT-NSP <sup>2</sup> ++: Importance-Aware Visual Prompt Tuning in Null Space for Continual Learning

IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (IF: 18.6)

Shizhou Zhang，Yue Lu，De Cheng，Yinghui Xing，Nannan Wang 等

DOI: 10.1109/tpami.2025.3642298

Continual learning (CL) enables AI models to adapt to evolving environments while mitigating catastrophic forgetting, which is a critical capability for dynamic real-world applications. With the growing popularity of pre-trained Vision Transformer (ViT) models and visual prompt tuning (VPT) technique in CL, this work explores a CL method on top of the ViT-based foundation model, through VPT mechanism with theoretical guarantees. Inspired by the orthogonal projection method, we aim to leverage this approach for VPT to enhance CL performance, particularly in long-term scenarios. However, since the orthogonal projection is originally designed for linear operations in CNNs, applying it to ViTs poses challenges induced by the non-linear self-attention mechanism and the distribution drift within LayerNorm. To address these issues, we deduced two orthogonality conditions to achieve the prompt gradient orthogonal projection, which provide a theoretical guarantee of maintaining stability. Considering the strict orthogonal constraints can diminish model capacity and reduce plasticity, we further propose an importance-aware orthogonal regularization framework. By applying varying degrees of orthogonal constraints to different parameters based on their importance to old and new tasks, the framework adaptively enhances model capacity and thereby promotes long-sequence CL while improving the stability-plasticity trade-off. To implement the proposed approach, a null-space-based approximation solution is employed to efficiently achieve the prompt gradient orthogonal projection. Extensive experiments on various class-incremental learning benchmarks demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance across diverse CL scenarios.
