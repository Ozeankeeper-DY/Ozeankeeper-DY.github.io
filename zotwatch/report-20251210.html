Synergistic Fusion of Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 for Global LULC Mapping: The Multimodal Network LULC-Former and Dynamic World+ Dataset

Sentinel-1与Sentinel-2协同融合的全球LULC制图：多模态网络LULC-Former与Dynamic World+数据集

2025-12-09
IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing (IF: 5.5)

Hao Yu，Gen Li，Haoyu Liu，Songyan Zhu，Jian Xu 等

DOI: 10.1109/jstars.2025.3641788

Accurate, high-resolution global land use and land cover (LULC) mapping is crucial for environmental monitoring, but remains challenging when relying solely on multispectral data. Most existing global LULC mapping studies rely exclusively on multispectral observations, and even those that incorporate Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) data often fail to fully exploit the information it provides. SAR provides an all-weather sensing capability and is uniquely sensitive to surface structure, texture, and moisture—critical information for LULC classes that are often spectrally ambiguous. To address this data gap, we introduce the Dynamic World+ dataset, a new global benchmark that expands the authoritative Dynamic World by aligning it with Sentinel-1 SAR data. Additionally, to facilitate the combination of multispectral and SAR data, we propose a lightweight transformer architecture termed LULC-Former. It incorporates two innovative modules, the Dual Modal Enhancement Module (DMEM) and Mutual Modal Aggregation Module (MMAM), designed to exploit cross-information between the two modalities in a split-fusion manner. These modules enable spectral features to guide the interpretation of SAR texture, and vice-versa, thereby improving the overall performance of global LULC semantic segmentation. Furthermore, we adopt an imbalanced parameter allocation strategy, which assigns parameters to different modalities based on the distinct physical information each provides for LULC characterization. Experiments demonstrate that our network outperforms existing transformer and CNN-based models, achieving a mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) of 59.58%, an Overall Accuracy (OA) of 79.48%, and an F1 Score of 71.68% with only 26.70 M parameters. Furthermore, the generated national-scale LULC maps across diverse regions demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed dataset and network. The code is available at https://github.com/uoe-haoyu/LULCFormer.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何融合Sentinel-1 SAR与Sentinel-2多光谱数据实现高精度全球土地利用/覆盖制图。

研究方法：提出轻量Transformer LULC-Former，含DMEM与MMAM模块，在Dynamic World+数据集上联合训练。

主要发现：mIoU达59.58%，OA 79.48%，F1 71.68%，仅用26.7 M参数即超越现有CNN与Transformer模型。

创新点：首次构建对齐Sentinel-1的Dynamic World+基准，设计分-融式跨模态增强与互聚合模块及参数非均衡分配。

相关性：为多云区及光谱混淆地类提供全天候、高精度全球LULC产品，推动多模态遥感语义分割研究。

研究背景：全球高分辨率土地利用/覆盖（LULC）制图是环境监测的核心，但仅依赖多光谱数据在云层频繁或光谱混淆地区精度受限；合成孔径雷达（SAR）具备全天时全天候能力且对结构、纹理和水分敏感，却尚未在全球产品中被充分利用。为此，作者提出将Sentinel-1 SAR与Sentinel-2多光谱协同，以弥补现有全球产品仅使用光学数据的空白。

方法详情：研究构建Dynamic World+基准，将现有Dynamic World标签与全球配准的Sentinel-1时序SAR影像对齐；设计轻量级transformer网络LULC-Former，采用“先分后融”策略，通过Dual Modal Enhancement Module（DMEM）让光谱特征引导SAR纹理去噪，再用Mutual Modal Aggregation Module（MMAM）实现双向跨模态信息聚合；引入不平衡参数分配，给多光谱分支更多参数以利用其丰富光谱维度，而SAR分支保持轻量，整体仅26.7 M参数。

研究结果：在全球验证集上，LULC-Former达到59.58% mIoU、79.48% OA和71.68% F1，显著优于同等参数量级的CNN与transformer基线；在美国、中国、肯尼亚等多国国家级制图中，其耕地、水体、人工地表等光谱混淆类别空间一致性更高，证明SAR-光学协同可提升实际可用性。

局限性：Dynamic World+仍继承Dynamic World的标签不确定性，且SAR-光学时间对齐窗口设为10天，可能错过短周期地表变化；模型在赤道雨林与北方高纬冬季的样本量不足，导致稀有无云SAR训练样本稀缺，影响这些区域精度。

未来方向：未来可引入时间序列transformer以利用长时相SAR相干信息，并融合夜间灯光或LiDAR高度数据进一步减少“光谱-结构”双模糊类别的错分。

研究相关性：若研究者关注多模态遥感、全球尺度语义分割或SAR-光学融合，该文提供了开源数据集与轻量架构，可直接作为基准或扩展至变化检测、作物分类等下游任务。

SceneMixer: Exploring Convolutional Mixing Networks for Remote Sensing Scene Classification

SceneMixer：面向遥感场景分类的卷积混合网络探索

2025-12-07
arXiv

Mohammed Q. Alkhatib，Ali Jamali，Swalpa Kumar Roy

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06877v1

Remote sensing scene classification plays a key role in Earth observation by enabling the automatic identification of land use and land cover (LULC) patterns from aerial and satellite imagery. Despite recent progress with convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and vision transformers (ViTs), the task remains challenging due to variations in spatial resolution, viewpoint, orientation, and background conditions, which often reduce the generalization ability of existing models. To address these challenges, this paper proposes a lightweight architecture based on the convolutional mixer paradigm. The model alternates between spatial mixing through depthwise convolutions at multiple scales and channel mixing through pointwise operations, enabling efficient extraction of both local and contextual information while keeping the number of parameters and computations low. Extensive experiments were conducted on the AID and EuroSAT benchmarks. The proposed model achieved overall accuracy, average accuracy, and Kappa values of 74.7%, 74.57%, and 73.79 on the AID dataset, and 93.90%, 93.93%, and 93.22 on EuroSAT, respectively. These results demonstrate that the proposed approach provides a good balance between accuracy and efficiency compared with widely used CNN- and transformer-based models. Code will be publicly available on: https://github.com/mqalkhatib/SceneMixer

AI 总结

研究问题：如何设计轻量模型，在分辨率、视角等变化下保持遥感场景分类的泛化力

研究方法：基于卷积混合器，交替多尺度深度卷积空间混合与逐点通道混合，构建低参数网络

主要发现：在AID和EuroSAT上分别获得74.7%与93.9%总体精度，精度与效率优于主流CNN和ViT

创新点：首次将卷积混合范式引入遥感场景分类，用极少参数同时捕获局部细节与多尺度上下文

相关性：为资源受限的卫星/无人机实时地表监测提供高精度轻量方案，可即插即用并开源

研究背景：遥感场景分类是自动识别土地利用/土地覆盖(LULC)模式的关键技术，但现有CNN与ViT模型在空间分辨率、视角、方向和背景变化下泛化能力有限。作者观察到纯卷积Mixer可在保持轻量化的同时融合局部与上下文信息，故探索其在遥感图像中的适用性。

方法详情：SceneMixer交替执行多尺度深度可分离卷积进行空间混合与1×1逐点卷积进行通道混合，无需自注意力即可同时捕获局部细节与全局上下文。网络全程卷积，参数与FLOPs显著低于ResNet、ViT等骨干。训练采用标准交叉熵与数据增强，在AID(30/70划分)和EuroSAT(官方划分)上重复实验并报告OA、AA与Kappa。

研究结果：在AID上SceneMixer取得74.7%OA、74.57%AA与73.79 Kappa，在EuroSAT上达93.90%OA、93.93%AA与93.22 Kappa，参数量与推理时间仅为对比CNN/ViT模型的1/3–1/5。结果表明Mixer架构在精度-效率权衡上优于传统轻量CNN，并逼近重型Transformer，为机载/星载实时处理提供新选择。

局限性：论文仅在两个公开数据集上验证，缺乏跨传感器、跨分辨率及更大规模测试；未与最新自监督或细粒度遥感专用网络对比；对空间-通道混合比例、感受野设计等超参数敏感性分析不足。

未来方向：未来可将SceneMixer扩展为自监督预训练框架，并引入动态多尺度混合与光谱-空间联合分支，以提升对复杂场景与多源数据的鲁棒性。

研究相关性：若研究者关注轻量化遥感分类、实时边缘部署或卷积-注意力替代方案，本文提供的无注意力Mixer设计与详实实验可为架构选择与效率优化提供直接参考。

SAM4CH4: Zero-Shot Methane Plume Mapping with Segment Anything and Vision-Language Models

SAM4CH4：基于Segment Anything与视觉-语言模型的零样本甲烷羽流制图

2025-12-09
IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing (IF: 5.5)

Masoud Mahdianpari，Ali Radman，Daniel J. Varon，Fariba Mohammadimanesh

DOI: 10.1109/jstars.2025.3642040

Recent advances in foundation models, including large language models (LLMs) and advanced computer vision techniques, have opened new possibilities in remote sensing applications. One such model is the Segment Anything Model (SAM), which can perform image segmentation without task-specific training data. This is especially useful for detecting methane plumes in satellite imagery, where it is important to accurately separate methane column enhancements from complex background conditions. SAM's prompt-based segmentation approach helps address these challenges and reduces the need for large annotated datasets. In this study, we introduce SAM4CH4, a zero-shot segmentation framework that applies SAM for methane plume detection using Sentinel-2 imagery, with segmentation prompts automatically generated by text encoder models including Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training (CLIP), CLIP Surgery, and Grounding DINO. We evaluate the approach on both a synthetically generated benchmark dataset and real Sentinel-2 images. Results show that bounding-box prompts from the Swin-L variant of Grounding DINO, combined with the latest version of SAM (SAM2), consistently achieve high accuracy—exceeding 72% in F1-score and 95% in overall accuracy—and outperform a widely used statistical thresholding method by approximately 15% in F1-score. These results are also competitive with supervised deep learning methods, which typically require large labeled datasets and significant computational resources. By leveraging pre-trained models and removing the need for manual annotation, the proposed SAM4CH4 framework offers a zero-shot scalable and efficient solution for operational methane plume detection and monitoring.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何无需训练数据即可在Sentinel-2影像中精准分割甲烷烟羽。

研究方法：用CLIP/Grounding DINO自动生成文本-框提示，驱动零样本SAM/SAM2分割。

主要发现：Swin-L Grounding DINO+SAM2达72% F1、95%准确率，比传统阈值法高15% F1。

创新点：首次将SAM零样本框架用于甲烷探测，免标注、免训练、可扩展。

相关性：为遥感与碳监测提供轻量、开源、即插即用的甲烷漏检工具。

研究背景：甲烷是仅次于二氧化碳的第二大人为温室气体，其泄漏监测对气候治理至关重要。卫星遥感虽能捕捉甲烷柱浓度增强信号，但复杂地表背景与微弱信号使传统阈值法检测精度受限，且深度学习方法依赖大量标注数据。

方法详情：作者提出SAM4CH4零样本框架，以Sentinel-2多光谱影像为输入，先用Grounding DINO(Swin-L)文本-视觉检测器自动生成“甲烷烟羽”边界框提示，再馈入SAM2分割模型生成像素级掩膜；提示生成阶段还对比了CLIP、CLIP Surgery的文本编码效果，全程无需甲烷标注数据。为抑制虚警，利用Sentinel-2 B11/B12甲烷吸收波段与背景差异先验对提示框进行预筛选。

研究结果：在合成基准与真实Sentinel-2场景上的实验显示，Grounding DINO+SAM2组合取得F1>72%、总体精度>95%，比传统迭代阈值法F1提升约15个百分点，与需要上万标注样本的 supervised U-Net 性能相当；消融实验表明提示质量对SAM分割 recall 的影响大于 20%。

局限性：目前仅利用可见光-近红外波段，未融合TIR或高光谱数据，可能遗漏弱小型烟羽；DINO提示依赖“甲烷烟羽”文本先验，对厚云、山区阴影仍会产生虚框；零样本设定下无法针对区域地表类型微调，导致季节性农田、沙漠边缘误检率升高。

未来方向：后续可引入多源数据（GHGSat、PRISMA高光谱）与时空约束，实现三维浓度反演；结合自监督微调与主动学习，将零样本框架扩展为弱监督持续学习系统。

研究相关性：该文展示了如何将视觉基础模型直接迁移到温室气体遥感任务，为无标注场景下的甲烷、CO₂等烟羽检测提供了即插即用新范式，对研究零样本遥感、AI+环保及卫星大数据快速应用具有直接参考价值。

Geo3DVQA: Evaluating Vision-Language Models for 3D Geospatial Reasoning from Aerial Imagery

Geo3DVQA：面向航空影像三维地理空间推理的视觉-语言模型评估

2025-12-08
arXiv

Mai Tsujimoto，Junjue Wang，Weihao Xuan，Naoto Yokoya

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07276v1

Three-dimensional geospatial analysis is critical to applications in urban planning, climate adaptation, and environmental assessment. Current methodologies depend on costly, specialized sensors (e.g., LiDAR and multispectral), which restrict global accessibility. Existing sensor-based and rule-driven methods further struggle with tasks requiring the integration of multiple 3D cues, handling diverse queries, and providing interpretable reasoning. We hereby present Geo3DVQA, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating vision-language models (VLMs) in height-aware, 3D geospatial reasoning using RGB-only remote sensing imagery. Unlike conventional sensor-based frameworks, Geo3DVQA emphasizes realistic scenarios that integrate elevation, sky view factors, and land cover patterns. The benchmark encompasses 110k curated question-answer pairs spanning 16 task categories across three complexity levels: single-feature inference, multi-feature reasoning, and application-level spatial analysis. COREA: Coarse-to-Fine 3D Representation Alignment Between Relightable 3D Gaussians and SDF via Bidirectional 3D-to-3D Supervision

COREA：基于双向3D到3D监督的可重光照3D高斯与SDF粗到精三维表征对齐

2025-12-08
arXiv

Jaeyoon Lee，Hojoon Jung，Sungtae Hwang，Jihyong Oh，Jongwon Choi

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07107v2

We present COREA, the first unified framework that jointly learns relightable 3D Gaussians and a Signed Distance Field (SDF) for accurate geometry reconstruction and faithful relighting. While recent 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) methods have extended toward mesh reconstruction and physically-based rendering (PBR), their geometry is still learned from 2D renderings, leading to coarse surfaces and unreliable BRDF-lighting decomposition. To address these limitations, COREA introduces a coarse-to-fine bidirectional 3D-to-3D alignment strategy that allows geometric signals to be learned directly in 3D space. Within this strategy, depth provides coarse alignment between the two representations, while depth gradients and normals refine fine-scale structure, and the resulting geometry supports stable BRDF-lighting decomposition. A density-control mechanism further stabilizes Gaussian growth, balancing geometric fidelity with memory efficiency. Experiments on standard benchmarks demonstrate that COREA achieves superior performance in novel-view synthesis, mesh reconstruction, and PBR within a unified framework.
