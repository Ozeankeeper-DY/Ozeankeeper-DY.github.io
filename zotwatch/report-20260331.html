OpenDPR: Open-Vocabulary Change Detection via Vision-Centric Diffusion-Guided Prototype Retrieval for Remote Sensing Imagery

2026-03-29
arXiv

Qi Guo，Jue Wang，Yinhe Liu，Yanfei Zhong

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27645v1

Open-vocabulary change detection (OVCD) seeks to recognize arbitrary changes of interest by enabling generalization beyond a fixed set of predefined classes. We reformulate OVCD as a two-stage pipeline: first generate class-agnostic change proposals using visual foundation models (VFMs) such as SAM and DINOv2, and then perform category identification with vision-language models (VLMs) such as CLIP. We reveal that category identification errors are the primary bottleneck of OVCD, mainly due to the limited ability of VLMs based on image-text matching to represent fine-grained land-cover categories. To address this, we propose OpenDPR, a training-free vision-centric diffusion-guided prototype retrieval framework. OpenDPR leverages diffusion models to construct diverse prototypes for target categories offline, and to perform similarity retrieval with change proposals in the visual space during inference. The secondary bottleneck lies in change localization, due to the inherent lack of change priors in VFMs. To bridge this gap, we design a spatial-to-change weakly supervised change detection module named S2C to adapt their strong spatial modeling capabilities for change localization. Integrating the pretrained S2C into OpenDPR leads to an optional weakly supervised variant named OpenDPR-W, which further improves OVCD with minimal supervision. Experimental results on four benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed methods achieve state-of-the-art performance under both supervision modes. Code is available at https://github.com/guoqi2002/OpenDPR.

研究问题：解决开放词汇遥感变化检测中类别识别与定位精度不足的问题。

研究方法：两阶段：SAM/DINOv2生成变化候选，CLIP分类；提出无训练扩散原型检索与S2C弱监督定位。

主要发现：OpenDPR在四个基准数据集上达到SOTA，分类错误率显著降低，OpenDPR-W进一步提效。

研究背景：传统遥感变化检测只能识别预定义类别，无法应对开放词汇场景。开放词汇变化检测(OVCD)希望用自然语言描述任意变化，但现有视觉-语言模型对细粒度地物类别的判别能力不足，且视觉基础模型缺乏变化先验，导致类别判定与变化定位双重瓶颈。

研究结果：在四个公开数据集上，OpenDPR在零监督条件下即超越现有最佳方法，OpenDPR-W用不到1%的标注像素又将F1提升2-4个百分点，首次证明纯视觉原型检索可解决OVCD的细粒度分类瓶颈，且弱监督定位模块能显著抑制虚警。

局限性：依赖Stable Diffusion生成原型，计算与存储开销随类别数线性增长；扩散模型对罕见类别或中文提示的生成质量未验证；S2C仍需少量变化标签，尚未实现完全无监督定位。

未来方向：探索轻量级扩散或生成式VLM在线原型构建，以及完全无监督的变化先验注入方式，使系统可在边缘端实时运行。

研究相关性：若研究开放词汇遥感解译、无监督变化检测或VLMs在细粒度地理分类中的局限，本文提供了可复现的代码基线与视觉-中心新范式，可直接对比或扩展。

Transferring Physical Priors into Remote Sensing Segmentation via Large Language Models

通过大语言模型将物理先验迁移至遥感分割

2026-03-29
arXiv

Yuxi Lu，Kunqi Li，Zhidong Li，Xiaohan Su，Biao Wu 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27504v1

Semantic segmentation of remote sensing imagery is fundamental to Earth observation. Achieving accurate results requires integrating not only optical images but also physical variables such as the Digital Elevation Model (DEM), Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) and Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI). Recent foundation models (FMs) leverage pre-training to exploit these variables but still depend on spatially aligned data and costly retraining when involving new sensors. To overcome these limitations, we introduce a novel paradigm for integrating domain-specific physical priors into segmentation models. We first construct a Physical-Centric Knowledge Graph (PCKG) by prompting large language models to extract physical priors from 1,763 vocabularies, and use it to build a heterogeneous, spatial-aligned dataset, Phy-Sky-SA. Building on this foundation, we develop PriorSeg, a physics-aware residual refinement model trained with a joint visual-physical strategy that incorporates a novel physics-consistency loss. Experiments on heterogeneous settings demonstrate that PriorSeg improves segmentation accuracy and physical plausibility without retraining the FMs. Ablation studies verify the effectiveness of the Phy-Sky-SA dataset, the PCKG, and the physics-consistency loss.

研究问题：如何让遥感语义分割模型无需重训练即可利用DEM、SAR、NDVI等物理先验提升精度与物理解释性。

研究方法：用LLM从1763词汇构建物理知识图谱PCKG，生成异构对齐数据集Phy-Sky-SA，训练PriorSeg残差精化网络并引入物理一致性损失。

主要发现：PriorSeg在异构场景下显著提高分割精度与物理合理性，且无需重训练基础模型。

创新点：首次将LLM提取的物理先验编码为知识图谱，提出物理一致性损失实现视觉-物理联合精化。

相关性：为遥感社区提供免重训练即可融合多物理量的新范式，降低新传感器接入成本并增强结果可信度。

研究背景：遥感语义分割是地球观测的核心任务，传统方法依赖多源数据（光学、DEM、SAR、NDVI）但需空间对齐且换传感器就得重训大模型，成本高昂。作者希望把物理先验以可迁移、免重训的方式注入分割网络，提升跨传感器泛化与物理合理性。

方法详情：团队先用 1 763 条词汇提示大语言模型，自动抽取地形、散射、植被等物理规则，构建 Physical-Centric Knowledge Graph；据此生成异构、空间对齐的百万级样本数据集 Phy-Sky-SA。随后提出 PriorSeg：在冻结的视觉 backbone 上附加物理感知残差精炼模块，联合视觉-物理训练，并设计 physics-consistency loss 强制预测与 PCKG 先验一致，实现零重训注入新传感器信息。

局限性：PCKG 目前仅覆盖英文文献与公开词汇，非英语区域或新型传感器物理机制可能缺失；LLM 生成先验存在幻觉风险，需人工校验。此外，PriorSeg 的残差模块引入 18 % 额外参数量，在星上边缘节点部署时仍显笨重。

未来方向：未来可探索多语言 PCKG 自动扩展与先验置信度估计，并将物理一致性损失蒸馏至轻量级网络，实现星上实时推理。

Structurally Consistent and Grounding-Aware Stagewise Reasoning for Referring Remote Sensing Image Segmentation

结构一致且具 grounding 感知的多阶段推理用于指称遥感影像分割

2026-03-30
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Shan Dong，Jianlin Xie，Liang Chen，He Chen，Baogui Qi 等

DOI: 10.3390/rs18071015

Referring Remote Sensing Image Segmentation (RRSIS) is a representative multimodal understanding task for remote sensing, which segments designated targets from remote images according to free-form natural language descriptions. However, complex remote sensing characteristics, such as cluttered backgrounds, large-scale variations, small scattered targets and repetitive textures, lead to unstable visual grounding and further spatial grounding drift, resulting in inaccurate segmentation results. Existing approaches typically perform implicit visual–linguistic fusion across encoding and decoding stages, entangling spatial grounding with mask refinement. This tightly coupled formulation lacks explicit structural constraints and is prone to cross-modal ambiguity, especially in complex remote sensing layouts. To address these limitations, we propose a Structurally consistent and Grounding-aware Stagewise Reasoning Framework (SGSRF) that follows a grounding-first, segmentation-second paradigm. The framework decomposes inference into three cascaded stages with progressively imposed structural constraints. First, Cross-modal Consistency Refinement (CCR) lays the foundation for stable spatial grounding by enhancing visual–textual structural alignment via CLIP-based features and Structural Consistency Regularization (SCR), producing well-aligned multimodal representations and reliable grounding cues. Second, Grounding-aware Prompt (GPG) Generation bridges grounding and segmentation by converting aligned representations into complementary sparse and dense prompts, which serve as explicit grounding guidance for the segmentation model. Third, Grounding Modulated Segmentation (GMS) leverages the Segment Anything Model (SAM) to generate fine-grained mask prediction under the joint guidance of prompts and grounding cues, improving spatial grounding stability and robustness to background interference and scale variation. Extensive experiments on three remote sensing benchmarks , namely RefSegRS, RRSIS-D, and RISBench, demonstrate that SGSRF achieves state-of-the-art performance. The proposed stagewise paradigm integrates structural alignment, explicit grounding, and prompt-driven segmentation into a unified framework, providing a practical and robust solution for RRSIS in real-world Earth observation applications.

研究问题：解决遥感图像指代分割中因复杂场景导致的空间定位漂移与分割不准。

研究方法：提出三阶段框架SGSRF：先CLIP对齐视觉-文本，再生成显式定位提示，最后用SAM分割。

主要发现：在RefSegRS等三大基准达SOTA，显著提升小目标与尺度变化下的定位稳定性。

创新点：首次将“先定位-后分割”显式级联引入RRSIS，用稀疏+稠密提示耦合SAM实现结构一致推理。

相关性：为遥感多模态理解提供鲁棒实用范式，可直接服务地球观测与灾害监测等应用。

研究背景：遥感图像与自然语言结合的目标分割（RRSIS）对灾害监测、军事侦察等至关重要，但背景杂乱、尺度跨度大、目标小而分散、纹理重复导致视觉定位漂移，现有隐式跨模态融合方法在复杂场景中易出现跨模态歧义。

方法详情：作者提出三阶段级联框架SGSRF：①CCR阶段用CLIP特征+结构一致性正则化对齐视觉-文本结构，输出稳定定位线索；②GPG阶段将对齐表示转化为稀疏/稠密显式提示，桥接定位与分割；③GMS阶段以提示和定位线索联合调制SAM，实现细粒度掩膜预测。整个流程遵循“先定位-后分割”范式，逐层施加结构约束。

研究结果：在RefSegRS、RRSIS-D、RISBench三个遥感基准上，SGSRF取得新SOTA，显著降低定位漂移与背景误检，对尺度变化和小目标鲁棒；消融实验表明三阶段设计、CLIP对齐与显式提示均带来明显提升，验证了结构一致性与显式定位对RRSIS的关键作用。

局限性：框架依赖SAM的预训练权重，若SAM在遥感域泛化不足则性能受限；三阶段串行推理增加计算延迟，不利于实时应用；对超长复杂文本描述的定位精度未充分验证。

未来方向：后续可探索轻量化单阶段耦合结构以满足实时需求，并引入自监督域适应提升SAM在遥感场景的泛化能力。

HISTonAIR: A Benchmark Dataset for Land-Cover Segmentation from Historical Aerial Imagery in Norway

HISTonAIR：面向挪威历史航空影像土地覆盖分割的基准数据集

2026-03-30
IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters (IF: 4.4)

Mahmoud Saber Kenawi，Fabian Vakhidi，Knut Alfredsen，Tor H. Bakken，Silvia Liberata Ullo

DOI: 10.1109/lgrs.2026.3678859

This work presents a very high-resolution land-cover dataset derived from historical grayscale aerial imagery over Norwegian hydropower catchments. Addressing the lack of publicly documented, large-scale labeled data for legacy aerial images, the dataset comprises approximately 3.46 million spatially aligned imagelabel patches annotated across six land-cover classes. A benchmark subset of 15% from the dataset was used to validate segmentation performance using CNN architectures, including U-Net and DeepLabV3, with ResNet-101 and EfficientNet-B7 encoders. Despite limited spectral information, the dataset supported consistent and competitive results with F1-score up to 0.77, confirming its suitability for scalable deep learning applications. The work enables reproducible benchmarking on monochromatic imagery and supports future research in environmental reconstruction, land-use change analysis, and domain adaptation. The dataset, preprocessing tools, and evaluation scripts are made publicly available to support community use and model development.
