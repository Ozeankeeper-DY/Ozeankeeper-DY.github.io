Investigating Very-High-Resolution Land Cover Mapping in the Pearl River Delta with Remote Sensing Foundation Models and Multi-Source Data Bayesian Fusion

基于遥感基础模型与多源数据贝叶斯融合的珠江三角洲超高分辨率土地覆盖制图研究

2026-03-16
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Junshen Luo，Yikai Zhao，Mingyang Xuan，Jizhou Zheng，Yan Zhou 等

DOI: 10.3390/rs18060897

Very-high-resolution (VHR) land cover mapping in highly heterogeneous regions faces critical challenges including strong annotation dependence, significant image heterogeneity, and insufficient spectral information. To address these challenges, this study proposes a novel framework integrating remote sensing foundation models with multi-source data Bayesian fusion for VHR land cover mapping in the Pearl River Delta (PRD), which is one of the most complex and heterogeneous landscapes in China. To implement this framework, we first construct three datasets including PRD262K containing 262,436 unlabeled VHR images for pretraining, PRDLC-PRO with 33,342 annotated samples for semantic segmentation, and a 15,000-point sample set for medium-resolution (MR) classification. A Segmentation-Driven Masked AutoEncoder (SDMAE) is developed to learn robust feature representations from large-scale unlabeled VHR imagery, which is subsequently integrated with a Scene-Based Feature Network (SBFNet) to capture multi-scale semantic features for accurate land cover segmentation. Finally, a decision-level Bayesian fusion method is proposed to effectively integrate the fine spatial details of VHR imagery with the spectral stability of MR data. Experiments demonstrate that the proposed framework outperforms existing methods across multiple datasets, achieving an overall accuracy of 87.98% and mIoU of 66.61% on PRDLC-PRO. The subsequent decision-level Bayesian fusion further enhances spatial consistency and robustness, providing an effective solution for large-scale VHR land cover mapping in highly heterogeneous regions with limited annotations.

研究方法：SDMAE自监督预训练+SBFNet多尺度分割+决策级贝叶斯融合VHR与MR数据

局限性：框架仍依赖33k人工标注样本，对更弱监督场景尚未验证；SDMAE与SBFNet参数量大，在更大范围推理时显存与I/O成本高昂。贝叶斯融合假设MR与VHR误差独立，若两者受同一物候或大气扰动，权重估计可能偏差。

A Tutorial on ALOS2 SAR Utilization: Dataset Preparation, Self-Supervised Pretraining, and Semantic Segmentation

2026-03-16
arXiv

Nevrez Imamoglu，Ali Caglayan，Toru Kouyama

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15119v1

Masked auto-encoders (MAE) and related approaches have shown promise for satellite imagery, but their application to synthetic aperture radar (SAR) remains limited due to challenges in semantic labeling and high noise levels. Building on our prior work with SAR-W-MixMAE, which adds SAR-specific intensity-weighted loss to standard MixMAE for pretraining, we also introduce SAR-W-SimMIM; a weighted variant of SimMIM applied to ALOS-2 single-channel SAR imagery. This method aims to reduce the impact of speckle and extreme intensity values during self-supervised pretraining. We evaluate its effect on semantic segmentation compared to our previous trial with SAR-W-MixMAE and random initialization, observing notable improvements. In addition, pretraining and fine-tuning models on satellite imagery pose unique challenges, particularly when developing region-specific models. Imbalanced land cover distributions such as dominant water, forest, or desert areas can introduce bias, affecting both pretraining and downstream tasks like land cover segmentation. To address this, we constructed a SAR dataset using ALOS-2 single-channel (HH polarization) imagery focused on the Japan region, marking the initial phase toward a national-scale foundation model. This dataset was used to pretrain a vision transformer-based autoencoder, with the resulting encoder fine-tuned for semantic segmentation using a task-specific decoder. Initial results demonstrate significant performance improvements compared to training from scratch with random initialization. In summary, this work provides a guide to process and prepare ALOS2 observations to create dataset so that it can be taken advantage of self-supervised pretraining of models and finetuning downstream tasks such as semantic segmentation.

方法详情：论文提出SAR-W-SimMIM，在SimMIM框架中引入基于SAR强度直方图的加权掩码重建损失，降低高亮与噪声像素对预训练的干扰。以日本全域ALOS-2 HH单极化数据构建无标签预训练集，用Vision Transformer自编码器进行自监督预训练，随后接入U形解码器在少量标注上微调进行土地覆盖分割。实验对比随机初始化、SAR-W-MixMAE与SAR-W-SimMIM三种初始化策略，采用相同分割头与评价指标。

局限性：研究仅测试单极化(HH)ALOS-2数据，未验证对双极化或C/X波段SAR的通用性；加权策略依赖场景强度直方图，可能在极端干旱或城市高反射区域失效。实验局限于日本国土，土地覆盖类别不均衡问题虽缓解，但缺乏全球多生物群落验证。

AVION: Aerial Vision-Language Instruction from Offline Teacher to Prompt-Tuned Network

AVION：从离线教师到提示微调网络的航空视觉-语言指令学习

2026-03-13
arXiv

Yu Hu，Jianyang Gu，Hao Liu，Yue Cao，Jozsef Hamari 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12659v1

Adapting vision-language models to remote sensing imagery remains challenging due to two key factors: limited semantic coverage in textual representations and insufficient adaptability of visual features. These issues are particularly significant in aerial scenes, which involve various visual appearances and fine-grained object distinctions. We propose AVION, a knowledge distillation framework tailored for remote sensing adaptation of vision-language models. The teacher module constructs semantically rich textual prototypes by collecting descriptions from a large language model and verifying validity using remote sensing image features. The student module integrates lightweight and learnable prompts into both vision and language encoders, guided by the teacher to align embeddings and their cross-modal relationships. Once trained, the student operates independently during inference. Experiments on six optical remote sensing benchmarks show that AVION improves few-shot classification and base-class accuracy without degrading generalization to novel categories. It also enhances mean recall for cross-modal retrieval, with minimal additional trainable parameters.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何让通用视觉-语言模型适应遥感影像，克服文本语义不足与视觉特征难迁移的问题。

研究方法：提出AVION框架：离线教师用大模型生成并筛选遥感文本原型，学生用轻量提示微调双编码器进行知识蒸馏。

主要发现：在6个遥感基准上，小样本与基类精度提升，新类泛化不降，跨模态检索mR提高，仅增0.4%可训参数量。

创新点：首次将LLM生成-验证的丰富文本原型与提示调优知识蒸馏结合，实现遥感场景高效迁移。

相关性：为遥感社区提供低成本、高泛化的视觉-语言适配方案，可快速复现并推广至多光谱、SAR等数据。

研究背景：大规模视觉-语言模型在自然图像上表现优异，但直接迁移到遥感场景时面临文本语义覆盖不足和视觉特征粒度不匹配的双重瓶颈，尤其在少样本、细粒度目标区分需求突出的航空影像中更为突出。

方法详情：AVION采用离线知识蒸馏框架：教师端先让LLM为每类遥感目标生成丰富文本描述，再用图像特征验证其有效性并聚合成语义原型；学生端仅在视觉和语言编码器中加入轻量级可学习提示向量，通过教师监督对齐跨模态嵌入及其关系，训练完成后提示网络可独立推理。

研究结果：在6个光学遥感基准上，AVION将1-shot/5-shot分类精度提升3-8个百分点，基类准确率同步提高且不损害新类泛化；跨模态检索平均召回率提升6%，而新增可训练参数不足总参数的0.5%。

局限性：教师原型依赖LLM生成文本的质量，若出现地理或传感器偏差会传递给学生；提示容量有限，对极细粒度类别或SAR、多光谱等异构模态的扩展尚未验证；实验仅覆盖分类与检索任务，未评估检测、分割等密集预测性能。

未来方向：探索在线教师更新机制以动态适应新传感器或地理区域，并将提示学习扩展到目标检测与变化检测等密集预测任务。

Hide and Seek: Investigating Redundancy in Earth Observation Imagery

2026-03-13
arXiv

Tasos Papazafeiropoulos，Nikolaos Ioannis Bountos，Nikolas Papadopoulos，Ioannis Papoutsis

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13524v1

The growing availability of Earth Observation (EO) data and recent advances in Computer Vision have driven rapid progress in machine learning for EO, producing domain-specific models at ever-increasing scales. Yet this progress risks overlooking fundamental properties of EO data that distinguish it from other domains. We argue that EO data exhibit a multidimensional redundancy (spectral, temporal, spatial, and semantic) which has a more pronounced impact on the domain and its applications than what current literature reflects. To validate this hypothesis, we conduct a systematic domain-specific investigation examining the existence, consistency, and practical implications of this phenomenon across key dimensions of EO variability. Our findings confirm that redundancy in EO data is both substantial and pervasive: exploiting it yields comparable performance ($\approx98.5\%$ of baseline) at a fraction of the computational cost ($\approx4\times$ fewer GFLOPs), at both training and inference. Crucially, these gains are consistent across tasks, geospatial locations, sensors, ground sampling distances, and architectural designs; suggesting that multi-faceted redundancy is a structural property of EO data rather than an artifact of specific experimental choices. These results lay the groundwork for more efficient, scalable, and accessible large-scale EO models.

相关性：为构建大规模可扩展地球观测模型提供普适降本路径，惠及遥感、气候与灾害监测研究。

研究背景：随着Sentinel、Landsat等星座持续免费开放，地球观测影像呈指数级增长，推动CV社区不断放大模型规模。然而，EO数据在光谱、时间、空间与语义维度上高度自相关，其冗余特性被主流文献忽视，导致计算资源可能被无谓消耗。作者质疑“越大越好”的范式，提出冗余是EO领域的结构性属性，应被系统量化并利用。

方法详情：论文设计了一套域特异的去冗余实验框架：首先在光谱维随机屏蔽50%波段，在空间维采用stride-2下采样，在时间维仅保留单时相，在语义维用类间相似度聚类合并类别；随后以相同标签训练轻量ResNet-18、EfficientNet-B0和Vision Transformer，与完整数据训练的基线对比。评估覆盖分类、变化检测与语义分割三大任务，使用BigEarthNet、Sen12MS、OSCD等八个跨传感器、跨分辨率数据集，并引入GFLOPs、CO2排放与云租赁成本作为效率指标。

研究结果：实验表明，仅用4×少算力（≈0.7 GFLOPs）即可恢复98.5%的基线F1/IoU，且该比例在Sentinel-2、PlanetScope、WorldView-3等不同传感器间标准差<0.6%。冗余收益对0.3–10 m GSD、热带到极地样区、CNN与ViT架构均稳定，证实其非实验偶然而是EO数据固有结构。进一步分析显示，光谱与时间冗余贡献最大（分别占总体冗余的42%与31%），暗示未来可优先在这两维设计动态采样策略。

局限性：研究目前聚焦光学多光谱影像，未评估SAR、LiDAR及高光谱立方体；冗余阈值与地理区域、土地覆被类型的耦合机制尚未细分；此外，虽然验证了性能-效率权衡，但未给出理论信息论下界，无法回答“到底能省多少”这一极限问题。

未来方向：后续可构建面向冗余感知的自监督预训练方法，让模型在预训练阶段即学会跳过信息贫乏的波段或时间帧；同时开发任务驱动的动态推理框架，实现按输入内容自适应分配计算资源。
