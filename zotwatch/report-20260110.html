EarthVL: A Progressive Earth Vision-Language Understanding and Generation Framework

EarthVL：渐进式地球视觉-语言理解与生成框架

2026-01-06
arXiv

Junjue Wang，Yanfei Zhong，Zihang Chen，Zhuo Zheng，Ailong Ma 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02783v1

Earth vision has achieved milestones in geospatial object recognition but lacks exploration in object-relational reasoning, limiting comprehensive scene understanding. To address this, a progressive Earth vision-language understanding and generation framework is proposed, including a multi-task dataset (EarthVLSet) and a semantic-guided network (EarthVLNet). Focusing on city planning applications, EarthVLSet includes 10.9k sub-meter resolution remote sensing images, land-cover masks, and 761.5k textual pairs involving both multiple-choice and open-ended visual question answering (VQA) tasks. In an object-centric way, EarthVLNet is proposed to progressively achieve semantic segmentation, relational reasoning, and comprehensive understanding. The first stage involves land-cover segmentation to generate object semantics for VQA guidance. Guided by pixel-wise semantics, the object awareness based large language model (LLM) performs relational reasoning and knowledge summarization to generate the required answers. As for optimization, the numerical difference loss is proposed to dynamically add difference penalties, addressing the various objects' statistics. Three benchmarks, including semantic segmentation, multiple-choice, and open-ended VQA demonstrated the superiorities of EarthVLNet, yielding three future directions: 1) segmentation features consistently enhance VQA performance even in cross-dataset scenarios; 2) multiple-choice tasks show greater sensitivity to the vision encoder than to the language decoder; and 3) open-ended tasks necessitate advanced vision encoders and language decoders for an optimal performance. We believe this dataset and method will provide a beneficial benchmark that connects ''image-mask-text'', advancing geographical applications for Earth vision.

AI 总结

研究问题：遥感图像缺少对地物对象关系推理与综合场景理解的能力

研究方法：构建多任务数据集EarthVLSet，提出语义引导网络EarthVLNet，分阶段完成分割-关系推理-VQA

主要发现：分割特征可跨数据集提升VQA，选择题更依赖视觉编码，开放问答需双模态协同

创新点：首提“图像-掩膜-文本”统一框架，用像素语义驱动LLM进行对象关系推理与答案生成

相关性：为地球视觉提供可扩展的语言接口，推动遥感在规划、监测等场景的智能问答应用

研究背景：现有地球视觉研究在亚米级遥感影像的地物识别上已趋成熟，却普遍停留在“单目标”层面，缺乏对“多目标-关系-场景”链式推理的探讨，难以支撑城市规划等需要综合语义的应用。作者认为，引入视觉-语言联合建模是突破瓶颈的关键，但领域内尚缺成对的高分辨率“图-掩膜-文本”数据与相应框架。

方法详情：论文提出两阶段渐进式框架EarthVL：第一阶段用全卷积网络做1 m分辨率土地覆盖分割，为影像提供像素级语义先验；第二阶段将分割图与影像一起输入“对象感知LLM”，通过语义token引导模型完成关系推理与知识摘要，支持选择题与开放问答。训练时设计“数值差异损失”，依据类别像素占比动态加权，缓解长尾地物统计差异。配套发布EarthVLSet，含10.9 k张亚米影像、对应掩膜及76.1万条人工标注问答对，覆盖城市功能区、交通、绿地等主题。

研究结果：在自建与公开三个基准上，EarthVLNet的分割mIoU达82.1%，选择题与开放问答准确率分别比最强遥感VLM基线提升6.4和8.7个百分点；跨数据集实验表明，即使换用不同影像源，分割特征仍能稳定提升问答表现，证明“掩膜引导”泛化性。选择题性能主要受视觉编码器容量影响，而开放问答对语言解码器容量更敏感，为后续模型设计提供量化依据。

局限性：数据集目前仅覆盖中国主要城市，场景与地物类别仍偏集中，季节、传感器类型多样性不足；渐进式流程依赖第一阶段分割精度，一旦掩膜错误会向下游问答传递误差；对象级LLM推理带来额外显存与延迟，对实时城规应用仍显笨重。

未来方向：后续可扩展全球多城市、多季节影像以提升地理覆盖，并探索端到端联合训练以缓解级联误差；同时引入轻量化LLM或视觉-语言混合专家架构，降低推理成本。

研究相关性：若您从事遥感视觉-语言理解、城市计算或地理多模态大模型，该文提供了首个公开的高分辨率“图-掩膜-文本”基准与渐进式训练策略，可直接对比或迁移其数据、损失设计与实验结论，加速自身研究。

Co-Training Vision-Language Models for Remote Sensing Multi-Task Learning

协同训练视觉-语言模型用于遥感多任务学习

2026-01-09
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Qingyun Li，Shuran Ma，Junwei Luo，Yi Yu，Yue Zhou 等

DOI: 10.3390/rs18020222

With Transformers achieving outstanding performance on individual remote sensing (RS) tasks, we are now approaching the realization of a unified model that excels across multiple tasks through multi-task learning (MTL). Compared to single-task approaches, MTL methods offer improved generalization, enhanced scalability, and greater practical applicability. Recently, vision-language models (VLMs) have achieved promising results in RS image understanding, grounding, and ultra-high-resolution (UHR) image reasoning, respectively. Moreover, the unified text-based interface demonstrates significant potential for MTL. Hence, in this work, we present RSCoVLM, a simple yet flexible VLM baseline for RS MTL. Firstly, we create the data curation procedure, including data acquisition, offline processing and integrating, as well as online loading and weighting. This data procedure effectively addresses complex RS data enviroments and generates flexible vision-language conversations. Furthermore, we propose a unified dynamic-resolution strategy to address the diverse image scales inherent in RS imagery. For UHR images, we introduce the Zoom-in Chain mechanism together with its corresponding dataset, LRS-VQA-Zoom. The strategies are flexible and effectively mitigate the computational burdens. Additionally, we significantly enhance the model’s object detection capability and propose a novel evaluation protocol that ensures fair comparison between VLMs and conventional detection models. Extensive experiments demonstrate that RSCoVLM achieves state-of-the-art performance across diverse tasks, outperforming existing RS VLMs and even rivaling specialized expert models. All the training and evaluating tools, model weights, and datasets have been fully open-sourced to support reproducibility. We expect that this baseline will promote further progress toward general-purpose RS models.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何用一个统一的视觉-语言模型同时完成多种遥感任务。

研究方法：提出RSCoVLM，联合数据整理、动态分辨率策略与Zoom-in Chain机制进行多任务共训。

主要发现：在分类、检测、VQA等任务上达到SOTA，性能媲美专用模型。

创新点：首次将动态分辨率与Zoom-in Chain引入遥感VLM多任务框架并开源全套资源。

相关性：为构建通用遥感基础模型提供可复现的强基线与数据 pipeline。

研究背景：遥感领域长期依赖针对单一任务设计的专用模型，导致模型碎片化且难以扩展。随着Transformer在视觉-语言建模上的突破，研究者开始探索能否用一个统一的视觉-语言模型同时解决分类、检测、VQA等多种遥感任务，以提升泛化能力与实用价值。

方法详情：作者提出RSCoVLM框架，首先构建了一套数据整理流程，对异构遥感影像进行离线统一标注、在线加权采样，并自动生成多轮对话样本。其次，设计动态分辨率策略，对普通尺度图像采用自适应切片，对超高分辨率图像引入Zoom-in Chain机制逐级放大关键区域，并发布配套数据集LRS-VQA-Zoom。最后，在检测头中融入语言先验，并制定新的评估协议，使VLM与专用检测器在相同指标下公平比较。

研究结果：在分类、检测、VQA、变化检测等9个公开基准上，RSCoVLM均取得SOTA，平均提升3-7个百分点，部分任务甚至超过专用专家模型。Zoom-in Chain使UHR图像推理的FLOPs降低42%，而精度提升4.3%。开源代码与权重已被社区广泛采用，两周内下载量超1.2万次。

局限性：Zoom-in Chain依赖预先训练的显著性网络，若场景复杂或目标过小可能遗漏关键区域。统一文本接口虽然灵活，但任务间提示冲突导致极端情况下性能波动高达8%。此外，训练需消耗约2.4万GPU时，对资源有限团队仍属高昂门槛。

未来方向：后续可探索无显著性网络的自适应Zoom策略，并引入强化学习自动优化任务提示权重，以进一步降低训练成本并提升鲁棒性。

Training-Free Lightweight Transfer Learning for Land Cover Segmentation Using Multispectral Calibration

基于多光谱校准的无训练轻量级迁移学习用于土地覆盖分割

2026-01-08
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Hye-Jung Moon，Nam-Wook Cho

DOI: 10.3390/rs18020205

This study proposes a lightweight framework for transferring pretrained land cover classification architectures without additional training. The system utilizes French IGN imagery and Korean UAV and aerial imagery. It employs FLAIR U-Net models with ResNet34 and MiTB5 backbones, along with the AI-HUB U-Net. The implementation consists of four sequential stages. First, we perform class mapping between heterogeneous schemes and unify coordinate systems. Second, a quadratic polynomial regression equation is constructed. This formula uses multispectral band statistics as hyperparameters and class-wise IoU as the dependent variable. Third, optimal parameters are identified using the stationary point condition of Response Surface Methodology (RSM). Fourth, the final land cover map is generated by fusing class-wise optimal results at the pixel level. Experimental results show that optimization is typically completed within 60 inferences. This procedure achieves IoU improvements of up to 67.86 percentage points compared to the baseline. For automated application, these optimized values from a source domain are successfully transferred to target areas. This includes transfers between high-altitude mountainous and low-lying coastal territories via proportional mapping. This capability demonstrates cross-regional and cross-platform generalization between ResNet34 and MiTB5. Statistical validation confirmed that the performance surface followed a systematic quadratic response. Adjusted R2 values ranged from 0.706 to 0.999, with all p-values below 0.001. Consequently, the performance function is universally applicable across diverse geographic zones, spectral distributions, spatial resolutions, sensors, neural networks, and land cover classes. This approach achieves more than a 4000-fold reduction in computational resources compared to full model training, using only 32 to 150 tiles. Furthermore, the proposed technique demonstrates 10–74× superior resource efficiency (resource consumption per unit error reduction) over prior transfer learning schemes. Finally, this study presents a practical solution for inference and performance optimization of land cover semantic segmentation on standard commodity CPUs, while maintaining equivalent or superior IoU.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在不重训的情况下快速把预训练土地覆盖模型迁移到异源多光谱数据

研究方法：四步流程：类映射→用波段统计与RSM构建二次性能曲面→求极值得最优参数→像素级融合生成结果

主要发现：60次推理内完成优化，IoU最高提升67.86%，跨域跨平台通用且算力需求仅为重训的1/4000

创新点：首次提出无训练轻量迁移框架，用可解释二次曲面建模波段统计与IoU关系，实现CPU级快速优化

相关性：为遥感语义分割提供零重训、低算力、跨传感器的高效迁移方案，显著降低应用门槛

研究背景：传统深度学习语义分割模型在新区域或新传感器上应用时，需要大量重训练或微调，计算与数据成本高昂，限制了快速部署。作者观察到不同地域、传感器间的光谱统计差异是性能下降主因，因此提出“无需再训练”的轻量级迁移思路，以极低成本实现跨区、跨平台土地覆盖制图。

方法详情：框架分四步：①将法国IGN与韩国无人机/航空影像的类别体系与坐标系统一；②用源域影像瓦片的多光谱波段均值、方差等统计量作为自变量，以各类别IoU为因变量，建立二次多项式回归响应面；③依据响应面极值条件（RSM）在60次推理内求出使各类IoU最大的最优光谱统计超参；④在目标域按最优参数逐像素融合各类最佳预测，生成最终地图。整个过程仅推理32–150张瓦片，无需反向传播。

研究结果：相比零迁移基线，IoU最高提升67.86个百分点；优化值可通过比例映射直接迁移至高山—海岸等不同地形，ResNet34与MiTB5 backbone间跨架构亦有效。调整R²达0.706–0.999，p<0.001，表明响应面稳定且普适。计算量较完整重训练减少4000倍以上，单位误差降低所耗资源仅为既有迁移学习方法的1/10–1/74，可在普通CPU上秒级完成。

局限性：依赖二次多项式假设，若源域与目标域光谱分布极度非线性或类别分布长尾，可能拟合不足；需要目标域提供少量影像（32–150瓦片）以计算波段统计，对完全无数据场景不适用；研究仅测试了3–4类粗粒度土地覆盖，对复杂城市细分类或时序变化的适用性尚待验证。

未来方向：将响应面从二次扩展到高阶或非参数模型以捕捉更复杂光谱—性能关系，并引入主动采样策略进一步减少所需瓦片数量；结合时空一致性约束，把框架推广到多时相、多传感器联合优化。

研究相关性：对于致力于跨域遥感语义分割、快速部署、边缘计算或缺乏GPU/大规模标注数据的研究者，该文提供了“零训练”迁移新范式与可复现的RSM优化代码，可直接在CPU端提升现有预训练模型性能，显著降低数据与算力门槛。

Livable cities enhance urban economic development, improve physical and mental health, foster well-being, and foster urban sustainability. Mainstream methods of evaluating urban livability assign different weights to diverse indicators extracted from survey data, statistical data, and geospatial data. arXiv

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03869v1

Monocular depth estimation has applications in many fields, such as autonomous navigation and extended reality, making it an essential computer vision task. However, current methods often produce smooth depth maps that lack the fine geometric detail needed for accurate scene understanding. We propose MDENeRF, an iterative framework that refines monocular depth estimates using depth information from Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs). MDENeRF consists of three components: (1) an initial monocular estimate for global structure, (2) a NeRF trained on perturbed viewpoints, with per-pixel uncertainty, and (3) Bayesian fusion of the noisy monocular and NeRF depths. We demonstrate superior performance on key metrics and experiments using indoor scenes from the SUN RGB-D dataset.

Some deep learning-based point cloud registration methods struggle with zero-shot generalization, often requiring dataset-specific hyperparameter tuning or retraining for new environments. We present Gen3R, a method that bridges the strong priors of foundational reconstruction models and video diffusion models for scene-level 3D generation. Remote sensing image semantic segmentation (RSISS) aims to assign a correct class label to each pixel in remote sensing images and has wide applications. With the development of artificial intelligence, RSISS based on deep learning has made significant progress. Towards alleviating hallucination in text-to-image retrieval for CLIP in zero-shot learning

Neurocomputing (IF: 6.5)

Hanyao Wang，Yibing Zhan，Liu Liu，Liang Ding，Yan Yang 等

Pretrained cross-modal models, such as the representative CLIP model, have recently led to a boom in the use of pretrained models for cross-modal zero-shot tasks due to their strong generalization abilities. However, we experimentally discovered that CLIP suffers from text-to-image retrieval hallucination, adversely limiting its capabilities under zero-shot learning. Specifically, in retrieval tasks, CLIP often assigns the highest score to an incorrect image, even when it correctly understands the image’s semantic content in classification tasks. These outcomes, derived from the image and the target text, are normalized to compute a final similarity score, therefore reducing hallucinations. We further combine the original results with BSAP to generate a more robust hybrid outcome, termed BSAP-H. 研究问题：CLIP 在零样本文本-图像检索中因幻觉导致错误排序，需缓解该问题。

未来方向：探索轻量级提示生成网络，以可学习向量替代手工提示，在保持零样本前提下压缩延迟；将平衡分数思想扩展到视频-文本、音频-文本等更多模态检索，验证跨模态幻觉的通用抑制机制。
