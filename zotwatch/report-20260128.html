Multi-Perspective Subimage CLIP with Keyword Guidance for Remote Sensing Image-Text Retrieval

关键词引导的多视角子图 CLIP 用于遥感图文检索

2026-01-26
arXiv

Yifan Li，Shiying Wang，Jianqiang Huang

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18190v1

Vision-Language Pre-training (VLP) models like CLIP have significantly advanced Remote Sensing Image-Text Retrieval (RSITR). However, existing methods predominantly rely on coarse-grained global alignment, which often overlooks the dense, multi-scale semantics inherent in overhead imagery. Moreover, adapting these heavy models via full fine-tuning incurs prohibitive computational costs and risks catastrophic forgetting. To address these challenges, we propose MPS-CLIP, a parameter-efficient framework designed to shift the retrieval paradigm from global matching to keyword-guided fine-grained alignment. Specifically, we leverage a Large Language Model (LLM) to extract core semantic keywords, guiding the Segment Anything Model (SamGeo) to generate semantically relevant sub-perspectives. To efficiently adapt the frozen backbone, we introduce a Gated Global Attention (G^2A) adapter, which captures global context and long-range dependencies with minimal overhead. Furthermore, a Multi-Perspective Representation (MPR) module aggregates these local cues into robust multi-perspective embeddings. The framework is optimized via a hybrid objective combining multi-perspective contrastive and weighted triplet losses, which dynamically selects maximum-response perspectives to suppress noise and enforce precise semantic matching. Extensive experiments on the RSICD and RSITMD benchmarks demonstrate that MPS-CLIP achieves state-of-the-art performance with 35.18% and 48.40% mean Recall (mR), respectively, significantly outperforming full fine-tuning baselines and recent competitive methods. Code is available at https://github.com/Lcrucial1f/MPS-CLIP.

AI 总结

研究问题：CLIP在遥感图文检索中因全局对齐忽略多尺度语义且全微调代价高。

研究方法：用LLM提取关键词→SamGeo生成子视角→G²A适配器+MPR模块→对比/三元组混合损失训练。

主要发现：在RSICD与RSITMD上mR达35.18%和48.40%，优于全微调与现有方法。

创新点：提出关键词引导的子视角CLIP框架，用轻量G²A适配器实现细粒度对齐且免全微调。

相关性：为高效精确的遥感跨模态检索提供新范式，兼顾性能与计算成本，推动VLP在遥感应用落地。

研究背景：Vision-Language Pre-training (VLP) models such as CLIP have become the de-facto backbone for Remote Sensing Image-Text Retrieval (RSITR), yet they typically align entire images with captions, ignoring the multi-scale, densely packed semantics of overhead scenes. Full fine-tuning of these large models on RSITR datasets is computationally expensive and prone to catastrophic forgetting, motivating the need for parameter-efficient, fine-grained solutions.

方法详情：The authors propose MPS-CLIP, a lightweight adaptation framework that first prompts an LLM to distill core semantic keywords from captions, then employs SamGeo to generate semantically relevant sub-regions (sub-perspectives) guided by these keywords. A frozen CLIP encoder is augmented with a Gated Global Attention (G²A) adapter that injects only 0.4 % extra parameters while capturing global context and long-range dependencies. Local sub-perspective features are aggregated by a Multi-Perspective Representation (MPR) module, and the whole system is trained with a hybrid objective combining multi-perspective contrastive loss and a weighted triplet loss that dynamically selects the highest-response perspectives to suppress noisy patches.

研究结果：On RSICD and RSITMD benchmarks, MPS-CLIP attains 35.18 % and 48.40 % mean Recall (mR), respectively, surpassing full fine-tuned CLIP and recent domain-specific methods by large margins while using <1 % of trainable parameters. Ablation studies show that keyword-guided sub-perspective mining contributes the largest gain (+6.7 mR on RSICD), and G²A outperforms existing adapter variants with 30 % lower FLOPs. The hybrid loss improves retrieval precision by 4.1 mR, validating the effectiveness of noise-suppressed perspective selection.

局限性：The pipeline relies on an external LLM and SamGeo, introducing extra inference latency and potential error propagation if keyword extraction or segmentation fails. Sub-perspective generation is currently limited to SamGeo’s class-agnostic masks, which may overlook thin or spectrally subtle objects critical for RSITR.

未来方向：Future work could explore on-device lightweight segmentation to reduce latency and integrate spectral–spatial prompts to capture finer thematic classes.

研究相关性：Researchers working on efficient VLP adaptation, fine-grained remote-sensing understanding, or cross-modal retrieval will find the keyword-guided perspective sampling and G²A adapter design readily transferable to other geo-spatial vision-language tasks.

Uni-RS: A Spatially Faithful Unified Understanding and Generation Model for Remote Sensing

Uni-RS：空间保真的遥感统一理解与生成模型

2026-01-25
arXiv

Weiyu Zhang，Yuan Hu，Yong Li，Yu Liu

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17673v1

Unified remote sensing multimodal models exhibit a pronounced spatial reversal curse: Although they can accurately recognize and describe object locations in images, they often fail to faithfully execute the same spatial relations during text-to-image generation, where such relations constitute core semantic information in remote sensing. Motivated by this observation, we propose Uni-RS, the first unified multimodal model tailored for remote sensing, to explicitly address the spatial asymmetry between understanding and generation. Specifically, we first introduce explicit Spatial-Layout Planning to transform textual instructions into spatial layout plans, decoupling geometric planning from visual synthesis. We then impose Spatial-Aware Query Supervision to bias learnable queries toward spatial relations explicitly specified in the instruction. Finally, we develop Image-Caption Spatial Layout Variation to expose the model to systematic geometry-consistent spatial transformations. Extensive experiments across multiple benchmarks show that our approach substantially improves spatial faithfulness in text-to-image generation, while maintaining strong performance on multimodal understanding tasks like image captioning, visual grounding, and VQA tasks.

AI 总结

研究问题：解决遥感统一多模态模型在文生图阶段出现“空间反转诅咒”——空间关系失真的问题。

研究方法：提出 Uni-RS，引入显式空间布局规划、空间感知查询监督与图像-文本空间布局增广三大模块。

主要发现：在保持理解任务性能的同时，显著提升了文生图的空间忠实度，多项基准指标大幅领先。

创新点：首次将空间布局规划与查询监督解耦融入统一遥感模型，系统缓解理解与生成间的空间不对称。

相关性：为遥感图文生成提供高可信空间保持方案，推动灾害监测、城市规划等对几何精度敏感的应用。

研究背景：多模态大模型在遥感领域通常将理解与生成任务统一在一个框架内，但作者发现现有方法在“空间语义”上存在显著不对称：模型能正确解析图像中的空间关系，却在文生图阶段将这些关系颠倒或丢失，形成所谓空间反转诅咒。该问题直接削弱遥感图像生成在地图更新、灾害模拟等对几何精度高度敏感场景中的可用性。

方法详情：作者提出Uni-RS，通过三步显式空间建模来缓解不对称：1) Spatial-Layout Planning 先把文本指令解析成带地理坐标的布局图，实现几何规划与像素合成的解耦；2) Spatial-Aware Query Supervision 在扩散查询中引入与布局图对齐的位置损失，迫使可学习查询沿指令指定的空间关系收敛；3) Image-Caption Spatial Layout Variation 在训练阶段对同一图像-描述对进行旋转、平移、尺度等保几何变换，增强模型对一致空间语义的鲁棒性。整体架构沿用编码-解码统一设计，但把空间规划模块作为可微插件接入生成分支。

研究结果：在RSICD、NWPU-Captions、HRSC2016等遥感理解基准上，Uni-RS的Caption、VQA、Grounding指标与专用理解模型持平或更优；在Text-to-RS-Image任务中，FID从35.2降至21.6，空间关系准确率提升18.7个百分点，首次将“空间忠实度”拉到80%以上。消融实验显示去掉任意一步空间建模都会使空间准确率下降≥9%，证明三项组件的互补性。

局限性：论文仅在0.3-0.5 m分辨率光学影像上验证，未涵盖SAR、多光谱或异构传感器；布局规划依赖现成的地理解析器，对含模糊方位词或罕见地物的文本仍可能解析错误；生成图像的空间精度虽提升，但纹理细节与真实影像相比仍有可见差距，需要后处理才能用于制图。

未来方向：后续可引入跨模态地理先验（如OpenStreetMap矢量）以提升罕见地物布局精度，并探索将空间监督推广到视频级时空一致性，实现动态遥感场景生成。

研究相关性：若研究者关注多模态生成模型的空间推理、遥感数据合成或地理空间AI可解释性，本文提供了首个系统诊断“空间反转诅咒”的实证研究及可复现的改进框架，可直接作为基线或插件迁移至相关任务。

A Multi-Class Bahadur–Lazarsfeld Expansion Framework for Pixel-Level Fusion in Multi-Sensor Land Cover Classification

用于多传感器土地覆盖分类像素级融合的多类Bahadur–Lazarsfeld扩展框架

2026-01-26
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Spiros Papadopoulos，Georgia Koukiou，Vassilis Anastassopoulos

DOI: 10.3390/rs18030399

In many land cover classification tasks, the limited precision of individual sensors hinders the accurate separation of certain classes, largely due to the complexity of the Earth’s surface morphology. To mitigate these issues, decision fusion methodologies are employed, allowing data from multiple sensors to be synthesized into robust and more conclusive classification outcomes. This study employs fully polarimetric Synthetic Aperture Radar (PolSAR) imagery and leverages the strengths of three decomposition methods, namely Pauli’s, Krogager’s, and Cloude’s, by extracting their respective components for improved detection. From each decomposition method, three scattering components are derived, enabling the extraction of informative features that describe the scattering behavior associated with various land cover types. The extracted scattering features, treated as independent sensors, were used to train three neural network classifiers. The resulting outputs were then considered as local decisions for each land cover type and subsequently fused through a decision fusion rule to generate more complete and accurate classification results. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed Multi-Class Bahadur–Lazarsfeld Expansion (MC-BLE) fusion significantly enhances classification performance, achieving an overall accuracy (OA) of 95.78% and a Kappa coefficient of 0.94. Compared to individual classification methods, the fusion notably improved per-class accuracy, particularly for complex land cover boundaries. The core innovation of this work is the transformation of the Bahadur–Lazarsfeld Expansion (BLE), originally designed for binary decision fusion into a multi-class framework capable of addressing multiple land cover types, resulting in a more effective and reliable decision fusion strategy.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何融合多源极化SAR特征以提升复杂地表覆盖分类精度

研究方法：用Pauli、Krogager、Cloude分解提取散射分量，训练三神经网络，再以MC-BLE决策融合

主要发现：MC-BLE融合总体精度95.78%，Kappa 0.94，显著改善复杂边界类精度

创新点：首次将Bahadur–Lazarsfeld Expansion由二元推广到多类决策融合

相关性：为遥感多传感器特征融合提供高鲁棒、可扩展的像素级决策框架

研究背景：单一遥感传感器在复杂地表形态下常因信息量不足而难以精确区分所有土地覆盖类别，限制了分类精度。为突破这一瓶颈，研究界普遍采用多源数据决策融合策略，以综合不同传感器的互补信息并提升分类可靠性。

方法详情：论文利用全极化SAR影像，分别通过Pauli、Krogager与Cloude三种极化分解提取各自的三维散射分量，共九维特征被视为九个“独立传感器”。每个特征子集训练一个神经网络基分类器，得到像素级软决策；随后将多分类器输出建模为多元离散变量，并把原本仅用于二分类的Bahadur–Lazarsfeld展开式推广为Multi-Class BLE，以估计高阶相关并在决策层进行融合，最终输出最可信的土地覆盖标签。

研究结果：在实验数据集上，MC-BLE融合将总体精度提升至95.78%，Kappa达0.94，比任一单分类器提高约6–10个百分点，尤其对混合边界与稀有类别的用户/制图精度改善显著。结果同时表明，BLE高阶项的引入有效抑制了基分类器间的冗余相关，使融合置信度更高且误差分布更均衡。

局限性：研究仅在单一全极化SAR场景上验证，缺乏与光学、激光雷达等多源异构数据的联合测试；BLE阶数与计算复杂度随类别数指数增长，可能限制其在大类别、大范围影像上的实时应用；基分类器皆采用同构神经网络，未探讨与弱分类器或深度模型集成的泛化表现。

未来方向：未来可将MC-BLE框架扩展至多源异构遥感数据融合，并结合近似推理或并行计算降低高阶项复杂度；同时探索自适应阶数选择与基分类器多样性度量，以进一步提升可扩展性与鲁棒性。

研究相关性：对于从事多源遥感分类、极化SAR应用或统计决策融合的研究者，该文提供了将经典Bahadur–Lazarsfeld理论推广到多类的完整推导与实现范式，可作为像素级融合新方法的重要参考。

Revisiting Aerial Scene Classification on the AID Benchmark

重新审视AID基准上的航空场景分类

2026-01-26
arXiv

Subhajeet Das，Susmita Ghosh，Abhiroop Chatterjee

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18263v1

Aerial images play a vital role in urban planning and environmental preservation, as they consist of various structures, representing different types of buildings, forests, mountains, and unoccupied lands. Due to its heterogeneous nature, developing robust models for scene classification remains a challenge. In this study, we conduct a literature review of various machine learning methods for aerial image classification. Our survey covers a range of approaches from handcrafted features (e.g., SIFT, LBP) to traditional CNNs (e.g., VGG, GoogLeNet), and advanced deep hybrid networks. In this connection, we have also designed Aerial-Y-Net, a spatial attention-enhanced CNN with multi-scale feature fusion mechanism, which acts as an attention-based model and helps us to better understand the complexities of aerial images. Evaluated on the AID dataset, our model achieves 91.72% accuracy, outperforming several baseline architectures.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何提升异质航拍图像场景分类的鲁棒性与精度

研究方法：综述手工特征至深度混合网络，提出空间注意力增强的多尺度CNN Aerial-Y-Net

主要发现：Aerial-Y-Net在AID数据集达91.72%准确率，优于多种基线模型

创新点：引入空间注意力与多尺度特征融合的Aerial-Y-Net架构

相关性：为城市规划与环境监测研究者提供航拍分类最新方法与高性能模型

However, current approaches predominantly rely on unimodal RGB pre-training, overlooking the complex 3D geometry inherent to surgical environments. Although several architectures support multimodal or geometry-aware inputs in general computer vision, the benefits of incorporating depth information in surgical settings remain underexplored. We conduct a large-scale empirical study comparing eight ViT-based VFMs that differ in pre-training domain, learning objective, and input modality (RGB vs. RGB-D). ScenePilot-Bench: A Large-Scale Dataset and Benchmark for Evaluation of Vision-Language Models in Autonomous Driving

2026-01-27
arXiv

Yujin Wang，Yutong Zheng，Wenxian Fan，Tianyi Wang，Hongqing Chu 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19582v1

In this paper, we introduce ScenePilot-Bench, a large-scale first-person driving benchmark designed to evaluate vision-language models (VLMs) in autonomous driving scenarios. ScenePilot-Bench is built upon ScenePilot-4K, a diverse dataset comprising 3,847 hours of driving videos, annotated with multi-granularity information including scene descriptions, risk assessments, key participant identification, ego trajectories, and camera parameters. The benchmark features a four-axis evaluation suite that assesses VLM capabilities in scene understanding, spatial perception, motion planning, and GPT-Score, with safety-aware metrics and cross-region generalization settings. We benchmark representative VLMs on ScenePilot-Bench, providing empirical analyses that clarify current performance boundaries and identify gaps for driving-oriented reasoning. ScenePilot-Bench offers a comprehensive framework for evaluating and advancing VLMs in safety-critical autonomous driving contexts.
