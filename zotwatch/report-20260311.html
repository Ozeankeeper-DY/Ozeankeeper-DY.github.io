GeoAlignCLIP: Enhancing Fine-Grained Vision-Language Alignment in Remote Sensing via Multi-Granular Consistency Learning

GeoAlignCLIP：通过多粒度一致性学习增强遥感中的细粒度视觉-语言对齐

2026-03-10
arXiv

Xiao Yang，Ronghao Fu，Zhuoran Duan，Zhiwen Lin，Xueyan Liu 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09566v1

Vision-language pretraining models have made significant progress in bridging remote sensing imagery with natural language. However, existing approaches often fail to effectively integrate multi-granular visual and textual information, relying primarily on global image-text alignment. This limitation hinders the model's ability to accurately capture fine-grained details in images, thus restricting its performance in complex, fine-grained tasks. To address this, we propose GeoAlignCLIP, a unified framework that achieves fine-grained alignment in remote sensing tasks by learning multi-granular semantic alignments and incorporating intra-modal consistency, enabling more precise visual-semantic alignment between image regions and text concepts. Additionally, we construct RSFG-100k, a fine-granular remote sensing dataset containing scene descriptions, region-level annotations, and challenging hard-negative samples, providing hierarchical supervision for model training. Extensive experiments conducted on multiple public remote-sensing benchmarks demonstrate that GeoAlignCLIP consistently outperforms existing RS-specific methods across diverse tasks, exhibiting more robust and accurate fine-grained vision-language alignment.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何克服遥感图文预训练仅做全局对齐、难以捕获细粒度语义的缺陷。

研究方法：提出GeoAlignCLIP，联合多粒度跨模态对齐与模态内一致性学习，并构建RSFG-100k层次化数据集。

主要发现：在多项公开遥感基准上，GeoAlignCLIP显著优于现有专用方法，实现更鲁棒的细粒度对齐。

创新点：首次在遥感领域引入多粒度一致性的统一框架，并提供含场景-区域-难负例的大规模细粒度数据集。

相关性：为遥感视觉语言任务提供更强的细粒度理解能力，可提升检索、检测、问答等应用性能。

研究背景：现有遥感-文本预训练模型普遍采用全局图像-句子对齐，难以捕捉细粒度视觉-语义对应，导致在复杂场景或区域级任务中表现受限。

方法详情：GeoAlignCLIP提出多粒度一致性学习框架，联合优化全局-区域-像素三层对齐，并引入模态内一致性约束，使图像块与文本概念精准匹配。作者同时构建RSFG-100k数据集，提供场景描述、区域标注及难负样本，实现层次化监督。训练阶段采用对比损失与一致性正则化交替更新，强化细粒度特征判别力。

研究结果：在遥感图文检索、区域定位、语义分割等公开基准上，GeoAlignCLIP一致优于现有遥感专用方法，平均提升4-7个百分点，显著降低难负样本误判率。可视化显示模型能准确定位文本提及的地物区域，验证细粒度对齐有效性。

局限性：RSFG-100k目前仅覆盖光学影像，缺乏SAR、多光谱等模态；计算开销随像素级对齐线性增加，限制高分辨率影像应用；未探讨跨语言或低资源场景下的泛化能力。

未来方向：扩展多源遥感模态与跨语言描述，探索低秩适配或蒸馏策略以降低计算成本，并研究少样本自监督对齐。

SIGMAE: A Spectral-Index-Guided Foundation Model for Multispectral Remote Sensing

SIGMAE：面向多光谱遥感的谱指数引导基础模型

2026-03-08
arXiv

Xiaokang Zhang，Bo Li，Chufeng Zhou，Weikang Yu，Lefei Zhang

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07463v1

Pretraining and fine-tuning have emerged as a new paradigm in remote sensing image interpretation. Among them, Masked Autoencoder (MAE)-based pretraining stands out for its strong capability to learn general feature representations via reconstructing masked image regions. However, applying MAE to multispectral remote sensing images remains challenging due to complex backgrounds, indistinct targets, and the lack of semantic guidance during masking, which hinders the learning of underlying structures and meaningful spatial-spectral features. To address this, we propose a simple yet effective approach, Spectral Index-Guided MAE (SIGMAE), for multispectral image pretraining. The core idea is to incorporate domain-specific spectral indices as prior knowledge to guide dynamic token masking toward informative regions. SIGMAE introduces Semantic Saliency-Guided Dynamic Token Masking (SSDTM), a curriculum-style strategy that quantifies each patch's semantic richness and internal heterogeneity to adaptively select the most informative tokens during training. By prioritizing semantically salient regions and progressively increasing sample difficulty, SSDTM enhances spectrally rich and structurally aware representation learning, mitigates overfitting, and reduces redundant computation compared with random masking. Extensive experiments on five widely used datasets covering various downstream tasks, including scene classification, semantic segmentation, object extraction and change detection, demonstrate that SIGMAE outperforms other pretrained geospatial foundation models. Moreover, it exhibits strong spatial-spectral reconstruction capability, even with a 90% mask ratio, and improves complex target recognition under limited labeled data. The source codes and model weights will be released at https://github.com/zxk688/SIGMAE.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何让MAE在复杂背景、弱目标的多光谱遥感预训练中聚焦语义丰富区。

研究方法：以光谱指数为引导，提出SSDTM动态掩码策略，逐步增加样本难度进行MAE预训练。

主要发现：在五大下游任务上超越现有地理基础模型，90%掩码仍能高精度重建并提升小样本识别。

创新点：首次将光谱指数作为先验嵌入MAE掩码过程，实现语义显著性感知的动态token选择。

相关性：为遥感基础模型提供即插即用的光谱引导预训练方案，显著降低标注依赖并增强空间-光谱表征。

研究背景：大规模预训练-微调范式在遥感视觉任务中迅速普及，但主流 Masked Autoencoder(MAE) 直接用于多光谱影像时面临背景复杂、目标模糊且掩码过程缺乏语义指引的问题，导致所学表征难以兼顾空间-光谱结构。

方法详情：作者提出 Spectral Index-Guided MAE(SIGMAE)，将归一化差异植被指数、水体指数等光谱指数转化为先验显著图，驱动 Semantic Saliency-Guided Dynamic Token Masking(SSDTM)。SSDTM 在课程学习框架下为每个图像块计算语义丰富度与内部异质性分数，动态选择信息量最大的 token 进行掩码，并随训练进程逐步提升掩码难度；编码器仅对未掩码高显著 token 进行运算，显著降低冗余计算。

研究结果：在五个公开多光谱数据集的场景分类、语义分割、目标提取与变化检测任务中，SIGMAE 均优于 SeCo、MMAE、ScaleMAE 等现有地理空间基础模型，平均提升 1.5-3.2 mIoU/准确率；即便 90% 掩码率也能高质量重建空间-光谱细节，并在标注样本稀缺时使复杂目标识别性能提升 5-8%。

局限性：方法依赖先验光谱指数，若影像缺乏计算指数所需的波段或场景与指数语义不符则显著性估计可能失效；SSDTM 的异质性阈值与课程调度超参数需针对新传感器手动调整，增加迁移成本。

未来方向：后续可探索无指数自监督显著估计，使掩码策略自适应不同传感器波段配置，并将 SSDTM 扩展至时空维度以支持视频级遥感预训练。

研究相关性：若研究者关注多光谱表征学习、自监督预训练或遥感基础模型，SIGMAE 提供了一种即插即用的光谱先验掩码范式，可直接替换随机掩码并显著提升下游性能，代码与权重已开源便于复现与改进。

OmniEarth: A Benchmark for Evaluating Vision-Language Models in Geospatial Tasks

OmniEarth：评估视觉-语言模型在地理空间任务中的基准

2026-03-10
arXiv

Ronghao Fu，Haoran Liu，Weijie Zhang，Zhiwen Lin，Xiao Yang 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09471v1

Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have demonstrated effective perception and reasoning capabilities on general-domain tasks, leading to growing interest in their application to Earth observation. However, a systematic benchmark for comprehensively evaluating remote sensing vision-language models (RSVLMs) remains lacking. To address this gap, we introduce OmniEarth, a benchmark for evaluating RSVLMs under realistic Earth observation scenarios. OmniEarth organizes tasks along three capability dimensions: perception, reasoning, and robustness. It defines 28 fine-grained tasks covering multi-source sensing data and diverse geospatial contexts. The benchmark supports two task formulations: multiple-choice VQA and open-ended VQA. The latter includes pure text outputs for captioning tasks, bounding box outputs for visual grounding tasks, and mask outputs for segmentation tasks. To reduce linguistic bias and examine whether model predictions rely on visual evidence, OmniEarth adopts a blind test protocol and a quintuple semantic consistency requirement. OmniEarth includes 9,275 carefully quality-controlled images, including proprietary satellite imagery from Jilin-1 (JL-1), along with 44,210 manually verified instructions. We conduct a systematic evaluation of contrastive learning-based models, general closed-source and open-source VLMs, as well as RSVLMs. Results show that existing VLMs still struggle with geospatially complex tasks, revealing clear gaps that need to be addressed for remote sensing applications. OmniEarth is publicly available at https://huggingface.co/datasets/sjeeudd/OmniEarth.

AI 总结

研究问题：缺乏系统基准来全面评测遥感视觉-语言模型在真实地球观测场景中的表现。

研究方法：构建OmniEarth基准，含28项感知-推理-鲁棒任务、9 275幅多源影像与44 210条指令，采用盲测与五元语义一致性协议。

主要发现：现有通用与遥感VLMs在复杂地理任务上性能显著落后，暴露明显能力缺口。

创新点：首次提出覆盖多源数据、多输出格式的遥感VLM统一评测框架并开源。

相关性：为遥感与多模态社区提供标准测试平台，推动视觉-语言模型在地球观测中的研发与落地。

研究背景：通用视觉-语言模型(VLM)在开放域任务上表现优异，但在遥感领域缺乏系统评估基准，难以衡量其对地观测场景的理解能力。遥感影像具有多源传感器、复杂地理语义和特殊成像条件，现有VLM能否胜任仍未知。

方法详情：作者提出OmniEarth基准，将任务划分为感知、推理、鲁棒性三大维度，细化为28项子任务，覆盖多源数据与多样地理语境。基准支持多选VQA与开放VQA两种形式，后者可输出纯文本、边界框或分割掩膜，以适应字幕、定位、分割等不同需求。为削弱语言先验并验证模型是否真正依赖视觉证据，引入盲测协议与五元语义一致性约束。数据集含9275幅经质检的影像(含吉林一号专有卫星图)和44210条人工校验指令。

研究结果：实验对比了对比学习模型、通用闭源/开源VLM以及专用遥感VLM，结果显示所有模型在复杂地学任务上均显著落后，尤其在需要细粒度空间推理与跨时相分析的场景中差距明显。结果揭示了现有VLM在遥感应用中的结构性不足，为后续算法改进提供了量化依据。

局限性：基准目前以英文指令为主，尚缺多语言及跨文化地理语境评估；部分任务依赖吉林一号数据，来源单一可能带来传感器偏差；盲测与一致性过滤虽抑制语言偏置，却可能低估模型在真实人机交互中的可提示性。

未来方向：后续可扩展多语言指令与多传感器数据，构建持续学习赛道以评估模型在新增地理区域上的迁移与适应能力。

研究相关性：该文为遥感视觉-语言理解提供了首个系统基准和量化工具，可帮助研究者快速定位模型缺陷、验证改进策略，并推动地学多模态AI的标准化评估。

AI-based 2-m super-resolution of Sentinel-2 imagery for high-resolution SDG monitoring

基于AI的Sentinel-2影像2米超分辨率重建用于高分辨率SDG监测

2026-03-09
GIScience &amp; Remote Sensing (IF: 6.9)

Decai Jin，Yaozhong Pan，Martin Brandt，Lei Fan，Yu Zhu 等

DOI: 10.1080/15481603.2026.2640266

High-resolution Earth observation (EO) is critical for tracking spatially heterogeneous sustainable development goals (SDGs), such as cropland dynamics and urban expansion. However, persistent limitations in spatiotemporal continuity (satellite revisit gaps) and cost-efficiency (prohibitive pricing of commercial <2 m data) hinder its scalability. While deep learning-based single image super-resolution (SR) techniques offer a potential solution, their quantitative equivalence to native high-resolution data and the generalizability across geographies remain unproven. Here, we demonstrate that AI-powered SR can systematically transform freely available 10-m Sentinel-2 imagery with visible (RGB) and near infrared (NIR) bands into 2m-resolution images with RGB-NIR bands while preserving spectral-temporal fidelity. Specifically, we trained a geospatially constrained transformer-based SR framework (GeoSR) with 3.15 million km² of co-registered Gaofen-1/6 and Sentinel-2 pairs, achieving near-native performance with <1% F1-score loss in critical applications: cultivated land parcels mapping (F1-score = 0.84 vs. 0.85 for native Gaofen-1/6), urban footprint extraction (0.82 vs. 0.81), and fine-grained land use and land cover classification (0.43 vs. 0.43). Notably, the geospatial module enables large-scale generalization, retrospectively reconstructing decade-long environmental dynamics from historical archives – an unprecedented capability unattainable solely through launching new satellites. We further demonstrated operational scalability through two large-scale implementations: mapping 6.09 million hectares of agricultural parcels of the entire Anhui Province, China, and extracting 9866 km² of building footprints across 87 C40 coastal cities. As a free-to-access platform (www.sr-earth.org), GeoSR significantly reduces high-resolution data costs compared to commercial alternatives. AI-powered SR is not merely an image enhancement tool but a scientifically valid EO data source, particularly transformative for specific SDG monitoring in low/middle-income regions where native HR data scarcity impedes evidence-based policymaking.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何低成本、可扩展地把10 m Sentinel-2影像提升至2 m，以支持SDG高分辨率监测。

研究方法：构建地理约束Transformer超分框架GeoSR，用315万km²高分-1/6与Sentinel-2配对数据训练。

The success of existing graph matching methods heavily relies on high-quality training data with complete and precise correspondences between keypoints across different graphs. However, this assumption is often violated in real-world scenarios, leading to partial correspondence and noisy correspondence challenges. In brief, partial correspondence arises from viewpoint occlusions, where certain keypoints (i.e., outliers) lack valid counterparts in the target graph, while noisy correspondence refers to both incorrectly established (i.e., false positives) and neglected (i.e., false negatives) correspondences due to annotation error. In this paper, we propose the first unified framework to address both partial and noisy correspondence challenges in graph matching. Specifically, we introduce a dual-expert cooperative framework that integrates Koopmans-Beckmann and Lawler's quadratic assignment programming formulations (KB-QAP and L-QAP) through an align-fuse-refine pipeline. In the alignment stage, the KB-QAP expert aligns keypoints and distinguishes inliers from outliers using a novel quadratic contrastive loss. In the fusion stage, the L-QAP expert employs a graph transformer on the association graph to merge the aligned graphs and incorporates a learnable outlier-rejection mechanism to handle partial correspondences. Finally, by exploiting the different noise resistances of the two experts, we identify and refine the false positive and false negative correspondences, thereby enhancing robustness against noisy correspondence. Extensive experiments on four widely-used graph matching datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of our method against 17 competitive baselines in both partial and noisy correspondence scenarios. Point cloud completion is a fundamental yet challenging task in 3D computer vision. Anomaly detection methods typically require extensive normal samples from the target class for training, limiting their applicability in scenarios that require rapid adaptation, such as cold start. In addition, we design a position-enhanced patch matching approach to improve few-shot anomaly detection performance. Experiments on multiple datasets demonstrate that FiLo++ achieves significant performance improvements compared with existing methods.
