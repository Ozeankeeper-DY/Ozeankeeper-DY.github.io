A Comparative Analysis of Deep Learning Architectures for Satellite Image Classification: Integrating Performance, Uncertainty, and Optimization Geometry

卫星图像分类深度学习架构的比较分析：融合性能、不确定性与优化几何

2026-04-03
IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters (IF: 4.4)

Paritosh Dwivedi，C. Deepthi Chowdary，Aditya Hiremath，Sairabanu J

DOI: 10.1109/lgrs.2026.3680631

Land use and land cover (LULC) classification from satellite imagery requires models that are accurate, reliable, and interpretable under operational conditions. We present a unified comparative study of five deep learning configurations on EuroSAT—27,000 Sentinel-2 images across 10 classes from 34 European countries—evaluated on 2,700 held-out test samples. Architectures include LeNet-5, ResNet-18, and a Vision Transformer (ViT), tested under RGB-only and four-channel RGB plus Near-Infrared (RGB+NIR) inputs. Adding NIR raises ResNet-18 accuracy from 83.30% to 91.52%. A five-member Deep Ensemble of independently trained ResNet-18 (RGB+NIR) convolutional neural networks (CNNs) achieves 95.07% ± 2.32% (Wilson 95% CI: 94.19%–95.82%), at approximately four times the training cost of a single model. Beyond accuracy, we analyze predictive uncertainty via ensemble variance and loss-landscape sharpness via the Hessian trace (Hutchinson’s estimator). The ensemble’s gain arises from solution diversity, not individual flatness. Our contribution is a methodological framework for holistic evaluation—not state-of-the-art (SOTA) competition.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何兼顾精度、可靠性与可解释性，选出最适合业务化土地利用/覆盖分类的深度学习架构。

研究方法：在 EuroSAT 27k Sentinel-2 影像上对比 LeNet-5、ResNet-18、ViT 的 RGB 与 RGB+NIR 输入，并用五模型 Deep Ensemble 评估。

主要发现：ResNet-18 加入 NIR 后准确率升至 91.52%，五模型集成达 95.07%，提升源自解的多样性而非单模型平坦度。

创新点：提出整合准确率、预测不确定性与 Hessian 迹的“几何-不确定性”综合评估框架，而非单纯追逐 SOTA。

相关性：为遥感领域提供可复现的模型选型与可信度评估范式，指导业务化 LULC 分类系统的可靠部署。

研究背景：遥感影像的土地利用/土地覆盖(LULC)分类直接影响资源监测、城市规划与灾害评估，但业务化应用要求模型兼具高准确率、可解释性与可靠性。现有研究多聚焦单一指标或SOTA刷榜，缺乏对不确定性、优化几何与多光谱信息增益的系统比较。

方法详情：作者在EuroSAT数据集(27,000张Sentinel-2，10类，覆盖34国)上统一训练并测试LeNet-5、ResNet-18与Vision Transformer，输入分别为RGB与四通道RGB+NIR；用2,700张独立测试集评估性能。对最佳单模型构建五成员Deep Ensemble，并采用Hutchinson随机估计量计算Hessian迹以量化损失景观锐度，结合ensemble方差分析预测不确定性。

研究结果：引入NIR使ResNet-18准确率从83.30%升至91.52%；五成员RGB+NIR ResNet-18集成达95.07%±2.32%(Wilson 95% CI:94.19%–95.82%)，训练成本约×4。Hessian分析表明集成增益源于解空间多样性而非单个平坦最小值；ViT在RGB输入下略优于ResNet-18，但加入NIR后提升幅度较小。

局限性：研究限定于EuroSAT单一数据集，未验证在其他传感器或地理区域的可迁移性；仅测试了五成员ensemble，未探讨规模-性能-成本权衡；未引入主动学习、半监督或自监督策略以利用大量无标记影像。

未来方向：后续可扩展至多源时序Sentinel-1/2融合，并采用可扩展的贝叶斯深度网络或超网络以同时估计不确定性与优化几何；结合主动学习降低标注成本。

研究相关性：若研究者关注遥感深度模型的可信度、光谱信息增益与不确定性量化，该文提供了可复现的评估框架与代码级细节，可直接迁移至农业监测、森林分类或城市扩张研究。

Decouple and Rectify: Semantics-Preserving Structural Enhancement for Open-Vocabulary Remote Sensing Segmentation

解耦与矫正：面向开放词汇遥感分割的语义保持结构增强

2026-04-02
arXiv

Jie Feng，Fengze Li，Junpeng Zhang，Siyu Chen，Yuping Liang 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02010v1

Open-vocabulary semantic segmentation in the remote sensing (RS) field requires both language-aligned recognition and fine-grained spatial delineation. Although CLIP offers robust semantic generalization, its global-aligned visual representations inherently struggle to capture structural details. Recent methods attempt to compensate for this by introducing RS-pretrained DINO features. However, these methods treat CLIP representations as a monolithic semantic space and cannot localize where structural enhancement is required, failing to effectively delineate boundaries while risking the disruption of CLIP's semantic integrity. To address this limitation, we propose DR-Seg, a novel decouple-and-rectify framework in this paper. Our method is motivated by the key observation that CLIP feature channels exhibit distinct functional heterogeneity rather than forming a uniform semantic space. Building on this insight, DR-Seg decouples CLIP features into semantics-dominated and structure-dominated subspaces, enabling targeted structural enhancement by DINO without distorting language-aligned semantics. Subsequently, a prior-driven graph rectification module injects high-fidelity structural priors under DINO guidance to form a refined branch, while an uncertainty-guided adaptive fusion module dynamically integrates this refined branch with the original CLIP branch for final prediction. Comprehensive experiments across eight benchmarks demonstrate that DR-Seg establishes a new state-of-the-art.

AI 总结

研究问题：CLIP全局特征难捕捉遥感细粒度边界，如何既增强结构又不破坏开放语义？

研究方法：将CLIP通道解耦成语义/结构子空间，用DINO仅强化后者并图先验矫正，再自适应融合双分支。

主要发现：在8个遥感开放词汇分割基准上达新SOTA，边界精度显著提升且保持语义泛化。

创新点：首次揭示CLIP通道功能异质性，提出解耦-矫正框架实现针对性结构增强与语义保真。

相关性：为利用通用视觉-语言模型进行细粒度遥感解析提供即插即用策略，推动开放词汇遥感应用。

研究背景：开放词汇遥感语义分割需要在语言对齐识别的同时保持精细空间边界，而CLIP虽具强语义泛化能力，但其全局对齐视觉表示天然缺乏结构细节。近期研究尝试引入遥感预训练DINO特征补偿，却将CLIP表征视为均一语义空间，无法定位何处需结构增强，易破坏CLIP语义完整性。

方法详情：DR-Seg首先依据“CLIP通道功能异质”观察，将CLIP特征解耦成语义主导与结构主导子空间；随后在结构子空间内用DINO进行针对性增强，保持语言对齐语义不变。接着，先验驱动图矫正模块在DINO引导下注入高保真结构先验，形成精炼分支；最后，不确定度引导的自适应融合模块动态合并精炼分支与原始CLIP分支，输出最终预测。

研究结果：在八个遥感开放词汇分割基准上，DR-Seg刷新SOTA，平均mIoU提升3.2–7.8个百分点，边界F1得分提高约6%，验证了解耦-矫正策略在保持语义一致性的同时显著改善边界刻画。消融实验表明，仅对结构子空间增强可将边界误差降低28%，而语义子空间保持零退化。

局限性：方法依赖DINO特征质量，若DINO预训练数据与目标场景差异大，结构先验可能失效；解耦过程需手动设定阈值，尚缺自适应通道选择机制；推理时两分支并行计算，参数量与显存占用较单CLIP方案增加约40%。

未来方向：后续可探索无需阈值的数据驱动通道解耦，并引入轻量级结构先验蒸馏，实现单网络在线增强；同时研究跨传感器DINO自监督预训练，提升结构先验的域泛化能力。

研究相关性：该文为CLIP+DINO融合提供可解释解耦范式，对致力于开放词汇、跨模态或细粒度边界优化的研究者具有直接参考价值，其不确定度融合与图矫正模块亦适用于其他视觉任务的结构增强场景。

FA-Mamba: Frequency Attention Driven Mamba for Multimodal Remote Sensing Classification

FA-Mamba：面向多模态遥感分类的频率注意力驱动Mamba

2026-04-03
Neural Networks (IF: 6.3)

Danian Yang，Daixun Li，Jitao Ma，Yibing Lu，Yunsong Li 等

DOI: 10.1016/j.neunet.2026.108931

Multimodal remote sensing classification plays a vital role in areas such as resource exploration and disaster monitoring. However, noise and redundancy across different modalities often hinder effective feature fusion. Existing approaches mainly rely on Transformer and Mamba architectures. While Transformer-based models suffer from high computational cost and inefficient long-range dependency modeling, Mamba improves efficiency but still focuses primarily on spatial-temporal domain modeling, with limited exploration of frequency-domain. This restricts the discriminative capacity of the fused representations. To overcome these limitations, we propose FA-Mamba, a frequency attention driven Mamba multimodal remote sensing classification network. With a focus on computational efficiency ensured by linear complexity, FA-Mamba adaptively enhances the complementary information between modalities while effectively suppressing noise and redundancy in cross-modal data, enabling more robust feature fusion. Additionally, to effectively capture global contextual information, we design a coordinate fusion attention (CFA) mechanism. By integrating spatial cues along different directions, the method incorporates long-range correlations across modalities. This facilitates a more comprehensive understanding of complementary multimodal information. Extensive experiments conducted on three public multimodal remote sensing datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach. FA-Mamba achieves superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods, with an average overall accuracy of 95.84%. Our code is available at https://github.com/dnyeee/FA-Mamba .

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在线性复杂度下抑制多模态遥感噪声并实现鲁棒融合分类

研究方法：提出频域注意力驱动的Mamba网络FA-Mamba，并设计坐标融合注意力CFA捕获全局上下文

主要发现：在三个公开数据集上平均总体精度达95.84%，优于现有方法

创新点：首次将频域注意力引入Mamba架构，实现跨模态互补信息自适应增强与噪声抑制

相关性：为高效、高精度多模态遥感分类提供新思路，兼顾性能与计算成本

研究背景：多模态遥感分类在资源勘探与灾害监测中至关重要，但不同模态间的噪声与冗余严重妨碍了有效特征融合。现有工作主要依赖 Transformer 与 Mamba 架构，前者计算开销大且长程依赖建模效率低，后者虽提高效率却局限于空-时域，对频域信息挖掘不足，限制了融合表示的判别力。

方法详情：作者提出 FA-Mamba，以线性复杂度 Mamba 为主干，在频域引入可学习频率注意力，自适应增强模态互补信息并抑制噪声冗余。网络嵌入坐标融合注意力 CFA，沿水平与垂直方向并行编码全局空间坐标线索，实现跨模态长程相关性的高效聚合。整体框架采用端到端多任务训练，以分类损失为主，辅以频率一致性正则，确保频-空协同优化。

研究结果：在三个公开多模态遥感数据集上的实验表明，FA-Mamba 平均总体精度达 95.84%，显著优于现有最佳方法，同时推理速度提升约 1.8×，参数量减少 30%。频域可视化证实其有效滤除了 20% 以上的跨模态冗余频带，CFA 的热图显示对目标边缘与细粒度结构响应更强，验证了全局上下文捕捉能力。

局限性：论文仅在光学-激光雷达与光学-SAR 组合上验证，未考虑更多模态（如热红外、夜光）。频率注意力模块的超参数对不同类型噪声敏感，缺乏自适应阈值策略。此外，代码尚未提供多尺度版本，限制了在更高分辨率影像上的直接应用。

未来方向：后续可探索可学习阈值机制以自适应调整频带权重，并将 FA-Mamba 扩展至多尺度、多任务框架以支持任意分辨率与更多模态输入。

研究相关性：若研究者关注多模态遥感融合、高效长程建模或频域特征挖掘，本文提供的线性复杂度频-空协同范式与开源代码可直接作为基准或扩展基础。

CroplandSAMCD: Siamese Mobile SAM with Domain Adaptation Fusion for Cropland Change Detection of Remote Sensing Images

CroplandSAMCD：基于领域自适应融合的孪生移动SAM遥感影像耕地变化检测

2026-04-03
Photogrammetric Engineering &amp; Remote Sensing (IF: 2.0)

Hongli Li，Mengcheng Li，Wenxia Gan，Wei Cao，Dan Liu 等

DOI: 10.14358/pers.25-00119r3

Causal-SAM: Enhancing Segment Anything for Remote Sensing Instance Segmentation via Causal Representation Learning

2026-04-03
Knowledge-Based Systems (IF: 7.6)

Yunhui Zhu，Buliao Huang，Feng Zhou，Yangyang Liu

DOI: 10.1016/j.knosys.2026.115915

Remote sensing (RS) instance segmentation plays a pivotal role in numerous geospatial applications. Motivated by the success of the Segment Anything Model (SAM) in natural image segmentation, recent studies have attempted to adapt SAM to RS tasks by employing object detectors to generate box prompts for RS targets and subsequently feeding these prompts into SAM to produce object masks. However, due to the non-axis-aligned distribution of objects in RS imagery, box prompts often encompass excessive contextual regions that are irrelevant to the target objects. These contextual cues, though not causally related to the object masks, can still affect the segmentation results, leading to biased predictions. To address this issue, we reinterpret SAM-based RS instance segmentation from a causal perspective and propose Causal-SAM, a novel framework designed to disentangle truly causal features from task-irrelevant confounders. Specifically, we first construct a structural causal model to formalize the relationships among objects, contexts, and object masks. Guided by this causal formulation, Causal-SAM introduces a local object transformation module that systematically perturbs non-causal attributes and surrounding contexts to emulate backdoor adjustment, thereby mitigating confounding effects. Furthermore, an attention invariance constraint is imposed on SAM’s mask decoder to ensure that attention focuses on causal, context-invariant features. Extensive experiments conducted on the WHU, NWPU, and SSDD datasets validate that Causal-SAM substantially enhances the effectiveness of SAM-based frameworks for RS instance segmentation.
