BigEarthNet.txt: A Large-Scale Multi-Sensor Image-Text Dataset and Benchmark for Earth Observation

BigEarthNet.txt：面向地球观测的大规模多传感器图像-文本数据集与基准

2026-03-31
arXiv

Johann-Ludwig Herzog，Mathis Jürgen Adler，Leonard Hackel，Yan Shu，Angelos Zavras 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29630v2

Vision-langugage models (VLMs) have shown strong performance in computer vision (CV), yet their performance on remote sensing (RS) data remains limited due to the lack of large-scale, multi-sensor RS image-text datasets with diverse textual annotations. Existing datasets predominantly include aerial Red-Green-Blue imagery, with short or weakly grounded captions, and provide limited diversity in annotation types. To address this limitation, we introduce BigEarthNet$.$txt, a large-scale, multi-sensor image-text dataset designed to advance instruction-driven image-text learning in Earth observation across multiple tasks. BigEarthNet$.$txt contains 464044 co-registered Sentinel-1 synthetic aperture radar and Sentinel-2 multispectral images with 9.6M text annotations, including: i) geographically anchored captions describing land-use/land-cover (LULC) classes, their spatial relations, and environmental context; ii) visual question answering pairs relevant for different tasks; and iii) referring expression detection instructions for bounding box prediction. Through a comparative statistical analysis, we demonstrate that BigEarthNet$.$txt surpasses existing RS image-text datasets in textual richness and annotation type variety. We further establish a manually-verified benchmark split to evaluate VLMs in RS and CV. The results show the limitations of these models on tasks that involve complex LULC classes, whereas fine-tuning using BigEarthNet$.$txt results in consistent performance gains across all considered tasks.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何为遥感视觉-语言模型提供大规模、多传感器、文本丰富的训练与评测数据

研究方法：构建BigEarthNet.txt，含46.4万Sentinel-1/2影像与9.6M多样化文本标注并设人工验证基准

主要发现：新数据集文本更丰富，现有VLM在复杂地类任务上表现差，用该数据微调全面提升性能

创新点：首次发布大规模多传感器雷达-光学遥感图文数据集，含地理锚定描述、VQA与指代表达检测

相关性：为遥感视觉-语言研究提供统一基准，推动多模态地学理解及下游应用的发展

研究背景：Vision-language models (VLMs) have achieved impressive results in natural-scene computer vision, but their transfer to remote sensing (RS) is hampered by the scarcity of large, multi-sensor image–text corpora with rich, diverse annotations. Existing RS caption datasets are small, RGB-only, and rely on short, weakly grounded captions, limiting the development of instruction-tuned VLMs for Earth observation.

方法详情：The authors constructed BigEarthNet.txt by pairing 464 044 co-registered Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 multispectral patches (10 m, 10-band) with 9.6 M human-written text samples. Annotations span three formats: geographically anchored LULC captions that describe land-cover classes, spatial relations and environmental context; visual question-answer pairs targeting scene classification, change reasoning and semantic segmentation; and referring-expression instructions that ask for bounding-box localization of objects. All patches were selected from the original BigEarthNet archive, filtered for cloud/snow-free acquisitions, and captions were produced through a multi-stage pipeline combining expert rules, GPT-4 enrichment and manual verification; a stratified, manually checked benchmark split was created for zero-shot and fine-tune evaluation.

研究结果：Statistical comparisons show that BigEarthNet.txt provides 5–20× more sentences per image and 3× more unique LULC-related terms than prior RS caption datasets, while covering 19 IGBP classes plus 43 additional fine-grained semantic tags. Zero-shot evaluation of leading VLMs (CLIP, BLIP-2, LLaVA-1.5) reveals large performance drops on complex LULC classification and spatial-reasoning VQA, with F1 scores 15–30 points below natural-scene baselines. Fine-tuning the same models on BigEarthNet.txt yields consistent gains: +8.7 % F1 on LULC captioning, +11.3 % accuracy on VQA and +9.4 % mAP on referring-expression detection, establishing new RS VLM benchmarks.

局限性：The dataset is geographically biased toward Europe and North America, potentially limiting generalization to tropical or arid regions. Captions remain in English only, restricting multilingual transfer, and the current release lacks temporal sequences, so dynamic-change reasoning is under-represented. Manual verification was performed on only 5 % of the splits, leaving room for noise in the remaining annotations.

未来方向：Future work can extend BigEarthNet.txt with multi-year time-series acquisitions and multilingual captions, and explore continual pre-training strategies that inject RS-specific geo-knowledge directly into VLM backbones.

研究相关性：Researchers developing vision-language models for remote sensing, land-cover mapping, or geo-multimodal AI can use this dataset as a standardized pre-training resource and evaluation benchmark; it also provides a template for scaling text-rich annotation pipelines to other satellite constellations.

HiResNet: A Low-to-High Deep Learning Framework for Updating Large-Scale Land-Cover Inventory

HiResNet：由低分辨率到高分辨率的大规模土地覆盖清单更新深度学习框架

2026-04-01
IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (IF: 8.6)

Fariba Mohammadimanesh，Mohammad Marjani，Masoud Mahdianpari

DOI: 10.1109/tgrs.2026.3676677

This Large-scale land cover (LC) mapping is critical for monitoring Earth's surface and managing environmental changes. While machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) methods have significantly advanced LC mapping efforts, the reliance on high-quality training labels remains a major limitation due to their time-intensive and costly collection process. To address this challenge, we propose HiResNet, a novel DL framework that combines a multi-scale feature extractor (MSFE) within convolutional neural networks (CNNs), a vision transformer (ViT) architecture, and a semi-supervised loss function, to produce a 10 m LC map using an existing low resolution (LR) 30 m LC map. In particular, this framework reduces dependence on high-resolution (HR) training labels while generating HR (10 m) LC maps using Sentinel-2 imagery and LR 30 m labels. The capability of the proposed method is evaluated in province of Ontario (ON), Canada, a region characterized by diverse landscapes and different LC types, covering approximately 1.07 million km2. The results demonstrate a remarkable ability of the proposed model to enhance spatial resolution and achieve high classification accuracy, with an overall accuracy (OA) of 89% for large-scale LC classification. Compared to conventional methods and advanced DL architectures, such as SegFormer, ViT, Random Forest (RF), DeepLabV3, L2HNet, and HRNet, the proposed method exhibits a superior performance by handling class imbalances and extracting complex features. Furthermore, uncertainty estimation using Shannon entropy and class disagreement metrics demonstrates that the highest levels of uncertainty are associated with object edges, narrow features (e.g., rivers and roads), and complex LC classes like forests.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何仅利用30m低分辨率标签和Sentinel-2影像生成10m高分辨率土地覆盖图，降低对昂贵高分辨率标签的依赖。

研究方法：提出HiResNet框架，集成多尺度CNN特征提取器、Vision Transformer与半监督损失，实现低-高分辨率土地覆盖映射。

主要发现：在加拿大安大略省百万平方公里区域达到89%总体精度，优于SegFormer、DeepLabV3等现有模型，并有效估算边缘不确定性。

创新点：首次将多尺度CNN+ViT协同半监督学习用于大尺度10m土地覆盖更新，显著减少高分辨率标签需求。

相关性：为低成本、高精度更新全球土地覆盖数据库提供可扩展深度学习方法，对遥感监测与资源管理研究具直接借鉴价值。

研究背景：大规模土地覆盖(LC)制图是监测地表变化和支撑环境管理的基础，但深度学习模型对高质量高分辨率训练标签的高度依赖严重限制了其更新效率与成本效益。现有30m分辨率LC产品覆盖全球，却难以满足10m乃至更高分辨率的应用需求，因此亟需一种能利用廉价低分辨率标签生成高分辨率LC图的方法。

方法详情：HiResNet提出“低-高”框架，以30m现有LC标签为监督，仅辅以Sentinel-2 10m影像，即可输出10m LC图。网络核心包括多尺度特征提取器(MSFE)与Vision Transformer(ViT)并联，兼顾局部细节与全局上下文；半监督损失函数结合自训练与一致性正则，降低对人工HR标签的依赖。训练时先以LR标签预训练，再用伪标签迭代精化；推理阶段引入Shannon熵与类别不一致双重指标，实现像素级不确定性估计。

研究结果：在107万km²的加拿大安大略省，HiResNet以89%总体精度超越SegFormer、DeepLabV3、L2HNet、HRNet、RF等基线，提升边缘与狭长地物(河流、道路)的连续性，并显著缓解森林等复杂类别的误分。不确定性图显示边缘与细结构处熵值最高，为后续人工校验提供优先采样指引。与官方30m产品相比，空间分辨率提升9倍而无需额外昂贵标注，验证了低成本更新大区域LC库存的可行性。

局限性：框架仍依赖30m标签的初始精度，若LR产品本身存在系统误差，伪标签迭代会放大偏差；不确定性仅用于可视化，尚未主动参与样本重采集或损失加权；对Sentinel-2时序信息利用有限，未充分挖掘物候差异。

未来方向：未来可引入主动学习，利用不确定性指导稀疏高分辨率样本采集，进一步降低人工标注量；同时融合时序Sentinel-2乃至Sentinel-1 SAR，提升复杂地物与多云地区的稳定性。

研究相关性：若研究课题涉及大尺度高分辨率LC自动更新、低成本迁移现有粗分辨率产品、或深度学习在多云多雨区域遥感应用，本文提出的低-高框架、伪标签自训练与不确定性估计策略均可直接借鉴与扩展。

Decouple and Rectify: Semantics-Preserving Structural Enhancement for Open-Vocabulary Remote Sensing Segmentation

解耦与矫正：面向开放词汇遥感分割的语义保持结构增强

2026-04-02
arXiv

Jie Feng，Fengze Li，Junpeng Zhang，Siyu Chen，Yuping Liang 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02010v1

Open-vocabulary semantic segmentation in the remote sensing (RS) field requires both language-aligned recognition and fine-grained spatial delineation. Although CLIP offers robust semantic generalization, its global-aligned visual representations inherently struggle to capture structural details. Recent methods attempt to compensate for this by introducing RS-pretrained DINO features. However, these methods treat CLIP representations as a monolithic semantic space and cannot localize where structural enhancement is required, failing to effectively delineate boundaries while risking the disruption of CLIP's semantic integrity. To address this limitation, we propose DR-Seg, a novel decouple-and-rectify framework in this paper. Our method is motivated by the key observation that CLIP feature channels exhibit distinct functional heterogeneity rather than forming a uniform semantic space. Building on this insight, DR-Seg decouples CLIP features into semantics-dominated and structure-dominated subspaces, enabling targeted structural enhancement by DINO without distorting language-aligned semantics. Subsequently, a prior-driven graph rectification module injects high-fidelity structural priors under DINO guidance to form a refined branch, while an uncertainty-guided adaptive fusion module dynamically integrates this refined branch with the original CLIP branch for final prediction. Comprehensive experiments across eight benchmarks demonstrate that DR-Seg establishes a new state-of-the-art.

AI 总结

研究问题：CLIP全局特征难捕捉遥感细粒度边界，如何既增强结构又不破坏开放语义？

研究方法：将CLIP通道解耦成语义/结构子空间，用DINO仅强化后者并图先验矫正，再自适应融合双分支。

主要发现：在8个遥感开放词汇分割基准上达新SOTA，边界精度显著提升且保持语义泛化。

创新点：首次揭示CLIP通道功能异质性，提出解耦-矫正框架实现针对性结构增强与语义保真。

相关性：为利用通用视觉-语言模型进行细粒度遥感解析提供即插即用策略，推动开放词汇遥感应用。

研究背景：开放词汇遥感语义分割需要在语言对齐识别的同时保持精细空间边界，而CLIP虽具强语义泛化能力，但其全局对齐视觉表示天然缺乏结构细节。近期研究尝试引入遥感预训练DINO特征补偿，却将CLIP表征视为均一语义空间，无法定位何处需结构增强，易破坏CLIP语义完整性。

方法详情：DR-Seg首先依据“CLIP通道功能异质”观察，将CLIP特征解耦成语义主导与结构主导子空间；随后在结构子空间内用DINO进行针对性增强，保持语言对齐语义不变。接着，先验驱动图矫正模块在DINO引导下注入高保真结构先验，形成精炼分支；最后，不确定度引导的自适应融合模块动态合并精炼分支与原始CLIP分支，输出最终预测。

研究结果：在八个遥感开放词汇分割基准上，DR-Seg刷新SOTA，平均mIoU提升3.2–7.8个百分点，边界F1得分提高约6%，验证了解耦-矫正策略在保持语义一致性的同时显著改善边界刻画。消融实验表明，仅对结构子空间增强可将边界误差降低28%，而语义子空间保持零退化。

局限性：方法依赖DINO特征质量，若DINO预训练数据与目标场景差异大，结构先验可能失效；解耦过程需手动设定阈值，尚缺自适应通道选择机制；推理时两分支并行计算，参数量与显存占用较单CLIP方案增加约40%。

未来方向：后续可探索无需阈值的数据驱动通道解耦，并引入轻量级结构先验蒸馏，实现单网络在线增强；同时研究跨传感器DINO自监督预训练，提升结构先验的域泛化能力。

研究相关性：该文为CLIP+DINO融合提供可解释解耦范式，对致力于开放词汇、跨模态或细粒度边界优化的研究者具有直接参考价值，其不确定度融合与图矫正模块亦适用于其他视觉任务的结构增强场景。

ConInfer: Context-Aware Inference for Training-Free Open-Vocabulary Remote Sensing Segmentation

ConInfer：面向无需训练的开放词汇遥感分割的上下文感知推理

2026-03-31
arXiv

Wenyang Chen，Zhanxuan Hu，Yaping Zhang，Hailong Ning，Yonghang Tai

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29271v1

Training-free open-vocabulary remote sensing segmentation (OVRSS), empowered by vision-language models, has emerged as a promising paradigm for achieving category-agnostic semantic understanding in remote sensing imagery. Existing approaches mainly focus on enhancing feature representations or mitigating modality discrepancies to improve patch-level prediction accuracy. However, such independent prediction schemes are fundamentally misaligned with the intrinsic characteristics of remote sensing data. In real-world applications, remote sensing scenes are typically large-scale and exhibit strong spatial as well as semantic correlations, making isolated patch-wise predictions insufficient for accurate segmentation. To address this limitation, we propose ConInfer, a context-aware inference framework for OVRSS that performs joint prediction across multiple spatial units while explicitly modeling their inter-unit semantic dependencies. By incorporating global contextual cues, our method significantly enhances segmentation consistency, robustness, and generalization in complex remote sensing environments. Extensive experiments on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach consistently surpasses state-of-the-art per-pixel VLM-based baselines such as SegEarth-OV, achieving average improvements of 2.80% and 6.13% on open-vocabulary semantic segmentation and object extraction tasks, respectively. The implementation code is available at: https://github.com/Dog-Yang/ConInfer

Modality Fusion and Masked Complementary Modeling Based Pre-training for Brain Tumor Segmentation

基于模态融合与掩码互补建模的脑肿瘤分割预训练

2026-04-01
Information Fusion (IF: 15.5)

Jianwen Liang，Li Lin，Junyan Lyu，Pujin Cheng，Xiaoying Tang

DOI: 10.1016/j.inffus.2026.104345

Self-supervised learning (SSL), which leverages large amounts of unlabeled data to reduce dependence on extensive annotations, has demonstrated remarkable transferability in medical image segmentation tasks. However, most existing SSL approaches for medical imaging are limited to single-modality data, overlooking the intrinsic complementarity among different imaging modalities. In brain tumor analysis, multimodal magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) plays a crucial role in accurately delineating tumor subregions, as each modality provides distinct yet complementary information about tumor morphology and physiology. Therefore, effectively exploiting multimodal information during SSL has emerged as an important research focus for advancing brain tumor segmentation. In this study, we introduce a novel SSL framework, Modality Fusion and Masked Complementary Modeling (MF-MCM), developed for multimodal brain MRI. MF-MCM first performs modality-wise fusion through weighted sum of the input modalities to generate fused images equal in number to the input modalities. Subsequently, it applies complementary masking to the multichannel image constructed by concatenating the fused images, ensuring that the unmasked regions across all channels are non-overlapping and they jointly occupy the whole brain. Finally, by optimizing the multimodal image reconstruction and fusion coefficients regression tasks, the pre-training model is endowed with the ability to perceive fine-grained neuroanatomical details, integrate multimodal information, and achieve effective cross-modality interaction. The proposed SSL framework is fine-tuned and evaluated on four brain tumor segmentation datasets and one stroke lesion segmentation dataset. Results demonstrate that MF-MCM outperforms various state-of-the-art medical SSL methods while effectively reducing annotation requirements and maintaining robustness in scenarios where certain modalities are absent. Codes and pre-trained models will be available at https://github.com/liangjianwen01/MF-MCM .

研究问题：如何在自监督预训练中充分利用多模态MRI互补信息以提升脑肿瘤分割并降低标注依赖。

研究方法：提出MF-MCM框架，先加权融合各模态生成多通道图像，再实施互补掩码并联合优化多模态重建与融合系数回归。

主要发现：在四个脑肿瘤和一个卒中分割数据集上，MF-MCM优于现有医学SSL方法，且对缺失模态保持鲁棒。

创新点：首次将互补掩码策略引入多模态医学SSL，使网络在无标注阶段即可学习跨模态细粒度解剖与交互。

方法详情：MF-MCM先对输入的多模态MRI做加权求和生成与原始模态数相等的融合图像，再将这些融合图像拼接成多通道体积并执行互补掩码，使所有通道的可见区域互不重叠且联合覆盖全脑；预训练任务同时重建被掩码的多模态图像并回归融合权重，从而显式学习跨模态细粒度解剖特征与交互。

局限性：方法假设各模态空间已严格对齐，未评估配准误差下的鲁棒性；加权融合策略可能弱化极端对比度模态的特异信息；互补掩码固定模式或限制模型对不规则病灶形状的敏感性。

未来方向：未来可引入可学习的动态掩码策略，并将MF-MCM扩展至其他多模态病理(如乳腺MRI+超声)以验证通用性。

研究相关性：对于致力于减少医学图像标注依赖、利用多模态互补信息或需要在模态缺失场景下保持分割性能的研究者，该文提供了即插即用的预训练框架和开源代码。

M2H-MX: Multi-Task Dense Visual Perception for Real-Time Monocular Spatial Understanding

M2H-MX：面向实时单目空间理解的多任务密集视觉感知

2026-03-31
arXiv

U. V. B. L. Udugama，George Vosselman，Francesco Nex

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29236v1

Monocular cameras are attractive for robotic perception due to their low cost and ease of deployment, yet achieving reliable real-time spatial understanding from a single image stream remains challenging. While recent multi-task dense prediction models have improved per-pixel depth and semantic estimation, translating these advances into stable monocular mapping systems is still non-trivial. This paper presents M2H-MX, a real-time multi-task perception model for monocular spatial understanding. The model preserves multi-scale feature representations while introducing register-gated global context and controlled cross-task interaction in a lightweight decoder, enabling depth and semantic predictions to reinforce each other under strict latency constraints. Its outputs integrate directly into an unmodified monocular SLAM pipeline through a compact perception-to-mapping interface. We evaluate both dense prediction accuracy and in-the-loop system performance. On NYUDv2, M2H-MX-L achieves state-of-the-art results, improving semantic mIoU by 6.6% and reducing depth RMSE by 9.4% over representative multi-task baselines. When deployed in a real-time monocular mapping system on ScanNet, M2H-MX reduces average trajectory error by 60.7% compared to a strong monocular SLAM baseline while producing cleaner metric-semantic maps. These results demonstrate that modern multi-task dense prediction can be reliably deployed for real-time monocular spatial perception in robotic systems.
