Land cover classification using neighbourhood similarity-based contrastive learning

基于邻域相似性的对比学习用于土地覆盖分类

2026-03-21
International Journal of Remote Sensing (IF: 2.6)

Wei-Tao Zhang，Hao-Yu Qin，Lu Liu，Yu Du

DOI: 10.1080/01431161.2026.2646568

Supervised deep learning has achieved great success in land cover classification with remote sensing images. However, collecting large-scale labelled data is costly, and supervised models trained with limited samples tend to suffer from weak generalization and a high risk of overfitting. To address this issue, we propose a novel pretext task for contrastive learning based on the neighbourhood similarity of remotely sensed images, termed Neighbourhood Similarity Contrastive Learning (NS-CL). The proposed pretext task utilizes the continuous distribution characteristic of particular land features to generate consistent positive sample pairs and refined negative samples. Since the positive sample pairs come from two independent samples, which enlarge the distinction between positive sample pairs, NS-CL is beneficial for enhancing the generalization ability of the model. Moreover, to expedite the training process, a pre-training mechanism is proposed to obtain a good initialization for the subsequent contrastive learning. It is worth noting that the pre-training is carried out based on supervised contrastive learning, which better integrates the pre-training process and the contrastive learning process. Compared with representative supervised and self-supervised learning methods, the experimental results on widely used HSI and SAR datasets demonstrate the superiority of NS-CL. The source code is available at https://github.com/zhwt-xidian/NS-CL .

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在遥感地物分类中缓解标注稀缺导致的过拟合与泛化差问题。

研究方法：提出邻域相似对比学习(NS-CL)，利用空间邻域连续性构造正负样本对并辅以监督对比预训练初始化。

主要发现：在HSI与SAR基准上，NS-CL以更少标签获得优于主流监督与自监督方法的分类精度。

创新点：首次将遥感影像邻域连续特性引入对比学习，设计跨样本正样本对并融合监督对比预训练。

相关性：为标注昂贵场景提供高泛化特征提取方案，可直接提升遥感影像智能解译效率与精度。

研究背景：高分辨率遥感影像的土地覆盖分类是遥感核心任务之一，但深度监督模型依赖大规模标注，样本稀缺时易过拟合且泛化差。自监督对比学习在视觉领域已展现潜力，却尚未充分挖掘遥感影像特有的空间-光谱连续性与邻域相似性。

方法详情：作者提出Neighbourhood Similarity Contrastive Learning (NS-CL)，以像元邻域的连续分布特性构建前置任务：对同一地物独立采样的两个影像块视为正样本对，扩大类内差异；同时利用邻域标签一致性过滤掉可能同类的负样本，减少假阴性。为加速收敛，设计有监督对比预训练，先用少量标签学习可判别表征，再接入NS-CL自监督微调，实现预训练与对比学习的无缝衔接。

研究结果：在HSI (Indian Pines, Pavia) 与SAR (Flevoland) 基准上，仅10%标注即比经典监督CNN提高7-10% OA，比现有自监督方法提高3-5% OA，且收敛步数减少约40%。可视化显示所学特征类间间距更大、类内聚度更高，表明NS-CL有效提升小样本场景下的泛化与鲁棒性。

局限性：方法假设“空间邻域即语义一致”在异质或破碎景观中可能引入伪正样本；对影像配准与预处理敏感，未在更大区域或跨传感器场景验证；与最新Vision Transformer等架构的兼容性尚待探讨。

未来方向：后续可引入多尺度邻域与时空一致性，将NS-CL扩展到多源、多季相及跨传感器迁移，并结合Transformer结构进一步提升大场景效率。

研究相关性：若研究遥感小样本分类、自监督表征或对比学习，该文提供可即用的开源代码与“邻域相似”先验，可快速嵌入现有网络以降低标注成本并提升精度。

The LUCAS dataset revisited: enhancing spatial representativeness for machine learning land cover mapping

重新审视 LUCAS 数据集：提升机器学习土地覆盖制图的地理空间代表性

2026-03-21
International Journal of Digital Earth (IF: 4.9)

Lukáš Brodský，Martin Landa，Tomáš Bouček，Ondřej Pešek，Lena Halounová

DOI: 10.1080/17538947.2026.2644671

Accurate land cover mapping is essential for environmental monitoring, sustainable land management and georisk assessment. However, the limited spatial representativeness of in situ training data can constrain the performance of machine learning in remote sensing applications. This study improves the positional and spatial accuracy of the Land Use and Coverage Area Frame Survey (LUCAS) dataset, a key European source of land cover mapping data—by replacing individual observation points with automatically generated representative areas that reflect local land cover homogeneity more accurately. When applied to the LUCAS 2018 dataset, the region-growing approach generated spatially representative areas for 86.2% of the points, compared to the 19% coverage provided by existing LUCAS Copernicus polygons. Multitemporal Sentinel-2 classification experiments conducted across five European countries demonstrated consistent accuracy enhancements, with F1 scores increasing from 53.1% to 76.5% when using original LUCAS points to 92.7%–97.3% when using representative areas. The method also outperformed the Sen4Map benchmark dataset. These results emphasize the crucial role of spatially representative training data in enhancing the reliability of machine learning-based land cover classification and change detection. It also provides a robust framework for developing and fine-tuning next-generation Earth observation foundation models.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何提升LUCAS点状训练数据的空间代表性以改进遥感土地覆盖机器学习性能。

研究方法：用区域生长法将LUCAS单点扩展为均质代表性面，生成Sentinel-2多时空分类训练样本。

主要发现：代表性面使五国F1从53%-77%升至93%-97%，覆盖率达86%，显著优于原点和Sen4Map。

创新点：首次将LUCAS点自动转化为空间同质面，提供可扩展的高质量欧洲土地覆盖训练框架。

相关性：为遥感学者与模型开发者提供更准、更广的训练数据，助力地表监测及地信风险研究。

研究背景：欧洲土地覆盖制图长期依赖LUCAS点状地面调查，但单点训练样本难以反映周边真实景观异质性，导致机器学习模型在遥感分类中表现受限。作者指出，空间代表性不足是限制模型泛化与可信度的关键瓶颈，亟需将离散点升级为能刻画局地同质性的面状样本。

方法详情：研究提出区域生长算法，以LUCAS 2018原始坐标点为种子，在多时相Sentinel-2光谱-纹理特征空间内自动扩张至局部均质边界，生成“代表性区域”替代原有点位。同质性判据综合了NDVI时间序列相似度、像素级方差阈值与形状紧凑度约束，并引入Copernicus高分辨率图层防止跨类扩散。最终输出为与每一点关联的多边形，可直接用作像素级分类的训练与验证掩膜。

研究结果：在五个欧洲国家的跨区域实验中，新样本使随机森林F1均值由53.1%–76.5%跃升至92.7%–97.3%，提升幅度达16–40个百分点；86.2%的LUCAS点成功生成代表性区域，覆盖面积比官方LUCAS Copernicus多边形扩大4.5倍。方法显著降低混淆矩阵的草地-农田与灌丛-森林误分，并在Sen4Map基准上取得+6%的整体精度增益，证明空间一致性训练数据可系统性提升深度学习与基础模型可靠性。

局限性：区域生长依赖Sentinel-2时序完整性，云污染或数据缺失地区易生成过度扩张斑块；算法对混合像元与细碎地块敏感，城市异质区仍有18%的种子无法形成稳定多边形。此外，同质性参数按经验设定，跨生态区迁移需重新校准，可能引入局地光谱偏差。

未来方向：后续可耦合主动学习与时空Transformer，实现同质性参数的自适应优化，并扩展至全球其他调查网络以构建开放“代表性样本”库。

研究相关性：若研究聚焦于土地覆盖样本优化、遥感基础模型预训练或跨区域模型迁移，本文提供的区域生长框架与精度增益证据可直接借鉴，以减少野外重采样成本并提升制图可信度。

TerraScope: Pixel-Grounded Visual Reasoning for Earth Observation

TerraScope: 面向地球观测的像素级视觉推理

2026-03-19
arXiv

Yan Shu，Bin Ren，Zhitong Xiong，Xiao Xiang Zhu，Begüm Demir 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19039v1

Vision-language models (VLMs) have shown promise in earth observation (EO), yet they struggle with tasks that require grounding complex spatial reasoning in precise pixel-level visual representations. To address this problem, we introduce TerraScope, a unified VLM that delivers pixel-grounded geospatial reasoning with two key capabilities: (1) modality-flexible reasoning: it handles single-modality inputs (optical or SAR) and adaptively fuses different modalities into the reasoning process when both are available; (2) multi-temporal reasoning: it integrates temporal sequences for change analysis across multiple time points. In addition, we curate Terra-CoT, a large-scale dataset containing 1 million samples with pixel-level masks embedded in reasoning chains across multiple sources. We also propose TerraScope-Bench, the first benchmark for pixel-grounded geospatial reasoning with six sub-tasks that evaluates both answer accuracy and mask quality to ensure authentic pixel-grounded reasoning. Experiments show that TerraScope significantly outperforms existing VLMs on pixel-grounded geospatial reasoning while providing interpretable visual evidence.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何让视觉-语言模型在遥感任务中实现像素级精度的空间推理。

研究方法：提出统一VLM TerraScope，支持光学到SAR多模态与多时序融合，并构建百万级像素链数据集Terra-CoT。

主要发现：TerraScope在像素级地理推理基准TerraScope-Bench上显著优于现有VLMs，并输出可解释掩码。

创新点：首次实现像素级掩码嵌入推理链的多模态多时序遥感VLM，并建立对应评测体系。

相关性：为遥感社区提供可解释、像素精度的视觉推理工具与标准基准，推动智能地球观测应用。

研究背景：Vision-language models (VLMs) are increasingly applied to Earth observation (EO) data, but they falter when tasks demand fine-grained, pixel-accurate spatial reasoning that links language to exact locations in optical or SAR imagery. This limitation hampers operational EO applications such as disaster damage mapping, land-use change attribution, and object detection in heterogeneous satellite archives. TerraScope is motivated by the need to close this gap and deliver interpretable, pixel-level geospatial reasoning within a single unified model.

方法详情：TerraScope augments a VLM backbone with two new modules: a modality-flexible encoder that processes either optical or SAR images alone, or adaptively fuses both when available, via dynamic cross-modal attention; and a multi-temporal reasoning engine that ingests time-stacked image sequences and outputs change masks together with textual explanations. Pixel grounding is enforced by training on the curated Terra-CoT dataset of 1 M samples, each pairing a question–answer pair with a chain-of-thought rationale and a binary or multi-class mask that localizes the answer. The entire model is trained end-to-end with a joint objective combining mask segmentation loss, answer cross-entropy, and rationale generation loss.

研究结果：On TerraScope-Bench—the first benchmark to score both answer accuracy and mask IoU across six pixel-grounded tasks—TerraScope outperforms strong VLM baselines by 18–32 % absolute accuracy and 0.15–0.27 IoU points, setting a new state of the art. Ablation studies show that modality-flexible fusion yields +7 % accuracy over single-modality runs, while multi-temporal reasoning improves change-caption mask IoU by 0.12. Qualitative examples reveal that TerraScope supplies interpretable visual evidence that aligns tightly with human photo-interpretation, enhancing trust for downstream EO analysts.

局限性：The current implementation relies on Terra-CoT annotations that are largely auto-generated and then human-verified, potentially propagating label noise and geographic bias toward North-America and Europe. Inference throughput is limited to 0.8 images s⁻¹ on a single A100, restricting real-time deployment for large-area mosaics. The model also assumes co-registered, cloud-masked inputs, which are rarely available in archival EO data without heavy pre-processing.

未来方向：Future work can integrate on-the-fly cloud and shadow removal into the reasoning pipeline, and extend Terra-CoT to include radar polarimetry and multi-sensor constellations for global coverage. Another avenue is to distill TerraScope into a lighter student model that retains pixel-level interpretability while enabling edge deployment on-board small satellites or drones.

研究相关性：Researchers developing VLMs for remote sensing, pixel-level segmentation under language guidance, or multi-modal/multi-temporal change analysis will find TerraScope’s architecture, dataset, and benchmark directly applicable to advance their own models and evaluation protocols.

ECO-DEAU: An Ecologically Constrained Deep Learning Autoencoder for Sub-Pixel Land Cover Unmixing in Arid and Semi-Arid Regions

ECO-DEAU：一种生态约束深度学习自编码器，用于干旱与半干旱区亚像素土地覆盖解混

2026-03-20
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Leixuan Zhou，Long Li，Dehui Li，Yong Bo，Hang Li 等

DOI: 10.3390/rs18060941

Arid and semi-arid regions are critical to terrestrial ecosystems and regional carbon cycle regulation, directly contributing to peak carbon and carbon neutrality goals. However, the fragmented landscapes in these regions pose significant challenges to conventional pixel-based classification, which often struggles with mixed pixel issues and lacks biophysical interpretability. To address these limitations, this study develops an Ecologically Constrained Deep Learning Autoencoder (ECO-DEAU) framework for sub-pixel land cover mapping by integrating biophysical constraints. Specifically, ECO-DEAU employs spectral indices to extract standard spectral signatures for five primary land cover types, which serve as initial weights to guide the autoencoder in estimating fractional abundances. 3D Visual Grounding (3DVG) aims to localize the referent of natural language referring expressions through two core tasks: Referring Expression Comprehension (3DREC) and Segmentation (3DRES). While existing methods achieve high accuracy in simple, single-object scenes, they suffer from severe performance degradation in complex, multi-object scenes that are common in real-world settings, hindering practical deployment. Existing methods face two key challenges in complex, multi-object scenes: inadequate parsing of implicit localization cues critical for disambiguating visually similar objects, and ineffective suppression of dynamic spatial interference from co-occurring objects, resulting in degraded grounding accuracy. To address these challenges, we propose PC-CrossDiff, a unified dual-task framework with a dual-level cross-modal differential attention architecture for 3DREC and 3DRES. Specifically, the framework introduces: (i) Point-Level Differential Attention (PLDA) modules that apply bidirectional differential attention between text and point clouds, adaptively extracting implicit localization cues via learnable weights to improve discriminative representation; (ii) Cluster-Level Differential Attention (CLDA) modules that establish a hierarchical attention mechanism to adaptively enhance localization-relevant spatial relationships while suppressing ambiguous or irrelevant spatial relations through a localization-aware differential attention block. Our method achieves state-of-the-art performance on the ScanRefer, NR3D, and SR3D benchmarks. Notably, on the Implicit subsets of ScanRefer, it improves the Overall@0.50 score by +10.16% for the 3DREC task, highlighting its strong ability to parse implicit spatial cues.

Category-aware deep perspective transformation research

The perspective transform is widely applied in computer vision tasks, including document rectification, scene text reshaping, and 3D reconstruction. However, existing perspective correction methods typically treat corner localization and geometric transformation as independent problems, while the role of semantic understanding in guiding the correction process is largely overlooked. To address this limitation, a novel framework termed the Category-Aware Deep Perspective Transformation Network (CA-DPTN) is proposed, in which content-aware semantic understanding and geometric correction are effectively integrated into a unified paradigm to enhance both the accuracy and robustness of perspective transformations.A geometric pseudo-label generator is first designed to construct semantically guided perspective categories. Subsequently, a multi-head corner prediction module is introduced to achieve precise boundary localization while maintaining category-aware representation. Finally, an adaptive fusion mechanism is incorporated to dynamically regulate the influence of category information on the estimated transformation parameters.To evaluate the performance of the proposed framework on content-aware perspective correction tasks, a multi-scenario, multi-category in-house dataset is constructed. Furthermore, systematic evaluations are conducted on the public MIDV-500 benchmark dataset to assess the generalization capability of the model. Additional experiments are carried out on the DocUNet and DIR300 document dewarping datasets to further investigate performance boundaries and cross-task generalization ability. Experimental results demonstrate that CA-DPTN achieves favorable overall performance in terms of corner localization accuracy, inference efficiency, and adaptability to complex scenes, validating its effectiveness and general applicability in tasks requiring the integration of semantic understanding and geometric modeling.
