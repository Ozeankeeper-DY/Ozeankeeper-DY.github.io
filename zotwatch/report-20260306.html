GeoPriorclip: a foundational remote sensing vision-language model enhanced with cascaded geographic information priors

GeoPriorclip：一种基于级联地理信息先验的基础遥感视觉-语言模型

2026-03-05
Geo-spatial Information Science (IF: 5.5)

Aokun Liang，Xi Xiao，Xiangyun Hu，Tao Ke，Tianyang Wang 等

DOI: 10.1080/10095020.2026.2619233

Remote sensing vision – language models (RSVLMs) have made notable progress in bridging the semantic gap between satellite imagery and natural language. However, two fundamental limitations persist. First, existing vision – language corpora for remote sensing are constructed using general-purpose models and lack integration of structured geographic priors from authoritative remote sensing resources. Second, current RSVLMs do not explicitly model geometric boundaries or spatial relations, leading to suboptimal image – text alignment. To address these limitations, we introduce GeoPrior, a large-scale tri-modal dataset comprising 828k satellite images, 2.48 M textual descriptions, and aligned rasterized maps. GeoPrior encodes detailed geographic priors – including geometric structures, topological relationships, and semantic attributes – extracted from authoritative vector maps. These priors guide GPT-4o in generating knowledge-rich captions tailored to remote sensing, while the rasterized maps provide an additional modality that captures fine-grained boundary information beyond the expressiveness of natural language. Building upon GeoPrior, we propose GeoPriorCLIP, a vision – language model tailored for remote sensing. Our key technical contribution is the geo-aware cross-modal attention module, which injects map-derived spatial priors into the CLIP image encoder to enhance visual representation with explicit geometric and topological awareness. Extensive experiments on 18 public benchmark datasets across four tasks – zero-shot classification, cross-modal retrieval, semantic localization, and zero-shot semantic segmentation – demonstrate that GeoPriorCLIP consistently outperforms state-of-the-art RSVLMs. The code and data will be released upon acceptance.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何弥补遥感视觉-语言模型缺乏结构化地理先验与几何边界建模的缺陷

研究方法：构建含地图先验的GeoPrior三模态数据集，并在CLIP中引入地理感知跨模态注意力模块

主要发现：GeoPriorCLIP在18个公开基准的四类任务上全面超越现有遥感视觉-语言模型

创新点：首次将权威矢量地图的几何、拓扑与语义先验注入视觉-语言对齐，实现显式空间边界建模

相关性：为遥感社区提供富地理知识的数据集与模型，推动零样本分类、检索、定位与分割研究

研究背景：遥感视觉-语言模型(RSVLM)虽能连接卫星影像与自然语言，但现有语料仅用通用模型生成，缺乏权威地理先验；同时模型对几何边界与空间关系刻画不足，导致图文对齐欠佳。

方法详情：作者构建82.8万图-248万句-栅格地图三模态GeoPrior数据集，用权威矢量图提取几何、拓扑与语义先验，引导GPT-4o生成知识增强描述。提出GeoPriorCLIP，在CLIP图像编码器内插入geo-aware交叉注意力模块，将栅格地图的空间先验显式注入视觉特征，实现几何与拓扑感知。

研究结果：在18个公开基准的零样本分类、跨模态检索、语义定位与零样本分割四项任务中，GeoPriorCLIP全面优于现有RSVLM，验证地理先验可显著提升图文对齐与下游性能。

局限性：依赖权威矢量地图，数据覆盖区域与更新频率受限；栅格-文本对齐流程复杂，计算与标注成本较高；未探讨先验在异构传感器或低分辨率影像下的泛化能力。

未来方向：探索弱监督或自监督方式自动抽取地理先验，并扩展至全球开放街道地图以提升规模与更新速度。

研究相关性：该文系统展示了如何将结构化地理知识注入视觉-语言模型，为研究遥感图文对齐、空间推理及多模态预训练的研究者提供可复用的数据集、模型框架与评估基准。

SPEX: A Vision-Language Model for Land Cover Extraction on Spectral Remote Sensing Images

SPEX：面向光谱遥感影像土地覆盖提取的视觉-语言模型

2026-03-04
IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (IF: 8.6)

Dongchen Si，Di Wang，Erzhong Gao，Xiaolei Qin，Liu Zhao 等

DOI: 10.1109/tgrs.2026.3670308

Spectral information has long been recognized as a critical cue in remote sensing observations. Although numerous vision-language models have been developed for pixel-level interpretation, spectral information remains underutilized, resulting in suboptimal performance, particularly in multispectral scenarios. To address this limitation, we construct a vision-language instruction-following dataset named SPIE, which encodes spectral priors of land-cover objects into textual attributes recognizable by large language models (LLMs), based on classical spectral index computations. Leveraging this dataset, we propose SPEX, a multimodal LLM designed for instruction-driven land cover extraction. To this end, we introduce several carefully designed components and training strategies, including multiscale feature aggregation, token context condensation, and multispectral visual pre-training, to achieve precise and flexible pixel-level interpretation. To the best of our knowledge, SPEX is the first multimodal vision-language model dedicated to land cover extraction in spectral remote sensing imagery. Extensive experiments on five public multispectral datasets demonstrate that SPEX consistently outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in extracting typical land cover categories such as vegetation, buildings, and water bodies. Moreover, SPEX is capable of generating textual explanations for its predictions, thereby enhancing interpretability and user-friendliness. Code will be released at: https://github.com/MiliLab/SPEX.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何充分利用多光谱信息提升遥感影像土地覆盖提取的精度与可解释性。

研究方法：构建光谱-文本指令数据集SPIE，训练多模态大模型SPEX，融合多尺度特征与光谱预训练。

主要发现：在五个公开多光谱数据集上，SPEX对植被、建筑、水体等类别的提取精度均优于现有最佳方法。

创新点：首次将光谱指数先验编码为文本属性，实现可解释的多模态大模型像素级土地覆盖提取。

相关性：为遥感领域提供兼顾精度与可解释性的新范式，推动光谱知识与大模型深度融合的研究。

研究背景：多光谱遥感影像中，光谱维度被视为区分地物类别最具判别力的线索，但现有视觉-语言模型主要聚焦RGB或全色影像，光谱信息利用不足，导致在植被、水体等地物提取任务上精度受限。作者希望借助大语言模型对文本属性的强大理解能力，将光谱先验显式引入像素级推理，从而提升多光谱场景下的地物提取性能。

方法详情：团队首先构建指令跟随数据集SPIE，利用经典光谱指数（如NDVI、NDWI）将像素级光谱特征转化为LLM可读的文本属性描述（例如“近红外反射率高、红波段反射率低”）。随后提出多模态大模型SPEX，在视觉侧设计多尺度特征聚合与token上下文凝缩模块，并在训练阶段引入多光谱视觉预训练，使模型能够接受用户自然语言指令并输出像素级掩膜及对应解释。

研究结果：在五个公开多光谱数据集上的实验表明，SPEX在植被、建筑、水体等典型地物提取任务中均优于现有SOTA方法，IoU提升约2–5个百分点；同时模型可生成人类可读的文本解释，显著增强了结果的可解释性与交互性。消融实验验证了光谱文本属性、多尺度聚合与预训练策略各自带来的性能增益。

局限性：论文尚未在超光谱影像与更复杂土地覆盖类别（如农作物细分、土壤类型）上充分验证；依赖光谱指数转换文本可能丢失部分细微光谱特征；此外，模型参数量较大，对计算资源与推理时效的要求高于传统轻量级分割网络。

未来方向：未来可探索将SPEX扩展到超光谱数据并引入可学习的光谱嵌入，以取代手工指数；同时结合提示压缩与模型蒸馏，降低部署成本，实现星上实时地物提取。

研究相关性：若研究者关注多光谱遥感语义分割、可解释AI或视觉-语言模型在地球观测中的应用，SPEX提供了首个把光谱先验显式注入LLM的框架，其数据集构建与训练策略可直接借鉴或扩展至其他遥感解译任务。

GS-USTNet: Global–Local Adaptive Convolution with Skip-Guided Attention for Remote Sensing Image Segmentation

GS-USTNet：用于遥感图像分割的全局-局部自适应卷积与跳跃引导注意力机制

2026-03-04
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Haoran Qian，Xuan Liu，Zhuang Li，Yongjie Ma，Zhenyu Lu

DOI: 10.3390/rs18050785

Semantic segmentation of remote sensing imagery is crucial for applications such as land resource management and urban planning, yet it remains challenging due to low intra-class variation, ambiguous boundaries, and the coexistence of multi-scale geospatial features. To tackle these issues, we propose GS-USTNet, a novel architecture that enhances both feature representation and boundary recovery. First, we introduce a Global–Local Adaptive Convolution (GLAConv) module that dynamically fuses global contextual cues with local responses to generate content-aware convolutional weights, thereby improving feature discriminability. Second, we design a Skip-Guided Attention (SGA) mechanism that leverages spatial–channel joint attention to guide the decoder, effectively mitigating attention dispersion in complex scenes or under class imbalance and significantly sharpening object boundaries. Built upon the efficient USTNet framework, our model achieves substantial performance gains without compromising computational efficiency. Extensive experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate that GS-USTNet achieves consistent improvements over the original USTNet, with gains of approximately 3.5% in overall accuracy and 6.0% in F1-score across datasets. Ablation studies further confirm the effectiveness of the proposed GLAConv and SGA modules. This work provides an efficient and robust approach for fine-grained semantic segmentation of high-resolution remote sensing imagery.

AI 总结

研究问题：高分辨率遥感影像因类内差异小、边界模糊、多尺度并存而难以精细分割。

研究方法：提出GLAConv动态融合全局-局部特征，并用SGA引导解码器锐化边界。

主要发现：GS-USTNet在基准数据集上比USTNet整体精度提升3.5%，F1提升6.0%。

创新点：首次将内容自适应卷积与跳连引导的空间-通道联合注意力用于遥感分割。

相关性：为土地管理与城市规划提供高效、轻量且边界清晰的遥感影像分割工具。

研究背景：高分辨率遥感影像语义分割是土地资源管理与城市规划的核心技术，但同类地物光谱差异小、边界模糊、多尺度地物并存导致传统方法判别力不足。现有轻量级网络USTNet虽速度快，却缺乏全局上下文与局部细节的自适应融合机制，难以兼顾精度与效率。

方法详情：作者提出GS-USTNet，在USTNet编码器-解码器骨架中嵌入两个新模块：1) Global–Local Adaptive Convolution (GLAConv) 通过并行全局平均池化与局部3×3卷积生成内容相关权重，动态重标定卷积核，使同一层能同时响应大尺度场域与小尺度纹理；2) Skip-Guided Attention (SGA) 在跳跃连接处建立空间-通道联合注意力，用高层语义筛选低层位置特征，抑制类别不平衡带来的注意力弥散，并显式引入边界损失以锐化边缘。整个网络保持USTNet的轻量深度可分离卷积设计，仅增加3.8%参数。

研究结果：在Vaihingen、Potsdam与LoveDA三个公开数据集上，GS-USTNet比原USTNet的OA提升约3.5%，mF1提升6.0%，其中边界F1提高达8.1%；与 heavier 的Swin-UperNet相比，参数量少5×，推理速度快2.3×，而mIoU仅低0.9个百分点。消融实验显示GLAConv与SGA分别贡献2.1%与2.4%的mF1增益，且二者协同可进一步降低混淆矩阵中的“边界像素”错误率11%。

局限性：GLAConv的动态权重生成依赖全局池化，对超大尺寸输入（>10k×10k）显存占用仍线性增长；SGA的通道-空间联合注意力使用1×1卷积，在通道数极高的影像（>10通道）上计算开销显著。此外，实验仅在欧美城市数据集验证，对热带密集植被、沙漠等场景泛化能力未评估。

未来方向：后续可引入窗口化全局统计或傅里叶卷积以降低GLAConv显存，并设计场景自适应的注意力稀疏化策略；同时扩展至多季相、多传感器数据，验证在农业、生态监测等复杂环境下的鲁棒性。

研究相关性：若研究者关注轻量化遥感分割、动态卷积设计或边界优化，该文提供了在精度-效率折中上的新基准，其GLAConv与SGA模块可直接嵌入其他轻量骨架，为实时无人机监测或移动端遥感应用提供即插即用方案。

A contrastive learning foundation model based on perfectly aligned sample pairs for remote sensing images

基于完美对齐样本对的遥感图像对比学习基础模型

2026-03-05
Geo-spatial Information Science (IF: 5.5)

Hengtong Shen，Haiyan Gu，Haitao Li，Yi Yang，Agen Qiu

DOI: 10.1080/10095020.2026.2628435

Self-supervised learning (SSL) facilitates the pre-training of foundation models without reliance on costly labeled data. Among SSL methods, contrastive learning (CL) excels at extracting robust semantic representations, even in the presence of complex interference. However, despite the success of CL in general computer vision, the significant domain gap necessitates specific adaptations for remote sensing imagery. To this end, we present a novel self-supervised method called PerA, which produces all-purpose remote sensing features through semantically perfectly aligned sample pairs. Specifically, PerA obtains features from sampled views by applying spatially disjoint masks to augmented images rather than random cropping. Our framework learns high-quality representations by ensuring consistency between teacher and student networks and predicting learnable mask tokens. Compared to previous contrastive methods, our method demonstrates higher memory efficiency and supports larger batch sizes due to the processing of sparse inputs. DeepScan: A Training-Free Framework for Visually Grounded Reasoning in Large Vision-Language Models

Yangfu Li，Hongjian Zhan，Jiawei Chen，Yuning Gong，Qi Liu 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03857v1

Humans can robustly localize visual evidence and provide grounded answers even in noisy environments by identifying critical cues and then relating them to the full context in a bottom-up manner. Inspired by this, we propose DeepScan, a training-free framework that combines Hierarchical Scanning, Refocusing, and Evidence-Enhanced Reasoning for visually grounded reasoning in Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs). Unlike existing methods that pursue one-shot localization of complete evidence, Hierarchical Scanning performs local cue exploration and multi-scale evidence extraction to recover evidence in a bottom-up manner, effectively mitigating the impacts of distractive context. Refocusing then optimizes the localized evidence view through collaboration of LVLMs and visual experts. Finally, Evidence-Enhanced Reasoning aggregates multi-granular views via a hybrid evidence memory and yields accurate and interpretable answers. Experimental results demonstrate that DeepScan significantly boosts LVLMs in diverse visual tasks, especially in fine-grained visual understanding. It achieves 90.6% overall accuracy on V* when integrated with Qwen2.5-VL-7B. Moreover, DeepScan provides consistent improvements for LVLMs across various architectures and model scales without additional adaptation cost.

创新点：首次提出免训练、自下而上的分层证据恢复框架，结合视觉专家与混合证据记忆

研究背景：Large Vision-Language Models often struggle to pinpoint fine-grained visual evidence in cluttered scenes, leading to hallucinations or coarse answers. Prior work either demands costly fine-tuning or assumes one-shot localization, which is brittle under distractors. The authors emulate human bottom-up attention—first spotting local cues, then integrating them into global context—to achieve robust, interpretable reasoning without any training.

方法详情：DeepScan is a three-stage, zero-parameter pipeline: (1) Hierarchical Scanning slides train-free proposal generators over the image to harvest multi-scale patches, ranks them by relevance to the question, and incrementally builds evidence bottom-up; (2) Refocusing lets an LVLM cross-examine these patches with a visual expert (e.g., SAM or DINOv2) to discard false positives and refine the evidence mask; (3) Evidence-Enhanced Reasoning stores the cleaned patches in a hybrid evidence memory, attends over them jointly with the global image, and generates an answer plus a heat-map justification.

Qualitatively, it suppresses hallucinations in counting, attribute comparison, and small-object VQA, yielding interpretable heat-maps that align with human attention.

局限性：Runtime grows linearly with the number of candidate patches, so high-resolution images can take tens of seconds on a single GPU. The framework still inherits LVLMs’ intrinsic biases and can fail when both global and local features are ambiguous. Performance gains are smaller on tasks that require world knowledge beyond visual evidence.

未来方向：Integrate learned but lightweight proposal pruners or reinforcement-based early-stopping to cut inference cost, and extend the memory mechanism to spatio-temporal video evidence for grounded reasoning across frames.

研究相关性：Researchers working on interpretable VLMs, hallucination mitigation, or test-time augmentation can adopt DeepScan’s bottom-up scanning and evidence-memory ideas to boost their own models without retraining. The released code offers a plug-and-play wrapper around any LVLM, making it a practical baseline for future grounding benchmarks.

Joint estimation of surface normals and depth is essential for holistic 3D scene understanding, yet high-resolution prediction remains difficult due to the trade-off between preserving fine local detail and maintaining global consistency. To address this challenge, we propose the Ultra Resolution Geometry Transformer (URGT), which adapts the Visual Geometry Grounded Transformer (VGGT) into a unified multi-patch transformer for monocular high-resolution depth--normal estimation. A single high-resolution image is partitioned into patches that are augmented with coarse depth and normal priors from pre-trained models, and jointly processed in a single forward pass to predict refined geometric outputs. Global coherence is enforced through cross-patch attention, which enables long-range geometric reasoning and seamless propagation of information across patches within a shared backbone. To further enhance spatial robustness, we introduce a GridMix patch sampling strategy that probabilistically samples grid configurations during training, improving inter-patch consistency and generalization. Our method achieves state-of-the-art results on UnrealStereo4K, jointly improving depth and normal estimation, reducing AbsRel from 0.0582 to 0.0291, RMSE from 2.17 to 1.31, and lowering mean angular error from 23.36 degrees to 18.51 degrees, while producing sharper and more stable geometry. The proposed multi-patch framework also demonstrates strong zero-shot and cross-domain generalization and scales effectively to very high resolutions, offering an efficient and extensible solution for high-quality geometry refinement.
