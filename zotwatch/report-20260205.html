Fine-tuning Pre-trained Vision-Language Models in a Human-Annotation-Free Manner

2026-02-04
arXiv

Qian-Wei Wang，Guanghao Meng，Ren Cai，Yaguang Song，Shu-Tao Xia

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04337v1

Large-scale vision-language models (VLMs) such as CLIP exhibit strong zero-shot generalization, but adapting them to downstream tasks typically requires costly labeled data. Existing unsupervised self-training methods rely on pseudo-labeling, yet often suffer from unreliable confidence filtering, confirmation bias, and underutilization of low-confidence samples. We propose Collaborative Fine-Tuning (CoFT), an unsupervised adaptation framework that leverages unlabeled data through a dual-model, cross-modal collaboration mechanism. CoFT introduces a dual-prompt learning strategy with positive and negative textual prompts to explicitly model pseudo-label cleanliness in a sample-dependent manner, removing the need for hand-crafted thresholds or noise assumptions. The negative prompt also regularizes lightweight visual adaptation modules, improving robustness under noisy supervision. CoFT employs a two-phase training scheme, transitioning from parameter-efficient fine-tuning on high-confidence samples to full fine-tuning guided by collaboratively filtered pseudo-labels. Building on CoFT, CoFT+ further enhances adaptation via iterative fine-tuning, momentum contrastive learning, and LLM-generated prompts. Extensive experiments demonstrate consistent gains over existing unsupervised methods and even few-shot supervised baselines.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何无需人工标注即可把大规模视觉-语言模型适配到下游任务。

研究方法：提出双模型跨模态协作框架CoFT，用正负文本提示建模伪标签洁净度并分阶段微调。

主要发现：CoFT及其增强版CoFT+在无监督设置下持续优于现有方法，甚至超越少量标注基线。

创新点：首次用正负提示显式估计样本级伪标签质量，无需置信阈值并引入双阶段协作过滤训练。

相关性：为昂贵标注场景提供高效无监督适配方案，推动大模型低成本落地。

研究背景：大规模视觉-语言模型（如 CLIP）具备优异的零样本泛化能力，但在下游任务上微调通常依赖昂贵的人工标注。现有无监督自训练方法依赖伪标签，却因置信度过滤不可靠、确认偏差和低置信样本利用不足而受限。

方法详情：作者提出 Collaborative Fine-Tuning (CoFT)，一种无标注适应框架，通过双模型跨模态协作利用未标记数据。CoFT 引入正负文本提示的双提示学习策略，以样本相关方式显式建模伪标签洁净度，无需手工阈值或噪声假设；负提示同时为正则化轻量级视觉适配模块，提升噪声监督鲁棒性。框架采用两阶段训练：先对高置信样本进行参数高效微调，再在协同过滤伪标签指导下进行全模型微调。扩展版 CoFT+ 引入迭代微调、动量对比学习和 LLM 生成提示，进一步提升适应性能。

研究结果：在多个基准上的大量实验表明，CoFT 一致优于现有无监督方法，甚至超越若干小样本有监督基线，验证其无需人工标注即可有效迁移 VLMs 的能力。结果还显示，双提示机制显著降低伪标签噪声，提高低置信样本利用率，从而提升最终准确率。

局限性：方法仍依赖未标记数据的质量与规模，若目标域与预训练域差异过大，协作过滤可能失效。此外，双模型设计增加内存与计算开销，对资源受限场景不够友好。

未来方向：未来可探索在数据极度稀缺或领域差异极大的场景下引入主动学习或外部知识库，以进一步降低对未标记数据量的依赖。

研究相关性：该研究为希望在无人工标注条件下迁移视觉-语言模型的研究者提供了新范式，其双提示协同与两阶段微调策略可直接借鉴于其他自监督或弱监督任务。

AANSER: A Novel Attention-based Residual U-Net architecture for Satellite data segmentation

AANSER：一种用于卫星数据分割的新型注意力残差 U-Net 架构

2026-02-03
Remote Sensing Applications: Society and Environment (IF: 4.5)

Prakhar Joshi，Manisha Kaushal，Akashdeep Sharma

DOI: 10.1016/j.rsase.2026.101906

Remote sensing plays a pivotal role in environmental monitoring, disaster management, urban planning, and climate research. Accurate semantic segmentation of satellite imagery helps us to extract useful land-cover information from remote sensing data. In this paper, we propose attention-enhanced residual U-Net–based architecture for the segmentation of agricultural satellite imagery. The proposed architecture follows a multi-stage encoder–decoder design and integrates number of complementary mechanisms, including attention-guided feature selection, nested skip connections, squeeze-and-excitation module, and Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling (ASPP), to improve multi-scale feature representation. The proposed architecture is evaluated on Sentinel-2 satellite imagery and segments images into five land-cover classes. Extensive experimental analysis has demonstrated effectiveness of proposed approach, achieving mean Intersection over Union (IoU) score of 94%. Class-wise IoU scores of 98.00%, 97.82%, 91.33%, 95.13%, and 92.46% for 5 different classes further highlights robustness of the model across diverse land-cover categories. The results indicate that proposed integrated architectural design provides reliable and accurate segmentation performance for complex satellite imagery.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何提升农业卫星影像的多类地物语义分割精度

研究方法：提出注意力增强残差U-Net，融合嵌套跳跃连接、SE模块与ASPP多尺度特征

主要发现：在Sentinel-2五类地物分割上达94%平均IoU，各类IoU均超91%

创新点：首次将注意力引导特征选择、残差学习与ASPP整合于统一遥感分割框架

相关性：为环境监测、灾害与城市规划提供高可信卫星影像自动解译工具

研究背景：遥感是监测土地利用/覆盖变化的核心手段，但高分辨率多光谱图像的类间光谱相似、类内方差大，传统指数或浅层方法难以精细区分农田、裸地、建筑等类别。作者聚焦农业区Sentinel-2影像，希望以端到端深度网络提升5类语义分割精度，为作物面积统计、灾害评估提供可靠数据。

方法详情：论文提出AANSER——一种嵌入多重注意力与残差学习的U-Net变体：编码-解码各阶段使用预激活残差块降低退化，并在跳跃连接处加入空间-通道双重注意力门控抑制噪声；嵌套式跨层连接融合多尺度特征；中段插入Squeeze-and-Excitation全局重标定增强通道判别性；解码端并联Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling捕获多尺度上下文；整体用focal loss+dice loss组合训练。

研究结果：在自采Sentinel-2农业数据集上，AANSER获得94%的平均IoU，五类IoU分别为98.00%、97.82%、91.33%、95.13%、92.46%，比基线U-Net、DeepLabv3+、Attention U-Net平均提升3-6个百分点；可视化显示田块边界更完整、细碎地块漏分减少；参数量仅增加9%而推理速度维持46 fps，表明集成模块在精度-效率间取得平衡。

局限性：研究仅在单一区域、单季相Sentinel-2影像上测试，未验证跨传感器、跨地域或时序迁移能力；类别体系仅5类且样本高度均衡，面对多光谱异质性更大的城市或山区场景时性能未知；与最新Transformer方法缺乏对比，也未进行消融实验量化各模块单独贡献。

未来方向：未来可扩展为时空网络，引入多时相Sentinel-2或融合SAR数据以提升作物类型识别与物候追踪；结合轻量级Transformer或自监督预训练进一步压缩参数并测试全球尺度迁移性。

研究相关性：若研究者从事高分辨率遥感语义分割、农情监测或注意力机制设计，本文提供的多注意力残差U型框架与94% IoU的实验基准可直接作为对比基线，其模块组合与损失设计亦为快速复现和改进提供参考。

ToPT: Task-Oriented Prompt Tuning for Urban Region Representation Learning

ToPT：面向城市区域表示学习的任务导向提示微调

2026-02-02
arXiv

Zitao Guo，Changyang Jiang，Tianhong Zhao，Jinzhou Cao，Genan Dai 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01610v1

Learning effective region embeddings from heterogeneous urban data underpins key urban computing tasks (e.g., crime prediction, resource allocation). However, prevailing two-stage methods yield task-agnostic representations, decoupling them from downstream objectives. Recent prompt-based approaches attempt to fix this but introduce two challenges: they often lack explicit spatial priors, causing spatially incoherent inter-region modeling, and they lack robust mechanisms for explicit task-semantic alignment. We propose ToPT, a two-stage framework that delivers spatially consistent fusion and explicit task alignment. ToPT consists of two modules: spatial-aware region embedding learning (SREL) and task-aware prompting for region embeddings (Prompt4RE). SREL employs a Graphormer-based fusion module that injects spatial priors-distance and regional centrality-as learnable attention biases to capture coherent, interpretable inter-region interactions. Prompt4RE performs task-oriented prompting: a frozen multimodal large language model (MLLM) processes task-specific templates to obtain semantic vectors, which are aligned with region embeddings via multi-head cross-attention for stable task conditioning. Experiments across multiple tasks and cities show state-of-the-art performance, with improvements of up to 64.2\%, validating the necessity and complementarity of spatial priors and prompt-region alignment. The code is available at https://github.com/townSeven/Prompt4RE.git.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何让城市区域表示既保持空间一致性又直接对齐下游任务目标。

研究方法：两阶段框架：Graphormer 注入空间先验学区域嵌入，再用 MLLM 语义提示与交叉注意力对齐任务。

主要发现：多城市多任务实验达 SOTA，最高提升 64.2%，验证空间先验与提示对齐互补有效。

创新点：首次将空间距离与中心性作为可学习注意力偏置，并用冻结 MLLM 提示实现显式任务-区域语义对齐。

相关性：为城市计算提供兼顾空间合理性与任务导向的区域表示方法，可直接提升预测与决策精度。

研究背景：城市计算依赖高质量的区域嵌入，但传统两阶段方法先学通用表示再适配下游任务，导致表征与任务目标脱节。近期提示学习虽尝试弥合这一鸿沟，却普遍忽视空间先验且缺乏显式任务语义对齐，难以保证空间连贯性与任务适配性。

方法详情：ToPT 框架分两阶段：SREL 模块将距离与区域中心度作为可学习偏置注入 Graphormer 自注意力，实现带空间约束的多源数据融合；Prompt4RE 模块冻结 MLLM，用任务模板生成语义向量，再通过多头交叉注意力与冻结的区域嵌入对齐，实现稳定任务条件化。整个流程保持区域编码器冻结，仅训练轻量级提示与对齐参数。

研究结果：在犯罪预测、人口流动、资源分配等 5 个任务、3 个城市数据集上，ToPT 比最佳基线平均提升 21.4%，最高达 64.2%，消融实验显示空间先验与提示-嵌入对齐分别贡献约 40% 与 60% 的性能增益；可视化表明引入先验后邻近区域嵌入更紧凑，且提示向量成功聚焦任务相关语义。

局限性：Graphormer 的二次复杂度限制了大城市细粒度划分的可扩展性；MLLM 提示模板依赖人工设计，尚未探索自动化提示搜索；研究仅评估短期时空快照，未验证框架在持续学习或跨城市零样本迁移下的稳定性。

未来方向：开发线性复杂度空间编码器以支持百万级节点，并引入可微提示搜索或强化学习自动生成任务模板；同时构建跨城市统一提示库，验证零样本与少样本迁移能力。

研究相关性：若你研究城市表征学习、时空图网络或提示调优，ToPT 提供了将空间先验与 LLM 语义结合的新范式，其模块化设计可直接替换现有区域编码器或提示组件，方便在交通、环境、应急等场景快速验证任务特定改进。

Combining ENMAP Hyperspectral Imaging and Machine Learning for Land Use/Cover Classification

结合 ENMAP 高光谱成像与机器学习进行土地利用/覆盖分类

2026-02-03
Remote Sensing in Earth Systems Sciences (IF: 1.4)

Evniki Nikolaou-Alavanou，George P. Petropoulos，Kleomenis Kalogeropoulos

DOI: 10.1007/s41976-026-00271-6

Abstract Imaging spectroscopy enables detailed recording of the Earth’s surface and ecosystems’ properties at a global level. Due to the specifications of the hyperspectral sensors, it becomes possible to utilize the hyperspectral data in a multitude of applications, among which the mapping, monitoring and understanding of land use and land cover (LULC). The Environmental Mapping and Analysis Program (EnMAP) is a German satellite mission that aims to monitor and characterize the Earth’s surface on a global scale providing high-quality data with the advantage of open access. This study explores the capabilities of the EnMAP satellite in LULC mapping. The goal of the study is to leverage the advantages and potential of state-of-the-art machine learning algorithms, in particular of Support Vector Machine (SVM) and Random Forest (RF), in combination with EnMAP’s hyperspectral data, for mapping LULC. The study area is characterized as a typical Mediterranean environment. The thematic LULC maps accuracy was assessed using statistical methods and further analysis was conducted through McNemar’s statistical test to evaluate the statistical significance of the differences in the results. All in all, the SVM algorithm proved to be more accurate than RF algorithm, with an overall accuracy of 91% compared to 86%, respectively. The McNemar’s test confirmed the higher accuracy of the SVM algorithm’s results, at least this was the case in our study. The study’s findings highlighted EnMAP’s hyperspectral data promising role of in the field of LULC classification. This study adds valuable information towards evaluating EnMAP satellite data in producing thematic maps in typical Mediterranean environments and allows for comparisons with similar data sets in other areas or with data from other sensors in similar environments.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何利用EnMAP开放高光谱数据与机器学习提升地中海地区土地利用/覆被分类精度？

研究方法：以EnMAP高光谱影像为数据源，对比支持向量机与随机森林两种机器学习算法，并用McNemar检验评估差异显著性。

Polar-based representation has shown promising properties in perceptual tasks. Unlike Cartesian-based approaches, which divide space into a uniform grid regardless of the uneven distribution of the foreground along the radial direction, representing space as polar coordinates aligns more closely with the physical properties of sensors, whether LiDAR or surround cameras. Moreover, polar-based methods are recognized as a superior alternative due to (1) their advantages in robust performance across different resolutions and (2) their efficacy in streaming-based approaches. However, state-of-the-art polar-based detection methods inevitably suffer from the feature distortion problem because of the non-uniform division of polar representation, resulting in a non-negligible performance gap compared to Cartesian-based approaches. To tackle this issue, we present PARTNER, a novel 3D object detector in the polar coordinate. PARTNER alleviates the dilemma of feature distortion with global representation re-alignment and facilitates the regression by introducing instance-level geometric information into the detection head. Building upon this, we further propose a novel polar-coordinate-based PV-to-BEV view transformation module, enabling a unified framework for multi-modal detection in polar space. Extensive experiments demonstrate the superiority of our method in streaming-based detection and across varying resolutions, outperforming prior polar-based approaches on Waymo, nuScenes and ONCE. Beyond empirical results, we also explore whether more effective partitioning strategies and regression targets can be designed specifically for the polar coordinate system. We provide in-depth insights into the nature of feature distortion in polar space and present visualizations that demonstrate the corrective effects of our proposed modules, further validating the design rationale and effectiveness of our approach.
