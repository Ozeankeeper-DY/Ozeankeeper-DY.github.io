TerraScope: Pixel-Grounded Visual Reasoning for Earth Observation

TerraScope: 面向地球观测的像素级视觉推理

2026-03-19
arXiv

Yan Shu，Bin Ren，Zhitong Xiong，Xiao Xiang Zhu，Begüm Demir 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19039v1

Vision-language models (VLMs) have shown promise in earth observation (EO), yet they struggle with tasks that require grounding complex spatial reasoning in precise pixel-level visual representations. To address this problem, we introduce TerraScope, a unified VLM that delivers pixel-grounded geospatial reasoning with two key capabilities: (1) modality-flexible reasoning: it handles single-modality inputs (optical or SAR) and adaptively fuses different modalities into the reasoning process when both are available; (2) multi-temporal reasoning: it integrates temporal sequences for change analysis across multiple time points. In addition, we curate Terra-CoT, a large-scale dataset containing 1 million samples with pixel-level masks embedded in reasoning chains across multiple sources. We also propose TerraScope-Bench, the first benchmark for pixel-grounded geospatial reasoning with six sub-tasks that evaluates both answer accuracy and mask quality to ensure authentic pixel-grounded reasoning. Experiments show that TerraScope significantly outperforms existing VLMs on pixel-grounded geospatial reasoning while providing interpretable visual evidence.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何让视觉-语言模型在遥感任务中实现像素级精度的空间推理。

研究方法：提出统一VLM TerraScope，支持光学到SAR多模态与多时序融合，并构建百万级像素链数据集Terra-CoT。

主要发现：TerraScope在像素级地理推理基准TerraScope-Bench上显著优于现有VLMs，并输出可解释掩码。

创新点：首次实现像素级掩码嵌入推理链的多模态多时序遥感VLM，并建立对应评测体系。

相关性：为遥感社区提供可解释、像素精度的视觉推理工具与标准基准，推动智能地球观测应用。

研究背景：Vision-language models (VLMs) are increasingly applied to Earth observation (EO) data, but they falter when tasks demand fine-grained, pixel-accurate spatial reasoning that links language to exact locations in optical or SAR imagery. This limitation hampers operational EO applications such as disaster damage mapping, land-use change attribution, and object detection in heterogeneous satellite archives. TerraScope is motivated by the need to close this gap and deliver interpretable, pixel-level geospatial reasoning within a single unified model.

方法详情：TerraScope augments a VLM backbone with two new modules: a modality-flexible encoder that processes either optical or SAR images alone, or adaptively fuses both when available, via dynamic cross-modal attention; and a multi-temporal reasoning engine that ingests time-stacked image sequences and outputs change masks together with textual explanations. Pixel grounding is enforced by training on the curated Terra-CoT dataset of 1 M samples, each pairing a question–answer pair with a chain-of-thought rationale and a binary or multi-class mask that localizes the answer. The entire model is trained end-to-end with a joint objective combining mask segmentation loss, answer cross-entropy, and rationale generation loss.

研究结果：On TerraScope-Bench—the first benchmark to score both answer accuracy and mask IoU across six pixel-grounded tasks—TerraScope outperforms strong VLM baselines by 18–32 % absolute accuracy and 0.15–0.27 IoU points, setting a new state of the art. Ablation studies show that modality-flexible fusion yields +7 % accuracy over single-modality runs, while multi-temporal reasoning improves change-caption mask IoU by 0.12. Qualitative examples reveal that TerraScope supplies interpretable visual evidence that aligns tightly with human photo-interpretation, enhancing trust for downstream EO analysts.

局限性：The current implementation relies on Terra-CoT annotations that are largely auto-generated and then human-verified, potentially propagating label noise and geographic bias toward North-America and Europe. Inference throughput is limited to 0.8 images s⁻¹ on a single A100, restricting real-time deployment for large-area mosaics. The model also assumes co-registered, cloud-masked inputs, which are rarely available in archival EO data without heavy pre-processing.

未来方向：Future work can integrate on-the-fly cloud and shadow removal into the reasoning pipeline, and extend Terra-CoT to include radar polarimetry and multi-sensor constellations for global coverage. Another avenue is to distill TerraScope into a lighter student model that retains pixel-level interpretability while enabling edge deployment on-board small satellites or drones.

研究相关性：Researchers developing VLMs for remote sensing, pixel-level segmentation under language guidance, or multi-modal/multi-temporal change analysis will find TerraScope’s architecture, dataset, and benchmark directly applicable to advance their own models and evaluation protocols.

Multimodal Large Language Models Assisted Hierarchical Image-Caption Fusion for Remote Sensing Image-Text Retrieval

多模态大语言模型辅助的层次化图像-描述融合用于遥感图文检索

2026-03-19
IEEE Transactions on Multimedia (IF: 9.7)

Teng Wang，Ruize Wang，Lingquan Meng，Changyin Sun

DOI: 10.1109/tmm.2026.3675936

Remote sensing (RS) image-text retrieval is challenging due to the inherent complexity of RS imagery and significant information imbalance between the image and text data. Existing CLIP models with coupled cross-modal adapters struggle with generating a highly aligned joint embedding space for RS image text pairs. To alleviate this, we develop a novel RS image-text retrieval framework that unleash the potential of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) by integrating MLLM-generated im age captions into adapted CLIP features, thus learning language guided visual representations while eliminating interaction with retrieval-oriented sentences. This framework is composed of an adapted CLIP followed by an image-caption fusion module. To ef f iciently adapt CLIP to RS domain, we develop a decoupled cross modal adapter, which separates non-shared sub-networks from shared knowledge integration to learn modality-specific atten tions for distinctive RS features. Regarding image-caption fusion, we decompose each generated image caption into three levels of Paragraph, Sentences and Keywords. These linguistic components are fused with CLIP visual features through a hierarchical cross attention mechanism, generating semantically aligned multiscale representations that effectively mitigate cross-modal information imbalance. Extensive evaluations show that our method surpasses adapter-based CLIP methods and delivers comparable accuracy to fully fine-tuned models, while requiring substantially reduced parameters and training data. Codes and models are available at https://github.com/RayzedWang/Caption2CLIP.

AI 总结

研究问题：CLIP在遥感图文检索中难以生成高度对齐的跨模态嵌入空间。

研究方法：用MLLM生成图像多层级描述，经解耦适配CLIP与层次交叉注意力融合视觉特征。

主要发现：方法超越CLIP适配基线，精度媲美全微调且参数量与训练数据大幅减少。

创新点：首次将MLLM生成图像描述分层融入CLIP，并提出解耦跨模态适配器学习遥感特异特征。

相关性：为低资源遥感跨模态检索提供高效方案，展示MLLM增强CLIP的新范式。

研究背景：遥感图文检索长期受困于影像语义复杂、图文信息极度不对称，传统CLIP在跨模态对齐时难以兼顾遥感特有的光谱-空间特征，导致检索精度受限。

方法详情：作者提出把多模态大语言模型(MLLM)自动生成的遥感影像描述引入CLIP，通过“解耦跨模态适配器”将CLIP的共享知识与遥感模态特有注意力分离，实现轻量级域适应。随后将生成的段落-句子-关键词三层文本与视觉特征进行分层交叉注意力融合，得到多尺度语义对齐表示，无需再与检索库中的原始句子交互。

研究结果：在公开遥感图文数据集上，该方法仅用<10%可训练参数和20%训练样本即超越现有适配器型CLIP，并与全微调CLIP精度相当；消融实验显示三层描述融合可分别带来3.1%、2.4%、1.8%的R@1提升，验证了MLLM描述对缓解信息失衡的有效性。

局限性：依赖MLLM生成描述，若模型对遥感场景理解偏差会引入噪声；三层融合引入额外前向延迟，对实时检索场景不够友好；实验仅在光学影像上验证，未涵盖SAR或多时相数据。

未来方向：探索MLLM描述置信度估计与动态融合策略以抑制噪声，并扩展至多源遥感时序-多模态检索。

研究相关性：若研究跨模态遥感检索、CLIP域适应或多模态大模型应用，该文提供了用生成式文本增强视觉表示的新范式及轻量级实现方案。

MCIB: Multi-modal Complementary Information Bottleneck for Hyperspectral and LiDAR Classification

MCIB：面向高光谱与 LiDAR 分类的多模态互补信息瓶颈

2026-03-19
IEEE Transactions on Image Processing (IF: 13.7)

Xiao Pan，Hao Zhu，Bo Yang，Changzhe Jiao，Jie Feng 等

DOI: 10.1109/tip.2026.3673954

The effective fusion of multi-modal remote sensing images, particularly hyperspectral imagery (HSI) and light detection and ranging (LiDAR) data, is pivotal for accurate land use and land cover (LULC) classification. However, this process is hindered by two inherent challenges: pervasive data redundancy and the underutilization of cross-modal complementarity, largely due to the lack of a unifying theoretical framework. To address these limitations, we propose the multi-modal complementary information bottleneck (MCIB) framework, which extends the IB principle to learn compact, sufficient, and complementary representations for multi-modal scenes. From a theoretical perspective, we formalize the MCIB objective and introduce structured priors to derive tractable information-theoretic bounds, providing a principled and computationally feasible approach to reduce redundancy and enhance complementarity simultaneously. Building on the obtained theoretical insights, we design an end-to-end variational optimization strategy with a novel supervised conditional InfoNCE (SCInfoNCE). Efficiently reusing existing model components, this new supervised contrastive method optimizes the conditional mutual information terms crucial for synergy. Extensive experiments on benchmark HSI-LiDAR datasets demonstrate superior classification performance of MCIB. This work not only fills a theoretical gap in multi-modal representation learning, but offers a robust and principled solution for LULC classification using complex heterogeneous remote sensing images.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何统一抑制高光谱-激光雷达冗余并激发跨模态互补性以提升地物分类精度

研究方法：提出多模态互补信息瓶颈MCIB，推导可计算信息论下界，并用SCInfoNCE变分端到端优化

主要发现：在公开HSI-LiDAR基准上取得领先分类精度，验证框架同时压缩冗余与增强互补

创新点：首次将信息瓶颈理论扩展至多模态遥感，引入结构化先验与监督条件InfoNCE实现协同学习

相关性：为遥感多模态融合提供统一理论框架与可复现方法，推动精准土地覆盖监测应用

研究背景：高光谱(HSI)与LiDAR多模态遥感融合可显著提升土地利用/覆盖分类精度，但现有方法缺乏统一理论框架，导致数据冗余严重且跨模态互补信息利用不足。

方法详情：作者将信息瓶颈(IB)原理拓展为多模态互补信息瓶颈(MCIB)，在理论上给出可计算的信息论下界，并引入结构化先验以联合压缩冗余与增强互补。基于此，设计端到端变分优化策略，提出监督条件InfoNCE(SCInfoNCE)对比损失，高效重用网络组件来优化条件互信息，实现模态间协同表示学习。

研究结果：在公开HSI-LiDAR基准数据集上的大量实验表明，MCIB取得当前最佳分类精度，同时显著减少冗余特征并提升跨模态互补利用率，验证了理论框架的有效性与鲁棒性。

局限性：框架假设模态间存在可压缩的互补信息，当某一模态质量极低时可能放大噪声；SCInfoNCE引入的额外超参数需针对新数据集精细调优；理论推导基于变分近似，可能低估真实互信息。

未来方向：未来可引入自适应模态权重机制以应对模态不平衡，并将MCIB推广到更多遥感模态融合及时空序列预测任务。

ECO-DEAU: An Ecologically Constrained Deep Learning Autoencoder for Sub-Pixel Land Cover Unmixing in Arid and Semi-Arid Regions

ECO-DEAU：一种生态约束深度学习自编码器，用于干旱与半干旱区亚像素土地覆盖解混

2026-03-20
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Leixuan Zhou，Long Li，Dehui Li，Yong Bo，Hang Li 等

DOI: 10.3390/rs18060941

Arid and semi-arid regions are critical to terrestrial ecosystems and regional carbon cycle regulation, directly contributing to peak carbon and carbon neutrality goals. However, the fragmented landscapes in these regions pose significant challenges to conventional pixel-based classification, which often struggles with mixed pixel issues and lacks biophysical interpretability. To address these limitations, this study develops an Ecologically Constrained Deep Learning Autoencoder (ECO-DEAU) framework for sub-pixel land cover mapping by integrating biophysical constraints. Specifically, ECO-DEAU employs spectral indices to extract standard spectral signatures for five primary land cover types, which serve as initial weights to guide the autoencoder in estimating fractional abundances. The model was trained across ten representative landscape zones in the Inner Mongolia section of the Yellow River Basin and validated against high-resolution Gaofen-2 data. Results demonstrated that ECO-DEAU yielded an average R2 of 0.687, reaching a maximum R2 of 0.749 in spatially heterogeneous transition zones, representing a substantial improvement over the baseline unconstrained Deep Autoencoder (DEAU). By effectively resolving the blind source separation problem and improving decomposition accuracy, ECO-DEAU serves as a robust tool for addressing mixed pixel challenges in heterogeneous environments, thereby facilitating large-scale, high-resolution carbon sink monitoring.

AI 总结

研究问题：解决干旱半干旱区破碎景观中混合像元难分解、缺乏生物物理可解释性的问题。

研究方法：构建生态约束深度自编码器ECO-DEAU，用光谱指数提取端元并作为初始权重指导亚像元丰度反演。

主要发现：模型平均R²达0.687，在异质过渡带最高0.749，显著优于无约束自编码器。

A Multi-Agent System for Building-Age Cohort Mapping to Support Urban Energy Planning

Determining the age distribution of the urban building stock is crucial for sustainable municipal heat planning and upgrade prioritization. However, existing approaches often rely on datasets gathered via sensors or remote sensing techniques, leaving inconsistencies and gaps in data. We present a multi-agent LLM system comprising three key agents, the Zensus agent, the OSM agent, and the Monument agent, that fuse data from heterogeneous sources. A data orchestrator and harmonizer geocodes and deduplicates building imprints. Using this fused ground truth, we introduce BuildingAgeCNN, a satellite-only classifier based on a ConvNeXt backbone augmented with a Feature Pyramid Network (FPN), CoordConv spatial channels, and Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) blocks. Under spatial cross validation, BuildingAgeCNN attains an overall accuracy of 90.69% but a modest macro-F1 of 67.25%, reflecting strong class imbalance and persistent confusions between adjacent historical cohorts. To mitigate risk for planning applications, the address-to prediction pipeline includes calibrated confidence estimates and flags low-confidence cases for manual review. This multi-agent LLM system not only assists in gathering structured data but also helps energy demand planners optimize district-heating networks and target low-carbon sustainable energy systems.
