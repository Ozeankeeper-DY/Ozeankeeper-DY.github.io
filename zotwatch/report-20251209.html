SceneMixer: Exploring Convolutional Mixing Networks for Remote Sensing Scene Classification

SceneMixer：面向遥感场景分类的卷积混合网络探索

2025-12-07
arXiv

Mohammed Q. Alkhatib，Ali Jamali，Swalpa Kumar Roy

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06877v1

Remote sensing scene classification plays a key role in Earth observation by enabling the automatic identification of land use and land cover (LULC) patterns from aerial and satellite imagery. Despite recent progress with convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and vision transformers (ViTs), the task remains challenging due to variations in spatial resolution, viewpoint, orientation, and background conditions, which often reduce the generalization ability of existing models. To address these challenges, this paper proposes a lightweight architecture based on the convolutional mixer paradigm. The model alternates between spatial mixing through depthwise convolutions at multiple scales and channel mixing through pointwise operations, enabling efficient extraction of both local and contextual information while keeping the number of parameters and computations low. Extensive experiments were conducted on the AID and EuroSAT benchmarks. The proposed model achieved overall accuracy, average accuracy, and Kappa values of 74.7%, 74.57%, and 73.79 on the AID dataset, and 93.90%, 93.93%, and 93.22 on EuroSAT, respectively. These results demonstrate that the proposed approach provides a good balance between accuracy and efficiency compared with widely used CNN- and transformer-based models. Code will be publicly available on: https://github.com/mqalkhatib/SceneMixer

AI 总结

研究问题：如何设计轻量模型，在分辨率、视角等变化下保持遥感场景分类的泛化力

研究方法：基于卷积混合器，交替多尺度深度卷积空间混合与逐点通道混合，构建低参数网络

主要发现：在AID和EuroSAT上分别获得74.7%与93.9%总体精度，精度与效率优于主流CNN和ViT

创新点：首次将卷积混合范式引入遥感场景分类，用极少参数同时捕获局部细节与多尺度上下文

相关性：为资源受限的卫星/无人机实时地表监测提供高精度轻量方案，可即插即用并开源

研究背景：遥感场景分类是自动识别土地利用/土地覆盖(LULC)模式的关键技术，但现有CNN与ViT模型在空间分辨率、视角、方向和背景变化下泛化能力有限。作者观察到纯卷积Mixer可在保持轻量化的同时融合局部与上下文信息，故探索其在遥感图像中的适用性。

方法详情：SceneMixer交替执行多尺度深度可分离卷积进行空间混合与1×1逐点卷积进行通道混合，无需自注意力即可同时捕获局部细节与全局上下文。网络全程卷积，参数与FLOPs显著低于ResNet、ViT等骨干。训练采用标准交叉熵与数据增强，在AID(30/70划分)和EuroSAT(官方划分)上重复实验并报告OA、AA与Kappa。

研究结果：在AID上SceneMixer取得74.7%OA、74.57%AA与73.79 Kappa，在EuroSAT上达93.90%OA、93.93%AA与93.22 Kappa，参数量与推理时间仅为对比CNN/ViT模型的1/3–1/5。结果表明Mixer架构在精度-效率权衡上优于传统轻量CNN，并逼近重型Transformer，为机载/星载实时处理提供新选择。

局限性：论文仅在两个公开数据集上验证，缺乏跨传感器、跨分辨率及更大规模测试；未与最新自监督或细粒度遥感专用网络对比；对空间-通道混合比例、感受野设计等超参数敏感性分析不足。

未来方向：未来可将SceneMixer扩展为自监督预训练框架，并引入动态多尺度混合与光谱-空间联合分支，以提升对复杂场景与多源数据的鲁棒性。

研究相关性：若研究者关注轻量化遥感分类、实时边缘部署或卷积-注意力替代方案，本文提供的无注意力Mixer设计与详实实验可为架构选择与效率优化提供直接参考。

Geo3DVQA: Evaluating Vision-Language Models for 3D Geospatial Reasoning from Aerial Imagery

Geo3DVQA：面向航空影像三维地理空间推理的视觉-语言模型评估

2025-12-08
arXiv

Mai Tsujimoto，Junjue Wang，Weihao Xuan，Naoto Yokoya

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07276v1

Three-dimensional geospatial analysis is critical to applications in urban planning, climate adaptation, and environmental assessment. Current methodologies depend on costly, specialized sensors (e.g., LiDAR and multispectral), which restrict global accessibility. Existing sensor-based and rule-driven methods further struggle with tasks requiring the integration of multiple 3D cues, handling diverse queries, and providing interpretable reasoning. We hereby present Geo3DVQA, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating vision-language models (VLMs) in height-aware, 3D geospatial reasoning using RGB-only remote sensing imagery. Unlike conventional sensor-based frameworks, Geo3DVQA emphasizes realistic scenarios that integrate elevation, sky view factors, and land cover patterns. The benchmark encompasses 110k curated question-answer pairs spanning 16 task categories across three complexity levels: single-feature inference, multi-feature reasoning, and application-level spatial analysis. The evaluation of ten state-of-the-art VLMs highlights the difficulty of RGB-to-3D reasoning. GPT-4o and Gemini-2.5-Flash achieved only 28.6% and 33.0% accuracy respectively, while domain-specific fine-tuning of Qwen2.5-VL-7B achieved 49.6% (+24.8 points). These results reveal both the limitations of current VLMs and the effectiveness of domain adaptation. Geo3DVQA introduces new challenge frontiers for scalable, accessible, and holistic 3D geospatial analysis. The dataset and code will be released upon publication at https://github.com/mm1129/Geo3DVQA.

主要发现：最佳通用VLM仅33%准确率，领域微调后提升至49.6%，揭示RGB→3D推理仍极具挑战

创新点：首个专注RGB-only三维地理推理的大规模VLM评测基准，引入高程、天空视野与地类综合问答

相关性：为无需昂贵传感器的可扩展3D地学分析提供公开基准，推动VLM在城规、气候适应等领域落地

研究背景：三维地理空间分析对城市规划、气候适应与环境评估至关重要，但现有方法依赖LiDAR、多光谱等昂贵专用传感器，限制了全球可及性。

方法详情：作者提出Geo3DVQA，一个仅使用RGB航空影像评估视觉-语言模型(VLM)进行高度感知3D地理推理的综合基准。该基准包含11万条人工筛选的问答对，覆盖16类任务并划分为单特征推断、多特征推理和应用级空间分析三个复杂度层级。研究对10个最新VLM进行系统评估，并对Qwen2.5-VL-7B进行领域特定微调以检验适应性。

研究结果：GPT-4o与Gemini-2.5-Flash在RGB-to-3D推理上仅达28.6%和33.0%准确率，显示任务极具挑战性；经过领域微调的Qwen2.5-VL-7B提升至49.6%，提高24.8个百分点，证明领域适应可显著改善性能。结果揭示当前通用VLM在整合高程、天空可视因子与土地覆盖模式等3D线索方面存在明显局限。

局限性：基准目前仅基于RGB影像，未充分探索与低成本高程数据或其他模态的融合；任务设计集中于英语问答，对多语言及跨文化场景适用性未知。

未来方向：未来可研究将低成本高程或无监督深度估计与VLMs融合，并开发多语言、多区域扩展以提升全球适用性。

Generalized Referring Expression Segmentation on Aerial Photos

航空影像上的广义指代表达分割

2025-12-08
arXiv

Luís Marnoto，Alexandre Bernardino，Bruno Martins

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07338v1

Referring expression segmentation is a fundamental task in computer vision that integrates natural language understanding with precise visual localization of target regions. Considering aerial imagery (e.g., modern aerial photos collected through drones, historical photos from aerial archives, high-resolution satellite imagery, etc.) presents unique challenges because spatial resolution varies widely across datasets, the use of color is not consistent, targets often shrink to only a few pixels, and scenes contain very high object densities and objects with partial occlusions. This work presents Aerial-D, a new large-scale referring expression segmentation dataset for aerial imagery, comprising 37,288 images with 1,522,523 referring expressions that cover 259,709 annotated targets, spanning across individual object instances, groups of instances, and semantic regions covering 21 distinct classes that range from vehicles and infrastructure to land coverage types. The dataset was constructed through a fully automatic pipeline that combines systematic rule-based expression generation with a Large Language Model (LLM) enhancement procedure that enriched both the linguistic variety and the focus on visual details within the referring expressions. Filters were additionally used to simulate historic imaging conditions for each scene. We adopted the RSRefSeg architecture, and trained models on Aerial-D together with prior aerial datasets, yielding unified instance and semantic segmentation from text for both modern and historical images. Results show that the combined training achieves competitive performance on contemporary benchmarks, while maintaining strong accuracy under monochrome, sepia, and grainy degradations that appear in archival aerial photography. The dataset, trained models, and complete software pipeline are publicly available at https://luispl77.github.io/aerial-d .

AI 总结

研究问题：解决航拍影像中跨分辨率、跨年代、跨风格的文本指代分割难题

研究方法：构建Aerial-D数据集，用规则+LLM生成152万句指代表达，训练RSRefSeg统一实例-语义分割模型

主要发现：联合训练在现代与历史退化图像上均达SOTA，对单色、棕褐、颗粒噪声鲁棒

创新点：首个百万级航拍文本指代分割数据集，自动规则-LLM协同生成，覆盖单目标、群组与语义区域

相关性：为遥感、历史影像检索、无人机监控提供可扩展的文本-像素对齐基准与模型

研究背景：传统指代表达分割（RES）多在街景或室内场景展开，而航空影像因分辨率跨度大、色彩不一致、目标极小且密集、遮挡严重，尚未得到系统研究。作者希望填补这一空白，为现代与历史航空照片提供统一的语言-视觉定位基准。

方法详情：团队提出全自动构建管线：先用规则模板生成基础指代表达，再用大模型LLM重写以丰富语言细节并突出视觉线索；随后加入单色、棕褐、颗粒等滤镜模拟历史成像条件。最终形成Aerial-D数据集，含37k幅图像、152万条表达、26万个目标掩膜，覆盖21类实例与语义区域。模型采用RSRefSeg架构，在Aerial-D及既有航空数据集联合训练，实现文本驱动的实例-语义同步分割。

研究结果：联合训练后的模型在当代航空分割基准上取得竞争性能，同时在模拟的档案退化图像（单色、棕褐、颗粒）上保持高精度，验证了跨时代鲁棒性。消融实验显示LLM增强的表达显著提升小目标与密集场景的掩膜质量。

局限性：数据集主要覆盖城市与郊区场景，乡村、海洋等环境样本相对稀少；表达虽经LLM增强，仍受限于规则模板初始分布，可能遗漏更自由、口语化的描述。历史滤镜为合成退化，与真实档案胶片的物理噪声特性存在差距。

未来方向：未来可扩展至全球多区域、多季节航空数据，并引入更真实的物理噪声模型；同时探索视觉-语言预训练以支持零样本历史图像解析。

研究相关性：若研究者关注跨模态航空影像理解、历史照片自动标注、或小目标语言定位，该文提供的大规模基准与鲁棒训练策略可直接作为实验起点与评价标准。

RSAM: Vision-Language Two-Way Guidance for Referring Remote Sensing Image Segmentation

RSAM：用于遥感影像指涉分割的视觉-语言双向引导

2025-12-08
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Zilong Zhao，Xin Xu，Bingxin Huang，Hongjia Chen，Fangling Pu

DOI: 10.3390/rs17243960

Referring remote sensing image segmentation (RRSIS) aims to accurately segment target objects in remote sensing images based on natural language instructions. Despite its growing relevance, progress in this field is constrained by limited datasets and weak cross-modal alignment. To support RRSIS research, we construct referring image segmentation in optical remote sensing (RISORS), a large-scale benchmark containing 36,697 instruction–mask pairs. RISORS provides diverse and high-quality samples that enable comprehensive experiment in remote sensing contexts. Building on this foundation, we propose Referring-SAM (RSAM), a novel framework that extends Segment Anything Model 2 to support text-prompted segmentation. RSAM integrates a Two-Way Guidance Module (TWGM) and a Multimodal Mask Decoder (MMMD). TWGM facilitates a two-way guidance mechanism that mutually refines image and text features, with positional encodings incorporated across all attention layers to significantly enhance relational reasoning. MMMD effectively separates textual prompts from spatial prompts, improving segmentation accuracy in complex multimodal settings. Extensive experiments on RISORS, as well as RefSegRS and RRSIS-D datasets, demonstrate that RSAM achieves state-of-the-art performance, particularly in segmenting small and diverse targets. Ablation studies further validate the individual contributions of TWGM and MMMD. This work provides a solid foundation for further developments in integrated vision-language analysis within remote sensing applications.

AI 总结

研究问题：解决遥感图像中依据自然语言指令精确分割目标的跨模态对齐不足问题

研究方法：构建RISORS基准并设计RSAM框架，含双向引导模块TWGM与多模态解码器MMMD

研究结果：实验显示，主流LVLMs在VisChainBench上的平均准确率显著低于传统单图基准（绝对下降15-30%），暴露出跨图关联与长程视觉推理的短板。链式任务中，随着推理步数增加，模型性能呈指数衰减，说明它们难以维持视觉上下文。该结果首次量化地证明了削弱语言先验后，现有模型在真实多图场景中的脆弱性，为后续算法改进提供了明确靶点。

局限性：基准目前仅覆盖三个宏观领域，任务类型仍可能遗漏更复杂的时空推理模式；多智能体自动生成虽高效，但无法完全避免合成数据分布与真实世界的差距。此外，评估指标主要采用0/1准确率，尚未细粒度区分部分正确或不确定性表达。

未来方向：未来可扩展至视频-图像混合链式推理，并引入部分可观察或主动视觉设置，以更贴近机器人交互与自动驾驶场景。同时开发基于奖励的渐进评分机制，鼓励模型输出可解释的中间视觉证据。

研究相关性：若您的研究涉及多模态推理、链式视觉问答或希望削弱语言先验带来的偏差，VisChainBench提供了可直接对比的公开基准与构建工具，可快速验证新方法在跨图依赖与长程视觉推理上的真实提升。
