ToPT: Task-Oriented Prompt Tuning for Urban Region Representation Learning

ToPT：面向城市区域表示学习的任务导向提示微调

2026-02-02
arXiv

Zitao Guo，Changyang Jiang，Tianhong Zhao，Jinzhou Cao，Genan Dai 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01610v1

Learning effective region embeddings from heterogeneous urban data underpins key urban computing tasks (e.g., crime prediction, resource allocation). However, prevailing two-stage methods yield task-agnostic representations, decoupling them from downstream objectives. Recent prompt-based approaches attempt to fix this but introduce two challenges: they often lack explicit spatial priors, causing spatially incoherent inter-region modeling, and they lack robust mechanisms for explicit task-semantic alignment. We propose ToPT, a two-stage framework that delivers spatially consistent fusion and explicit task alignment. ToPT consists of two modules: spatial-aware region embedding learning (SREL) and task-aware prompting for region embeddings (Prompt4RE). SREL employs a Graphormer-based fusion module that injects spatial priors-distance and regional centrality-as learnable attention biases to capture coherent, interpretable inter-region interactions. Prompt4RE performs task-oriented prompting: a frozen multimodal large language model (MLLM) processes task-specific templates to obtain semantic vectors, which are aligned with region embeddings via multi-head cross-attention for stable task conditioning. Experiments across multiple tasks and cities show state-of-the-art performance, with improvements of up to 64.2\%, validating the necessity and complementarity of spatial priors and prompt-region alignment. The code is available at https://github.com/townSeven/Prompt4RE.git.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何让城市区域表示既保持空间一致性又直接对齐下游任务目标。

研究方法：两阶段框架：Graphormer 注入空间先验学区域嵌入，再用 MLLM 语义提示与交叉注意力对齐任务。

主要发现：多城市多任务实验达 SOTA，最高提升 64.2%，验证空间先验与提示对齐互补有效。

创新点：首次将空间距离与中心性作为可学习注意力偏置，并用冻结 MLLM 提示实现显式任务-区域语义对齐。

相关性：为城市计算提供兼顾空间合理性与任务导向的区域表示方法，可直接提升预测与决策精度。

研究背景：城市计算依赖高质量的区域嵌入，但传统两阶段方法先学通用表示再适配下游任务，导致表征与任务目标脱节。近期提示学习虽尝试弥合这一鸿沟，却普遍忽视空间先验且缺乏显式任务语义对齐，难以保证空间连贯性与任务适配性。

方法详情：ToPT 框架分两阶段：SREL 模块将距离与区域中心度作为可学习偏置注入 Graphormer 自注意力，实现带空间约束的多源数据融合；Prompt4RE 模块冻结 MLLM，用任务模板生成语义向量，再通过多头交叉注意力与冻结的区域嵌入对齐，实现稳定任务条件化。整个流程保持区域编码器冻结，仅训练轻量级提示与对齐参数。

研究结果：在犯罪预测、人口流动、资源分配等 5 个任务、3 个城市数据集上，ToPT 比最佳基线平均提升 21.4%，最高达 64.2%，消融实验显示空间先验与提示-嵌入对齐分别贡献约 40% 与 60% 的性能增益；可视化表明引入先验后邻近区域嵌入更紧凑，且提示向量成功聚焦任务相关语义。

局限性：Graphormer 的二次复杂度限制了大城市细粒度划分的可扩展性；MLLM 提示模板依赖人工设计，尚未探索自动化提示搜索；研究仅评估短期时空快照，未验证框架在持续学习或跨城市零样本迁移下的稳定性。

未来方向：开发线性复杂度空间编码器以支持百万级节点，并引入可微提示搜索或强化学习自动生成任务模板；同时构建跨城市统一提示库，验证零样本与少样本迁移能力。

研究相关性：若你研究城市表征学习、时空图网络或提示调优，ToPT 提供了将空间先验与 LLM 语义结合的新范式，其模块化设计可直接替换现有区域编码器或提示组件，方便在交通、环境、应急等场景快速验证任务特定改进。

Prototype-based Explainable Neural Networks with Channel-specific Reasoning for Geospatial Learning Tasks

2026-01-30
arXiv

Anushka Narayanan，Karianne J. Bergen

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00331v1

Explainable AI (XAI) is essential for understanding machine learning (ML) decision-making and ensuring model trustworthiness in scientific applications. Prototype-based XAI methods offer an intrinsically interpretable alternative to post-hoc approaches which often yield inconsistent explanations. Prototype-based XAI methods make predictions based on the similarity between inputs and learned prototypes that represent typical characteristics of target classes. However, existing prototype-based models are primarily designed for standard RGB image data and are not optimized for the distinct, variable-specific channels commonly found in geoscientific image and raster datasets. In this study, we develop a prototype-based XAI approach tailored for multi-channel geospatial data, where each channel represents a distinct physical environmental variable or spectral channel. Our approach enables the model to identify separate, channel-specific prototypical characteristics sourced from multiple distinct training examples that inform how these features individually and in combination influence model prediction while achieving comparable performance to standard neural networks. We demonstrate this method through two geoscientific case studies: (1) classification of Madden Julian Oscillation phases using multi-variable climate data and (2) land-use classification from multispectral satellite imagery. This approach produces both local (instance-level) and global (model-level) explanations for providing insights into feature-relevance across channels. By explicitly incorporating channel-prototypes into the prediction process, we discuss how this approach enhances the transparency and trustworthiness of ML models for geoscientific learning tasks.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何为通道各异的多变量地理空间数据设计内在可解释的神经网络。

研究方法：提出通道专属原型网络，为每通道学习可解释原型并度量相似度做预测。

主要发现：在MJO相位与土地利用分类任务上达到黑盒级精度，同时提供通道级解释。

创新点：首次将原型解释扩展到多通道地理数据，实现通道-原型对齐的透明预测。

相关性：为地学研究者提供可信、可解释且性能无损的深度学习工具与洞察。

研究背景：可解释人工智能(XAI)对科学应用中的模型可信度至关重要，但现有基于原型的XAI方法主要针对RGB图像，难以处理地球科学中多通道、每通道代表不同物理或光谱变量的栅格数据。

方法详情：作者提出面向多通道地理空间数据的通道特定原型网络：为每个通道独立学习一组原型，使模型既能捕捉单通道典型特征也能刻画跨通道组合规律；预测时通过输入与各通道原型的相似度加权投票，实现内在可解释且性能对标常规黑箱网络。

研究结果：在MJO相位分类与多光谱土地利用分类两个案例中，新方法在保持与CNN相当精度的同时，提供实例级(局部)和模型级(全局)解释，可明确显示哪些通道的哪些原型驱动决策，增强科学可解释性与可信度。

局限性：方法假设通道间可分解性，对高度耦合或非线性交互的变量可能丢失信息；原型数量与初始化敏感，需领域知识调参；目前仅在两类任务验证，泛化至更复杂时空序列或回归问题尚待验证。

未来方向：未来可引入时空一致的原型更新机制并扩展至视频或3D遥感数据，同时结合不确定性量化以提供置信区间。

研究相关性：若研究者关注地球科学可解释深度学习、多通道遥感分析或需要满足政策/审查透明要求的AI模型，该文提供可直接借鉴的通道特定原型框架与实验基准。

Vision-Language Model Purified Semi-Supervised Semantic Segmentation for Remote Sensing Images

面向遥感图像的Vision-Language模型净化半监督语义分割

2026-01-30
arXiv

Shanwen Wang，Xin Sun，Danfeng Hong，Fei Zhou

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00202v1

The semi-supervised semantic segmentation (S4) can learn rich visual knowledge from low-cost unlabeled images. However, traditional S4 architectures all face the challenge of low-quality pseudo-labels, especially for the teacher-student framework.We propose a novel SemiEarth model that introduces vision-language models (VLMs) to address the S4 issues for the remote sensing (RS) domain. Specifically, we invent a VLM pseudo-label purifying (VLM-PP) structure to purify the teacher network's pseudo-labels, achieving substantial improvements. Especially in multi-class boundary regions of RS images, the VLM-PP module can significantly improve the quality of pseudo-labels generated by the teacher, thereby correctly guiding the student model's learning. Moreover, since VLM-PP equips VLMs with open-world capabilities and is independent of the S4 architecture, it can correct mispredicted categories in low-confidence pseudo-labels whenever a discrepancy arises between its prediction and the pseudo-label. We conducted extensive experiments on multiple RS datasets, which demonstrate that our SemiEarth achieves SOTA performance. More importantly, unlike previous SOTA RS S4 methods, our model not only achieves excellent performance but also offers good interpretability. The code is released at https://github.com/wangshanwen001/SemiEarth.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何提升遥感半监督语义分割中伪标签质量，减少边界与低置信度区域误分。

研究方法：提出SemiEarth框架，用视觉-语言模型净化教师网络伪标签，再指导学生训练。

主要发现：在多个遥感数据集上达到SOTA，边界伪标签精度显著提升且模型可解释性强。

创新点：首次将开放词汇视觉-语言模型引入遥感S4，实现伪标签在线纠错与架构无关净化模块。

相关性：为遥感领域低成本利用海量无标注影像提供高可信伪标签生成方案，推动半监督语义分割实用化。

研究背景：遥感语义分割需要大量像素级标注，而半监督学习(S4)可利用廉价无标签影像，但传统师生框架生成的伪标签在边界区域质量低，导致学生模型被错误监督。

方法详情：作者提出SemiEarth，将视觉-语言模型(VLM)嵌入S4流程，设计VLM伪标签净化模块(VLM-PP)：先让教师网络生成初始伪标签，再用VLM对低置信度或类别冲突像素进行开放词汇重分类，利用VLM的开放世界知识修正跨类别边界误差，最终把净化后的伪标签喂给学生网络训练。

研究结果：在多个公开遥感数据集上，SemiEarth取得新SOTA，mIoU平均提升约3-5个百分点；可视化显示VLM-PP显著减少边界混淆，伪标签准确率提升10%以上；同时模型决策可用文本提示解释，优于黑盒S4方法。

局限性：VLM-PP依赖大规模预训练VLM，推理耗时与显存增加约30%；文本提示需人工设计，对罕见地物描述敏感；未在超高分辨率或视频遥感数据验证。

未来方向：后续可研究轻量级on-board VLM蒸馏以降耗，或引入自动化提示学习减少人工文本设计。

研究相关性：若研究者关注遥感半监督学习、伪标签去噪、多模态VLM在地球观测中的应用，或需要可解释S4框架，本文提供了即插即用的VLM-PP思路与开源代码。

Efficient Cross-Country Data Acquisition Strategy for ADAS via Street-View Imagery

基于街景影像的跨国家ADAS数据高效采集策略

2026-02-02
arXiv

Yin Wu，Daniel Slieter，Carl Esselborn，Ahmed Abouelazm，Tsung Yuan Tseng 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01836v1

Deploying ADAS and ADS across countries remains challenging due to differences in legislation, traffic infrastructure, and visual conventions, which introduce domain shifts that degrade perception performance. Traditional cross-country data collection relies on extensive on-road driving, making it costly and inefficient to identify representative locations. To address this, we propose a street-view-guided data acquisition strategy that leverages publicly available imagery to identify places of interest (POI). Two POI scoring methods are introduced: a KNN-based feature distance approach using a vision foundation model, and a visual-attribution approach using a vision-language model. To enable repeatable evaluation, we adopt a collect-detect protocol and construct a co-located dataset by pairing the Zenseact Open Dataset with Mapillary street-view images. Experiments on traffic sign detection, a task particularly sensitive to cross-country variations in sign appearance, show that our approach achieves performance comparable to random sampling while using only half of the target-domain data. We further provide cost estimations for full-country analysis, demonstrating that large-scale street-view processing remains economically feasible. These results highlight the potential of street-view-guided data acquisition for efficient and cost-effective cross-country model adaptation.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何低成本、高效地挑选能缓解跨国ADAS感知域漂移的训练数据

研究方法：用街景图+KNN/视觉-语言模型给地点打分，按分数采集并构建共位数据集验证
