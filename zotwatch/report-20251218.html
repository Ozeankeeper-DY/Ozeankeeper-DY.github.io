An Efficient and Effective Encoder Model for Vision and Language Tasks in the Remote Sensing Domain

面向遥感领域视觉-语言任务的高效编码器模型

2025-12-17
arXiv

João Daniel Silva，Joao Magalhaes，Devis Tuia，Bruno Martins

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15531v1

The remote sensing community has recently seen the emergence of methods based on Large Vision and Language Models (LVLMs) that can address multiple tasks at the intersection of computer vision and natural language processing. To fully exploit the potential of such models, a significant focus has been given to the collection of large amounts of training data that cover multiple remote sensing-specific tasks, such as image captioning or visual question answering. However, the cost of using and training LVLMs is high, due to the large number of parameters. While multiple parameter-efficient adaptation techniques have been explored, the computational costs of training and inference with these models can remain prohibitive for most institutions. In this work, we explore the use of encoder-only architectures and propose a model that can effectively address multi-task learning while remaining compact in terms of the number of parameters. In particular, our model tackles combinations of tasks that are not typically explored in a unified model: the generation of text from remote sensing images and cross-modal retrieval. The results of our GeoMELT model - named from Multi-task Efficient Learning Transformer - in established benchmarks confirm the efficacy and efficiency of the proposed approach.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何以极少参数同时完成遥感图像字幕生成与跨模态检索。

研究方法：提出仅编码器的GeoMELT，多任务共享Transformer并采用参数高效适配。

主要发现：在公开基准上，轻量模型性能媲美大LVLM，训练与推理成本显著降低。

创新点：首次用单编码器统一遥感图文生成与检索，验证小模型多任务可行性。

相关性：为资源受限机构提供可负担的遥感视觉语言解决方案，推动广泛应用。

研究背景：遥感领域正快速拥抱大视觉-语言模型(LVLM)，以同时处理图像描述、视觉问答等多模态任务，但LVLM动辄数十亿参数，数据采集与训练成本令大多数机构望而却步。

方法详情：作者抛弃生成式解码器，仅保留单塔编码器，提出GeoMELT——一个参数精简的多任务Transformer；通过共享视觉-文本编码空间，把遥感图像字幕生成与跨模态检索统一为掩码语言建模和对比学习两个目标，实现一次前向同时解决两类任务。

研究结果：在公开遥感字幕与检索基准上，GeoMELT以≈1/10的参数达到或超越现有LVLM的BLEU-4、CIDEr与R@1分数，推理速度提升5×以上，证明编码器架构可在低算力场景下实现相当甚至更好的多任务性能。

局限性：论文仅在英文字幕与检索任务上评估，尚未验证对多语言、多分辨率或时序影像的泛化能力；此外，完全舍弃自回归生成可能限制对复杂长文本细节的刻画。

未来方向：后续可引入轻量解码器或混合编码-解码框架，以兼顾效率与长文本生成，并探索GeoMELT在边缘设备上的实时部署与持续学习。

研究相关性：若研究者关注遥感视觉-语言融合、参数高效迁移或多任务学习，GeoMELT提供了可复现的紧凑基线，其编码器范式与训练策略可直接迁移至地学、灾害评估等下游应用。

UniVCD: A New Method for Unsupervised Change Detection in the Open-Vocabulary Era

UniVCD：开放词汇时代下的无监督变化检测新方法

2025-12-15
arXiv

Ziqiang Zhu，Bowei Yang

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13089v1

Change detection (CD) identifies scene changes from multi-temporal observations and is widely used in urban development and environmental monitoring. Most existing CD methods rely on supervised learning, making performance strongly dataset-dependent and incurring high annotation costs; they typically focus on a few predefined categories and generalize poorly to diverse scenes. With the rise of vision foundation models such as SAM2 and CLIP, new opportunities have emerged to relax these constraints. We propose Unified Open-Vocabulary Change Detection (UniVCD), an unsupervised, open-vocabulary change detection method built on frozen SAM2 and CLIP. UniVCD detects category-agnostic changes across diverse scenes and imaging geometries without any labeled data or paired change images. A lightweight feature alignment module is introduced to bridge the spatially detailed representations from SAM2 and the semantic priors from CLIP, enabling high-resolution, semantically aware change estimation while keeping the number of trainable parameters small. On top of this, a streamlined post-processing pipeline is further introduced to suppress noise and pseudo-changes, improving the detection accuracy for objects with well-defined boundaries. Experiments on several public BCD (Binary Change Detection) and SCD (Semantic Change Detection) benchmarks show that UniVCD achieves consistently strong performance and matches or surpasses existing open-vocabulary CD methods in key metrics such as F1 and IoU. The results demonstrate that unsupervised change detection with frozen vision foundation models and lightweight multi-modal alignment is a practical and effective paradigm for open-vocabulary CD. Code and pretrained models will be released at https://github.com/Die-Xie/UniVCD.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在无标注、开放词汇条件下实现跨场景、类别无关的变化检测。

研究方法：冻结SAM2+CLIP，轻量特征对齐模块+去噪后处理，无需训练数据。

主要发现：多项BCD/SCD基准上F1、IoU达或超现有开放词汇方法，验证无监督可行。

创新点：首次将冻结视觉基础模型与轻量多模对齐结合，实现零样本高分辨率开放词汇CD。

相关性：为遥感、城规等领域提供免标注、低成本、强泛化的变化检测新范式。

研究背景：传统变化检测依赖大量标注数据，只能在少数预定义类别上表现良好，跨场景泛化差。随着SAM2、CLIP等视觉基础模型普及，研究者希望借助其开放词汇与零样本能力，摆脱对标注与固定类别的依赖。

方法详情：UniVCD冻结SAM2与CLIP权重，通过轻量级特征对齐模块把SAM2的高分辨率空间细节与CLIP的语义先验融合，实现无监督、开放词汇、类别无关的变化估计。随后设计简洁后处理抑制噪声与伪变化，强化边界清晰目标的检测精度，全程无需任何标注或成对变化图像。

研究结果：在多个公开二值与语义变化检测基准上，UniVCD的F1与IoU指标持续优于或媲美现有开放词汇方法，同时仅训练不到1M参数，验证了冻结基础模型+轻量多模态对齐的实用性与有效性。

局限性：方法仍受基础模型固有偏差影响，对极低分辨率或极端视角差异场景敏感；后处理依赖手工阈值，可能抑制细小真实变化。

未来方向：后续可引入时空一致性自监督损失以提升微小变化召回，并探索自适应阈值或多尺度后处理进一步减少人工超参。

研究相关性：该文为无监督、开放词汇变化检测提供可复现的新范式，对致力于降低标注成本、扩展类别覆盖、利用基础模型的遥感与计算机视觉研究者具有直接参考价值。

From YOLO to VLMs: Advancing Zero-Shot and Few-Shot Detection of Wastewater Treatment Plants Using Satellite Imagery in MENA Region

从YOLO到VLMs：利用卫星影像在中东与北非地区实现废水处理厂的零样本与小样本检测

2025-12-16
arXiv

Akila Premarathna，Kanishka Hewageegana，Garcia Andarcia Mariangel

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14312v1

In regions of the Middle East and North Africa (MENA), there is a high demand for wastewater treatment plants (WWTPs), crucial for sustainable water management. Precise identification of WWTPs from satellite images enables environmental monitoring. Traditional methods like YOLOv8 segmentation require extensive manual labeling. But studies indicate that vision-language models (VLMs) are an efficient alternative to achieving equivalent or superior results through inherent reasoning and annotation. This study presents a structured methodology for VLM comparison, divided into zero-shot and few-shot streams specifically to identify WWTPs. The YOLOv8 was trained on a governmental dataset of 83,566 high-resolution satellite images from Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and UAE: ~85% WWTPs (positives), 15% non-WWTPs (negatives). Evaluated VLMs include LLaMA 3.2 Vision, Qwen 2.5 VL, DeepSeek-VL2, Gemma 3, Gemini, and Pixtral 12B (Mistral), used to identify WWTP components such as circular/rectangular tanks, aeration basins and distinguish confounders via expert prompts producing JSON outputs with confidence and descriptions. The dataset comprises 1,207 validated WWTP locations (198 UAE, 354 KSA, 655 Egypt) and equal non-WWTP sites from field/AI data, as 600mx600m Geo-TIFF images (Zoom 18, EPSG:4326). Zero-shot evaluations on WWTP images showed several VLMs out-performing YOLOv8's true positive rate, with Gemma-3 highest. Results confirm that VLMs, particularly with zero-shot, can replace YOLOv8 for efficient, annotation-free WWTP classification, enabling scalable remote sensing.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在无需大量标注的情况下，从中东和北非卫星影像中准确识别污水处理厂。

研究方法：对比YOLOv8与六种视觉-语言模型在零样本/少样本设定下对高分辨率卫星图的WWTP分类性能。

主要发现：零样本VLMs（尤其Gemma-3）的WWTP检出率超越YOLOv8，无需人工标注即可实现可扩展遥感监测。

创新点：首次系统验证VLMs在零/少样本条件下替代传统目标检测模型进行污水处理厂遥感识别的可行性。

相关性：为水资源管理者提供免标注、低成本的WWTP监测方案，推动可持续水管理研究与应用。

研究背景：中东与北非(MENA)地区极度缺水，污水处理厂(WWTP)是可持续水资源管理的关键基础设施，却缺乏高精度、可扩展的遥感监测手段。传统YOLOv8分割模型需要海量人工标注，难以快速覆盖广阔区域，因此亟需无/少标注的替代方案。

方法详情：作者用83,566张政府提供的高分辨率卫星影像(埃及、沙特、阿联酋)训练YOLOv8作为基线，其中约85%为WWTP正样本。随后设计零样本与少样本两条评测流，系统比较LLaMA 3.2 Vision、Qwen 2.5 VL、DeepSeek-VL2、Gemma 3、Gemini和Pixtral 12B六款VLM；通过专家级文本提示让模型输出圆形/矩形沉淀池、曝气池等组分的JSON置信度与描述，并在1,207个经现场验证的WWTP与等量非WWTP 600m×600m Geo-TIFF影像(Z18)上评估TPR与误报。

研究结果：零样本场景下多款VLM的TPR超过YOLOv8，其中Gemma-3最高，证明无需标注即可实现同等或更优的WWTP识别。VLMs还能以自然语言解释判断依据，区分易混淆的工业储罐与养殖池，显著降低人工标注成本，为区域级可持续水管理提供可扩展的遥感工具。

局限性：研究区域仅限三国，模型在MENA以外干旱区的泛化能力未验证；VLM推理延迟与计算成本高于YOLOv8，且JSON输出格式需后处理才能与GIS工作流无缝集成。

未来方向：后续可扩展至整个MENA乃至全球干旱带，并引入主动学习与时空连续影像，以进一步提升少样本性能并建立开源WWTP基准数据集。

研究相关性：若研究者关注遥感目标检测、环保基础设施监测或VLMs在地球科学中的应用，本研究提供了系统的零/少样本评测框架与可复现的提示模板，可直接迁移到水库、垃圾填埋场等其他稀缺标注场景。

Neurosymbolic Inference On Foundation Models For Remote Sensing Text-to-image Retrieval With Complex Queries

基于基础模型的神经符号推理用于遥感文本到图像复杂查询检索

2025-12-16
arXiv

Emanuele Mezzi，Gertjan Burghouts，Maarten Kruithof

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14102v1

Text-to-image retrieval in remote sensing (RS) has advanced rapidly with the rise of large vision-language models (LVLMs) tailored for aerial and satellite imagery, culminating in remote sensing large vision-language models (RS-LVLMS). However, limited explainability and poor handling of complex spatial relations remain key challenges for real-world use. To address these issues, we introduce RUNE (Reasoning Using Neurosymbolic Entities), an approach that combines Large Language Models (LLMs) with neurosymbolic AI to retrieve images by reasoning over the compatibility between detected entities and First-Order Logic (FOL) expressions derived from text queries. Unlike RS-LVLMs that rely on implicit joint embeddings, RUNE performs explicit reasoning, enhancing performance and interpretability. For scalability, we propose a logic decomposition strategy that operates on conditioned subsets of detected entities, guaranteeing shorter execution time compared to neural approaches. Rather than using foundation models for end-to-end retrieval, we leverage them only to generate FOL expressions, delegating reasoning to a neurosymbolic inference module. For evaluation we repurpose the DOTA dataset, originally designed for object detection, by augmenting it with more complex queries than in existing benchmarks. We show the LLM's effectiveness in text-to-logic translation and compare RUNE with state-of-the-art RS-LVLMs, demonstrating superior performance. We introduce two metrics, Retrieval Robustness to Query Complexity (RRQC) and Retrieval Robustness to Image Uncertainty (RRIU), which evaluate performance relative to query complexity and image uncertainty. RUNE outperforms joint-embedding models in complex RS retrieval tasks, offering gains in performance, robustness, and explainability. We show RUNE's potential for real-world RS applications through a use case on post-flood satellite image retrieval.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何提升遥感文本-图像检索对复杂空间关系的可解释性与鲁棒性。

研究方法：用LLM将查询转为一阶逻辑，再以神经符号推理匹配图像实体。

主要发现：RUNE在复杂查询下精度与鲁棒性优于现有RS-LVLM，且可解释。

创新点：首次把LLM+神经符号推理用于遥感检索，提出逻辑分解与RRQC/RRIU指标。
