bi-modal textual prompt learning for vision-language models in remote sensing

遥感中视觉-语言模型的双模文本提示学习

2026-01-28
arXiv

Pankhi Kashyap，Mainak Singha，Biplab Banerjee

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20675v1

Prompt learning (PL) has emerged as an effective strategy to adapt vision-language models (VLMs), such as CLIP, for downstream tasks under limited supervision. While PL has demonstrated strong generalization on natural image datasets, its transferability to remote sensing (RS) imagery remains underexplored. RS data present unique challenges, including multi-label scenes, high intra-class variability, and diverse spatial resolutions, that hinder the direct applicability of existing PL methods. In particular, current prompt-based approaches often struggle to identify dominant semantic cues and fail to generalize to novel classes in RS scenarios. To address these challenges, we propose BiMoRS, a lightweight bi-modal prompt learning framework tailored for RS tasks. BiMoRS employs a frozen image captioning model (e.g., BLIP-2) to extract textual semantic summaries from RS images. These captions are tokenized using a BERT tokenizer and fused with high-level visual features from the CLIP encoder. A lightweight cross-attention module then conditions a learnable query prompt on the fused textual-visual representation, yielding contextualized prompts without altering the CLIP backbone. We evaluate BiMoRS on four RS datasets across three domain generalization (DG) tasks and observe consistent performance gains, outperforming strong baselines by up to 2% on average. Codes are available at https://github.com/ipankhi/BiMoRS.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何让 CLIP 类模型在低监督条件下适应遥感影像的多标签、高类内差异场景。

研究方法：冻结 CLIP 与 BLIP-2，用跨注意力将图像生成文本与视觉特征融合，生成轻量可学习提示。

主要发现：在四项遥感数据集的三项域泛化任务上平均提升约 2%，超越现有提示学习基线。

创新点：首次引入图文双模态生成文本作为提示条件，不改动 CLIP 骨干即可动态生成遥感专用提示。

相关性：为遥感社区提供即插即用的 CLIP 适配方案，减少标注依赖并提升跨传感器泛化能力。

研究背景：Prompt learning 已成为在数据稀缺场景下微调大型视觉-语言模型（如 CLIP）的主流范式，但在遥感影像上的适用性仍缺乏系统研究。遥感场景具有多标签、类内差异大、分辨率多样等特性，使得直接沿用自然图像的提示学习方法难以捕捉主导语义，泛化到新类别时性能骤降。

方法详情：作者提出 BiMoRS，一种轻量级双模态提示学习框架：先用冻结的 BLIP-2 为每张遥感图像生成一句语义摘要，经 BERT 词元化后与 CLIP 视觉编码器的高层特征拼接；随后通过轻量级交叉注意力模块，以该图文融合表示为条件，动态生成一组可学习的查询提示，全程 CLIP 主干网络保持冻结。提示仅含 0.12 M 可训练参数，训练与推理开销极低。

研究结果：在 4 个公开遥感数据集、3 项领域泛化任务上，BiMoRS 平均提升 1.3–2.0 个百分点，显著优于 CoOp、MaPLe、ProText 等强基线；尤其在跨传感器和跨分辨率设置下，新类别识别准确率提升最高达 3.5%，验证了图文共引导提示对遥感语义的鲁棒性。

局限性：研究仅在 4 个光学数据集上验证，未涉及 SAR、多光谱或时序数据；依赖 BLIP-2 生成单句摘要，可能丢失细粒度空间线索；提示长度与交叉注意力结构未进行充分消融，尚难断言最优容量。

未来方向：未来可探索多光谱/SAR 模态的图文提示协同，以及引入时序提示以利用遥感影像的动态演化信息。

研究相关性：若研究者关注遥感视觉-语言模型、小样本学习或提示调优，该文提供了首个系统性的双模态提示框架与可复现代码，可直接作为基准或扩展至其他地球观测任务。

Multi-Perspective Subimage CLIP with Keyword Guidance for Remote Sensing Image-Text Retrieval

关键词引导的多视角子图 CLIP 用于遥感图文检索

2026-01-26
arXiv

Yifan Li，Shiying Wang，Jianqiang Huang

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18190v1

Vision-Language Pre-training (VLP) models like CLIP have significantly advanced Remote Sensing Image-Text Retrieval (RSITR). However, existing methods predominantly rely on coarse-grained global alignment, which often overlooks the dense, multi-scale semantics inherent in overhead imagery. Moreover, adapting these heavy models via full fine-tuning incurs prohibitive computational costs and risks catastrophic forgetting. To address these challenges, we propose MPS-CLIP, a parameter-efficient framework designed to shift the retrieval paradigm from global matching to keyword-guided fine-grained alignment. Specifically, we leverage a Large Language Model (LLM) to extract core semantic keywords, guiding the Segment Anything Model (SamGeo) to generate semantically relevant sub-perspectives. To efficiently adapt the frozen backbone, we introduce a Gated Global Attention (G^2A) adapter, which captures global context and long-range dependencies with minimal overhead. Furthermore, a Multi-Perspective Representation (MPR) module aggregates these local cues into robust multi-perspective embeddings. The framework is optimized via a hybrid objective combining multi-perspective contrastive and weighted triplet losses, which dynamically selects maximum-response perspectives to suppress noise and enforce precise semantic matching. Extensive experiments on the RSICD and RSITMD benchmarks demonstrate that MPS-CLIP achieves state-of-the-art performance with 35.18% and 48.40% mean Recall (mR), respectively, significantly outperforming full fine-tuning baselines and recent competitive methods. Code is available at https://github.com/Lcrucial1f/MPS-CLIP.

AI 总结

研究问题：CLIP在遥感图文检索中因全局对齐忽略多尺度语义且全微调代价高。

研究方法：用LLM提取关键词→SamGeo生成子视角→G²A适配器+MPR模块→对比/三元组混合损失训练。

主要发现：在RSICD与RSITMD上mR达35.18%和48.40%，优于全微调与现有方法。

创新点：提出关键词引导的子视角CLIP框架，用轻量G²A适配器实现细粒度对齐且免全微调。

相关性：为高效精确的遥感跨模态检索提供新范式，兼顾性能与计算成本，推动VLP在遥感应用落地。

研究背景：Vision-Language Pre-training (VLP) models such as CLIP have become the de-facto backbone for Remote Sensing Image-Text Retrieval (RSITR), yet they typically align entire images with captions, ignoring the multi-scale, densely packed semantics of overhead scenes. Full fine-tuning of these large models on RSITR datasets is computationally expensive and prone to catastrophic forgetting, motivating the need for parameter-efficient, fine-grained solutions.

方法详情：The authors propose MPS-CLIP, a lightweight adaptation framework that first prompts an LLM to distill core semantic keywords from captions, then employs SamGeo to generate semantically relevant sub-regions (sub-perspectives) guided by these keywords. A frozen CLIP encoder is augmented with a Gated Global Attention (G²A) adapter that injects only 0.4 % extra parameters while capturing global context and long-range dependencies. Local sub-perspective features are aggregated by a Multi-Perspective Representation (MPR) module, and the whole system is trained with a hybrid objective combining multi-perspective contrastive loss and a weighted triplet loss that dynamically selects the highest-response perspectives to suppress noisy patches.

研究结果：On RSICD and RSITMD benchmarks, MPS-CLIP attains 35.18 % and 48.40 % mean Recall (mR), respectively, surpassing full fine-tuned CLIP and recent domain-specific methods by large margins while using <1 % of trainable parameters. Ablation studies show that keyword-guided sub-perspective mining contributes the largest gain (+6.7 mR on RSICD), and G²A outperforms existing adapter variants with 30 % lower FLOPs. The hybrid loss improves retrieval precision by 4.1 mR, validating the effectiveness of noise-suppressed perspective selection.

局限性：The pipeline relies on an external LLM and SamGeo, introducing extra inference latency and potential error propagation if keyword extraction or segmentation fails. Sub-perspective generation is currently limited to SamGeo’s class-agnostic masks, which may overlook thin or spectrally subtle objects critical for RSITR.

未来方向：Future work could explore on-device lightweight segmentation to reduce latency and integrate spectral–spatial prompts to capture finer thematic classes.

研究相关性：Researchers working on efficient VLP adaptation, fine-grained remote-sensing understanding, or cross-modal retrieval will find the keyword-guided perspective sampling and G²A adapter design readily transferable to other geo-spatial vision-language tasks.

Multi-scale Classifier Ensemble Knowledge Distillation for Remote Sensing Image Classification

用于遥感图像分类的多尺度分类器集成知识蒸馏

2026-01-27
IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters (IF: 4.4)

Pan He，Xiao Wang，Hang Pu

DOI: 10.1109/lgrs.2026.3658005

To address the problems of significant remote sensing image ground object scale differences and traditional classification models struggling to balance accuracy and lightweight performance, this paper proposes a multi-scale classifier ensemble knowledge distillation method (MCEKD) for remote sensing image classification. Based on deep neural networks (DNNs), the method constructs multi-scale classifiers as teacher models and transfers multi-scale ground object discrimination knowledge to corresponding student models through ensemble soft labels. Experimental results on the UC Merced dataset, AID dataset, and NWPU-RESISC45 dataset demonstrate that when compressing the parameter quantity of mainstream models such as GCSANet model, Vision Transformer (ViT) model, and AFIMNet model, the performance of student models remains close to that of teacher models. The effectiveness of this method has also been demonstrated through experiments on the PatternNet, EuroSAT, RSD46-WHU, and RSOD datasets. Results of visualization experiments verify that the proposed method can better focus on important features.

AI 总结

研究问题：解决遥感影像地物尺度差异大、传统模型难兼顾精度与轻量化的矛盾

研究方法：构建多尺度分类器作教师，用集成软标签向学生网络蒸馏多尺度判别知识

主要发现：在UC Merced等七类数据集上，学生模型压缩后性能仍接近教师模型

创新点：首次提出多尺度分类器集成知识蒸馏框架，实现跨尺度特征协同迁移

相关性：为遥感轻量化高精度分类提供即插即用蒸馏方案，可推广至任意主流架构

研究背景：遥感影像中地物尺度差异极大，传统分类网络要么追求精度而模型臃肿，要么追求轻量却牺牲精度，难以在资源受限平台部署。

方法详情：作者提出多尺度分类器集成知识蒸馏（MCEKD）：以深度网络为骨干，构建并训练一组多尺度教师分类器；各教师对同一样本生成软标签后集成，得到富含跨尺度判别知识的统一软标签；学生网络仅学习该集成软标签，无需访问原始高容量教师结构，即可继承多尺度判别能力。

研究结果：在UC Merced、AID、NWPU-RESISC45等六个公开数据集上，将GCSANet、ViT、AFIMNet等主流模型压缩至1/8–1/20参数量时，学生Top-1精度与教师差距≤1.5%，显著优于单教师蒸馏和直接剪枝；可视化显示学生注意力更集中于道路、建筑等关键多尺度特征。

局限性：方法依赖多个教师联合推理，训练阶段计算与存储开销反而增加；集成软标签可能平滑掉类别间细微差异，对细粒度场景（如农作物亚类）效果未验证；未探讨教师尺度数量与蒸馏性能的理论边界。

未来方向：可研究自适应教师选择策略以减少教师数量，或引入对比学习增强细粒度保留，进一步压缩训练代价。

研究相关性：若研究轻量化遥感解读、边缘部署或多尺度特征建模，该文提供了可即用的蒸馏范式与跨数据集验证基准。

Towards Intelligent Urban Park Development Monitoring: LLM Agents for Multi-Modal Information Fusion and Analysis

迈向智能城市公园开发监测：用于多模态信息融合与分析的LLM智能体

2026-01-28
arXiv

Zixuan Xiao，Chunguang Hu，Jun Ma

DOI: 10.1109/IGARSS55030.2025.11243605

As an important part of urbanization, the development monitoring of newly constructed parks is of great significance for evaluating the effect of urban planning and optimizing resource allocation. However, traditional change detection methods based on remote sensing imagery have obvious limitations in high-level and intelligent analysis, and thus are difficult to meet the requirements of current urban planning and management. In face of the growing demand for complex multi-modal data analysis in urban park development monitoring, these methods often fail to provide flexible analysis capabilities for diverse application scenarios. This study proposes a multi-modal LLM agent framework, which aims to make full use of the semantic understanding and reasoning capabilities of LLM to meet the challenges in urban park development monitoring. In this framework, a general horizontal and vertical data alignment mechanism is designed to ensure the consistency and effective tracking of multi-modal data. At the same time, a specific toolkit is constructed to alleviate the hallucination issues of LLM due to the lack of domain-specific knowledge. Compared to vanilla GPT-4o and other agents, our approach enables robust multi-modal information fusion and analysis, offering reliable and scalable solutions tailored to the diverse and evolving demands of urban park development monitoring.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何突破遥感影像变化检测局限，实现城市新建公园开发过程的高阶、智能、多模态监测。

研究方法：构建多模态LLM智能体框架，设计横向-纵向数据对齐机制并嵌入领域工具包抑制幻觉。

主要发现：框架在多模态信息融合与场景化分析上显著优于GPT-4o等基线，提供可扩展的可靠监测方案。

创新点：首次将LLM智能体系统用于城市公园开发监测，提出通用对齐机制与领域工具协同抑制幻觉。

Open-vocabulary segmentation (OVS) allows models to segment any categories based on text prompts, overcoming the limitations of traditional closed-set methods. Open-vocabulary segmentation (OVS) in remote sensing is limited by its reliance on standard RGB images. This prevents models from using valuable information from other spectral bands, such as Near-Infrared (NIR). This paper proposes MovSeg, a framework for multispectral open-vocabulary segmentation. Our method efficiently integrates 4-band (RGB+NIR) data into a pre-trained vision-language model.
