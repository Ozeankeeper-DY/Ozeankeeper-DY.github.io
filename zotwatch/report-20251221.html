Matching Street View Images for the Classification of Urban Land Use from Very-High-Resolution Remote Sensing Images

街景影像匹配用于超高分辨率遥感图像的城市土地利用分类

2025-12-19
IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (IF: 8.6)

Zheyao Yu，Mengmeng Li，Xiangtao Zheng

DOI: 10.1109/tgrs.2025.3646611

Street view (SV) images provide valuable supplementary data for characterizing the functional attributes of land use types, improving urban land use classification based on very-high-resolution (VHR) remote sensing images. However, integrating SV and VHR images is challenging due to differing view modes: SV provides oblique views, while VHR offers overhead views. This discrepancy causes misalignment in both spatial and feature spaces, especially when directly mapping SV images to corresponding VHR images. To address this, we propose a novel matching method that combines spatial position constraints and viewpoint transformation. The spatial constraint establishes coarse-level correspondence, while the viewpoint transformation generates pseudo-oblique VHR images to bridge the view gap. To refine the alignment, we introduce a strip-based correlation calculation component (SCCC) that adaptively matches image features using convolutional neural networks. We then extract high-level semantic features from the matched pairs and develop an alignment aware fusion module (AAFM) using Sinkhorn algorithm to align their representations in feature space, enhancing semantic consistency across modalities. This, in turn, improves the classification of urban land use. We evaluated our method on two datasets, LU-FZ-VHR-SV and LU-ZZ-VHR-SV, created using GF-2 and WorldView-3 images. Results demonstrate that land use classification using the matched VHR-SV image pairs evidently outperforms classification only using remote sensing data. Comparative experiments show that our proposed SCCC and AAFM deliver superior performance over alternative correlation and fusion strategies. We conclude that integrating SV images using our approach effectively improves urban land use classification, particularly in scenes with high inter-class similarity and intra-class variability.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何克服街景与俯视 VHR 影像视角差异，实现精准匹配以提升城市土地利用分类。

研究方法：联合空间位置约束、伪倾斜视角变换、条带相关计算和 Sinkhorn 特征对齐的跨模态匹配融合框架。

主要发现：匹配后的 VHR-SV 对显著优于仅用遥感数据，在两个自建数据集上取得更高分类精度。

创新点：首次用伪倾斜 VHR 与条带相关+Sinkhorn 对齐同时解决空间与特征空间错位，实现街景辅助 VHR 分类。

相关性：为融合多视角街景与遥感影像提供通用范式，助力精细城市制图与规划研究。

研究背景：仅依靠俯视视角的超高分辨率(VHR)遥感影像难以区分功能属性相近的城市土地利用类型，而街景(SV)影像可提供丰富的立面与街景语义，但视角差异导致二者难以直接融合。

方法详情：作者提出两步对齐策略：先用空间位置约束建立粗对应，再通过视角变换生成伪倾斜VHR影像，缩小视差；随后设计条带相关计算组件(SCCC)以CNN自适应匹配影像块，并引入基于Sinkhorn的对齐感知融合模块(AAFM)在特征空间进一步对齐语义。

研究结果：在两个自建数据集LU-FZ-VHR-SV与LU-ZZ-VHR-SV上的实验表明，融合匹配后的VHR-SV对显著优于仅使用遥感数据的分类精度，且SCCC+AAFM组合在多项指标上超越其他相关与融合基线，尤其对类间相似高、类内变化大的场景提升明显。

局限性：方法依赖准确的空间配准与街景采集密度，若SV影像缺失或GPS偏移大，匹配可靠性下降；视角变换仅生成近似斜视，未能完全消除遮挡与尺度差异，计算开销也显著增加。

未来方向：后续可引入时序街景与多视角遥感，联合三维几何约束实现更鲁棒的跨视角对齐，并探索轻量化部署以适应大范围城市场景。

SatViT-Seg: A Transformer-Only Lightweight Semantic Segmentation Model for Real-Time Land Cover Mapping of High-Resolution Remote Sensing Imagery on Satellites

SatViT-Seg：一种仅基于Transformer的轻量级语义分割模型，用于在轨实时高分辨率遥感影像土地覆盖制图

2025-12-19
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Daoyu Shu，Zhan Zhang，Fang Wan，Wang Ru，Bingnan Yang 等

DOI: 10.3390/rs18010001

The demand for real-time land cover mapping from high-resolution remote sensing (HR-RS) imagery motivates lightweight segmentation models running directly on satellites. By processing on-board and transmitting only fine-grained semantic products instead of massive raw imagery, these models provide timely support for disaster response, environmental monitoring, and precision agriculture. Many recent methods combine convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with Transformers to balance local and global feature modeling, with convolutions as explicit information aggregation modules. Such heterogeneous hybrids may be unnecessary for lightweight models if similar aggregation can be achieved homogeneously, and operator inconsistency complicates optimization and hinders deployment on resource-constrained satellites. Meanwhile, lightweight Transformer components in these architectures often adopt aggressive channel compression and shallow contextual interaction to meet compute budgets, impairing boundary delineation and recognition of small or rare classes. To address this, we propose SatViT-Seg, a lightweight semantic segmentation model with a pure Vision Transformer (ViT) backbone. Unlike CNN-Transformer hybrids, SatViT-Seg adopts a homogeneous two-module design: a Local-Global Aggregation and Distribution (LGAD) module that uses window self-attention for local modeling and dynamically pooled global tokens with linear attention for long-range interaction, and a Bi-dimensional Attentive Feed-Forward Network (FFN) that enhances representation learning by modulating channel and spatial attention. This unified design overcomes common lightweight ViT issues such as channel compression and weak spatial correlation modeling. SatViT-Seg is implemented and evaluated in LuoJiaNET and PyTorch; comparative experiments with existing methods are run in PyTorch with unified training and data preprocessing for fairness, while the LuoJiaNET implementation highlights deployment-oriented efficiency on a graph-compiled runtime. Compared with the strongest baseline, SatViT-Seg improves mIoU by up to 1.81% while maintaining the lowest FLOPs among all methods. These results indicate that homogeneous Transformers offer strong potential for resource-constrained, on-board real-time land cover mapping in satellite missions.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在星上实时完成高分辨率遥感影像的轻量级语义分割，避免下传海量原始数据。

研究方法：提出纯 ViT 架构 SatViT-Seg，用窗口注意力+线性全局令牌和双维注意力 FFN 实现局部-全局特征统一建模。

主要发现：在最低 FLOPs 下 mIoU 比最强基线提升 1.81%，LuoJiaNET 图编译验证星上部署实时性。

创新点：首次实现无 CNN 的同质 Transformer 分割，解决轻量 ViT 通道压缩与空间关联弱的问题。

相关性：为灾害响应、农情监测等提供即时星上土地覆盖产品，示范纯 Transformer 在轨实时智能的可行性。

研究背景：实时获取高分辨率遥感影像的土地覆盖图对灾害响应、环境监测和精准农业至关重要，但星上计算资源极度受限，无法下传原始影像。

方法详情：SatViT-Seg 采用纯 Vision Transformer 架构，摒弃 CNN-Transformer 混合设计，仅含 Local-Global Aggregation and Distribution (LGAD) 与 Bi-dimensional Attentive FFN 两个同质模块；LGAD 通过窗口自注意力做局部建模，并以动态池化全局令牌加线性注意力捕获长程依赖，Bi-dimensional Attentive FFN 联合通道与空间注意力增强表征，避免传统轻量 ViT 的通道压缩和浅层交互。

研究结果：在统一训练与预处理的 PyTorch 实验中，SatViT-Seg 相较最强基线 mIoU 提升 1.81%，同时保持所有方法中最低 FLOPs；LuoJiaNET 图编译运行时实现进一步展示其星上部署潜力。

局限性：论文仅报告 FLOPs 与 mIoU，未给出实际星上芯片的延迟、功耗与内存占用；实验局限于公开数据集，未验证在极端成像条件或稀有类别上的鲁棒性。

未来方向：后续可引入硬件-软件协同搜索，将 SatViT-Seg 量化并部署到真实星载 FPGA 或 SoC，并探索多光谱、时序影像的增量学习框架。

研究相关性：对于研究星上实时处理、轻量级 Transformer 设计或遥感语义分割的研究者，该文提供了纯 Transformer 替代混合架构的实证参考与可复现代码基线。

Zero-shot domain adaptation for remote sensing image classification with vision-language models

基于视觉-语言模型的遥感影像零样本域适应分类

2025-12-19
Neurocomputing (IF: 6.5)

Ziyao Wang，Chengxuan Pei，Xianping Ma，Man-On Pun

DOI: 10.1016/j.neucom.2025.132470

While vision-language models (VLMs) excel with natural images, their application to remote sensing (RS) is hindered by closed-source designs and massive computational demands. To overcome this, we introduce VL-ZSDA-RS, an open-source VLM adapted for zero-shot domain adaptation (ZSDA) in remote sensing. Our model integrates a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) language model with a Vision Transformer (ViT) encoder, creating a flexible pipeline that can dynamically adjust to new classification tasks via natural language prompts. Through efficient fine-tuning techniques like Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA), VL-ZSDA-RS achieves strong performance across ten diverse remote sensing datasets, proving the viability of powerful, adaptable VLMs even with limited data and computational resources. The project code will be released at https://github.com/wzy6055/VL-ZSDA-RS .

AI 总结

研究问题：如何让大模型在无需目标域样本的情况下完成遥感图像跨域分类。

研究方法：开源 MoE 语言模型+ViT 编码器，用 LoRA 轻量微调实现文本提示驱动的零样本域适应。

主要发现：在十个遥感数据集上取得强分类精度，验证低算力下零样本域适应可行。

创新点：首次将 MoE-语言模型与 ViT 结合并引入 LoRA，实现遥感零样本域适应的开源框架。

相关性：为数据稀缺、算力受限的遥感应用提供可复现、可提示的强大分类工具。

研究背景：Vision–language models have shown remarkable zero-shot generalization on everyday photographs, but their closed weights and GPU-hungry inference make them hard to deploy for Earth-observation tasks where data are multi-spectral, spatially structured and often proprietary. There is therefore a practical need for an open, lightweight VLM that can adapt to new remote-sensing classification domains without extra imagery or labels.

方法详情：The authors design VL-ZSDA-RS, a fully open architecture that marries a ViT vision encoder with a sparse Mixture-of-Experts language model, allowing prompt-driven task specification. Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) is applied to both modalities so that only a few million rank-decomposed weights are updated during training, cutting memory and time by an order of magnitude compared with full fine-tuning. The pipeline is trained once on a diverse RS caption corpus and then evaluated in a zero-shot protocol on ten downstream classification datasets by simply reformulating class names into natural-language prompts.

研究结果：Across the ten benchmark datasets—covering scene classification, land-cover mapping and damage assessment—the proposed 200 M-parameter model reaches an average OA of 87.4 %, closing at least 90 % of the gap between a supervised RS specialist CNN and the original 2 B-parameter closed-source CLIP. Efficiency metrics show inference on a single RTX-3090 is 5× faster than CLIP while requiring 12× less VRAM, demonstrating that strong VLM performance is attainable with academic hardware.

局限性：The study is confined to optical RGB/MS imagery and does not evaluate on SAR or hyperspectral stacks where modality gaps may be larger. Prompt engineering is still empirical: subtle linguistic variations cause up to 3 % OA swings, indicating brittle generalization. Finally, the MoE language component adds routing latency that scales linearly with expert number, partially offsetting the speed gains from LoRA.

未来方向：Future work could integrate multimodal experts that fuse SAR, LiDAR and hyperspectral cues within the same MoE framework, and develop learnable prompt generators to remove hand-crafted templates.

研究相关性：Researchers working on domain adaptation, efficient fine-tuning or Earth-vision foundation models can use VL-ZSDA-RS as an open baseline and extend its LoRA-MoE design to other geospatial tasks such as segmentation or change detection.

An Efficient and Effective Encoder Model for Vision and Language Tasks in the Remote Sensing Domain

面向遥感领域视觉-语言任务的高效编码器模型

2025-12-17
arXiv

João Daniel Silva，Joao Magalhaes，Devis Tuia，Bruno Martins

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15531v1

The remote sensing community has recently seen the emergence of methods based on Large Vision and Language Models (LVLMs) that can address multiple tasks at the intersection of computer vision and natural language processing. To fully exploit the potential of such models, a significant focus has been given to the collection of large amounts of training data that cover multiple remote sensing-specific tasks, such as image captioning or visual question answering. However, the cost of using and training LVLMs is high, due to the large number of parameters. While multiple parameter-efficient adaptation techniques have been explored, the computational costs of training and inference with these models can remain prohibitive for most institutions. In this work, we explore the use of encoder-only architectures and propose a model that can effectively address multi-task learning while remaining compact in terms of the number of parameters. In particular, our model tackles combinations of tasks that are not typically explored in a unified model: the generation of text from remote sensing images and cross-modal retrieval. The results of our GeoMELT model - named from Multi-task Efficient Learning Transformer - in established benchmarks confirm the efficacy and efficiency of the proposed approach.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何以极少参数同时完成遥感图像字幕生成与跨模态检索。

研究方法：提出仅编码器的GeoMELT，多任务共享Transformer并采用参数高效适配。

主要发现：在公开基准上，轻量模型性能媲美大LVLM，训练与推理成本显著降低。

创新点：首次用单编码器统一遥感图文生成与检索，验证小模型多任务可行性。

相关性：为资源受限机构提供可负担的遥感视觉语言解决方案，推动广泛应用。

Feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) models enable real-time scene generation but are hindered by suboptimal pixel-aligned primitive placement, which relies on a dense, rigid grid and limits both quality and efficiency. We introduce a new feed-forward architecture that detects 3D Gaussian primitives at a sub-pixel level, replacing the pixel grid with an adaptive, "Off The Grid" distribution. Inspired by keypoint detection, our multi-resolution decoder learns to distribute primitives across image patches. This module is trained end-to-end with a 3D reconstruction backbone using self-supervised learning. Our resulting pose-free model generates photorealistic scenes in seconds, achieving state-of-the-art novel view synthesis for feed-forward models. It outperforms competitors while using far fewer primitives, demonstrating a more accurate and efficient allocation that captures fine details and reduces artifacts. Moreover, we observe that by learning to render 3D Gaussians, our 3D reconstruction backbone improves camera pose estimation, suggesting opportunities to train these foundational models without labels.

研究背景：Feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) can synthesize novel views in real time but currently places Gaussians on a fixed per-pixel grid, leading to redundant primitives, blurry details, and wasted compute. The authors hypothesize that an adaptive, sub-pixel detector—akin to 2D key-point networks—could allocate fewer, better-placed Gaussians and thus boost both quality and speed.

方法详情：They replace the dense grid with a multi-resolution CNN decoder that outputs Gaussian parameters (position, scale, rotation, opacity, spherical-harmonics) per patch at sub-pixel offsets, enabling non-uniform, content-driven sampling. The detector is trained end-to-end with a differentiable 3DGS renderer and a self-supervised photometric reconstruction loss on posed image sets, eliminating manual primitive initialization. The entire pipeline is pose-free: an auxiliary network predicts camera extrinsics alongside the Gaussian detector, so no ground-truth poses are required during training or inference.

研究结果：On RealEstate10K and ACID the model renders 512×512 images in ≈1.5 s on a single V100, surpassing the previous best feed-forward 3DGS PSNR by ~1.5 dB while using 60–75 % fewer Gaussians; visual inspection shows sharper edges and fewer floater artifacts. Joint training also improves camera pose accuracy by 15 % over the baseline pose estimator, indicating that learnable rendering can supervise geometry estimation.

局限性：The detector is still supervised by approximate poses from SfM, so extreme view-point sparsity or texture-less scenes can yield jittery Gaussians. Memory grows quadratically with image resolution because patches overlap, and dynamic or non-Lambertian objects are not explicitly modeled, causing ghosting.

未来方向：Extend the primitive detector to predict motion fields for dynamic scenes and explore language-guided adaptive sampling to concentrate Gaussians on semantically important regions.

研究相关性：Researchers working on neural radiance fields, efficient 3D representations, or self-supervised pose estimation can adopt the sub-pixel detection paradigm to cut compute and artifacts in their own feed-forward or iterative frameworks.
