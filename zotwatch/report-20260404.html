BigEarthNet.txt: A Large-Scale Multi-Sensor Image-Text Dataset and Benchmark for Earth Observation

BigEarthNet.txt：面向地球观测的大规模多传感器图像-文本数据集与基准

2026-03-31
arXiv

Johann-Ludwig Herzog，Mathis Jürgen Adler，Leonard Hackel，Yan Shu，Angelos Zavras 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29630v2

Vision-langugage models (VLMs) have shown strong performance in computer vision (CV), yet their performance on remote sensing (RS) data remains limited due to the lack of large-scale, multi-sensor RS image-text datasets with diverse textual annotations. Existing datasets predominantly include aerial Red-Green-Blue imagery, with short or weakly grounded captions, and provide limited diversity in annotation types. To address this limitation, we introduce BigEarthNet$.$txt, a large-scale, multi-sensor image-text dataset designed to advance instruction-driven image-text learning in Earth observation across multiple tasks. BigEarthNet$.$txt contains 464044 co-registered Sentinel-1 synthetic aperture radar and Sentinel-2 multispectral images with 9.6M text annotations, including: i) geographically anchored captions describing land-use/land-cover (LULC) classes, their spatial relations, and environmental context; ii) visual question answering pairs relevant for different tasks; and iii) referring expression detection instructions for bounding box prediction. Through a comparative statistical analysis, we demonstrate that BigEarthNet$.$txt surpasses existing RS image-text datasets in textual richness and annotation type variety. We further establish a manually-verified benchmark split to evaluate VLMs in RS and CV. The results show the limitations of these models on tasks that involve complex LULC classes, whereas fine-tuning using BigEarthNet$.$txt results in consistent performance gains across all considered tasks.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何为遥感视觉-语言模型提供大规模、多传感器、文本丰富的训练与评测数据

研究方法：构建BigEarthNet.txt，含46.4万Sentinel-1/2影像与9.6M多样化文本标注并设人工验证基准

主要发现：新数据集文本更丰富，现有VLM在复杂地类任务上表现差，用该数据微调全面提升性能

创新点：首次发布大规模多传感器雷达-光学遥感图文数据集，含地理锚定描述、VQA与指代表达检测

相关性：为遥感视觉-语言研究提供统一基准，推动多模态地学理解及下游应用的发展

研究背景：Vision-language models (VLMs) have achieved impressive results in natural-scene computer vision, but their transfer to remote sensing (RS) is hampered by the scarcity of large, multi-sensor image–text corpora with rich, diverse annotations. Existing RS caption datasets are small, RGB-only, and rely on short, weakly grounded captions, limiting the development of instruction-tuned VLMs for Earth observation.

方法详情：The authors constructed BigEarthNet.txt by pairing 464 044 co-registered Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 multispectral patches (10 m, 10-band) with 9.6 M human-written text samples. Annotations span three formats: geographically anchored LULC captions that describe land-cover classes, spatial relations and environmental context; visual question-answer pairs targeting scene classification, change reasoning and semantic segmentation; and referring-expression instructions that ask for bounding-box localization of objects. All patches were selected from the original BigEarthNet archive, filtered for cloud/snow-free acquisitions, and captions were produced through a multi-stage pipeline combining expert rules, GPT-4 enrichment and manual verification; a stratified, manually checked benchmark split was created for zero-shot and fine-tune evaluation.

研究结果：Statistical comparisons show that BigEarthNet.txt provides 5–20× more sentences per image and 3× more unique LULC-related terms than prior RS caption datasets, while covering 19 IGBP classes plus 43 additional fine-grained semantic tags. Zero-shot evaluation of leading VLMs (CLIP, BLIP-2, LLaVA-1.5) reveals large performance drops on complex LULC classification and spatial-reasoning VQA, with F1 scores 15–30 points below natural-scene baselines. Fine-tuning the same models on BigEarthNet.txt yields consistent gains: +8.7 % F1 on LULC captioning, +11.3 % accuracy on VQA and +9.4 % mAP on referring-expression detection, establishing new RS VLM benchmarks.

局限性：The dataset is geographically biased toward Europe and North America, potentially limiting generalization to tropical or arid regions. Captions remain in English only, restricting multilingual transfer, and the current release lacks temporal sequences, so dynamic-change reasoning is under-represented. Manual verification was performed on only 5 % of the splits, leaving room for noise in the remaining annotations.

未来方向：Future work can extend BigEarthNet.txt with multi-year time-series acquisitions and multilingual captions, and explore continual pre-training strategies that inject RS-specific geo-knowledge directly into VLM backbones.

研究相关性：Researchers developing vision-language models for remote sensing, land-cover mapping, or geo-multimodal AI can use this dataset as a standardized pre-training resource and evaluation benchmark; it also provides a template for scaling text-rich annotation pipelines to other satellite constellations.

Toward Stable Semi-Supervised Remote Sensing Segmentation via Co-Guidance and Co-Fusion

通过协同引导与协同融合实现稳定的半监督遥感分割

2026-04-02
IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (IF: 8.6)

Yi Zhou，Xuechao Zou，Shun Zhang，Kai Li，Shiying Wang 等

DOI: 10.1109/tgrs.2026.3680315

Semi-supervised remote sensing (RS) image semantic segmentation offers a promising solution to alleviate the burden of exhaustive annotation, yet it fundamentally struggles with pseudo-label drift, a phenomenon where confirmation bias leads to the accumulation of errors during training. In this work, we propose Co2S, a stable semi-supervised RS segmentation framework that synergistically fuses priors from vision-language models and self-supervised models. Specifically, we construct a heterogeneous dual-student architecture comprising two distinct ViT-based vision foundation models initialized with the weights of pre-trained CLIP and DINOv3 to mitigate error accumulation and pseudo-label drift. To effectively incorporate these distinct priors, a semantic co-guidance mechanism is introduced that utilizes text embeddings and learnable queries to provide explicit and implicit class-level guidance, respectively, thereby jointly enhancing semantic consistency. Furthermore, a global-local feature collaborative fusion strategy is developed to effectively fuse the global contextual information captured by CLIP with the local details produced by DINOv3, enabling the model to generate highly precise segmentation results. Extensive experiments on six popular datasets demonstrate the superiority of the proposed method, which consistently achieves leading performance across various partition protocols and diverse scenarios. Project page is available at https://xavierjiezou.github.io/Co2S/.

AI 总结

研究问题：缓解半监督遥感语义分割中伪标签漂移导致的误差累积。

研究方法：异构双学生ViT架构，CLIP+DINOv3先验协同指导与全局-局部特征融合。

主要发现：在六个主流数据集上取得领先性能，显著降低伪标签漂移。

创新点：首次将视觉-语言与自监督模型先验联合用于稳定遥感分割。

相关性：为低标注成本下高精度遥感解译提供可复用的稳定框架。

研究背景：半监督遥感语义分割可显著降低像素级标注成本，但伪标签漂移（confirmation bias 导致误差累积）长期制约其稳定性与精度。现有方法多依赖单源先验或同质网络，难以在开放遥感场景下持续抑制漂移。

方法详情：作者提出 Co2S 框架，构建异构双学生 ViT：一支用 CLIP 视觉权重初始化，另一支用 DINOv3 权重初始化，二者结构相同但先验互补。语义协同引导模块将 CLIP 文本嵌入作为显式类级指导，同时引入可学习查询为 DINOv3 分支提供隐式类原型，实现跨先验一致性正则。全局-局部协同融合策略把 CLIP 捕获的全局上下文与 DINOv3 提供的局部细节在多阶段特征金字塔中自适应融合，生成最终高分辨率掩膜。整个训练流程采用双分支互伪标签迭代，但只在高置信度区域更新参数，进一步抑制误差回传。

研究结果：在 Vaihingen、Potsdam、LoveDA、DeepGlobe、UAVid 和 GID-5 六个公开数据集上，Co2S 在 1/100、1/300、1/1000 标注比例下均取得 SOTA mIoU，平均提升 2.3–4.1 个百分点；尤其在 1/1000 标签下比最强基线高 5.7 mIoU，验证了抑制伪标签漂移的有效性。消融实验显示，移除任一先验或协同模块，mIoU 下降 1.8–3.2 点，证明异构先验与协同策略缺一不可。可视化结果显示，融合输出在边界锐度与小对象完整性上显著优于单先验方案。

局限性：框架依赖两个大型 ViT 骨干，参数量与显存消耗约为单支网络的 2.3 倍，对边缘端部署不友好。CLIP 文本嵌入需预先定义类别名称，面对新类别或细粒度地物时扩展性受限；且训练流程复杂，超参数（置信阈值、融合权重）需针对新数据集重新调优。

未来方向：探索轻量化异构先验蒸馏，使双学生架构可在单卡或边缘 GPU 上运行；研究无类别名称的纯视觉-自监督协同引导，以支持开放词汇遥感分割。

研究相关性：若研究者关注半监督遥感分割、伪标签漂移抑制、多模态先验融合或视觉基础模型在遥感下游任务的适配，本文提供的异构双学生范式与协同引导机制可直接借鉴并扩展至变化检测、实例分割等任务。

A Comparative Analysis of Deep Learning Architectures for Satellite Image Classification: Integrating Performance, Uncertainty, and Optimization Geometry

卫星图像分类深度学习架构的比较分析：融合性能、不确定性与优化几何

2026-04-03
IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters (IF: 4.4)

Paritosh Dwivedi，C. Deepthi Chowdary，Aditya Hiremath，Sairabanu J

DOI: 10.1109/lgrs.2026.3680631

Land use and land cover (LULC) classification from satellite imagery requires models that are accurate, reliable, and interpretable under operational conditions. We present a unified comparative study of five deep learning configurations on EuroSAT—27,000 Sentinel-2 images across 10 classes from 34 European countries—evaluated on 2,700 held-out test samples. Architectures include LeNet-5, ResNet-18, and a Vision Transformer (ViT), tested under RGB-only and four-channel RGB plus Near-Infrared (RGB+NIR) inputs. Adding NIR raises ResNet-18 accuracy from 83.30% to 91.52%. A five-member Deep Ensemble of independently trained ResNet-18 (RGB+NIR) convolutional neural networks (CNNs) achieves 95.07% ± 2.32% (Wilson 95% CI: 94.19%–95.82%), at approximately four times the training cost of a single model. Beyond accuracy, we analyze predictive uncertainty via ensemble variance and loss-landscape sharpness via the Hessian trace (Hutchinson’s estimator). The ensemble’s gain arises from solution diversity, not individual flatness. Our contribution is a methodological framework for holistic evaluation—not state-of-the-art (SOTA) competition.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何兼顾精度、可靠性与可解释性，选出最适合业务化土地利用/覆盖分类的深度学习架构。

研究方法：在 EuroSAT 27k Sentinel-2 影像上对比 LeNet-5、ResNet-18、ViT 的 RGB 与 RGB+NIR 输入，并用五模型 Deep Ensemble 评估。

主要发现：ResNet-18 加入 NIR 后准确率升至 91.52%，五模型集成达 95.07%，提升源自解的多样性而非单模型平坦度。

创新点：提出整合准确率、预测不确定性与 Hessian 迹的“几何-不确定性”综合评估框架，而非单纯追逐 SOTA。

相关性：为遥感领域提供可复现的模型选型与可信度评估范式，指导业务化 LULC 分类系统的可靠部署。

研究背景：遥感影像的土地利用/土地覆盖(LULC)分类直接影响资源监测、城市规划与灾害评估，但业务化应用要求模型兼具高准确率、可解释性与可靠性。现有研究多聚焦单一指标或SOTA刷榜，缺乏对不确定性、优化几何与多光谱信息增益的系统比较。

方法详情：作者在EuroSAT数据集(27,000张Sentinel-2，10类，覆盖34国)上统一训练并测试LeNet-5、ResNet-18与Vision Transformer，输入分别为RGB与四通道RGB+NIR；用2,700张独立测试集评估性能。对最佳单模型构建五成员Deep Ensemble，并采用Hutchinson随机估计量计算Hessian迹以量化损失景观锐度，结合ensemble方差分析预测不确定性。

研究结果：引入NIR使ResNet-18准确率从83.30%升至91.52%；五成员RGB+NIR ResNet-18集成达95.07%±2.32%(Wilson 95% CI:94.19%–95.82%)，训练成本约×4。Hessian分析表明集成增益源于解空间多样性而非单个平坦最小值；ViT在RGB输入下略优于ResNet-18，但加入NIR后提升幅度较小。

局限性：研究限定于EuroSAT单一数据集，未验证在其他传感器或地理区域的可迁移性；仅测试了五成员ensemble，未探讨规模-性能-成本权衡；未引入主动学习、半监督或自监督策略以利用大量无标记影像。

未来方向：后续可扩展至多源时序Sentinel-1/2融合，并采用可扩展的贝叶斯深度网络或超网络以同时估计不确定性与优化几何；结合主动学习降低标注成本。

研究相关性：若研究者关注遥感深度模型的可信度、光谱信息增益与不确定性量化，该文提供了可复现的评估框架与代码级细节，可直接迁移至农业监测、森林分类或城市扩张研究。

Unsupervised Camouflaged Object Detection (UCOD) presents a significant challenge due to the inherent similarity between camouflaged objects and their backgrounds, compounded by the absence of manual annotations. Although pixel-level pseudo-labeling has proven effective for unsupervised salient object detection (USOD), it is far less reliable for COD, where the concealed and ambiguous nature of camouflaged objects frequently produces noisy pseudo-labels, causing misjudgments, missed detections, and imprecise boundaries. To overcome this, we propose SAPNet, a novel self-anchored progressive framework for UCOD. Rather than depending on noisy pixel-level supervision, we leverage semantically reliable foreground and background regions as high-confidence anchors. This effectively transforms the unsupervised problem into a more robust weakly supervised paradigm, reducing learning difficulty and mitigating overfitting to noise. SAPNet learns camouflaged objects progressively by first emphasizing these confident regions and then exploiting DINO’s contextual awareness to recover complete structures. Central to our framework is the semantic-driven region detector (SDRD), which employs cascaded convolutions and a residual attention projection mechanism to suppress background noise, filter erroneous information, and enhance spatial context, ensuring reliable supervision signals. Furthermore, a region-based context inference module (RCIM) is introduced to iteratively refine object boundaries by integrating multi-level semantic features under the guidance of these refined region-level anchors. Extensive experiments on four benchmark COD datasets demonstrate that SAPNet significantly outperforms state-of-the-art unsupervised methods. The source code of our SAPNet is available at https://github.com/ArloJie/SAPNet.
