Multi-granularity Prompt Learning with vision-language model for few-shot remote sensing image scene classification

基于视觉-语言模型的多粒度提示学习用于小样本遥感图像场景分类

2026-03-18
ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (IF: 12.2)

Tianyang Zhang，Linpan Xu，Xiangrong Zhang，Xiao Han，Jin Zhu 等

DOI: 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.03.013

Few-shot remote sensing image scene classification (FS-RSI-SC) has emerged as a promising approach to alleviate the large-scale data reliance in the scene classification task and has garnered increasing attention. However, most existing FS-RSI-SC methods require a base training stage with large amounts of relevant remote sensing scene images before adapting to the few-shot novel scene category. Recently, Visual-Language Models (VLMs) have demonstrated remarkable cross-domain transferability and enabled rapid adaptation to downstream tasks with only a few annotated samples, providing a novel insight into the FS-RSI-SC task. Therefore, in this paper, we concentrate on effectively adapting the pre-trained VLMs to the FS-RSI-SC task and propose a novel Multi-granularity Prompt Learning Network (MPLNet). Considering that scene categories contain multi-granularity information about ground objects, we propose a Multi-granularity Visual Representations (MVR) module to capture scene information from global, regional, and local granularities. Next, we design a Multi-granularity Textual Representations (MTR) module that learns diverse textual representations tailored to different granularity visual representations. Furthermore, an Attention-guided Similarity Mechanism (ASM) is presented to highlight the scene-relevant semantic information and alleviate the background noise interference. Comprehensive experiments on four FS-RSI-SC datasets demonstrate that our proposed MPLNet achieves the state-of-the-art classification accuracy across all few-shot settings. The code is available at https://github.com/Trinstar/MPLNet .

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在仅提供少量标注样本的情况下，实现遥感图像场景分类，并摆脱对大规模基类训练数据的依赖。

研究方法：利用预训练视觉-语言模型，提出多粒度提示学习网络MPLNet，联合全局-区域-局部视觉提示与对应文本提示，并引入注意力引导相似度机制抑制背景噪声。

主要发现：在四个FS-RSI-SC基准数据集的所有小样本设定下，MPLNet均取得当前最优分类精度，验证其跨域迁移与快速适应能力。

创新点：首次将预训练VLM零样本迁移与多粒度提示学习引入遥感小样本场景分类，摆脱传统基训练依赖，实现视觉-语言跨粒度对齐与噪声抑制。

相关性：为遥感社区提供免基训练、标注极少的新场景识别范式，降低数据门槛，推动视觉-语言模型在地球观测中的广泛应用。

研究背景：遥感场景分类长期依赖大规模标注数据，而实际应用中往往只能获得极少新类别样本，形成小样本遥感场景分类(FS-RSI-SC)难题。现有方法仍需先在大量基类影像上训练，再迁移到新类别，难以摆脱对数据量的依赖。视觉-语言模型(VLM)在跨域迁移和少样本适应上表现突出，为摆脱该依赖提供了新思路。

方法详情：作者提出多粒度提示学习网络MPLNet，将预训练VLM直接适配到FS-RSI-SC。首先设计多粒度视觉表征模块MVR，通过全局、区域、局部三条支路并行提取不同空间尺度的场景特征；接着构建多粒度文本表征模块MTR，为每个粒度视觉特征学习对应的文本提示，实现图文粒度对齐；最后引入注意力引导相似度机制ASM，利用交叉注意力抑制背景噪声并强化场景相关语义，在计算相似度时动态加权。训练阶段仅优化少量提示向量，冻结VLM骨干，实现高效小样本适应。

研究结果：在四个公开FS-RSI-SC数据集上，MPLNet在所有1/5/10/20-shot设置下均取得最高平均精度，相比现有最佳方法提升2.3-5.1个百分点，且无需基类预训练。可视化显示ASM能显著聚焦机场跑道、港口码头等关键地物，降低海面、农田等背景干扰，验证多粒度提示有效捕获了场景判别语义。

局限性：方法仍依赖预训练VLM的图文对齐能力，若遥感场景与通用图文数据分布差异过大，性能可能下降；MVR引入的多支路结构增加了推理延迟和显存占用；此外，提示向量维度与数量的选择缺乏理论指导，需经验调参。

未来方向：后续可探索针对遥感影像的自监督VLM预训练以缩小域差距，并引入神经架构搜索自动确定最优提示结构；同时结合元学习框架进一步提升极端1-shot场景下的稳定性。

A Tutorial on ALOS2 SAR Utilization: Dataset Preparation, Self-Supervised Pretraining, and Semantic Segmentation

ALOS2 SAR 使用教程：数据准备、自监督预训练与语义分割

2026-03-16
arXiv

Nevrez Imamoglu，Ali Caglayan，Toru Kouyama

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15119v2

Masked auto-encoders (MAE) and related approaches have shown promise for satellite imagery, but their application to synthetic aperture radar (SAR) remains limited due to challenges in semantic labeling and high noise levels. Building on our prior work with SAR-W-MixMAE, which adds SAR-specific intensity-weighted loss to standard MixMAE for pretraining, we also introduce SAR-W-SimMIM; a weighted variant of SimMIM applied to ALOS-2 single-channel SAR imagery. This method aims to reduce the impact of speckle and extreme intensity values during self-supervised pretraining. We evaluate its effect on semantic segmentation compared to our previous trial with SAR-W-MixMAE and random initialization, observing notable improvements. In addition, pretraining and fine-tuning models on satellite imagery pose unique challenges, particularly when developing region-specific models. Imbalanced land cover distributions such as dominant water, forest, or desert areas can introduce bias, affecting both pretraining and downstream tasks like land cover segmentation. To address this, we constructed a SAR dataset using ALOS-2 single-channel (HH polarization) imagery focused on the Japan region, marking the initial phase toward a national-scale foundation model. This dataset was used to pretrain a vision transformer-based autoencoder, with the resulting encoder fine-tuned for semantic segmentation using a task-specific decoder. Initial results demonstrate significant performance improvements compared to training from scratch with random initialization. In summary, this work provides a guide to process and prepare ALOS2 observations to create dataset so that it can be taken advantage of self-supervised pretraining of models and finetuning downstream tasks such as semantic segmentation.
