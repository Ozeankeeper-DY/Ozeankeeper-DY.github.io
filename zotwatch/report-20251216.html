VLM2GeoVec: Toward Universal Multimodal Embeddings for Remote Sensing

VLM2GeoVec：面向遥感的通用多模态嵌入

2025-12-12
arXiv

Emanuel Sánchez Aimar，Gulnaz Zhambulova，Fahad Shahbaz Khan，Yonghao Xu，Michael Felsberg

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11490v1

Satellite imagery differs fundamentally from natural images: its aerial viewpoint, very high resolution, diverse scale variations, and abundance of small objects demand both region-level spatial reasoning and holistic scene understanding. Current remote-sensing approaches remain fragmented between dual-encoder retrieval models, which excel at large-scale cross-modal search but cannot interleave modalities, and generative assistants, which support region-level interpretation but lack scalable retrieval capabilities. We propose $\textbf{VLM2GeoVec}$, an instruction-following, single-encoder vision-language model trained contrastively to embed interleaved inputs (images, text, bounding boxes, and geographic coordinates) in a unified vector space. Our single encoder interleaves all inputs into one joint embedding trained with a contrastive loss, eliminating multi-stage pipelines and task-specific modules. To evaluate its versatility, we introduce $\textbf{RSMEB}$, a novel benchmark covering key remote-sensing embedding applications: scene classification; cross-modal search; compositional retrieval; visual-question answering; visual grounding and region-level reasoning; and semantic geospatial retrieval. On RSMEB, it achieves $\textbf{26.6%}$ P@1 on region-caption retrieval (+25 pp vs. dual-encoder baselines), $\textbf{32.5%}$ P@1 on referring-expression retrieval (+19 pp), and $\textbf{17.8%}$ P@1 on semantic geo-localization retrieval (over $3\times$ prior best), while matching or exceeding specialized baselines on conventional tasks such as scene classification and cross-modal retrieval. VLM2GeoVec unifies scalable retrieval with region-level spatial reasoning, enabling cohesive multimodal analysis in remote sensing. We will publicly release the code, checkpoints, and data upon acceptance.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何用一个统一模型同时完成遥感图像区域级推理与大规模跨模态检索。

研究方法：构建单编码器对比学习框架，将图像、文本、框、坐标交错嵌入同一向量空间。

主要发现：在RSMEB六项任务中，区域-文本检索P@1提升25pp，语义地理定位提升3倍，同时保持传统任务性能。

创新点：首次把交错多模态输入与对比学习结合，实现无需专用模块的遥感通用嵌入。

相关性：为遥感研究者提供兼顾检索与细粒度推理的一站式工具，推动多模态地理空间应用落地。

研究背景：遥感影像与自然影像在视角、分辨率、尺度变化和小目标密度上差异显著，需要同时完成区域级空间推理与整体场景理解。现有方法分裂为只能做跨模态检索的双编码器模型和只能做区域问答的生成式助手，无法兼顾大规模检索与细粒度推理。

方法详情：作者提出单编码器架构VLM2GeoVec，将图像、文本、边界框与地理坐标交错拼接后一次性映射到统一对比学习空间，无需多阶段或任务专用模块。训练采用对比损失，使不同模态的同一地理实体在嵌入空间相近、不同实体相远。模型支持指令式提示，可在同一向量空间内完成检索、分类、问答、定位等多种任务。

研究结果：在新建基准RSMEB的六项任务中，VLM2GeoVec将区域-描述检索P@1从1.6%提升至26.6%，指代表达检索提升19个百分点，语义地理定位检索提升3倍以上，同时在传统场景分类与跨模态检索上仍持平或优于专用基线。单编码器统一框架首次把大规模检索与区域级空间推理合二为一，为遥感多模态分析提供了新的范式。

局限性：论文未公开训练数据规模与地理覆盖细节，可能限制在其他区域或传感器上的泛化验证。单编码器同时处理高分辨率整景与小块区域，计算与显存开销随图像尺寸线性增长，对超大幅影像的扩展性尚未验证。此外，RSMEB基准目前仅覆盖英文描述，缺乏多语言或细粒度属性表述的评估。

未来方向：后续可引入多语言文本与多时相影像，构建时间-语言-空间一致的多模态嵌入，并设计层级编码策略以支持任意大幅影像的高效推理。

研究相关性：若研究遥感跨模态检索、区域级问答或地理定位，该文提供了统一对比学习框架与评测基准，可直接对比或扩展其单编码器思路，减少为每项任务设计专用网络的重复工作。

ML-Mamba: A Dual-Stream State Space Model with Atrous Scanning for Multi-Label Remote Sensing Image Classification

ML-Mamba：用于多标签遥感影像分类的双流状态空间模型与空洞扫描

2025-12-15
IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing (IF: 5.5)

Shuai Liu，Xiangtao Jiang

DOI: 10.1109/jstars.2025.3644375

Remote sensing images exhibit multi-semantic char acteristics with abundant geographical and contextual information. Although multi-label learning methods demonstrate remark able advantages in enhancing image semantic understanding, multi-label remote sensing image classification still confronts three major challenges: inconsistent target resolution variations, inadequate utilization of label semantic features, and insufficient exploration of complementary relationships between semantic information and visual features. To address these challenges, a novel multimodal dual-stream collaborative network (ML Mamba) is proposed, which simulates human perception mechanisms by integrating visual and linguistic streams for accurate multi-category target recognition. Firstly, an AMamba visual extraction module is developed, employing atrous 2D selective scanning and global-local feature fusion to generate enhanced visual stream features that improve both representation capacity and spatial resolution. Secondly, a label-guided linguistic feature generation scheme (Graph-Optimized Language Encoder, GOLE) is introduced to dynamically produce more adaptive and accurate linguistic stream features. Finally, a bimodal collaborative decoder (BCD) is designed to focus on the most relevant information, reducing intermodal fusion noise and conflicts while enhancing complementarity and correlation between visual and linguistic streams. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves mAP scores of 96.18% and 97.46% on the UCM and DFC15 datasets, respectively, showing superior recognition accuracy compared to existing multi-label classification approaches and validating the effectiveness of our methodology.

研究方法：提出ML-Mamba双流连体网络：空洞扫描AMamba提取视觉特征，GOLE动态生成标签语义，BCD协同解码去噪融合

主要发现：在UCM与DFC15数据集mAP分别达96.18%与97.46%，显著优于现有方法

创新点：首次将状态空间模型与空洞扫描引入遥感多标签任务，实现类人类感知的多模态协同识别

相关性：为遥感图像多语义理解提供高效轻量新框架，可推广至多模态地学分析与解释任务

研究背景：多标签遥感影像分类旨在为同一幅影像同时赋予多个语义标签，是遥感大数据智能解译的核心任务之一。然而，现有方法普遍受限于目标尺度差异大、标签语义信息利用不足以及视觉-语义互补关系挖掘不充分，导致在复杂场景下分类精度受限。本文受此驱动，借鉴人类“视觉-语言”协同认知机制，提出双路协同框架以提升多标签识别性能。

局限性：论文仅在两个中等规模公开数据集上验证，缺乏更大规模、多传感器、多分辨率影像的泛化评估；状态空间模型的计算复杂度随影像尺寸呈二次增长，对高分影像的实时处理能力尚未验证；此外，标签语义图依赖训练集共现统计，面对新类别或长尾分布时可能出现适配不足。

未来方向：后续可探索面向大规模多分辨率影像的轻量化Mamba结构，并引入无监督或自监督预训练以缓解标签依赖；结合基础视觉-语言大模型进行零样本/少样本遥感多标签迁移也值得尝试。

研究相关性：若研究者关注遥感视觉-语言协同、状态空间模型在密集预测任务中的应用，或多标签学习中的跨模态互补机制，本文提供的双路Mamba框架与空洞扫描策略可直接借鉴并扩展至场景分类、目标检测等下游任务。

TerraSAP: Spatially-Aware Prompt-Based Framework for Few-Shot Class-Incremental Learning in Remote Sensing Image Classification

TerraSAP：面向遥感影像分类中少样本类增量学习的空间感知提示框架

2025-12-15
IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing (IF: 5.5)

Jiaxing Zeng，Yifeng Tan，Lina Yang，Siwei Zhang，Lianhui Liang

DOI: 10.1109/jstars.2025.3644602

The core challenge of Few-Shot Class-Incremental Learning (FSCIL) lies in continuously learning new categories with extremely few samples without forgetting old knowledge. However, the performance of general FSCIL solutions is often limited when directly applied to the remote sensing domain. This limitation stems from two unique challenges of remote sensing images, namely complex and explicit spatial structural relationships and significant intra-class heterogeneity. Most existing methods fail to adequately model these characteristics. To address this issue, this paper proposes an efficient framework designed specifically for remote sensing scenarios, called TerraSAP. To tackle the aforementioned challenges, the framework systematically addresses the unique problems of remote sensing FSCIL through two core designs. First, to overcome the limitation of lacking spatial priors, we propose a Spatially-Aware Prompt Module. It explicitly injects the inherent spatial topological structure priors of remote sensing images into deep features through deterministic position encoding and attention mechanisms. Second, to overcome the learning difficulties caused by complex visual characteristics, we design a synergistic mechanism for new-class learning and knowledge retention. This mechanism includes two core parts. First, it enhances the learning efficiency for sparse new classes through an NCAC-based prototype initialization strategy. Second, it achieves a fine balance between retaining old knowledge and learning new knowledge with an innovative Dual-Path Exponential Moving Average (EMA) mechanism. Experimental results show that TerraSAP achieves an average accuracy of 84.52% on the NWPU-RESISC45 dataset, with a significant improvement in new-class learning ability. It also achieves efficient parameter utilization with a 47.10% reduction in total parameters, a 93.20% reduction in trainable parameters, and a 27.80% increase in inference speed.

AI 总结

研究问题：解决遥感小样本类增量学习中空间结构复杂且类内差异大导致的灾难性遗忘与分类精度低问题。

研究方法：提出TerraSAP框架，含空间感知提示模块与双路径EMA机制，分别注入空间先验并平衡新旧知识。

主要发现：在NWPU-RESISC45上平均精度84.52%，新类学习能力显著提升，参数量减半且推理提速27.8%。

创新点：首次将确定性位置编码与拓扑注意力引入提示学习，并设计NCAC原型初始化+双路径EMA协同机制。

相关性：为遥感智能解译提供轻量级持续学习方案，缓解样本稀缺与空间结构利用不足的行业痛点。

研究背景：Few-shot class-incremental learning (FSCIL) aims to add new categories from only a handful of samples without forgetting previously learned ones; this is critical for ever-growing remote-sensing archives where annotating new land-cover types is expensive.

方法详情：A dual-path Exponential-Moving-Average (EMA) module is then applied: one path slowly updates the prompt pool to retain old knowledge while the other rapidly adapts prompts to new data, balancing plasticity and stability without storing exemplars.

研究结果：Ablation studies show that removing spatial prompts drops new-class accuracy by 4.7% and that the dual-path EMA halves forgetting rate versus single-path alternatives, confirming the efficacy of both core designs.

局限性：The method still requires a pre-trained ViT backbone and assumes that geometric priors can be encoded by fixed 2-D grids, which may break for oblique or non-nadir acquisitions.

未来方向：Future work could extend TerraSAP to multi-sensor, multi-resolution scenarios and integrate temporal prompts for joint few-shot spatio-temporal incremental learning.

研究相关性：Researchers working on lifelong learning, prompt-based adaptation, or efficient deployment of vision transformers for Earth observation will find the explicit spatial-prompt design and parameter-efficient update rules directly applicable and extendable to their own tasks.

Cross-modal Context-aware Learning for Visual Prompt Guided Multimodal Image Understanding in Remote Sensing

跨模态上下文感知学习用于视觉提示引导的遥感多模态图像理解

2025-12-12
arXiv

Xu Zhang，Jiabin Fang，Zhuoming Ding，Jin Yuan，Xuan Liu 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11680v1

Recent advances in image understanding have enabled methods that leverage large language models for multimodal reasoning in remote sensing. However, existing approaches still struggle to steer models to the user-relevant regions when only simple, generic text prompts are available. Moreover, in large-scale aerial imagery many objects exhibit highly similar visual appearances and carry rich inter-object relationships, which further complicates accurate recognition. To address these challenges, we propose Cross-modal Context-aware Learning for Visual Prompt-Guided Multimodal Image Understanding (CLV-Net). CLV-Net lets users supply a simple visual cue, a bounding box, to indicate a region of interest, and uses that cue to guide the model to generate correlated segmentation masks and captions that faithfully reflect user intent. Central to our design is a Context-Aware Mask Decoder that models and integrates inter-object relationships to strengthen target representations and improve mask quality. In addition, we introduce a Semantic and Relationship Alignment module: a Cross-modal Semantic Consistency Loss enhances fine-grained discrimination among visually similar targets, while a Relationship Consistency Loss enforces alignment between textual relations and visual interactions. Comprehensive experiments on two benchmark datasets show that CLV-Net outperforms existing methods and establishes new state-of-the-art results. The model effectively captures user intent and produces precise, intention-aligned multimodal outputs.

研究问题：仅靠简单文本提示难以聚焦用户关注区，且航拍图中相似目标与复杂关系导致识别困难。

SignRAG: A Retrieval-Augmented System for Scalable Zero-Shot Road Sign Recognition

SignRAG：面向可扩展零样本路标识别的检索增强系统

2025-12-14
arXiv

Minghao Zhu，Zhihao Zhang，Anmol Sidhu，Keith Redmill

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12885v1

Automated road sign recognition is a critical task for intelligent transportation systems, but traditional deep learning methods struggle with the sheer number of sign classes and the impracticality of creating exhaustive labeled datasets. This paper introduces a novel zero-shot recognition framework that adapts the Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) paradigm to address this challenge. Our method first uses a Vision Language Model (VLM) to generate a textual description of a sign from an input image. This description is used to retrieve a small set of the most relevant sign candidates from a vector database of reference designs. Subsequently, a Large Language Model (LLM) reasons over the retrieved candidates to make a final, fine-grained recognition. We validate this approach on a comprehensive set of 303 regulatory signs from the Ohio MUTCD. Experimental results demonstrate the framework's effectiveness, achieving 95.58% accuracy on ideal reference images and 82.45% on challenging real-world road data. This work demonstrates the viability of RAG-based architectures for creating scalable and accurate systems for road sign recognition without task-specific training.

研究背景：传统深度学习在道路标志识别中需为每类标志准备大量标注，而全球标志体系庞大且持续更新，导致零样本、可扩展的识别需求迫切。作者观察到检索增强生成(RAG)在NLP中无需重训练即可扩展知识，遂将其迁移到视觉任务，以缓解标注瓶颈。
