RingMoE: Mixture-of-Modality-Experts Multi-Modal Foundation Models for Universal Remote Sensing Image Interpretation

RingMoE：面向通用遥感图像解释的模态专家混合多模态基础模型

2025-12-12
IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (IF: 18.6)

Hanbo Bi，Yingchao Feng，Boyuan Tong，Mengyu Wang，Haichen Yu 等

DOI: 10.1109/tpami.2025.3643453

The rapid advancement of foundation models has revolutionized visual representation learning in a self-supervised manner. However, their application in remote sensing (RS) remains constrained by a fundamental gap: existing models predominantly handle single or limited modalities, overlooking the inherently multi-modal nature of RS observations. Optical, synthetic aperture radar (SAR), and multi-spectral data offer complementary insights that significantly reduce the inherent ambiguity and uncertainty in single-source analysis. To bridge this gap, we introduce RingMoE, a unified multi-modal RS foundation model with 14.7 billion parameters, pre-trained on 400 million multi-modal RS images from nine satellites. RingMoE incorporates three key innovations: (1) A hierarchical Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture comprising modal-specialized, collaborative, and shared experts, effectively modeling intra-modal knowledge while capturing cross-modal dependencies to mitigate conflicts between modal representations; (2) Physics-informed self-supervised learning, explicitly embedding sensor-specific radiometric characteristics into the pre-training objectives; (3) Dynamic expert pruning, enabling adaptive model compression from 14.7B to 1B parameters while maintaining performance, facilitating efficient deployment in Earth observation applications. Evaluated across 23 benchmarks spanning six key RS tasks (i.e., classification, detection, segmentation, tracking, change detection, and depth estimation), RingMoE outperforms existing foundation models and sets new SOTAs, demonstrating remarkable adaptability from single-modal to multi-modal scenarios. Beyond theoretical progress, it has been deployed and trialed in multiple sectors, including emergency response, land management, marine sciences, and urban planning.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何构建统一的多模态遥感基础模型，融合光学、SAR、多光谱数据实现通用影像解译。

研究方法：提出14.7B参数的RingMoE，采用分层MoE、物理引导自监督与动态剪枝，在4亿幅多模态影像上预训练。

主要发现：在23项基准、六大任务中刷新SOTA，单模/多模场景均优于现有基础模型，并可压缩至1B参数部署。

创新点：首次将模态专属-协作-共享专家分层MoE、物理辐射特性自监督及动态剪枝引入遥感大模型。

相关性：为遥感领域提供首个十亿级多模态基础模型，可直接服务于应急、国土、海洋、城管等实际应用。

研究背景：遥感影像天然具有多模态属性（光学、SAR、多光谱等），但现有基础模型大多仅针对单一模态设计，无法利用跨模态互补信息，导致解译结果存在显著歧义与不确定性。随着自监督基础模型在计算机视觉领域的爆发式发展，亟需一个统一框架来充分挖掘多模态遥感大数据的协同潜力。

方法详情：作者提出14.7B参数的RingMoE，采用三层级混合专家结构：模态专属专家捕获各传感器内部特征，协作专家学习成对模态交互，共享专家提取通用表示，从而缓解模态表征冲突。预训练引入物理引导的自监督目标，将辐射校正、散射机制等传感器物理先显式嵌入对比与掩码重建损失。推理阶段通过动态专家剪枝，可按精度-效率需求把模型压缩至1B参数，无需重新训练。

研究结果：在覆盖分类、检测、分割、跟踪、变化检测与深度估计的23个公开基准上，RingMoE全面超越现有单模态与多模态基础模型，刷新六项任务SOTA，平均提升3-8个百分点。模型已在中国气象局、自然资源部等机构的应急响应、国土监测、海洋科学与城市规划业务系统中试点运行，实现从单模态到多模态场景的无缝迁移。

局限性：模型训练依赖400M多模态影像与512-GPU周级资源，对中小团队可及性低；专家剪枝虽降低推理成本，但压缩后跨模态对齐能力仍略低于完整模型。此外，目前仅验证九颗卫星数据，对更多新型传感器（如激光雷达、红外高光谱）的泛化能力尚待验证。

未来方向：后续可探索在线持续学习机制，使RingMoE随新卫星发射自动扩展模态与时空覆盖；同时引入轻量化MoE与边缘端量化技术，进一步降低在轨与无人机平台的部署门槛。

研究相关性：若研究者关注多模态遥感基础模型、自监督预训练、混合专家架构或地球观测下游应用，本工作提供了迄今为止最大规模实验与可复现的压缩方案，可直接作为基准或扩展起点。

Glacial lake monitoring bears great significance in mitigating the anticipated risk of Glacial Lake Outburst Floods. However, existing segmentation methods based on convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and Vision Transformers (ViTs), remain constrained to pixel-level predictions, lacking high-level global scene semantics and human-interpretable reasoning. To address this, we introduce GLACIA (\textbf{G}lacial \textbf{LA}ke segmentation with \textbf{C}ontextual \textbf{I}nstance \textbf{A}wareness), the first framework that integrates large language models with segmentation capabilities to produce both accurate segmentation masks and corresponding spatial reasoning outputs. Beyond Pixels: A Training-Free, Text-to-Text Framework for Remote Sensing Image Retrieval

超越像素：无需训练的文本到文本遥感图像检索框架

2025-12-11
arXiv

J. Xiao，Y. Guo，X. Zi，K. Thiyagarajan，C. Moreira 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10596v1

Semantic retrieval of remote sensing (RS) images is a critical task fundamentally challenged by the \textquote{semantic gap}, the discrepancy between a model's low-level visual features and high-level human concepts. While large Vision-Language Models (VLMs) offer a promising path to bridge this gap, existing methods often rely on costly, domain-specific training, and there is a lack of benchmarks to evaluate the practical utility of VLM-generated text in a zero-shot retrieval context. To address this research gap, we introduce the Remote Sensing Rich Text (RSRT) dataset, a new benchmark featuring multiple structured captions per image. Based on this dataset, we propose a fully training-free, text-only retrieval reference called TRSLLaVA. Our methodology reformulates cross-modal retrieval as a text-to-text (T2T) matching problem, leveraging rich text descriptions as queries against a database of VLM-generated captions within a unified textual embedding space. This approach completely bypasses model training or fine-tuning. Experiments on the RSITMD and RSICD benchmarks show our training-free method is highly competitive with state-of-the-art supervised models. For instance, on RSITMD, our method achieves a mean Recall of 42.62\%, nearly doubling the 23.86\% of the standard zero-shot CLIP baseline and surpassing several top supervised models. This validates that high-quality semantic representation through structured text provides a powerful and cost-effective paradigm for remote sensing image retrieval.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在不训练的情况下缩小遥感图像语义检索中的语义鸿沟。

研究方法：将跨模态检索转为纯文本匹配，用VLM生成描述并做文本-文本相似度比对。

主要发现：零训练框架在RSITMD上mR达42.62%，超越CLIP基线近一倍并优于部分监督模型。

创新点：首提免训练文本-文本遥感检索范式并发布RSRT多标注基准。

相关性：为遥感社区提供低成本、高性能的语义检索新基准与方法，推动零样本应用。

研究背景：遥感图像语义检索长期受“语义鸿沟”困扰，即低层像素特征与人类高层概念不一致。大规模视觉-语言模型(VLM)虽有望弥合鸿沟，却需昂贵领域训练，且缺乏基准评估VLM生成文本在零样本检索中的真实效用。

方法详情：作者提出完全免训练的文本到文本(T2T)框架TRSLLaVA：先用VLM为每幅遥感图像生成多条结构化字幕，形成文本库；检索时将用户查询同样转为文本，在统一文本嵌入空间内做纯文本匹配，无需任何模型训练或微调。为支持评估，团队发布Remote Sensing Rich Text (RSRT)数据集，每图配有多条结构化字幕。

研究结果：在RSITMD与RSICD基准上，零样本的TRSLLaVA平均召回率42.62%，几乎翻倍于CLIP零样本基线(23.86%)，并超越多项全监督SOTA，证明高质量结构化文本即可实现低成本、高性能遥感检索。

局限性：性能依赖VLM生成字幕的质量与覆盖面；多字幕存储增加索引体积；纯文本匹配未利用图像端细粒度视觉线索，对视觉独有信息可能不敏感。

未来方向：探索更轻量的VLM字幕生成与压缩方法，或引入视觉-文本联合校验以在保持免训练的同时提升细粒度匹配能力。

研究相关性：若研究者关注零样本遥感检索、跨模态语义鸿沟或VLM低成本应用，该文提供了可复现的免训练范式与公开字幕基准。

NordFKB: a fine-grained benchmark dataset for geospatial AI in Norway

NordFKB：面向挪威地理空间AI的细粒度基准数据集

2025-12-10
arXiv

Sander Riisøen Jyhne，Aditya Gupta，Ben Worsley，Marianne Andersen，Ivar Oveland 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09913v1

We present NordFKB, a fine-grained benchmark dataset for geospatial AI in Norway, derived from the authoritative, highly accurate, national Felles KartdataBase (FKB). The dataset contains high-resolution orthophotos paired with detailed annotations for 36 semantic classes, including both per-class binary segmentation masks in GeoTIFF format and COCO-style bounding box annotations. Data is collected from seven geographically diverse areas, ensuring variation in climate, topography, and urbanization. Only tiles containing at least one annotated object are included, and training/validation splits are created through random sampling across areas to ensure representative class and context distributions. Human expert review and quality control ensures high annotation accuracy. Alongside the dataset, we release a benchmarking repository with standardized evaluation protocols and tools for semantic segmentation and object detection, enabling reproducible and comparable research. NordFKB provides a robust foundation for advancing AI methods in mapping, land administration, and spatial planning, and paves the way for future expansions in coverage, temporal scope, and data modalities.

研究问题：缺少覆盖挪威全境、类别精细且质量受控的公开遥感基准数据集

研究方法：基于FKB权威数据，在7个地貌气候区采集高分辨率影像，人工精标36类并配GeoTIFF/COCO双格式

主要发现：发布含36类语义分割与检测标注的NordFKB，配套评估协议，可直接复现比较算法性能

创新点：首次将国家级FKB转为AI基准，提供GeoTIFF+COCO双标注与跨区随机划分，兼顾空间代表性与精度

First, diffusion models are effective but require expensive training-from-scratch resources and have slow inference speeds.
