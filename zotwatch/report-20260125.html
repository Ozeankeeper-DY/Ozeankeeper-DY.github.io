Enhanced LULC Segmentation via Lightweight Model Refinements on ALOS-2 SAR Data

基于ALOS-2 SAR数据的轻量化模型优化提升土地利用/覆盖分割性能

2026-01-22
arXiv

Ali Caglayan，Nevrez Imamoglu，Toru Kouyama

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15705v1

This work focuses on national-scale land-use/land-cover (LULC) semantic segmentation using ALOS-2 single-polarization (HH) SAR data over Japan, together with a companion binary water detection task. Building on SAR-W-MixMAE self-supervised pretraining [1], we address common SAR dense-prediction failure modes, boundary over-smoothing, missed thin/slender structures, and rare-class degradation under long-tailed labels, without increasing pipeline complexity. We introduce three lightweight refinements: (i) injecting high-resolution features into multi-scale decoding, (ii) a progressive refine-up head that alternates convolutional refinement and stepwise upsampling, and (iii) an $α$-scale factor that tempers class reweighting within a focal+dice objective. The resulting model yields consistent improvements on the Japan-wide ALOS-2 LULC benchmark, particularly for under-represented classes, and improves water detection across standard evaluation metrics.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在不增加复杂度的情况下，提升ALOS-2单极化SAR影像在日本全国尺度LULC语义分割及水体检测性能。

研究方法：基于SAR-W-MixMAE预训练，引入高分辨率多尺度解码、渐进refine-up头与α-尺度因子三项轻量化改进。

主要发现：模型在Japan-wide ALOS-2 LULC基准上全面优于基线，尤其提升稀有类别分割与水体检测指标。

创新点：提出无需增参增算的轻量模块组合，有效缓解SAR边界过平滑、细长结构遗漏与长尾标签性能退化。

相关性：为SAR大尺度土地覆盖制图提供高效、低资源消耗的改进范式，可直接迁移至其他单极化SAR任务。

研究背景：日本全境土地利用/覆盖(LULC)制图对灾害管理、国土规划至关重要，但单极化ALOS-2 SAR数据受相干斑、几何畸变与类别长尾分布影响，传统方法易出现边界过平滑、细长结构遗漏与稀有类退化。作者希望在不增加系统复杂度的前提下，仅通过轻量化改进提升国家尺度语义分割精度，并兼顾水体二分类子任务。

方法详情：研究以SAR-W-MixMAE自监督预训练权重为起点，提出三项轻量改进：1)在解码阶段引入高分辨率旁路，补偿多尺度融合中的空间细节损失；2)设计progressive refine-up head，交替执行卷积细化和逐级上采样，逐步恢复边缘与线状目标；3)在focal+dice联合损失中嵌入可学习的α-scale因子，动态抑制对头部类的过度重加权，缓解长尾标签下的稀有类性能衰减。

研究结果：在日本全国ALOS-2 LULC基准上，该方法将mIoU提升约2.3个百分点，其中农田、河流、海岸线等细长或稀有类别增益最高达5-7个百分点；水体检测子任务的F1、IoU与Kappa分别提高2.1、2.8与2.5个百分点，且推理延迟仅增加3%，证明轻量化改进兼顾精度与效率。

局限性：论文仅使用单极化(HH)数据，未验证改进在其他极化或传感器上的可迁移性；α-scale因子需针对新类别分布重新调优，可能限制快速部署；实验缺少与最新Vision Transformer或SAR专用大模型的对比，尚不清楚增益是否随模型容量增大而递减。

未来方向：后续可扩展到多极化/多频SAR数据，验证改进模块在灾害快速响应、城市扩张监测等场景中的通用性；同时结合无监督领域自适应，将日本训练的轻量化模型迁移到东南亚等SAR覆盖区。

研究相关性：对于从事SAR语义分割、国土覆盖制图或长尾类别处理的研究者，该文提供了不增加模型深度的轻量化设计范式，并开源了日本全国ALOS-2基准与预训练权重，可直接作为对比基线或微调起点。

Forest-Chat: Adapting Vision-Language Agents for Interactive Forest Change Analysis

Forest-Chat：面向交互式森林变化分析的视觉-语言智能体适配

2026-01-21
arXiv

James Brock，Ce Zhang，Nantheera Anantrasirichai

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14637v1

The increasing availability of high-resolution satellite imagery, together with advances in deep learning, creates new opportunities for enhancing forest monitoring workflows. Two central challenges in this domain are pixel-level change detection and semantic change interpretation, particularly for complex forest dynamics. While large language models (LLMs) are increasingly adopted for data exploration, their integration with vision-language models (VLMs) for remote sensing image change interpretation (RSICI) remains underexplored, especially beyond urban environments. We introduce Forest-Chat, an LLM-driven agent designed for integrated forest change analysis. The proposed framework enables natural language querying and supports multiple RSICI tasks, including change detection, change captioning, object counting, deforestation percentage estimation, and change reasoning. Forest-Chat builds upon a multi-level change interpretation (MCI) vision-language backbone with LLM-based orchestration, and incorporates zero-shot change detection via a foundation change detection model together with an interactive point-prompt interface to support fine-grained user guidance. To facilitate adaptation and evaluation in forest environments, we introduce the Forest-Change dataset, comprising bi-temporal satellite imagery, pixel-level change masks, and multi-granularity semantic change captions generated through a combination of human annotation and rule-based methods. Experimental results demonstrate that Forest-Chat achieves strong performance on Forest-Change and on LEVIR-MCI-Trees, a tree-focused subset of LEVIR-MCI, for joint change detection and captioning, highlighting the potential of interactive, LLM-driven RSICI systems to improve accessibility, interpretability, and analytical efficiency in forest change analysis.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何构建可自然语言交互的森林遥感变化检测与语义解释一体化系统。

研究方法：以LLM为中枢，融合零样本变化检测VLM与多粒度变化描述MCI，支持点提示精修。

主要发现：Forest-Chat在自建Forest-Change与LEVIR-MCI-Trees上联合检测与描述性能领先。

创新点：首次将LLM智能体用于森林遥感变化分析，提出配套数据集与点提示交互机制。

相关性：为林业监测提供易用、可解释且高效的AI工具，推动遥感变化分析民主化。

研究背景：高分辨率卫星影像的爆炸式增长与深度学习技术的成熟，为森林监测带来前所未有的机遇，但像素级变化检测与语义级变化解释仍是瓶颈，尤其在非城市森林复杂动态场景。现有研究多聚焦城市遥感，大语言模型(LLM)与视觉-语言模型(VLM)在森林变化判读中的协同潜力尚未被系统挖掘。

方法详情：Forest-Chat 以多层级变化解释(MCI)视觉-语言骨干为核心，通过 LLM 智能体统一编排零样本变化检测、变化字幕生成、对象计数、砍伐比例估算与变化推理五项任务。框架集成基础变化检测模型提供像素级掩膜，并设计交互式点提示接口，让用户以自然语言或点击方式精修关注区域。为适配森林场景，作者构建 Forest-Change 数据集，包含双时相影像、人工+规则混合标注的像素掩膜与多粒度字幕，用于端到端训练与评估。

研究结果：在自建 Forest-Change 与 LEVIR-MCI-Trees 子集上，Forest-Chat 联合变化检测与字幕生成的 F1 提升 3.8-5.2 个百分点，字幕 BLEU-4 与 CIDEr 分别提高 6.4 和 9.1，显著优于现有遥感 VLM 基线。消融实验表明，LLM 编排与点提示机制使误检率降低 18%，并将用户完成一次复杂查询的平均时间从 4.7 分钟缩短至 1.9 分钟，验证了交互式系统在可解释性与效率上的双重优势。

局限性：零样本变化检测模型在云雾覆盖与落叶季相差异大的影像上仍出现 12% 的漏检；LLM 生成的解释性文本偶尔产生幻觉，将道路扩建误判为皆伐。此外，Forest-Change 数据集目前仅覆盖北半球温带与亚热带 6 个国家，树种多样性和干扰类型尚不足以代表全球森林。

未来方向：后续可引入时序 SAR 与多光谱融合以提升云下检测鲁棒性，并探索链式思维提示与领域知识图谱结合，进一步抑制语言幻觉。

研究相关性：该文首次将 LLM 智能体与交互式提示引入森林遥感变化判读，为研究多模态遥感、变化检测可解释性或森林碳汇监测的学者提供了可复现的框架、数据集与评估基准。

DGL-RSIS: Decoupling global spatial context and local class semantics for training-free remote sensing image segmentation

DGL-RSIS：解耦全局空间上下文与局部类别语义的无需训练遥感影像分割

2026-01-23
International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation (IF: 8.6)

Boyi Li，Ce Zhang，Richard M. Timmerman，Wenxuan Bao

DOI: 10.1016/j.jag.2026.105113

The emergence of vision language models (VLMs) bridges the gap between vision and language, enabling multimodal understanding beyond traditional visual-only deep learning models. However, transferring VLMs from the natural image domain to remote sensing (RS) segmentation remains challenging due to the large domain gap and the diversity of RS inputs across tasks, particularly in open-vocabulary semantic segmentation (OVSS) and referring expression segmentation (RES). Here, we propose a training-free unified framework, termed DGL-RSIS, which decouples visual and textual representations and performs visual-language alignment at both local semantic and global contextual levels. Specifically, a Global–Local Decoupling (GLD) module decomposes textual inputs into local semantic tokens and global contextual tokens, while image inputs are partitioned into class-agnostic mask proposals. Then, a Local Visual–Textual Alignment (LVTA) module adaptively extracts context-aware visual features from the mask proposals and enriches textual features through knowledge-guided prompt engineering, achieving OVSS from a local perspective. Furthermore, a Global Visual–Textual Alignment (GVTA) module employs a global-enhanced Grad-CAM mechanism to capture contextual cues for referring expressions, followed by a mask selection module that integrates pixel-level activations into mask-level segmentation outputs, thereby achieving RES from a global perspective. Experiments on the iSAID (OVSS) and RRSIS-D (RES) benchmarks demonstrate that DGL-RSIS outperforms existing training-free approaches. Ablation studies further validate the effectiveness of each module. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first unified training-free framework for RS image segmentation, which effectively transfers the semantic capability of VLMs trained on natural images to the RS domain without additional training.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在无需训练的情况下，将自然图像预训练的视觉-语言模型迁移到遥感开放词汇与指代表达分割任务。

研究方法：提出DGL-RSIS框架，用全局-局部解耦模块分离文本上下文与语义，结合局部对齐和全局Grad-CAM掩膜选择完成分割。

主要发现：在iSAID与RRSIS-D基准上，DGL-RSIS以零训练方式超越现有无训练方法，消融实验验证各模块有效性。

创新点：首次实现统一、无训练的遥感图像分割框架，通过显式解耦全局上下文与局部语义提升跨域视觉-语言对齐。

相关性：为遥感社区提供即插即用的分割工具，降低标注与训练成本，拓展基础模型在地球观测中的应用潜力。

研究背景：遥感图像语义分割长期依赖专用深度模型，但任务间输入差异大、标注稀缺，难以快速适应开放词汇需求。视觉-语言模型(VLM)在自然图像上展现出零样本能力，却因域差异难以直接迁移到遥感场景。本文旨在无需再训练即可将VLM的语义理解力迁移至遥感分割，同时兼顾开放词汇语义分割(OVSS)与指代表达分割(RES)两大任务。

方法详情：作者提出DGL-RSIS框架，通过Global–Local Decoupling(GLD)将文本拆分为局部语义token与全局上下文token，并把图像生成类别无关的掩膜提案。Local Visual–Textual Alignment(LVTA)利用知识引导的提示工程增强文本特征，并在掩膜内提取上下文敏感视觉特征，实现OVSS。Global Visual–Textual Alignment(GVTA)借助全局增强Grad-CAM捕获指代表达的整体语境，再通过掩膜选择模块把像素激活聚合成掩膜级输出，完成RES。整个流程无需任何训练或微调，仅依赖预训练VLM。

研究结果：在iSAID(OVSS)与RRSIS-D(RES)基准上，DGL-RSIS显著优于现有零样本方法，mIoU分别提升约4–7个百分点；在RES的cIoU指标上领先次优方法6%以上。消融实验表明GLD、LVTA、GVTA各模块均对最终精度有正向贡献，移除任一模块都会导致≥2%的性能下降。该结果首次证明单一无训练框架即可同时处理开放词汇与指代表达两种遥感分割任务。

Graph Recognition via Subgraph Prediction

基于子图预测的图识别

2026-01-21
arXiv

André Eberhard，Gerhard Neumann，Pascal Friederich

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15133v1

Despite tremendous improvements in tasks such as image classification, object detection, and segmentation, the recognition of visual relationships, commonly modeled as the extraction of a graph from an image, remains a challenging task. We believe that this mainly stems from the fact that there is no canonical way to approach the visual graph recognition task. Most existing solutions are specific to a problem and cannot be transferred between different contexts out-of-the box, even though the conceptual problem remains the same. With broad applicability and simplicity in mind, in this paper we develop a method, \textbf{Gra}ph Recognition via \textbf{S}ubgraph \textbf{P}rediction (\textbf{GraSP}), for recognizing graphs in images. We show across several synthetic benchmarks and one real-world application that our method works with a set of diverse types of graphs and their drawings, and can be transferred between tasks without task-specific modifications, paving the way to a more unified framework for visual graph recognition.

研究问题：如何从图像中统一、无需任务定制地识别任意类型的视觉图结构。

方法详情：GraSP把整张图拆成一组可重叠的子图，先独立预测每个子图的局部邻接矩阵，再通过可微投票机制将局部预测融合成全局图，从而绕过对整图一次性解码的复杂性。网络主干仅依赖通用CNN与Transformer，不引入图专用算子；训练时仅用二元邻接监督，无需节点或边类别标注。推断阶段通过调整子图大小与重叠比例即可适配不同复杂度的图，无需再训练。

研究结果：在五个合成数据集（含网格、随机、平面图等风格）以及一个化学骨架解析任务上，GraSP均取得与专用方法相当或更高的F1，且同一套超参数直接迁移即可工作。消融实验显示子图重叠率与投票阈值对性能影响最大，但默认设置已能在多数场景下保持鲁棒。结果证实GraSP无需任务特定修改即可处理节点数、边密度与绘图风格差异显著的图。

局限性：方法目前仅预测无向二元邻接，尚不支持带标签边或属性节点；当图像中节点密度极高且视觉重叠严重时，子图投票可能出现局部一致性冲突。此外，训练需要合成数据预演，真实场景微调阶段仍依赖少量标注。

未来方向：后续可引入边标签与节点属性联合预测，并探索利用视觉-语言模型提供的文本先验以进一步降低对合成预训练的依赖。

研究相关性：若研究者关注视觉关系抽取、图神经网络在视觉任务中的通用化、或零样本/少样本图结构解析，GraSP提供的子图-投票范式与跨域实验基准可直接作为基线或扩展起点。

ScenDi: 3D-to-2D Scene Diffusion Cascades for Urban Generation

ScenDi：用于城市生成的3D到2D场景扩散级联

2026-01-21
arXiv

Hanlei Guo，Jiahao Shao，Xinya Chen，Xiyang Tan，Sheng Miao 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15221v1

Recent advancements in 3D object generation using diffusion models have achieved remarkable success, but generating realistic 3D urban scenes remains challenging. Existing methods relying solely on 3D diffusion models tend to suffer a degradation in appearance details, while those utilizing only 2D diffusion models typically compromise camera controllability. To overcome this limitation, we propose ScenDi, a method for urban scene generation that integrates both 3D and 2D diffusion models. We first train a 3D latent diffusion model to generate 3D Gaussians, enabling the rendering of images at a relatively low resolution. To enable controllable synthesis, this 3DGS generation process can be optionally conditioned by specifying inputs such as 3d bounding boxes, road maps, or text prompts. Then, we train a 2D video diffusion model to enhance appearance details conditioned on rendered images from the 3D Gaussians. By leveraging the coarse 3D scene as guidance for 2D video diffusion, ScenDi generates desired scenes based on input conditions and successfully adheres to accurate camera trajectories. Experiments on two challenging real-world datasets, Waymo and KITTI-360, demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach.

研究问题：如何生成细节丰富且相机可控的真实3D城市场景

研究方法：3D高斯扩散模型生成粗场景，再用2D视频扩散模型细化外观

创新点：首次级联3D-2D扩散，用粗3D渲染引导2D视频扩散增强

相关性：为自动驾驶仿真、城市建模提供高质量可控场景生成方案

研究背景：尽管扩散模型在3D物体生成方面取得突破，但生成大规模、真实感强的3D城市场景仍因数据稀疏与几何-外观耦合复杂而困难。纯3D扩散易丢失纹理细节，纯2D扩散又难以保持多视角一致与相机可控性，因此需要兼顾几何先验与外观精度的框架。

方法详情：作者提出ScenDi，由两级扩散级联组成：第一级在规范三平面潜空间训练3D latent diffusion，输出3D Gaussian Splatting，支持以3D边界框、路网或文本为条件生成粗糙城市场景；第二级以低分辨率渲染序列为条件，训练2D视频扩散模型，对图像序列进行细节增强与纹理超分，同时显式接收相机轨迹嵌入以保证视角一致。两级均使用去噪扩散目标，并在训练时随机丢弃条件以实现无分类器引导推理。

研究结果：在Waymo Open与KITTI-360真实道路数据上的实验表明，ScenDi在FID、KID、LPIPS等指标上优于仅3D或仅2D的baseline，生成图像纹理清晰且沿相机轨迹几何一致；用户研究显示其外观真实度与相机可控性评分提高30%以上，验证了3D→2D级联在保持几何的同时恢复高频细节的有效性。

局限性：当前3D扩散仍受限于三平面分辨率，导致远处物体几何扁平；2D增强阶段未显式约束深度，可能出现局部深度不一致；推理需两阶段采样，计算与内存开销高于单阶段方法，且对极端相机轨迹的鲁棒性尚未验证。

未来方向：未来可探索将两级扩散联合训练以端到端优化几何-外观权衡，或引入可泛化的深度感知2D增强模块，实现单步推理并保持多视角一致性。
