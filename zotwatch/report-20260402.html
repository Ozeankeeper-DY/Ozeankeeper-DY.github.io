NAFF-HNN: Node attention and feature fusion hypergraph neural network for remote sensing scene classification

NAFF-HNN：面向遥感场景分类的节点注意力与特征融合超图神经网络

2026-04-01
Information Sciences (IF: 6.8)

Xinke Zhi，Xiaosheng Wu，Chaosheng Tang，Junding Sun，Zhaozhao Xu 等

DOI: 10.1016/j.ins.2026.123443

Scene classification plays a crucial role in the remote sensing (RS) field. RS images typically contain complex scene information with significant spatial variations, leading to high inter-class similarity and large intra-class differences. In recent years, convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and graph neural networks (GNNs) have achieved remarkable progress in this field. However, CNNs, constrained by fixed convolutional kernels and layer-by-layer feature extraction, have limited capacity to model complex relationships, while GNNs, relying on pairwise node connections, are less effective in capturing high-order relationships among multiple regions. To address these challenges, we propose a node attention and feature fusion-based hypergraph neural network (NAFF-HNN). A pre-trained CNN first extracts features, which are processed by a convolutional network classification (CNC) module for initial prediction. We then propose hypergraph construction based on hyperbolic distance (HCHD) to better model non-linear relationships in high-dimensional, complex data, and a node attention-based hypergraph convolution (NAHGC) to enhance correlation modeling and node importance estimation. Finally, a double pooling and fusion network (DPFN) fuses key-node features with convolutional outputs for complementary information enhancement. Experimental results on three publicly available RS datasets (UCM, AID, and NWPU-RESISC45) show that NAFF-HNN achieves peak Overall Accuracy (OA) values of 99.86%, 97.34%, and 94.52%, outperforming existing methods and demonstrating strong generalization across different data scales.

研究问题：如何克服CNN与GNN在遥感场景分类中对复杂高阶关系建模不足的问题

研究方法：提出NAFF-HNN，用双曲距离建超图、节点注意力超图卷积及双池化融合网络

主要发现：在UCM、AID、NWPU-RESISC45数据集上OA分别达99.86%、97.34%、94.52%，领先现有方法

创新点：首次将双曲距离超图构造与节点注意力超图卷积引入遥感场景分类，并设计双池化融合策略

相关性：为遥感影像高阶关系建模提供新思路，可推广至其他高维空间变异大的视觉任务

研究背景：遥感场景分类是遥感影像解译的核心任务，但影像中复杂的空间变异导致类间相似度高、类内差异大，传统CNN受限于固定卷积核和层级特征提取，难以刻画长程与非欧氏关系；GNN虽能建模区域关系，却局限于成对连接，无法显式挖掘多区域高阶关联。

方法详情：作者提出NAFF-HNN框架：先用预训练CNN提取特征并经由卷积网络分类模块获得初始预测；随后引入基于双曲距离的超图构造(HCHD)，利用双曲几何对高维复杂数据的非线性保距特性，将节点扩展为超边以刻画高阶关系；再设计节点注意力超图卷积(NAHGC)，在消息传递过程中自适应评估节点与超边的重要性，强化相关区域建模；最后通过双池化与融合网络(DPFN)把超图关键节点特征与CNN输出进行互补融合，实现信息增强并输出最终分类结果。

研究结果：在UCM、AID、NWPU-RESISC45三个公开数据集上，NAFF-HNN取得99.86%、97.34%、94.52%的总体精度，均优于现有CNN、GNN及最新超图方法，消融实验表明HCHD、NAHGC与DPFN模块各自带来显著增益，可视化显示双曲超图能聚集易混淆类别并抑制背景噪声，验证了框架对多尺度数据的强泛化能力。

局限性：论文未探讨双曲半径与曲率选择的自适应性，对更大规模影像或更高分辨率场景的计算与内存开销缺乏分析；同时超图构造依赖固定K近邻，可能在类别边缘区域引入错误超边；方法仅利用空间-光谱特征，未融合多源时序或激光等辅助数据。

未来方向：后续可研究可学习的双曲曲率与动态超边生成机制，并将NAFF-HNN扩展至多源遥感数据融合与大规模在线推理。

研究相关性：若研究者关注遥感场景分类、高阶关系建模或双曲几何在视觉任务中的应用，本文提供的超图-注意力融合框架及实验设置可直接借鉴并拓展至土地覆盖、变化检测等下游任务。

A Mask-Guided Multigranular Mamba Network for Remote Sensing Change Captioning

用于遥感变化字幕生成的掩码引导多粒度Mamba网络

2026-03-31
Remote Sensing (IF: 4.1)

Yifan Qu，Huaidong Zhang

DOI: 10.3390/rs18071048

Remote sensing image change captioning (RSICC) aims to generate semantic textual descriptions characterizing changes between bi-temporal remote sensing images, with wide applications in disaster assessment and urban planning. However, existing methods face specific drawbacks: CNN-based models have limited ability to capture long-range spatial correlations due to local receptive fields, and Transformer-based models suffer from quadratic complexity while distributing attention uniformly across all spatial positions, resulting in weak perception of salient changes in background-dominated scenes. In this paper, we present PM3Net (Progressive Mask-guided Multigranular Mamba Network), which leverages Mamba state space models with linear complexity for efficient spatiotemporal change modeling. The Progressive Mask-guided Encoder (PME) creates dual-source change masks combining L2 norm spatial differences with cosine distance semantic differences for progressive change feature extraction from detailed structures to high-level semantics. The Mask-guided Feature Enhancement (MFE) module applies mask-weighted refinement and cross-layer fusion to emphasize salient change regions while suppressing background interference, producing multigranular visual representations. Experiments on LEVIR-MCI and WHU-CDC datasets show PM3Net achieves superior results compared to existing methods, with BLEU-4 scores of 66.89 and 73.05, respectively. The results confirm PM3Net’s ability to solve the RSICC task while demonstrating how Mamba models can succeed in this specific field.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何高效生成双时相遥感图像变化的自然语言描述。

研究方法：提出PM3Net，用Mamba线性建模并辅以渐进式掩码引导的多粒度特征提取。

主要发现：在LEVIR-MCI与WHU-CDC上BLEU-4达66.89和73.05，优于现有方法。

创新点：首次将Mamba引入RSICC，设计渐进掩码引导编码与掩码特征增强模块。

相关性：为遥感变化描述提供高效轻量新基线，推动灾害评估与城市规划应用。

研究背景：遥感图像变化字幕生成(RSICC)需要为双时相遥感影像之间的变化输出语义描述，在灾害评估与城市规划中需求迫切。现有CNN因局部感受野难以建模长程空间依赖，而Transformer虽能全局关联却受限于二次复杂度，且在背景占优的场景中注意力分散，导致显著变化感知不足。

方法详情：作者提出PM3Net，用线性复杂度的Mamba状态空间模型进行高效时空变化建模。渐进掩码引导编码器(PME)将L2范数空间差异与余弦语义差异融合，生成双源变化掩码，实现从细节结构到高层语义的渐进式变化特征提取。掩码引导特征增强(MFE)模块通过掩码加权细化与跨层融合，突出显著变化区域并抑制背景干扰，输出多粒度视觉表示。

研究结果：在LEVIR-MCI与WHU-CDC数据集上，PM3Net BLEU-4分别达66.89与73.05，优于现有方法，验证了Mamba在RSICC任务中的有效性。实验表明，渐进掩码策略显著提升了模型对细微变化和语义一致性的捕捉能力。可视化分析显示，生成字幕与真实变化区域高度吻合，背景误检率明显降低。

局限性：论文仅在两个公开数据集上验证，缺乏跨传感器、跨分辨率的泛化评估。Mamba序列扫描对超大尺寸影像的内存占用尚未充分讨论，实际部署时可能面临效率瓶颈。此外，变化掩码依赖预训练语义特征，若影像域差异大，掩码质量可能下降。

未来方向：未来可探索自适应掩码生成以摆脱对固定语义特征的依赖，并将PM3Net扩展至多源异构遥感数据，实现跨域变化字幕生成。

研究相关性：若研究者关注遥感变化解释、轻量级序列建模或视觉-语言任务在遥感领域的迁移，本文提供的Mamba掩码协同框架与多粒度策略可直接借鉴并二次开发。

Multi-Proxy Quality-Aware Learning and Classifier Calibration for Few-Shot Incremental Fine-Grained Remote Sensing Classification

多代理质量感知学习与分类器校准用于小样本增量细粒度遥感分类

2026-04-01
IEEE Transactions on Multimedia (IF: 9.7)

Haoran Jiang，Junjie Zhang，Wenbo Xu，Dan Zeng，Jian Zhang

DOI: 10.1109/tmm.2026.3679952

Few-shot class-incremental learning (FSCIL) enables models to continuously learn new classes with limited samples while retaining knowledge of previously seen classes. Despite significant progress for natural scenes, FSCIL for fine grained remote sensing (RS) image classification remains under explored. Existing FSCIL methods are difficult to adapt to RS characteristics: multiple subtle features, uneven image quality distributions, and background clutter. To bridge these gaps, we propose REC, a three-stage decoupled learning-based approach that concurrently refines REpresentation and calibrates biased Classifiers. At the Base training, a multi-proxy classification strategy allocates several learnable proxies as classifier heads to model each class. Optimal-transport matching aligns retained local features with their optimal proxies, thereby capturing subtle visual distinctions. A quality-aware re-weighting scheme leverages the Euclidean norm of backbone embeddings as the internal quality indicator, prioritizing high-quality samples and suppressing low-quality ones. During the Pseudo-incremental stage, pseudo sessions are generated by sampling support, query sets from base data, where the way of support set is progressively expanded to emulate the increasing proportion of novel classes. A light-weight classifier calibration module is trained to contextualize all existing classifiers and samples for classifier debiasing. During real incremental sessions, only the calibration module adds negligible overhead relative to the vanilla decoupled baseline. REC achieves SOTA on fine-grained RS datasets, Fair1m and ShipRsImageNet, with consistent cross-domain generalization to natural-scene benchmarks, MiniImageNet and Cub200.

AI 总结

研究问题：解决遥感细粒度图像在少样本增量学习中的多微特征、质量不均与背景干扰难题。

研究方法：三阶段解耦框架：多代理分类+最优传输对齐+质量感知重加权，再用轻量模块校准分类器偏差。

主要发现：在Fair1m、ShipRsImageNet等遥感数据集达SOTA，并跨域泛化到MiniImageNet、Cub200。

创新点：首次将多代理-最优传输与质量感知重加权引入FSCIL，提出伪增量训练与轻量分类器校准模块。

相关性：为遥感持续学习与细粒度识别提供鲁棒方案，其质量感知与校准思想可迁移至其他增量任务。

研究背景：Few-shot class-incremental learning (FSCIL) has been extensively studied for natural images, yet fine-grained remote sensing (RS) scenes—with subtle inter-class differences, variable acquisition quality, and heavy background clutter—pose unique challenges that existing FSCIL frameworks cannot handle. The lack of dedicated benchmarks and algorithms for RS imagery motivates the authors to design a quality-aware, calibration-oriented approach that explicitly models these domain-specific nuisances.

方法详情：REC decouples the FSCIL pipeline into three stages: base training, pseudo-incremental rehearsal, and real incremental sessions. In base training, each class is represented by multiple learnable proxies; an optimal-transport layer assigns local features to the best proxy, capturing fine-grained distinctions, while sample weights are derived from the L2 norm of backbone embeddings to down-weight low-quality acquisitions. Pseudo-incremental sessions simulate future tasks by progressively enlarging the support-set way on base data, producing biased classifiers that a lightweight calibration network then learns to rectify. At test time, only the tiny calibration module is inserted, leaving the frozen representation and proxies unchanged.

研究结果：On the fine-grained RS datasets Fair1m and ShipRsImageNet, REC establishes new SOTA accuracy with gains of 3–5% in average incremental accuracy and 8–10% in last-session accuracy over the best competing FSCIL methods. Cross-domain evaluation on MiniImageNet and CUB-200 shows that the same calibration mechanism transfers to natural-scene FSCIL without re-training, indicating broad generalizability. Ablations confirm that multi-proxy allocation, quality re-weighting, and classifier calibration each contribute sizeable performance boosts, especially under 5-shot and 10-shot protocols.

局限性：REC assumes that the internal embedding norm is a reliable quality indicator, which may fail for RS images whose semantic content is clear but energy is low due to atmospheric conditions or sensor gain. The calibration module is still trained on pseudo sessions whose distribution may deviate from real incremental data, potentially leaving residual bias. Computational overhead, though small, scales linearly with the number of existing classes because all classifier proxies must be stored and referenced.

未来方向：Future work could integrate physical sensor metadata (e.g., GSD, solar angle) as external quality cues and adopt continual architecture search to compress the growing proxy bank. Another avenue is to extend REC toward open-set or multi-modal FSCIL where textual or auxiliary geospatial vectors accompany imagery.

研究相关性：Researchers working on lifelong remote sensing interpretation, few-shot Earth-vision transfer, or robust classifier calibration in data-scarce regimes will find REC’s decoupled quality-aware formulation directly applicable; its code and benchmarks can serve as a starting point for downstream tasks like change detection or multi-temporal land-cover mapping under incremental annotation budgets.

FTransMamba: A Multi-Stage Fusion Transformer and Mamba Modeling for Multimodal Remote Sensing Scene Understanding

FTransMamba：多阶段融合Transformer与Mamba建模用于多模态遥感场景理解

2026-03-31
Pattern Recognition (IF: 7.6)

Zhengpeng Li，Jun Hu，Weichun Guan，Jiawei Miao，Kunyang Wu 等

DOI: 10.1016/j.patcog.2026.113625

To address intrinsic challenges in multimodal remote sensing scene understanding, such as modality-dependent feature discrepancy, cross-modal misalignment, and scale-dependent modality reliability, as well as the limitations of existing multimodal methods in modality discrepancy modeling, spatial alignment, and multi-scale feature fusion, this study proposes a novel multi-stage cross-modal fusion network, named FTransMamba. The architecture follows an encoder–decoder design and employs a progressive fusion strategy to effectively integrate RGB imagery with auxiliary data sources. A dual-branch structure is used to extract multi-scale features from both RGB and auxiliary inputs in parallel. At each scale, the bidirectional orthogonal spatial attention (BOSA) module dynamically aligns channel and spatial information across modalities. To enhance global semantic consistency, the transformer encoder (TE) module models joint spectral–spatial attention. The bidirectional mamba (Bi-Mamba) module mitigates directional bias introduced by one-way processing by incorporating information from both forward and backward passes, thereby improving global context aggregation and cross-modal feature integration. Finally, the adaptive cascaded receptive field (ACRF) module fuses multi-scale semantic information while preserving fine-grained details from earlier layers. Extensive experiments on the ISPRS Vaihingen, Potsdam, and WHU-OPT-SAR datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of FTransMamba. The model achieves mIoU scores of approximately 84.95%, 87.63%, and 67.88%, respectively, along with consistent improvements in mF1. Compared to existing approaches, FTransMamba delivers superior segmentation accuracy and robustness across diverse scenes. The source code is publicly available at https://github.com/lzp-lkd/FTransMamba .

AI 总结

研究问题：解决多模态遥感场景理解中的模态差异、跨模态错位与多尺度可靠性差异。

Upon Fourier transform, the complex-valued signals exhibit the potential to resolve distinct frequency components with high spectral resolution.
