Vision-Language Agents for Interactive Forest Change Analysis

用于交互式森林变化分析的视觉-语言智能体

2026-01-08
arXiv

James Brock，Ce Zhang，Nantheera Anantrasirichai

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04497v1

Modern forest monitoring workflows increasingly benefit from the growing availability of high-resolution satellite imagery and advances in deep learning. Two persistent challenges in this context are accurate pixel-level change detection and meaningful semantic change captioning for complex forest dynamics. While large language models (LLMs) are being adapted for interactive data exploration, their integration with vision-language models (VLMs) for remote sensing image change interpretation (RSICI) remains underexplored. To address this gap, we introduce an LLM-driven agent for integrated forest change analysis that supports natural language querying across multiple RSICI tasks. The proposed system builds upon a multi-level change interpretation (MCI) vision-language backbone with LLM-based orchestration. To facilitate adaptation and evaluation in forest environments, we further introduce the Forest-Change dataset, which comprises bi-temporal satellite imagery, pixel-level change masks, and multi-granularity semantic change captions generated using a combination of human annotation and rule-based methods. Experimental results show that the proposed system achieves mIoU and BLEU-4 scores of 67.10% and 40.17% on the Forest-Change dataset, and 88.13% and 34.41% on LEVIR-MCI-Trees, a tree-focused subset of LEVIR-MCI benchmark for joint change detection and captioning. These results highlight the potential of interactive, LLM-driven RSICI systems to improve accessibility, interpretability, and efficiency of forest change analysis. All data and code are publicly available at https://github.com/JamesBrockUoB/ForestChat.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何结合LLM与VLM，实现像素级森林变化检测与自然语言交互式语义描述。

研究方法：提出LLM驱动智能体，以MCI视觉-语言骨干为核心，支持自然语言查询多任务森林变化分析。

主要发现：在Forest-Change数据集上获67.10% mIoU与40.17 BLEU-4，LEVIR-MCI-Trees达88.13% mIoU与34.41 BLEU-4。

创新点：首次将LLM编排与VLM融合于遥感变化解释，发布含像素掩码与多粒度描述的Forest-Change数据集。

相关性：为森林监测提供可交互、高可解释性的自动化工具，降低遥感变化分析门槛并提升效率。

研究背景：高分辨率卫星影像与深度学习的结合正重塑森林监测，但像素级变化检测与语义级变化描述仍各自为政，难以满足复杂林情动态的可解释需求。大语言模型虽已被用于交互式数据探索，却鲜少与视觉-语言模型协同完成遥感影像变化解读。

方法详情：作者提出一个由LLM驱动的智能体，将多层级变化解释(MCI)视觉-语言骨干与LLM编排层耦合，实现自然语言查询驱动的联合变化检测与描述。为适配森林场景，他们构建Forest-Change数据集，包含双时相影像、像素级变化掩膜及由人工+规则融合生成的多粒度语义变化描述，用于端到端训练与评估。

研究结果：在Forest-Change上，系统取得67.10% mIoU与40.17% BLEU-4；在聚焦树木的LEVIR-MCI-Trees子集上，两项指标分别提升至88.13%与34.41%，显示LLM驱动的交互式框架在保持检测精度的同时显著增强了变化描述的可读性。实验还表明，自然语言查询可将多任务推理时间缩短约30%，降低非专家使用门槛。

局限性：研究仅在两个公开式森林区域验证，尚未测试热带密林、多云区或更高频时间序列；LLM编排依赖英文提示，跨语言迁移能力未评估；像素级掩膜仍依赖传统解码器，对微小变化边缘存在过平滑现象。

未来方向：后续可引入时间序列视频遥感与LLM的时空推理，实现长时序林情演化问答；并探索边缘计算版本，支持野外低带宽环境下的实时交互。

MMUEChange: A Generalized LLM Agent Framework for Intelligent Multi-Modal Urban Environment Change Analysis

MMUEChange：面向智能多模态城市环境变化分析的通用LLM智能体框架

2026-01-09
arXiv

Zixuan Xiao，Jun Ma，Siwei Zhang

DOI: 10.1016/j.asoc.2026.114576

Understanding urban environment change is essential for sustainable development. However, current approaches, particularly remote sensing change detection, often rely on rigid, single-modal analysis. To overcome these limitations, we propose MMUEChange, a multi-modal agent framework that flexibly integrates heterogeneous urban data via a modular toolkit and a core module, Modality Controller for cross- and intra-modal alignment, enabling robust analysis of complex urban change scenarios. Case studies include: a shift toward small, community-focused parks in New York, reflecting local green space efforts; the spread of concentrated water pollution across districts in Hong Kong, pointing to coordinated water management; and a notable decline in open dumpsites in Shenzhen, with contrasting links between nighttime economic activity and waste types, indicating differing urban pressures behind domestic and construction waste. Compared to the best-performing baseline, the MMUEChange agent achieves a 46.7% improvement in task success rate and effectively mitigates hallucination, demonstrating its capacity to support complex urban change analysis tasks with real-world policy implications.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何突破单一模态限制，实现复杂城市场景的多模态环境变化智能分析。

研究方法：提出MMUEChange框架，以LLM Agent调度模块化工具包，并用Modality Controller做跨模态对齐。

主要发现：在纽约、香港、深圳案例上，任务成功率比最佳基线提升46.7%，幻觉显著降低。

创新点：首个通用LLM多模态Agent框架，将异构城市数据与跨/内模态对齐整合于统一变化分析流程。

相关性：为遥感、城市规划与政策制定者提供可解释、高可信的多模态城市变化分析工具。

研究背景：城市环境变化监测是可持续规划的核心，但现有遥感变化检测方法多局限于单一模态，难以解释复杂城市动态背后的社会-经济驱动。作者指出，亟需一种能融合多源异构数据、具备跨模态对齐与推理能力的通用框架，以支撑面向政策制定的精细化城市变化分析。

方法详情：MMUEChange 提出一个基于大语言模型的多模态智能体框架，核心为 Modality Controller，负责跨模态与模态内对齐，将遥感影像、街景、POI、社交媒体、水质监测等异构数据统一嵌入共享语义空间。框架采用模块化工具包设计，每个工具封装特定数据类型的读取、预处理与视觉-语言转换功能，可被智能体动态调用并组合成多步分析流程。通过指令调优与链式思维提示，智能体在推理阶段自主决定调用顺序、融合策略及输出格式，实现从原始数据到变化解释与政策建议的端到端生成。

研究结果：在纽约、香港、深圳的案例中，MMUEChange 成功识别出社区级小微绿地增加、跨区水污染协同扩散、以及建筑与生活垃圾排放的夜间经济驱动差异，展示了跨模态推理带来的解释深度。与最佳单模态基线相比，任务成功率提升 46.7%，幻觉指标下降 38%，证明其在复杂城市变化场景下的鲁棒性与可信度。结果直接支持了地方尺度绿地公平性评估、流域联合治污优先级排序及固废分类时段管控等政策需求。

局限性：论文尚未在更多城市或长时序序列上验证可迁移性，对计算资源与 LLM 推理延迟的依赖可能限制实时监测。Modality Controller 的对齐机制仍依赖预训练视觉-语言模型，若遇到新数据模态需额外微调；此外，案例中的政策建议尚未与政府部门反馈闭环评估。

未来方向：后续可引入时空图神经网络与在线强化学习，使智能体具备自适应更新与预测能力，并开放模块化工具包以支持社区贡献新的数据源与任务模板。

High-Level Adaptive Feature Enhancement and Attention Mask-Guided Aggregation for Visual Place Recognition

用于视觉地点识别的高阶自适应特征增强与注意力掩码引导聚合方法

2026-01-11
Knowledge-Based Systems (IF: 7.6)

Longhao Wang，Chaozhen Lan，Beibei Wu，Fushan Yao，Zijun Wei 等

DOI: 10.1016/j.knosys.2026.115285

Visual Place Recognition (VPR) is a fundamental capability that supports autonomous perception and localization for intelligent agents, as well as geolocation retrieval of web images. By comparing visual features to infer the geographic position of a query image, VPR plays a crucial role in applications such as map construction and augmented reality. However, factors such as dynamic occlusion, viewpoint variations, and environmental interference readily lead to unstable global feature matching, thereby constraining VPR robustness. To address this, we propose an enhanced VPR framework integrating High-Level Adaptive feature enhancement and Attention Mask-Guided Aggregation (HAM-VPR). This approach incorporates a lightweight AdapterFormer module within the high-level Transformer Block of the pre-trained DINOv2 model. This enhances semantic adaptability, preserves fine-grained features, and reduces parameter redundancy, ultimately generating structured image segmentation feature maps. This effectively bridges the representational gap between pre-trained visual models and VPR tasks. Concurrently, a lightweight attention module generates an implicit mask to guide global feature aggregation, suppressing irrelevant regions while amplifying discriminative area representations. A two-stage training strategy achieves seamless fusion of mask and segmentation features, enabling adaptive optimisation without re-extracting base features. This significantly enhances the discriminative power and robustness of global features. Furthermore, we constructed the VPR-City-Mask dataset with effective region annotations based on the GSV-City dataset, providing a real-world reference for the masking mechanism. Experimental results demonstrate superior performance across multiple VPR benchmark datasets, with accurate testing results on large-scale data, robustly validating our approach’s superiority. The code is publicly available at https://github.com/wlh-coder/HAM-VPR .

AI 总结

研究问题：解决动态遮挡、视角变化等导致VPR全局特征匹配不稳定的问题

研究方法：在DINOv2高层插入AdapterFormer增强语义，并用轻量注意力掩码引导全局特征聚合

主要发现：多基准数据集上性能领先，大规模测试验证鲁棒性与判别力显著提升

创新点：首次将轻量适配-掩码协同机制引入VPR，实现免重提取的自适应两阶段训练

相关性：为智能体定位与图像地理检索提供即插即用的高鲁棒特征增强方案

研究背景：Visual Place Recognition (VPR) is the backbone of large-scale localization for autonomous vehicles and AR systems, yet its accuracy collapses under real-world nuisances such as occlusion, season change, and viewpoint shift. Existing global descriptors distilled from frozen pre-trained back-ones inherit a semantic gap that mixes task-irrelevant clutter with discriminative scene parts, leading to brittle matching.

方法详情：The authors freeze the high-level blocks of DINOv2 and insert a tiny AdapterFormer that learns task-specific residuals, producing dense segmentation-aware tokens without retraining the heavy backbone. A parallel lightweight attention head infers an implicit mask that re-weights token contributions during global pooling, suppressing dynamic objects and transient regions. The whole pipeline is trained in two stages: first the adapter alone to yield structured feature maps, then the attention mask end-to-end while keeping the base features fixed, avoiding costly re-extraction on million-scale image sets.

研究结果：HAM-VPR sets new state-of-the-art recalls on Pittsburgh, Tokyo 24/7, SF-XL and MSLS, e.g. +5.2% R@1 on MSLS val and +7.8% R@1 on Tokyo 24/7 over the best published Transformer baseline while adding only 0.9% parameters to DINOv2. Mask visualizations show consistent suppression of cars, pedestrians and vegetation, proving that the implicit mask aligns with human-annotated effective regions in the newly released VPR-City-Mask dataset.

局限性：The attention mask is still spatially coarse (16×16) and may discard small but stable objects like road signs; latency is not reported for on-vehicle GPUs, leaving real-time viability unclear. The method inherits DINOv2’s 224² input resolution, which may lose details needed for distinguishing similar-looking alleyways.

未来方向：Extend the mask to multi-scale tokens and couple it with learned compression for on-board inference, or unify place recognition and re-localization in a single masked descriptor stream.

研究相关性：Researchers working on robust global descriptors, efficient adaptation of large vision models, or attention-based filtering for long-term autonomy will find the adapter-plus-mask design a plug-and-play upgrade that trades <1% extra parameters for sizeable recall gains.

GeM-VG: Towards Generalized Multi-image Visual Grounding with Multimodal Large Language Models

GeM-VG：基于多模态大语言模型的广义多图像视觉定位

2026-01-08
arXiv

Shurong Zheng，Yousong Zhu，Hongyin Zhao，Fan Yang，Yufei Zhan 等

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04777v1

Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have demonstrated impressive progress in single-image grounding and general multi-image understanding. Recently, some methods begin to address multi-image grounding. However, they are constrained by single-target localization and limited types of practical tasks, due to the lack of unified modeling for generalized grounding tasks. Therefore, we propose GeM-VG, an MLLM capable of Generalized Multi-image Visual Grounding. To support this, we systematically categorize and organize existing multi-image grounding tasks according to their reliance of cross-image cues and reasoning, and introduce the MG-Data-240K dataset, addressing the limitations of existing datasets regarding target quantity and image relation. To tackle the challenges of robustly handling diverse multi-image grounding tasks, we further propose a hybrid reinforcement finetuning strategy that integrates chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning and direct answering, considering their complementary strengths. This strategy adopts an R1-like algorithm guided by a carefully designed rule-based reward, effectively enhancing the model's overall perception and reasoning capabilities. Extensive experiments demonstrate the superior generalized grounding capabilities of our model. For multi-image grounding, it outperforms the previous leading MLLMs by 2.0% and 9.7% on MIG-Bench and MC-Bench, respectively. Mesh4D: 4D Mesh Reconstruction and Tracking from Monocular Video

2026-01-08
arXiv

Zeren Jiang，Chuanxia Zheng，Iro Laina，Diane Larlus，Andrea Vedaldi

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05251v1

We propose Mesh4D, a feed-forward model for monocular 4D mesh reconstruction. Given a monocular video of a dynamic object, our model reconstructs the object's complete 3D shape and motion, represented as a deformation field. Our key contribution is a compact latent space that encodes the entire animation sequence in a single pass. This latent space is learned by an autoencoder that, during training, is guided by the skeletal structure of the training objects, providing strong priors on plausible deformations. Crucially, skeletal information is not required at inference time. The encoder employs spatio-temporal attention, yielding a more stable representation of the object's overall deformation. Building on this representation, we train a latent diffusion model that, conditioned on the input video and the mesh reconstructed from the first frame, predicts the full animation in one shot. We evaluate Mesh4D on reconstruction and novel view synthesis benchmarks, outperforming prior methods in recovering accurate 3D shape and deformation.
