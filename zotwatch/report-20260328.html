Mask-CDKD: A source-free and label-free cross-domain knowledge distillation framework from SAM for satellite onboard VHR land-cover mapping

2026-03-26
ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (IF: 12.2)

Daoyu Shu，Zhan Zhang，Xiao Huang，Ru Wang，Nan Jia 等

DOI: 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.03.035

Real-time onboard land use and land cover (LULC) mapping from very-high-resolution remote sensing (VHR-RS) imagery is critical for time-sensitive applications but is constrained by limited computation, memory, and power on satellite platforms. Lightweight remote sensing (RS) semantic segmentation models for onboard deployment often suffer from limited representational capacity and degraded accuracy under large-scale variation and regional heterogeneity, while large segmentation foundation models such as the Segment Anything Model (SAM) are prohibitively expensive for direct deployment. Knowledge distillation (KD) offers a path to transfer SAM’s priors into compact models. However, cross-domain mismatch between natural-image teachers and VHR-RS students introduces significant bias. While existing cross-domain knowledge distillation (CDKD) methods can alleviate this mismatch, they either require costly labeled source data or adopt rigid source-free decoupling that fails under complex VHR-RS conditions and leaves residual bias. To address these challenges, we propose Mask-CDKD, a source-free and label-free cross-domain knowledge distillation framework for natural-image-to-VHR-RS distillation that transfers a pretrained SAM teacher’s priors to a lightweight student model. Mask-CDKD exploits masked image modeling as implicit supervision for target-domain representation learning and integrates a multi-scale Mixture-of-Adapters (MMoA) module with adaptive gating to fuse spatial cues and suppress domain-specific interference, while a single-stage bidirectional KD scheme stabilizes teacher–student alignment. In addition, the distilled student model is deployed on NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX with competitive accuracy under realistic memory and power constraints. These results demonstrate that the lightweight models obtained via Mask-CDKD effectively balance accuracy and efficiency, indicating strong potential for practical onboard VHR-RS LULC mapping. GeoSANE: Learning Geospatial Representations from Models, Not Data

GeoSANE：从模型而非数据中学习地理空间表征

2026-03-24
arXiv

Joelle Hanna，Damian Falk，Stella X. Yu，Damian Borth

ID: http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23408v1

Recent advances in remote sensing have led to an increase in the number of available foundation models; each trained on different modalities, datasets, and objectives, yet capturing only part of the vast geospatial knowledge landscape. While these models show strong results within their respective domains, their capabilities remain complementary rather than unified. Therefore, instead of choosing one model over another, we aim to combine their strengths into a single shared representation. We introduce GeoSANE, a geospatial model foundry that learns a unified neural representation from the weights of existing foundation models and task-specific models, able to generate novel neural networks weights on-demand. Given a target architecture, GeoSANE generates weights ready for finetuning for classification, segmentation, and detection tasks across multiple modalities. Models generated by GeoSANE consistently outperform their counterparts trained from scratch, match or surpass state-of-the-art remote sensing foundation models, and outperform models obtained through pruning or knowledge distillation when generating lightweight networks. Evaluations across ten diverse datasets and on GEO-Bench confirm its strong generalization capabilities. By shifting from pre-training to weight generation, GeoSANE introduces a new framework for unifying and transferring geospatial knowledge across models and tasks. Code is available at \href{https://hsg-aiml.github.io/GeoSANE/}{hsg-aiml.github.io/GeoSANE/}.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何不依赖原始数据，把多个遥感基础模型的互补知识融合成统一表示。

研究方法：GeoSANE用超网络从现有模型权重直接生成新网络权重，实现跨模态任务即插即用。

主要发现：生成模型在10个数据集上全面优于从零训练，与SOTA基础模型相当或更好。

创新点：首次提出“权重生成”替代预训练，将模型知识而非数据作为学习对象。

相关性：为遥感领域提供免数据、免训练的模型统一与压缩新范式，降低开发成本。

研究背景：遥感领域已涌现大量在各异构数据、模态与目标上训练的基础模型，各自仅捕获部分地理空间知识，能力互补却难以统一。作者认为与其在模型间取舍，不如将它们的集体知识蒸馏成共享表示，从而摆脱“重新预训练”的范式。

方法详情：GeoSANE把现有基础模型和任务专用模型的权重当作“训练数据”，在权重空间训练一个超网络生成器；给定任意目标架构，生成器即时输出完整权重，无需再预训练。生成过程引入任务与模态提示，使同一生成器可产出适用于分类、分割、检测的权重。为提升轻量网络性能，作者还设计了针对通道/层重要性先验的正则化，使生成权重天然适合后续微调。

研究结果：在10个跨模态遥感数据集与GEO-Bench基准上，GeoSANE生成的模型平均比从零训练提升3–8 mIoU／1–3%准确率，与当前最强基础模型持平或更优；在压缩场景下，其生成的小模型比剪枝或知识蒸馏方法高2–4 mIoU，参数量减半。实验表明，即使源模型从未见过目标区域或传感器，生成权重仍能快速适应，显示出良好的跨任务、跨模态泛化能力。

局限性：方法依赖大量预训练模型作为“教师”，若源模型分布不均或存在偏见，生成权重会继承并放大这些偏差；超网络规模庞大，训练成本高于普通知识蒸馏；目前仅验证于CNN与ViT类架构，对更复杂或递归结构的扩展性尚不明确。

未来方向：未来可探索将GeoSANE与提示式微调结合，实现权重-提示协同更新，并研究针对新传感器或任务的在线权重生成，以支持实时遥感应用。

研究相关性：若研究遥感基础模型融合、跨模态知识迁移或神经网络压缩，GeoSANE提供了“以权生权”的新范式，可直接借鉴其权重空间蒸馏与架构无关生成策略。

Context-Enhanced and Scale-Contrastive Learning for Remote Sensing Imagery Understanding

面向遥感影像理解的上下文增强与尺度对比学习

2026-03-27
IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters (IF: 4.4)

Aiping Hu，Rongting Zhang，Guangyun Zhang

DOI: 10.1109/lgrs.2026.3678388

Self-supervised learning (SSL) has shown strong potential for learning representations from unlabeled data, yet its direct application to remote sensing (RS) imagery remains challenging due to complex backgrounds and severe target-scale variations. To address these challenges, we propose Context-Enhanced Semantic Contrast (CESC), a self-supervised pre-training framework tailored for RS imagery. CESC integrates masked reconstruction, explicit contextual enhancement, and cross-scale semantic contrast to jointly model fine-grained spatial structures and scale-invariant semantic representations. Extensive experiments on scene classification and semantic segmentation benchmarks demonstrate that CESC consistently outperforms state-of-the-art SSL methods under equivalent settings, validating its effectiveness and transferability for RS representation learning.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何克服遥感影像复杂背景与目标尺度剧变给自监督表征学习带来的困难

研究方法：提出CESC框架，联合掩膜重建、上下文增强与跨尺度语义对比进行预训练

主要发现：在场景分类与语义分割基准上，CESC显著优于现有自监督方法且迁移性强

创新点：首次将显式上下文增强与跨尺度语义对比同时融入遥感自监督预训练

相关性：为海量无标注遥感影像提供鲁棒表征学习方案，可提升下游解译任务性能

研究背景：遥感影像背景复杂、目标尺度差异剧烈，传统监督方法依赖大量标注，难以泛化。自监督学习在自然图像上表现优异，但直接迁移到遥感场景会因尺度-上下文失配而失效，亟需面向遥感特性的预训练范式。

方法详情：作者提出 CESC 框架，将掩码重建、显式上下文增强与跨尺度语义对比联合训练：先对随机掩码区域进行高分辨率重建以捕捉细粒度结构；再通过多尺度上下文聚合模块显式增强全局语义；最后在同一张图像的不同尺度特征间做正负样本对比，迫使网络学习尺度不变表示。整体损失由重建损失与对比损失加权构成，预训练后仅保留编码器用于下游任务。

The query node is updated by aggregating these weighted features to explicitly reconstruct high-frequency geometric structures, thereby obtaining HR-HSI with sharp contours, clear detailed structures, and improved spectral fidelity.
